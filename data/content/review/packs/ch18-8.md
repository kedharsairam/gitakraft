# Pack ch18-8 — Chapter 18: Freedom and Surrender

## 18:71

**Sanskrit:** श्रद्धावाननसूयश्च शृणुयादपि यो नरः ।
सोऽपि मुक्तः शुभाँल्लोकान्प्राप्नुयात्पुण्यकर्मणाम्

**IAST:** śraddhāvānanasūyaśca śṛṇuyādapi yo naraḥ |
so'pi muktaḥ śubhām̐llokānprāpnuyātpuṇyakarmaṇām

**Meaning:** Who listens faithfully, without scoffing, is freed — rising to the worlds of the good.

**Takeaway:** Listening counts. Faithfully.

**Provenance:** sa:18:71, telang:T16, arnold:D28-shared

**Telang [T16]:** "without  carping" "holy regions"

## 18:72

**Sanskrit:** कच्चिदेतच्छ्रुतं पार्थ त्वयैकाग्रेण चेतसा ।
कच्चिदज्ञानसंमोहः प्रनष्टस्ते धनंजय

**IAST:** kaccidetacchrutaṃ pārtha tvayaikāgreṇa cetasā |
kaccidajñānasaṃmohaḥ pranaṣṭaste dhanaṃjaya

**Meaning:** Did you hear with focused mind? Is ignorance-born delusion gone, Arjuna?

**Takeaway:** The teacher's final question.

**Provenance:** sa:18:72, telang:T16, arnold:D29

**Telang [T16]:** "one point only" "destroyed"

## 18:73 (अर्जुन)

**Sanskrit:** नष्टो मोहः स्मृतिर्लब्धा त्वत्प्रसादान्मयाच्युत ।
स्थितोऽस्मि गतसन्देहः करिष्ये वचनं तव

**IAST:** naṣṭo mohaḥ smṛtirlabdhā tvatprasādānmayācyuta |
sthito'smi gatasandehaḥ kariṣye vacanaṃ tava

**Meaning:** Arjuna said: delusion gone. Memory regained by Your grace. Doubts fled. I stand ready. I will do Your word.

**Takeaway:** The student's final answer. War can begin.

**Provenance:** sa:18:73, telang:T17+T18, arnold:D30

**Telang [T17]:** "Ar^una said :"
**Telang [T18]:** "recollect" "doubts"

## 18:74 (सञ्जय)

**Sanskrit:** इत्यहं वासुदेवस्य पार्थस्य च महात्मनः ।
संवादमिममश्रौषमद्भुतं रोमहर्षणम्

**IAST:** ityahaṃ vāsudevasya pārthasya ca mahātmanaḥ |
saṃvādamimamaśrauṣamadbhutaṃ romaharṣaṇam

**Meaning:** Sanjaya said: thus I heard this wondrous, hair-raising dialogue of Krishna and the great-souled prince.

**Takeaway:** The narrator signs his report.

**Provenance:** sa:18:74, telang:T19+T20-head, arnold:D31-shared

**Telang [T19]:** "Sa«^aya said ;"
**Telang [T20]:** "hear this dialogue" "stand on end"

## 18:75

**Sanskrit:** व्यासप्रसादाच्छ्रुतवानेतद्गुह्यमहं परम् ।
योगं योगेश्वरात्कृष्णात्साक्षात्कथयतः स्वयम्

**IAST:** vyāsaprasādācchrutavānetadguhyamahaṃ param |
yogaṃ yogeśvarātkṛṣṇātsākṣātkathayataḥ svayam

**Meaning:** By Vyasa's grace I heard this supreme mystery of devotion. From Krishna Himself. Lord of discipline. Speaking in person.

**Takeaway:** Lineage stated. Source named.

**Provenance:** sa:18:75, telang:T20, arnold:D31-shared

**Telang [T20]:** "favour of Vydsa" "in person"

## 18:76

**Sanskrit:** राजन्संस्मृत्य संस्मृत्य संवादमिममद्भुतम् ।
केशवार्जुनयोः पुण्यं हृष्यामि च मुहुर्मुहुः

**IAST:** rājansaṃsmṛtya saṃsmṛtya saṃvādamimamadbhutam |
keśavārjunayoḥ puṇyaṃ hṛṣyāmi ca muhurmuhuḥ

**Meaning:** Remembering this holy dialogue again and again, O king, I rejoice endlessly.

**Takeaway:** Joy on repeat.

**Provenance:** sa:18:76, telang:T20, arnold:D31-shared

**Telang [T20]:** "holy dialogue" "rejoice over"

## 18:77

**Sanskrit:** तच्च संस्मृत्य संस्मृत्य रूपमत्यद्भुतं हरेः ।
विस्मयो मे महान् राजन्हृष्यामि च पुनः पुनः

**IAST:** tacca saṃsmṛtya saṃsmṛtya rūpamatyadbhutaṃ hareḥ |
vismayo me mahān rājanhṛṣyāmi ca punaḥ punaḥ

**Meaning:** Remembering Hari's awesome form again and again — great is my wonder, and my joy.

**Takeaway:** Wonder on repeat.

**Provenance:** sa:18:77, telang:T20+T22-head, arnold:D31-shared

**Notes:** Split across T20-tail/T22-head (page-131 T21 between).

**Telang [T20]:** "remembering that"
**Telang [T22]:** "wonderful form" "amazement"

## 18:78

**Sanskrit:** यत्र योगेश्वरः कृष्णो यत्र पार्थो धनुर्धरः ।
तत्र श्रीर्विजयो भूतिर्ध्रुवा नीतिर्मतिर्मम

**IAST:** yatra yogeśvaraḥ kṛṣṇo yatra pārtho dhanurdharaḥ |
tatra śrīrvijayo bhūtirdhruvā nītirmatirmama

**Meaning:** Where Krishna leads and Arjuna strives. There stand fortune, victory, prosperity, and justice.

**Takeaway:** The last word: the winning side.

**Provenance:** sa:18:78, telang:T22, arnold:D31-shared

**Telang [T22]:** "archer" "victory"
