# Pack ch03-2 — Chapter 3: Selfless Action

Traditional: The Yoga of Action · Verses 3:11–3:20 · Status: **pending**

## 3:11

**Sanskrit:** देवान्भावयतानेन ते देवा भावयन्तु वः ।
परस्परं भावयन्तः श्रेयः परमवाप्स्यथ

**IAST:** devānbhāvayatānena te devā bhāvayantu vaḥ |
parasparaṃ bhāvayantaḥ śreyaḥ paramavāpsyatha

**Meaning:** Nourish the gods with sacrifice, and the gods will nourish you. Nourishing each other, you will reach the highest good.

**Takeaway:** Reciprocity runs the universe.

**Provenance:** sa:3:11, telang:T04, arnold:C03-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "Please the gods" "highest good"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.86), Arnold does not (0.14)

## 3:12

**Sanskrit:** इष्टान्भोगान्हि वो देवा दास्यन्ते यज्ञभाविताः ।
तैर्दत्तानप्रदायैभ्यो यो भुङ्क्ते स्तेन एव सः

**IAST:** iṣṭānbhogānhi vo devā dāsyante yajñabhāvitāḥ |
tairdattānapradāyaibhyo yo bhuṅkte stena eva saḥ

**Meaning:** The gods give you the enjoyments you ask for. Whoever enjoys them without giving back is a thief.

**Takeaway:** Gratitude is the rent you pay for existence.

**Provenance:** sa:3:12, telang:T04, arnold:C03-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "P'or pleased with the  sacrifices" "indeed, a thief"

**Witness [gretil]:** SANDHI-JOINT

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.86), Arnold does not (0.29)

## 3:13

**Sanskrit:** यज्ञशिष्टाशिनः सन्तो मुच्यन्ते सर्वकिल्बिषैः ।
भुञ्जते ते त्वघं पापा ये पचन्त्यात्मकारणात्

**IAST:** yajñaśiṣṭāśinaḥ santo mucyante sarvakilbiṣaiḥ |
bhuñjate te tvaghaṃ pāpā ye pacantyātmakāraṇāt

**Meaning:** Good people who eat what remains after offering are freed from all wrong. The selfish, who cook only for themselves, eat sin.

**Takeaway:** Share first, eat second.

**Provenance:** sa:3:13, telang:T04, arnold:C03-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "eat the leavings" "incur sin"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.64), Arnold does not (0.00)

## 3:14

**Sanskrit:** अन्नाद्भवन्ति भूतानि पर्जन्यादन्नसम्भवः ।
यज्ञाद्भवति पर्जन्यो यज्ञः कर्मसमुद्भवः

**IAST:** annādbhavanti bhūtāni parjanyādannasambhavaḥ |
yajñādbhavati parjanyo yajñaḥ karmasamudbhavaḥ

**Meaning:** From food come all creatures. From rain comes food. From sacrifice comes rain. And sacrifice comes from action.

**Takeaway:** Everything you are stands on a chain of giving.

**Provenance:** sa:3:14, telang:T05-head, arnold:C04-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "From food are born" "Indestructible"

## 3:15

**Sanskrit:** कर्म ब्रह्मोद्भवं विद्धि ब्रह्माक्षरसमुद्भवम् ।
तस्मात्सर्वगतं ब्रह्म नित्यं यज्ञे प्रतिष्ठितम्

**IAST:** karma brahmodbhavaṃ viddhi brahmākṣarasamudbhavam |
tasmātsarvagataṃ brahma nityaṃ yajñe pratiṣṭhitam

**Meaning:** Know that action comes from the Vedas, and the Vedas from the Imperishable. So the all-seeing Vedas forever turn around sacrifice.

**Takeaway:** Trace anything back far enough and you reach mystery.

**Provenance:** sa:3:15, telang:T05, arnold:C04-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "does not turn" "lives in vain"

## 3:16

**Sanskrit:** एवं प्रवर्तितं चक्रं नानुवर्तयतीह यः ।
अघायुरिन्द्रियारामो मोघं पार्थ स जीवति

**IAST:** evaṃ pravartitaṃ cakraṃ nānuvartayatīha yaḥ |
aghāyurindriyārāmo moghaṃ pārtha sa jīvati

**Meaning:** Whoever refuses to turn this wheel here — living in sin, slave to the senses — lives a wasted life.

**Takeaway:** Opt out of the cycle and you opt out of the point.

**Provenance:** sa:3:16, telang:T05, arnold:C04-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "attached to his self only" "nothing to do"

## 3:17

**Sanskrit:** यस्त्वात्मरतिरेव स्यादात्मतृप्तश्च मानवः ।
आत्मन्येव च सन्तुष्टस्तस्य कार्यं न विद्यते

**IAST:** yastvātmaratireva syādātmatṛptaśca mānavaḥ |
ātmanyeva ca santuṣṭastasya kāryaṃ na vidyate

**Meaning:** But one who delights only in the self, content in the self, pleased with the self — has nothing left to do.

**Takeaway:** The exception that proves the whole teaching.

**Provenance:** sa:3:17, telang:T05, arnold:C04-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "no interest at all" "dependent"

## 3:18

**Sanskrit:** नैव तस्य कृतेनार्थो नाकृतेनेह कश्चन ।
न चास्य सर्वभूतेषु कश्चिदर्थव्यपाश्रयः

**IAST:** naiva tasya kṛtenārtho nākṛteneha kaścana |
na cāsya sarvabhūteṣu kaścidarthavyapāśrayaḥ

**Meaning:** Such a person gains nothing by acting and loses nothing by resting. And nobody's help does such a one need.

**Takeaway:** Need nothing, owe nothing. (Almost nobody lives here — yet.)

**Provenance:** sa:3:18, telang:T05, arnold:C04-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "perform action" "Supreme"

## 3:19

**Sanskrit:** तस्मादसक्तः सततं कार्यं कर्म समाचर ।
असक्तो ह्याचरन्कर्म परमाप्नोति पूरुषः

**IAST:** tasmādasaktaḥ satataṃ kāryaṃ karma samācara |
asakto hyācarankarma paramāpnoti pūruṣaḥ

**Meaning:** By action alone did Janaka and others reach perfection. Act — if only to hold the world together.

**Takeaway:** The masters worked. So should you.

**Provenance:** sa:3:19, telang:T05, arnold:C04-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "6anaka" "perfection"

## 3:20

**Sanskrit:** कर्मणैव हि संसिद्धिमास्थिता जनकादयः ।
लोकसंग्रहमेवापि संपश्यन्कर्तुमर्हसि

**IAST:** karmaṇaiva hi saṃsiddhimāsthitā janakādayaḥ |
lokasaṃgrahamevāpi saṃpaśyankartumarhasi

**Meaning:** Whatever a great person does, others copy. People follow whatever such a one treats as right.

**Takeaway:** You are always being watched. Act like it.

**Provenance:** sa:3:20, telang:T05, arnold:C04-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "great man does" "follow whatever"
