# Pack ch02-4 — Chapter 2: Wisdom of the Soul

Traditional: The Yoga of Knowledge · Verses 2:31–2:40 · Status: **pending**

## 2:31

**Sanskrit:** स्वधर्ममपि चावेक्ष्य न विकम्पितुमर्हसि ।
धर्म्याद्धि युद्धाच्छ्रेयोऽन्यत्क्षत्रियस्य न विद्यते

**IAST:** svadharmamapi cāvekṣya na vikampitumarhasi |
dharmyāddhi yuddhācchreyo'nyatkṣatriyasya na vidyate

**Meaning:** Looking to your own duty as well, you must not waver. For a warrior, nothing is better than a righteous battle.

**Takeaway:** Duty is not passion — it is assignment.

**Provenance:** sa:2:31, telang:T12, arnold:B11-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "Having regard" "righteous battle"

## 2:32

**Sanskrit:** यदृच्छया चोपपन्नं स्वर्गद्वारमपावृतम् ।
सुखिनः क्षत्रियाः पार्थ लभन्ते युद्धमीदृशम्

**IAST:** yadṛcchayā copapannaṃ svargadvāramapāvṛtam |
sukhinaḥ kṣatriyāḥ pārtha labhante yuddhamīdṛśam

**Meaning:** Happy are the warriors who find such a battle come of itself — an open door to heaven.

**Takeaway:** Rare chances are doors. Walk through.

**Provenance:** sa:2:32, telang:T12, arnold:B11-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "Happy those Kshatriyas" "open door to heaven"

## 2:33

**Sanskrit:** अथ चेत्त्वमिमं धर्म्यं संग्रामं न करिष्यसि ।
ततः स्वधर्मं कीर्तिं च हित्वा पापमवाप्स्यसि

**IAST:** atha cettvamimaṃ dharmyaṃ saṃgrāmaṃ na kariṣyasi |
tataḥ svadharmaṃ kīrtiṃ ca hitvā pāpamavāpsyasi

**Meaning:** But if you refuse this righteous battle, you abandon duty and honor — and take on sin.

**Takeaway:** Refusal is also a choice, with its own bill.

**Provenance:** sa:2:33, telang:T12, arnold:B11-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "will not fight" "incur"

## 2:34

**Sanskrit:** अकीर्तिं चापि भूतानि कथयिष्यन्ति तेऽव्ययाम् ।
सम्भावितस्य चाकीर्तिर्मरणादतिरिच्यते

**IAST:** akīrtiṃ cāpi bhūtāni kathayiṣyanti te'vyayām |
sambhāvitasya cākīrtirmaraṇādatiricyate

**Meaning:** All beings will tell of your lasting shame. And for an honored person, shame is worse than death.

**Takeaway:** Reputation is currency. Spend it knowingly.

**Provenance:** sa:2:34, telang:T12, arnold:B11-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "All beings" "greater" "than death"

## 2:35

**Sanskrit:** भयाद्रणादुपरतं मंस्यन्ते त्वां महारथाः ।
येषां च त्वं बहुमतो भूत्वा यास्यसि लाघवम्

**IAST:** bhayādraṇāduparataṃ maṃsyante tvāṃ mahārathāḥ |
yeṣāṃ ca tvaṃ bahumato bhūtvā yāsyasi lāghavam

**Meaning:** The great warriors will think you fled from fear. Fallen from their esteem, you will sink low.

**Takeaway:** Cowardice has compound interest.

**Provenance:** sa:2:35, telang:T12, arnold:B11-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "masters of great cars" "littleness"

## 2:36

**Sanskrit:** अवाच्यवादांश्च बहून्वदिष्यन्ति तवाहिताः ।
निन्दन्तस्तव सामर्थ्यं ततो दुःखतरं नु किम्

**IAST:** avācyavādāṃśca bahūnvadiṣyanti tavāhitāḥ |
nindantastava sāmarthyaṃ tato duḥkhataraṃ nu kim

**Meaning:** Your enemies will mock your strength with cruel words. What could hurt more than that?

**Takeaway:** He counts the cost of walking away, rupee by rupee.

**Provenance:** sa:2:36, telang:T12+T13-head, arnold:B11-shared

## 2:37

**Sanskrit:** हतो वा प्राप्स्यसि स्वर्गं जित्वा वा भोक्ष्यसे महीम् ।
तस्मादुत्तिष्ठ कौन्तेय युद्धाय कृतनिश्चयः

**IAST:** hato vā prāpsyasi svargaṃ jitvā vā bhokṣyase mahīm |
tasmāduttiṣṭha kaunteya yuddhāya kṛtaniścayaḥ

**Meaning:** Killed, you win heaven; victorious, you enjoy the earth. So rise up, determined to fight.

**Takeaway:** Both outcomes honored. Only standing still is shameful.

**Provenance:** sa:2:37, telang:T13+T14-head, arnold:B11-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "Killed"

## 2:38

**Sanskrit:** सुखदुःखे समे कृत्वा लाभालाभौ जयाजयौ ।
ततो युद्धाय युज्यस्व नैवं पापमवाप्स्यसि

**IAST:** sukhaduḥkhe same kṛtvā lābhālābhau jayājayau |
tato yuddhāya yujyasva naivaṃ pāpamavāpsyasi

**Meaning:** Treating pleasure and pain, gain and loss, victory and defeat as the same, fight — and you will incur no sin.

**Takeaway:** The equation that runs the whole book.

**Provenance:** sa:2:38, telang:T14, arnold:B11-shared

**Telang [T14]:** "pleasure" "incur sin"

## 2:39

**Sanskrit:** एषा तेऽभिहिता सांख्ये बुद्धिर्योगे त्विमां शृणु ।
बुद्ध्या युक्तो यया पार्थ कर्मबन्धं प्रहास्यसि

**IAST:** eṣā te'bhihitā sāṃkhye buddhiryoge tvimāṃ śṛṇu |
buddhyā yukto yayā pārtha karmabandhaṃ prahāsyasi

**Meaning:** This wisdom of understanding has been taught to you. Now hear the wisdom of discipline — holding it, you will break the bonds of action.

**Takeaway:** Two paths, one destination.

**Provenance:** sa:2:39, telang:T14, arnold:B12-shared

**Telang [T14]:** "Sdhkhya" "cast off the bonds"

## 2:40

**Sanskrit:** नेहाभिक्रमनाशोऽस्ति प्रत्यवायो न विद्यते ।
स्वल्पमप्यस्य धर्मस्य त्रायते महतो भयात्

**IAST:** nehābhikramanāśo'sti pratyavāyo na vidyate |
svalpamapyasya dharmasya trāyate mahato bhayāt

**Meaning:** On this path no effort is wasted and no obstacle exists. Even a little of this discipline saves you from great fear.

**Takeaway:** Small steps count in full.

**Provenance:** sa:2:40, telang:T14, arnold:B12-shared
