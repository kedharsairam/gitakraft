# Pack ch04-1 — Chapter 4: Wisdom in Action

Traditional: The Yoga of Knowledge and Renunciation · Verses 4:1–4:10 · Status: **pending**

## 4:1

**Sanskrit:** इमं विवस्वते योगं प्रोक्तवानहमव्ययम् ।
विवस्वान्मनवे प्राह मनुरिक्ष्वाकवेऽब्रवीत्

**IAST:** imaṃ vivasvate yogaṃ proktavānahamavyayam |
vivasvānmanave prāha manurikṣvākave'bravīt

**Meaning:** Krishna said: this timeless discipline I taught to the sun-god long ago. He taught Manu, and Manu taught Ikshvaku.

**Takeaway:** Wisdom travels teacher to student, age to age.

**Provenance:** sa:4:1, telang:T00+T01, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T00]:** "The Deity said :"
**Telang [T01]:** "declared  to the sun" "highest mystery"

## 4:2

**Sanskrit:** एवं परम्पराप्राप्तमिमं राजर्षयो विदुः ।
स कालेनेह महता योगो नष्टः परन्तप

**IAST:** evaṃ paramparāprāptamimaṃ rājarṣayo viduḥ |
sa kāleneha mahatā yogo naṣṭaḥ parantapa

**Meaning:** Passed down this way, kings and sages knew it. But over long time, Arjuna, this discipline faded from the world.

**Takeaway:** Even the best teachings need retelling.

**Provenance:** sa:4:2, telang:T01, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T01]:** "royal sages" "was lost"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.60), Arnold does not (0.20)

## 4:3

**Sanskrit:** स एवायं मया तेऽद्य योगः प्रोक्तः पुरातनः ।
भक्तोऽसि मे सखा चेति रहस्यं ह्येतदुत्तमम्

**IAST:** sa evāyaṃ mayā te'dya yogaḥ proktaḥ purātanaḥ |
bhakto'si me sakhā ceti rahasyaṃ hyetaduttamam

**Meaning:** That same ancient discipline I teach you today. Because you are my devotee and my friend. And this is the deepest secret.

**Takeaway:** Truth goes to the ready heart.

**Provenance:** sa:4:3, telang:T01, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T01]:** "primeval" "mystery"

**Witness [gretil]:** SANDHI-JOINT

**Consensus [ORPHAN]:** neither source covers (telang 0.33, arnold 0.44) — check: becaus, deepest, secret, teach, today

## 4:4 (अर्जुन)

**Sanskrit:** अपरं भवतो जन्म परं जन्म विवस्वतः ।
कथमेतद्विजानीयां त्वमादौ प्रोक्तवानिति

**IAST:** aparaṃ bhavato janma paraṃ janma vivasvataḥ |
kathametadvijānīyāṃ tvamādau proktavāniti

**Meaning:** Arjuna said: you were born later, and the sun was born long before. How am I to understand that you taught this first?

**Takeaway:** A fair question deserves a straight answer.

**Provenance:** sa:4:4, telang:T02+T03, arnold:D02

**Telang [T02]:** "Arguna said :"
**Telang [T03]:** "Later is your birth" "understand"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.56), Arnold does not (0.33)

## 4:5

**Sanskrit:** श्रीभगवानुवाच
बहूनि मे व्यतीतानि जन्मानि तव चार्जुन ।
तान्यहं वेद सर्वाणि न त्वं वेत्थ परन्तप

**IAST:** śrībhagavānuvāca
bahūni me vyatītāni janmāni tava cārjuna |
tānyahaṃ veda sarvāṇi na tvaṃ vettha parantapa

**Meaning:** Krishna said: I have lived through many births, Arjuna, and so have you. I remember them all. You do not.

**Takeaway:** Memory is the difference between you and Me. For now.

**Provenance:** sa:4:5, telang:T04+T05-head, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "The Deity said :"
**Telang [T05]:** "many births" "know them all"

## 4:6

**Sanskrit:** अजोऽपि सन्नव्ययात्मा भूतानामीश्वरोऽपि सन् ।
प्रकृतिं स्वामधिष्ठाय संभवाम्यात्ममायया

**IAST:** ajo'pi sannavyayātmā bhūtānāmīśvaro'pi san |
prakṛtiṃ svāmadhiṣṭhāya saṃbhavāmyātmamāyayā

**Meaning:** Though I am unborn and undying in my being. Though I am lord of all creatures. I take charge of my own nature. And I am born — by my own power of illusion.

**Takeaway:** The unlimited puts on limits, on purpose.

**Provenance:** sa:4:6, telang:T05+T07-head, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "unborn" "am born"
**Telang [T07]:** "means of my delusive power"

## 4:7

**Sanskrit:** यदा यदा हि धर्मस्य ग्लानिर्भवति भारत ।
अभ्युत्थानमधर्मस्य तदात्मानं सृजाम्यहम्

**IAST:** yadā yadā hi dharmasya glānirbhavati bhārata |
abhyutthānamadharmasya tadātmānaṃ sṛjāmyaham

**Meaning:** Whenever goodness fades, Arjuna, and evil rises up — then I make Myself.

**Takeaway:** Help arrives on schedule: when things are worst.

**Provenance:** sa:4:7, telang:T07, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "born age  after" "establishment of  piety"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Arnold covers (0.80), Telang does not (0.40)

## 4:8

**Sanskrit:** परित्राणाय साधूनां विनाशाय च दुष्कृताम् ।
धर्मसंस्थापनार्थाय सम्भवामि युगे युगे

**IAST:** paritrāṇāya sādhūnāṃ vināśāya ca duṣkṛtām |
dharmasaṃsthāpanārthāya sambhavāmi yuge yuge

**Meaning:** Age after age I am born — to guard the good, destroy the wicked, and set goodness firmly back in place.

**Takeaway:** The most quoted promise in the book.

**Provenance:** sa:4:8, telang:T07, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "born age  after age" "establishment of  piety"

## 4:9

**Sanskrit:** जन्म कर्म च मे दिव्यमेवं यो वेत्ति तत्त्वतः ।
त्यक्त्वा देहं पुनर्जन्म नैति मामेति सोऽर्जुन

**IAST:** janma karma ca me divyamevaṃ yo vetti tattvataḥ |
tyaktvā dehaṃ punarjanma naiti māmeti so'rjuna

**Meaning:** Whoever truly knows My divine birth and work like this leaves the body and is never born again. Such a one comes to Me.

**Takeaway:** Know it for real, and you are free.

**Provenance:** sa:4:9, telang:T07, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "truly knows" "comes to me"

## 4:10

**Sanskrit:** वीतरागभयक्रोधा मन्मया मामुपाश्रिताः ।
बहवो ज्ञानतपसा पूता मद्भावमागताः

**IAST:** vītarāgabhayakrodhā manmayā māmupāśritāḥ |
bahavo jñānatapasā pūtā madbhāvamāgatāḥ

**Meaning:** Freed from craving, fear, and anger. Filled with Me. Depending on Me. Purified by the fire of wisdom. Many have entered into My being.

**Takeaway:** The entry requirements, stated plainly.

**Provenance:** sa:4:10, telang:T07, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "affection, fear" "my  essence"
