# Pack ch11-3 — Chapter 11: The Universe in One

Traditional: The Vision of the Cosmic Form · Verses 11:21–11:30 · Status: **pending**

## 11:21

**Sanskrit:** अमी हि त्वां सुरसंघा विशन्ति
केचिद्भीताः प्राञ्जलयो गृणन्ति ।
स्वस्तीत्युक्त्वा महर्षिसिद्धसंघाः
स्तुवन्ति त्वां स्तुतिभिः पुष्कलाभिः

**IAST:** amī hi tvāṃ surasaṃghā viśanti
kecidbhītāḥ prāñjalayo gṛṇanti |
svastītyuktvā maharṣisiddhasaṃghāḥ
stuvanti tvāṃ stutibhiḥ puṣkalābhiḥ

**Meaning:** Hosts of gods stream into You. Some pray in fear with folded hands. Sages chant blessings and praise.

**Takeaway:** Surrender, fear, and song together.

**Provenance:** sa:11:21, telang:T09, arnold:D14-shared

**Notes:** Arnold 11:21 runs D14->D15 ('Hail! Highest Majesty').

**Telang [T09]:** "entering into you" "joined hands"

## 11:22

**Sanskrit:** रुद्रादित्या वसवो ये च साध्या
विश्वेऽश्विनौ मरुतश्चोष्मपाश्च ।
गन्धर्वयक्षासुरसिद्धसंघा
वीक्षन्ते त्वां विस्मिताश्चैव सर्वे

**IAST:** rudrādityā vasavo ye ca sādhyā
viśve'śvinau marutaścoṣmapāśca |
gandharvayakṣāsurasiddhasaṃghā
vīkṣante tvāṃ vismitāścaiva sarve

**Meaning:** Storm-gods, sun-gods, circlers, ancestors. Twins, winds, musicians, demons, adepts. All stare amazed.

**Takeaway:** The whole pantheon, reduced to spectators.

**Provenance:** sa:11:22, telang:T09, arnold:D16

**Telang [T09]:** "Sddhyas" "amazed"

## 11:23

**Sanskrit:** रूपं महत्ते बहुवक्त्रनेत्रं
महाबाहो बहुबाहूरुपादम् ।
बहूदरं बहुदंष्ट्राकरालं
दृष्ट्वा लोकाः प्रव्यथितास्तथाहम्

**IAST:** rūpaṃ mahatte bahuvaktranetraṃ
mahābāho bahubāhūrupādam |
bahūdaraṃ bahudaṃṣṭrākarālaṃ
dṛṣṭvā lokāḥ pravyathitāstathāham

**Meaning:** Many mouths, eyes, arms, thighs, feet, bellies, dreadful teeth. All peoples terrified — and I with them.

**Takeaway:** Fear honestly confessed.

**Provenance:** sa:11:23, telang:T09, arnold:D17-shared

**Notes:** Arnold 11:23 runs D17->D18 ('Flanks, lit with sun and star').

**Telang [T09]:** "many jaws" "I likewise"

## 11:24

**Sanskrit:** नभःस्पृशं दीप्तमनेकवर्णं
व्यात्ताननं दीप्तविशालनेत्रम् ।
दृष्ट्वा हि त्वां प्रव्यथितान्तरात्मा
धृतिं न विन्दामि शमं च विष्णो

**IAST:** nabhaḥspṛśaṃ dīptamanekavarṇaṃ
vyāttānanaṃ dīptaviśālanetram |
dṛṣṭvā hi tvāṃ pravyathitāntarātmā
dhṛtiṃ na vindāmi śamaṃ ca viṣṇo

**Meaning:** Touching the sky, radiant, many-colored. Jaws agape, eyes ablaze. I shake inside, courage and calm gone.

**Takeaway:** The bravest archer unmanned.

**Provenance:** sa:11:24, telang:T09, arnold:D19

**Telang [T09]:** "touching the skies" "no courage"

## 11:25

**Sanskrit:** दंष्ट्राकरालानि च ते मुखानि
दृष्ट्वैव कालानलसन्निभानि ।
दिशो न जाने न लभे च शर्म
प्रसीद देवेश जगन्निवास

**IAST:** daṃṣṭrākarālāni ca te mukhāni
dṛṣṭvaiva kālānalasannibhāni |
diśo na jāne na labhe ca śarma
prasīda deveśa jagannivāsa

**Meaning:** These tusk-ringed mouths glow like doomsday fire. I lose my bearings and my peace. Be gracious, all-pervading Lord.

**Takeaway:** Lost directions. One address for mercy.

**Provenance:** sa:11:25, telang:T09+T11-head, arnold:D20-shared

**Notes:** Split across T09-tail/T11-head (running head T10 between). Arnold 11:25 runs D20->D21 ('Fierce as those flames').

**Telang [T09]:** "terrible by the jaws"
**Telang [T11]:** "directions" "no comfort"

## 11:26

**Sanskrit:** अमी च त्वां धृतराष्ट्रस्य पुत्राः
सर्वे सहैवावनिपालसंघैः ।
भीष्मो द्रोणः सूतपुत्रस्तथासौ
सहास्मदीयैरपि योधमुख्यैः

**IAST:** amī ca tvāṃ dhṛtarāṣṭrasya putrāḥ
sarve sahaivāvanipālasaṃghaiḥ |
bhīṣmo droṇaḥ sūtaputrastathāsau
sahāsmadīyairapi yodhamukhyaiḥ

**Meaning:** Dhritarashtra's sons with all their kings. Bhishma, Drona, Karna. Our own captains too.

**Takeaway:** The muster roll of the doomed.

**Provenance:** sa:11:26, telang:T11, arnold:D22

**Telang [T11]:** "Dhrftarash/ra" "principal warriors"

## 11:27

**Sanskrit:** वक्त्राणि ते त्वरमाणा विशन्ति
दंष्ट्राकरालानि भयानकानि ।
केचिद्विलग्ना दशनान्तरेषु
संदृश्यन्ते चूर्णितैरुत्तमाङ्गैः

**IAST:** vaktrāṇi te tvaramāṇā viśanti
daṃṣṭrākarālāni bhayānakāni |
kecidvilagnā daśanāntareṣu
saṃdṛśyante cūrṇitairuttamāṅgaiḥ

**Meaning:** All rush into Your dreadful toothed mouths. Some stick, heads crushed, between the teeth.

**Takeaway:** The war's ending, previewed.

**Provenance:** sa:11:27, telang:T11, arnold:D23-shared

**Notes:** Arnold simile runs D23->D24 ('Like streams down-driven').

**Telang [T11]:** "rapidly  entering" "between the  teeth"

## 11:28

**Sanskrit:** यथा नदीनां बहवोऽम्बुवेगाः
समुद्रमेवाभिमुखा द्रवन्ति ।
तथा तवामी नरलोकवीरा
विशन्ति वक्त्राण्यभिविज्वलन्ति

**IAST:** yathā nadīnāṃ bahavo'mbuvegāḥ
samudramevābhimukhā dravanti |
tathā tavāmī naralokavīrā
viśanti vaktrāṇyabhivijvalanti

**Meaning:** As rivers race to the sea, so these heroes pour into Your blazing mouths.

**Takeaway:** Courage flowing one way only.

**Provenance:** sa:11:28, telang:T11, arnold:D24

**Telang [T11]:** "rapid currents" "human world"

## 11:29

**Sanskrit:** यथा प्रदीप्तं ज्वलनं पतङ्गा
विशन्ति नाशाय समृद्धवेगाः ।
तथैव नाशाय विशन्ति लोका-
स्तवापि वक्त्राणि समृद्धवेगाः

**IAST:** yathā pradīptaṃ jvalanaṃ pataṅgā
viśanti nāśāya samṛddhavegāḥ |
tathaiva nāśāya viśanti lokā-
stavāpi vaktrāṇi samṛddhavegāḥ

**Meaning:** As moths dive into flame, so these multitudes hasten to their ruin.

**Takeaway:** The oldest simile for self-destruction.

**Provenance:** sa:11:29, telang:T11, arnold:D25

**Telang [T11]:** "butterflies" "blazing fire"

## 11:30

**Sanskrit:** लेलिह्यसे ग्रसमानः समन्ता-
ल्लोकान्समग्रान्वदनैर्ज्वलद्भिः ।
तेजोभिरापूर्य जगत्समग्रं
भासस्तवोग्राः प्रतपन्ति विष्णो

**IAST:** lelihyase grasamānaḥ samantā-
llokānsamagrānvadanairjvaladbhiḥ |
tejobhirāpūrya jagatsamagraṃ
bhāsastavogrāḥ pratapanti viṣṇo

**Meaning:** Swallowing all from every side, licking them with flaming tongues. Your splendor fills and scorches the universe.

**Takeaway:** Appetite as cosmology.

**Provenance:** sa:11:30, telang:T11, arnold:D26

**Telang [T11]:** "licking them over" "heating it"
