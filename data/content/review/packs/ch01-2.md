## 1:11

**Sanskrit:** अयनेषु च सर्वेषु यथाभागमवस्थिताः ।
भीष्ममेवाभिरक्षन्तु भवन्तः सर्व एव हि

**IAST:** ayaneṣu ca sarveṣu yathābhāgamavasthitāḥ |
bhīṣmamevābhirakṣantu bhavantaḥ sarva eva hi

**Meaning:** So hold the positions given to you, all of you. And protect Bhishma above everything.

**Takeaway:** Guard whatever holds everything else together.

**Provenance:** sa:1:11, telang:T06, arnold:A02-shared

**Telang [T06]:** "And therefore" "Bhishma ^ only"

**Witness [gretil]:** SANDHI-JOINT

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.50), Arnold does not (0.17)

## 1:12

**Sanskrit:** तस्य संजनयन्हर्षं कुरुवृद्धः पितामहः ।
सिंहनादं विनद्योच्चैः शङ्खं दध्मौ प्रतापवान्

**IAST:** tasya saṃjanayanharṣaṃ kuruvṛddhaḥ pitāmahaḥ |
siṃhanādaṃ vinadyoccaiḥ śaṅkhaṃ dadhmau pratāpavān

**Meaning:** To cheer Duryodhana up, old grandsire Bhishma roared like a lion and blew his conch.

**Takeaway:** A small signal can lift a whole side.

**Provenance:** sa:1:12, telang:T07-head, arnold:A03-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "powerful grandsire" "delight to Duryodhana"

## 1:13

**Sanskrit:** ततः शङ्खाश्च भेर्यश्च पणवानकगोमुखाः ।
सहसैवाभ्यहन्यन्त स शब्दस्तुमुलोऽभवत्

**IAST:** tataḥ śaṅkhāśca bheryaśca paṇavānakagomukhāḥ |
sahasaivābhyahanyanta sa śabdastumulo'bhavat

**Meaning:** Then all at once conches, drums, cymbals and horns sounded, and the noise was tremendous.

**Takeaway:** Momentum has a sound.

**Provenance:** sa:1:13, telang:T07, arnold:A03-shared

## 1:14

**Sanskrit:** ततः श्वेतैर्हयैर्युक्ते महति स्यन्दने स्थितौ ।
माधवः पाण्डवश्चैव दिव्यौ शङ्खौ प्रदध्मतुः

**IAST:** tataḥ śvetairhayairyukte mahati syandane sthitau |
mādhavaḥ pāṇḍavaścaiva divyau śaṅkhau pradadhmatuḥ

**Meaning:** Then Krishna and Arjuna, standing in their grand chariot yoked with white horses, blew their divine conches.

**Takeaway:** Answer noise with presence.

**Provenance:** sa:1:14, telang:T07, arnold:A03-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "Madhava" "heavenly" "conchs"

## 1:15

**Sanskrit:** पाञ्चजन्यं हृषीकेशो देवदत्तं धनञ्जयः ।
पौण्ड्रं दध्मौ महाशङ्खं भीमकर्मा वृकोदरः

**IAST:** pāñcajanyaṃ hṛṣīkeśo devadattaṃ dhanañjayaḥ |
pauṇḍraṃ dadhmau mahāśaṅkhaṃ bhīmakarmā vṛkodaraḥ

**Meaning:** Hrishikesha blew Panchajanya, Arjuna blew Devadatta, and fearsome Bhima blew the great Paundra.

**Takeaway:** In this army, even the instruments have names.

**Provenance:** sa:1:15, telang:T07, arnold:A03-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "Hrfshike^a blew" "Pau/^flfra"

## 1:17

**Sanskrit:** काश्यश्च परमेष्वासः शिखण्डी च महारथः ।
धृष्टद्युम्नो विराटश्च सात्यकिश्चापराजितः

**IAST:** kāśyaśca parameṣvāsaḥ śikhaṇḍī ca mahārathaḥ |

**Meaning:** The king of Kashi with his fine bow, Shikhandi, Dhrishtadyumna, Virata, and the unbeaten Satyaki.

**Takeaway:** No one on that field is nameless.

**Provenance:** sa:1:17, telang:T08, arnold:A03-shared

## 1:18

**Sanskrit:** द्रुपदो द्रौपदेयाश्च सर्वशः पृथिवीपते ।
सौभद्रश्च महाबाहुः शङ्खान्दध्मुः पृथक्पृथक्

## 1:19

**Sanskrit:** स घोषो धार्तराष्ट्राणां हृदयानि व्यदारयत् ।
नभश्च पृथिवीं चैव तुमुलो व्यनुनादयन्
