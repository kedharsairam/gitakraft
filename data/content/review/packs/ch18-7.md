# Pack ch18-7 — Chapter 18: Freedom and Surrender

Traditional: Liberation and Renunciation · Verses 18:61–18:70 · Status: **pending**

## 18:61

**Sanskrit:** ईश्वरः सर्वभूतानां हृद्देशेऽर्जुन तिष्ठति ।
भ्रामयन्सर्वभूतानि यन्त्रारूढानि मायया

**IAST:** īśvaraḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ hṛddeśe'rjuna tiṣṭhati |
bhrāmayansarvabhūtāni yantrārūḍhāni māyayā

**Meaning:** The Lord sits in every heart, spinning all beings round as on a machine by His power.

**Takeaway:** The puppeteer with love in His hands.

**Provenance:** sa:18:61, telang:T14, arnold:D24-shared

**Telang [T14]:** "heart" "machine"

## 18:62

**Sanskrit:** तमेव शरणं गच्छ सर्वभावेन भारत ।
तत्प्रसादात्परां शान्तिं स्थानं प्राप्स्यसि शाश्वतम्

**IAST:** tameva śaraṇaṃ gaccha sarvabhāvena bhārata |
tatprasādātparāṃ śāntiṃ sthānaṃ prāpsyasi śāśvatam

**Meaning:** Surrender to Him wholly. By His grace find supreme peace and the eternal home.

**Takeaway:** Total surrender. Total peace.

**Provenance:** sa:18:62, telang:T14, arnold:D24-shared

**Telang [T14]:** "every way" "tranquillity"

## 18:63

**Sanskrit:** इति ते ज्ञानमाख्यातं गुह्याद्‌गुह्यतरं मया ।
विमृश्यैतदशेषेण यथेच्छसि तथा कुरु

**IAST:** iti te jñānamākhyātaṃ guhyād‌guhyataraṃ mayā |
vimṛśyaitadaśeṣeṇa yathecchasi tathā kuru

**Meaning:** Thus I have told the most hidden wisdom. Think it through fully — then act as you will.

**Takeaway:** Freedom, after full disclosure.

**Provenance:** sa:18:63, telang:T14, arnold:D25

**Telang [T14]:** "mysterious" "act as you like"

## 18:64

**Sanskrit:** सर्वगुह्यतमं भूयः शृणु मे परमं वचः ।
इष्टोऽसि मे दृढमिति ततो वक्ष्यामि ते हितम्

**IAST:** sarvaguhyatamaṃ bhūyaḥ śṛṇu me paramaṃ vacaḥ |
iṣṭo'si me dṛḍhamiti tato vakṣyāmi te hitam

**Meaning:** Hear once more My highest word. You are dearly loved — so I speak your good.

**Takeaway:** Love is the reason for the encore.

**Provenance:** sa:18:64, telang:T14, arnold:D26-shared

**Telang [T14]:** "excellent words" "welfare"

## 18:65

**Sanskrit:** मन्मना भव मद्भक्तो मद्याजी मां नमस्कुरु ।
मामेवैष्यसि सत्यं ते प्रतिजाने प्रियोऽसि मे

**IAST:** manmanā bhava madbhakto madyājī māṃ namaskuru |
māmevaiṣyasi satyaṃ te pratijāne priyo'si me

**Meaning:** Mind on Me. Love Me. Serve Me. Bow to Me. You shall reach Me — truly promised, for you are dear.

**Takeaway:** Four imperatives and a vow.

**Provenance:** sa:18:65, telang:T14, arnold:D26-shared

**Telang [T14]:** "reverence me" "dear to me"

## 18:66

**Sanskrit:** सर्वधर्मान्परित्यज्य मामेकं शरणं व्रज ।
अहं त्वां सर्वपापेभ्यो मोक्षयिष्यामि मा शुचः

**IAST:** sarvadharmānparityajya māmekaṃ śaraṇaṃ vraja |
ahaṃ tvāṃ sarvapāpebhyo mokṣayiṣyāmi mā śucaḥ

**Meaning:** Drop every duty-code. Take Me alone as refuge. I will free you from all wrongs. Do not grieve.

**Takeaway:** The most quoted verse. Earned its fame.

**Provenance:** sa:18:66, telang:T14, arnold:D27

**Telang [T14]:** "sole refuge" "all sins"

## 18:67

**Sanskrit:** इदं ते नातपस्काय नाभक्ताय कदाचन ।
न चाशुश्रूषवे वाच्यं न च मां योऽभ्यसूयति

**IAST:** idaṃ te nātapaskāya nābhaktāya kadācana |
na cāśuśrūṣave vācyaṃ na ca māṃ yo'bhyasūyati

**Meaning:** Never tell this to the harsh, the faithless, the closed, or the scoffer.

**Takeaway:** The teaching has a lock. Respect it.

**Provenance:** sa:18:67, telang:T14, arnold:D28-shared

**Notes:** Sridhara gloss ('who performs no pious acts') dropped as footnote (T15).

**Telang [T14]:** "no penance" "calumniates  me"

## 18:68

**Sanskrit:** य इमं परमं गुह्यं मद्भक्तेष्वभिधास्यति ।
भक्तिं मयि परां कृत्वा मामेवैष्यत्यसंशयः

**IAST:** ya imaṃ paramaṃ guhyaṃ madbhakteṣvabhidhāsyati |
bhaktiṃ mayi parāṃ kṛtvā māmevaiṣyatyasaṃśayaḥ

**Meaning:** Who shares this supreme secret with My lovers, loving Me deeply — reaches Me, doubt-free.

**Takeaway:** Transmission is itself devotion.

**Provenance:** sa:18:68, telang:T14, arnold:D28-shared

**Telang [T14]:** "proclaim" "doubts"

## 18:69

**Sanskrit:** न च तस्मान्मनुष्येषु कश्चिन्मे प्रियकृत्तमः ।
भविता न च मे तस्मादन्यः प्रियतरो भुवि

**IAST:** na ca tasmānmanuṣyeṣu kaścinme priyakṛttamaḥ |
bhavitā na ca me tasmādanyaḥ priyataro bhuvi

**Meaning:** None serves Me more dearly among humans. None ever will.

**Takeaway:** The highest service, ranked first.

**Provenance:** sa:18:69, telang:T14+T16-head, arnold:D28-shared

**Notes:** Split across T14-tail/T16-head (footnote T15 between).

**Telang [T14]:** "No one"
**Telang [T16]:** "superior to him" "dearer"

## 18:70

**Sanskrit:** अध्येष्यते च य इमं धर्म्यं संवादमावयोः ।
ज्ञानयज्ञेन तेनाहमिष्टः स्यामिति मे मतिः

**IAST:** adhyeṣyate ca ya imaṃ dharmyaṃ saṃvādamāvayoḥ |
jñānayajñena tenāhamiṣṭaḥ syāmiti me matiḥ

**Meaning:** Who studies this holy dialogue offers Me wisdom's own sacrifice. So I hold.

**Takeaway:** Study as offering. Reading as rite.

**Provenance:** sa:18:70, telang:T16, arnold:D28-shared

**Telang [T16]:** "study" "knowledge"
