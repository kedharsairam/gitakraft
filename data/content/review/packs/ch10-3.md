## 10:21

**Sanskrit:** आदित्यानामहं विष्णुर्ज्योतिषां रविरंशुमान् ।
मरीचिर्मरुतामस्मि नक्षत्राणामहं शशी

**IAST:** ādityānāmahaṃ viṣṇurjyotiṣāṃ raviraṃśumān |
marīcirmarutāmasmi nakṣatrāṇāmahaṃ śaśī

**Meaning:** Among sun-gods, Vishnu. Among lights, the radiant sun. Among storm-gods, Marichi. Among stars, the moon.

**Takeaway:** The best of each class, claimed in turn.

**Provenance:** sa:10:21, telang:T07, arnold:D03-shared
