# Pack ch13-4 — Chapter 13: The Field and the Witness

Traditional: Field and Knower of the Field · Verses 13:31–13:34 · Status: **pending**

## 13:31

**Sanskrit:** अनादित्वान्निर्गुणत्वात्परमात्मायमव्ययः ।
शरीरस्थोऽपि कौन्तेय न करोति न लिप्यते

**IAST:** anāditvānnirguṇatvātparamātmāyamavyayaḥ |
śarīrastho'pi kaunteya na karoti na lipyate

**Meaning:** The imperishable Self is beginningless. Beyond qualities. Lodged in the body. It neither acts nor stains.

**Takeaway:** Present everywhere. Guilty nowhere.

**Provenance:** sa:13:31, telang:T07, arnold:D12-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "does not act" "stationed in the  body"

## 13:32

**Sanskrit:** यथा सर्वगतं सौक्ष्म्यादाकाशं नोपलिप्यते ।
सर्वत्रावस्थितो देहे तथात्मा नोपलिप्यते

**IAST:** yathā sarvagataṃ saukṣmyādākāśaṃ nopalipyate |
sarvatrāvasthito dehe tathātmā nopalipyate

**Meaning:** All-pervading space stays unstained. So the Self in every body stays unstained.

**Takeaway:** Space never gets dirty. Neither do you.

**Provenance:** sa:13:32, telang:T07, arnold:D12-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "space is not tainted" "every body"

## 13:33

**Sanskrit:** यथा प्रकाशयत्येकः कृत्स्नं लोकमिमं रविः ।
क्षेत्रं क्षेत्री तथा कृत्स्नं प्रकाशयति भारत

**IAST:** yathā prakāśayatyekaḥ kṛtsnaṃ lokamimaṃ raviḥ |
kṣetraṃ kṣetrī tathā kṛtsnaṃ prakāśayati bhārata

**Meaning:** As one sun lights the whole world, the knower lights the entire field.

**Takeaway:** One lamp per universe. It suffices.

**Provenance:** sa:13:33, telang:T07, arnold:D12-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "singly lights" "whole Kshetra"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.86), Arnold does not (0.43)

## 13:34

**Sanskrit:** क्षेत्रक्षेत्रज्ञयोरेवमन्तरं ज्ञानचक्षुषा ।
भूतप्रकृतिमोक्षं च ये विदुर्यान्ति ते परम्

**IAST:** kṣetrakṣetrajñayorevamantaraṃ jñānacakṣuṣā |
bhūtaprakṛtimokṣaṃ ca ye viduryānti te param

**Meaning:** Seeing with wisdom's eye the field-knower divide — and beings freed from nature — one reaches the supreme.

**Takeaway:** The final distinction. Then no more distinctions.

**Provenance:** sa:13:34, telang:T07, arnold:D12-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "eye of knowledge" "go to  the supreme"
