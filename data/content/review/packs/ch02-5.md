# Pack ch02-5 — Chapter 2: Wisdom of the Soul

Traditional: The Yoga of Knowledge · Verses 2:41–2:50 · Status: **pending**

## 2:41

**Sanskrit:** व्यवसायात्मिका बुद्धिरेकेह कुरुनन्दन ।
बहुशाखा ह्यनन्ताश्च बुद्धयोऽव्यवसायिनाम्

**IAST:** vyavasāyātmikā buddhirekeha kurunandana |
bahuśākhā hyanantāśca buddhayo'vyavasāyinām

**Meaning:** Here there is one firm resolve. The thoughts of the irresolute branch endlessly.

**Takeaway:** One pointed mind beats a hundred clever ones.

**Provenance:** sa:2:41, telang:T14, arnold:B12-shared

**Telang [T14]:** "one state" "manifold and endless"

## 2:42

**Sanskrit:** यामिमां पुष्पितां वाचं प्रवदन्त्यविपश्चितः ।
वेदवादरताः पार्थ नान्यदस्तीति वादिनः

**IAST:** yāmimāṃ puṣpitāṃ vācaṃ pravadantyavipaścitaḥ |
vedavādaratāḥ pārtha nānyadastīti vādinaḥ

**Meaning:** The unwise love flowery Vedic phrases. They promise heaven and declare nothing else exists.

**Takeaway:** Beware beautiful words that end thinking.

**Provenance:** sa:2:42, telang:T14+T15-head, arnold:B12-shared

**Telang [T14]:** "flowery"
**Telang [T15]:** "unwise ones" "nothing"

## 2:43

**Sanskrit:** कामात्मानः स्वर्गपरा जन्मकर्मफलप्रदाम् ।
क्रियाविशेषबहुलां भोगैश्वर्यगतिं प्रति

**IAST:** kāmātmānaḥ svargaparā janmakarmaphalapradām |
kriyāviśeṣabahulāṃ bhogaiśvaryagatiṃ prati

**Meaning:** Full of desires, heaven-bent, promising rebirth as the fruit of rites.

**Takeaway:** Wanting heaven can be its own trap.

**Provenance:** sa:2:43, telang:T15, arnold:B12-shared

**Telang [T15]:** "full of desires" "heaven"

## 2:44

**Sanskrit:** भोगैश्वर्यप्रसक्तानां तयापहृतचेतसाम् ।
व्यवसायात्मिका बुद्धिः समाधौ न विधीयते

**IAST:** bhogaiśvaryaprasaktānāṃ tayāpahṛtacetasām |
vyavasāyātmikā buddhiḥ samādhau na vidhīyate

**Meaning:** Attached to pleasures and power, their minds stolen by such talk — they never reach steady resolve.

**Takeaway:** Wanting everything is how you hold nothing.

**Provenance:** sa:2:44, telang:T14-tail+T15-head, arnold:B12-shared

**Notes:** Shares one flowing sentence with the 2:42 span (Telang renders 2:44's clause inside the flowery-talk sentence); both verses cite it.

**Telang [T14]:** "attached to (worldly)" "minds are drawn  away"

## 2:45

**Sanskrit:** त्रैगुण्यविषया वेदा निस्त्रैगुण्यो भवार्जुन ।
निर्द्वन्द्वो नित्यसत्त्वस्थो निर्योगक्षेम आत्मवान्

**IAST:** traiguṇyaviṣayā vedā nistraiguṇyo bhavārjuna |
nirdvandvo nityasattvastho niryogakṣema ātmavān

**Meaning:** The Vedas deal with nature's three qualities. Rise above them, Arjuna — beyond opposites, steady, self-possessed.

**Takeaway:** Transcend the categories; do not redecorate them.

**Provenance:** sa:2:45, telang:T15, arnold:B12-shared

**Telang [T15]:** "three qualities" "self-controlled"

## 2:46

**Sanskrit:** यावानर्थ उदपाने सर्वतः संप्लुतोदके ।
तावान्सर्वेषु वेदेषु ब्राह्मणस्य विजानतः

**IAST:** yāvānartha udapāne sarvataḥ saṃplutodake |
tāvānsarveṣu vedeṣu brāhmaṇasya vijānataḥ

**Meaning:** To the knower, all the Vedas are as useful as a water tank in a flooded land.

**Takeaway:** Outgrow the map once you have seen the territory.

**Provenance:** sa:2:46, telang:T15, arnold:B12-shared

**Telang [T15]:** "reservoir" "all sides"

## 2:47

**Sanskrit:** कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन ।
मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि

**IAST:** karmaṇyevādhikāraste mā phaleṣu kadācana |
mā karmaphalaheturbhūrmā te saṅgo'stvakarmaṇi

**Meaning:** Your right is to action alone, never to its fruits. Never let results be your motive — and never sit idle either.

**Takeaway:** THE verse. Act fully, clutch nothing.

**Provenance:** sa:2:47, telang:T15, arnold:B12-shared

**Telang [T15]:** "business is with action" "fixed) on inaction"

## 2:48

**Sanskrit:** योगस्थः कुरु कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा धनंजय ।
सिद्ध्यसिद्ध्योः समो भूत्वा समत्वं योग उच्यते

**IAST:** yogasthaḥ kuru karmāṇi saṅgaṃ tyaktvā dhanaṃjaya |
siddhyasiddhyoḥ samo bhūtvā samatvaṃ yoga ucyate

**Meaning:** Established in discipline, do your work, dropping attachment. Steadiness in success and failure — that steadiness is discipline.

**Takeaway:** Discipline, defined in one line.

**Provenance:** sa:2:48, telang:T15+T16-head, arnold:B12-shared+B13-head

**Telang [T15]:** "Action,"
**Telang [T16]:** "far inferior" "devotion of"

## 2:49

**Sanskrit:** दूरेण ह्यवरं कर्म बुद्धियोगाद्धनंजय ।
बुद्धौ शरणमन्विच्छ कृपणाः फलहेतवः

**IAST:** dūreṇa hyavaraṃ karma buddhiyogāddhanaṃjaya |
buddhau śaraṇamanviccha kṛpaṇāḥ phalahetavaḥ

**Meaning:** Action done for gain sits far below the discipline of understanding. Take shelter in understanding. The profit-hunters are pitiful.

**Takeaway:** Work for wages, or work for wisdom. Know which.

**Provenance:** sa:2:49, telang:T16, arnold:B13-shared

**Telang [T16]:** "seek shelter" "fruit"

## 2:50

**Sanskrit:** बुद्धियुक्तो जहातीह उभे सुकृतदुष्कृते ।
तस्माद्योगाय युज्यस्व योगः कर्मसु कौशलम्

**IAST:** buddhiyukto jahātīha ubhe sukṛtaduṣkṛte |
tasmādyogāya yujyasva yogaḥ karmasu kauśalam

**Meaning:** Joined to understanding, one drops both good and bad deeds here. So devote yourself to discipline — skill in action is discipline.

**Takeaway:** Skill plus detachment. That is the whole formula.

**Provenance:** sa:2:50, telang:T16, arnold:B13-shared

**Telang [T16]:** "casts off both merit and sin" "is wisdom"
