## 18:31

**Sanskrit:** यया धर्ममधर्मं च कार्यं चाकार्यमेव च ।
अयथावत्प्रजानाति बुद्धिः सा पार्थ राजसी

**IAST:** yayā dharmamadharmaṃ ca kāryaṃ cākāryameva ca |
ayathāvatprajānāti buddhiḥ sā pārtha rājasī

**Meaning:** Seeing right and wrong, fit and unfit, only crookedly — that is passionate wisdom.

**Takeaway:** Half-light, half-blind.

**Provenance:** sa:18:31, telang:T09, arnold:D14-shared

**Telang [T09]:** "imperfectly" "impiety"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Arnold covers (0.62), Telang does not (0.25)

## 18:32

**Sanskrit:** अधर्मं धर्ममिति या मन्यते तमसावृता ।
सर्वार्थान्विपरीतांश्च बुद्धिः सा पार्थ तामसी

**IAST:** adharmaṃ dharmamiti yā manyate tamasāvṛtā |
sarvārthānviparītāṃśca buddhiḥ sā pārtha tāmasī

## 18:33

योगेनाव्यभिचारिण्या धृतिः सा पार्थ सात्त्विकी

## 18:34

प्रसङ्गेन फलाकाङ्क्षी धृतिः सा पार्थ राजसी

**IAST:** yayā tu dharmakāmārthāndhṛtyā dhārayate'rjuna |

## 18:35

**Sanskrit:** यया स्वप्नं भयं शोकं विषादं मदमेव च ।
न विमुञ्चति दुर्मेधा धृतिः सा पार्थ तामसी

**IAST:** yayā svapnaṃ bhayaṃ śokaṃ viṣādaṃ madameva ca |
na vimuñcati durmedhā dhṛtiḥ sā pārtha tāmasī

**Meaning:** The will that will not drop sleep, fear, grief, gloom, or folly — that is dark resolve.

**Takeaway:** Stubbornness in the wrong things.

**Provenance:** sa:18:35, telang:T10, arnold:D15-shared

**Telang [T10]:** "despondency" "folly"

## 18:37

**Sanskrit:** यत्तदग्रे विषमिव परिणामेऽमृतोपमम् ।
तत्सुखं सात्त्विकं प्रोक्तमात्मबुद्धिप्रसादजम्

**IAST:** yattadagre viṣamiva pariṇāme'mṛtopamam |

**Meaning:** Poison at first, nectar at last, born of self-knowledge — that joy is clear.

**Takeaway:** The slow joy that lasts.

**Provenance:** sa:18:37, telang:T10, arnold:D17-shared

**Telang [T10]:** "nectar" "clear knowledge"

## 18:40

सत्त्वं प्रकृतिजैर्मुक्तं यदेभिः स्यात्त्रिभिर्गुणैः

**IAST:** na tadasti pṛthivyāṃ vā divi deveṣu vā punaḥ |
sattvaṃ prakṛtijairmuktaṃ yadebhiḥ syāttribhirguṇaiḥ

**Meaning:** Nothing on earth or in heaven stands free of nature's three moods.

**Takeaway:** No exemptions. None.

**Provenance:** sa:18:40, telang:T10, arnold:D18
