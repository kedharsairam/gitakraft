# Pack ch02-7 — Chapter 2: Wisdom of the Soul

Traditional: The Yoga of Knowledge · Verses 2:61–2:70 · Status: **pending**

## 2:61

**Sanskrit:** तानि सर्वाणि संयम्य युक्त आसीत मत्परः ।
वशे हि यस्येन्द्रियाणि तस्य प्रज्ञा प्रतिष्ठिता

**IAST:** tāni sarvāṇi saṃyamya yukta āsīta matparaḥ |
vaśe hi yasyendriyāṇi tasya prajñā pratiṣṭhitā

**Meaning:** Holding them all in check, sit devoted, with Me as the highest goal. Who masters the senses owns a steady mind.

**Takeaway:** Devotion here is a technique, not a sentiment.

**Provenance:** sa:2:61, telang:T19, arnold:B15-shared

**Telang [T21]:** "Restraining them" "under his control"

## 2:62

**Sanskrit:** ध्यायतो विषयान्पुंसः सङ्गस्तेषूपजायते ।
सङ्गात्संजायते कामः कामात्क्रोधोऽभिजायते

**IAST:** dhyāyato viṣayānpuṃsaḥ saṅgasteṣūpajāyate |
saṅgātsaṃjāyate kāmaḥ kāmātkrodho'bhijāyate

**Meaning:** Dwelling on sense objects breeds attachment. From attachment comes desire. From desire, anger.

**Takeaway:** The chain reaction, link by link.

**Provenance:** sa:2:62, telang:T19, arnold:B15-shared

**Telang [T21]:** "ponders over objects" "anger is produced"

## 2:63

**Sanskrit:** क्रोधाद्भवति संमोहः संमोहात्स्मृतिविभ्रमः ।
स्मृतिभ्रंशाद्बुद्धिनाशो बुद्धिनाशात्प्रणश्यति

**IAST:** krodhādbhavati saṃmohaḥ saṃmohātsmṛtivibhramaḥ |
smṛtibhraṃśādbuddhināśo buddhināśātpraṇaśyati

**Meaning:** From anger comes confusion. From confusion, memory fails. With reason gone, one is destroyed.

**Takeaway:** Read it backwards as an escape manual.

**Provenance:** sa:2:63, telang:T19+T20-head, arnold:B15-shared

**Telang [T21]:** "want of dis"
**Telang [T22]:** "utterly ruined"

## 2:64

**Sanskrit:** रागद्वेषवियुक्तैस्तु विषयानिन्द्रियैश्चरन् ।
आत्मवश्यैर्विधेयात्मा प्रसादमधिगच्छति

**IAST:** rāgadveṣaviyuktaistu viṣayānindriyaiścaran |
ātmavaśyairvidheyātmā prasādamadhigacchati

**Meaning:** But one who moves among sense objects with senses ruled by the self will find peace. Free of likes and dislikes.

**Takeaway:** Not escape from the world. Mastery inside it.

**Provenance:** sa:2:64, telang:T20, arnold:B15-shared

**Telang [T22]:** "self-restrained man who moves" "obtains tranquillity"

## 2:65

**Sanskrit:** प्रसादे सर्वदुःखानां हानिरस्योपजायते ।
प्रसन्नचेतसो ह्याशु बुद्धिः पर्यवतिष्ठते

**IAST:** prasāde sarvaduḥkhānāṃ hānirasyopajāyate |
prasannacetaso hyāśu buddhiḥ paryavatiṣṭhate

**Meaning:** In peace, all sorrows end; and the peaceful mind soon grows steady.

**Takeaway:** Peace first, steadiness follows. Not the reverse.

**Provenance:** sa:2:65, telang:T20, arnold:B15-shared

**Telang [T22]:** "tranquillity, all his" "becomes steady"

## 2:66

**Sanskrit:** नास्ति बुद्धिरयुक्तस्य न चायुक्तस्य भावना ।
न चाभावयतः शान्तिरशान्तस्य कुतः सुखम्

**IAST:** nāsti buddhirayuktasya na cāyuktasya bhāvanā |
na cābhāvayataḥ śāntiraśāntasya kutaḥ sukham

**Meaning:** Without self-rule there is no understanding, no contemplation — and without contemplation, where is peace? Without peace, where is happiness?

**Takeaway:** Four rungs. Miss one, miss all.

**Provenance:** sa:2:66, telang:T20, arnold:B15-shared

**Telang [T22]:** "no Steadiness of mind" "happiness for one"

## 2:67

**Sanskrit:** इन्द्रियाणां हि चरतां यन्मनोऽनु विधीयते ।
तदस्य हरति प्रज्ञां वायुर्नावमिवाम्भसि

**IAST:** indriyāṇāṃ hi caratāṃ yanmano'nu vidhīyate |
tadasya harati prajñāṃ vāyurnāvamivāmbhasi

**Meaning:** The mind that chases wandering senses loses judgment. As wind carries a boat on water.

**Takeaway:** Guard the helm.

**Provenance:** sa:2:67, telang:T20, arnold:B15-shared

**Telang [T22]:** "heart which follows" "boat astray"

## 2:68

**Sanskrit:** तस्माद्यस्य महाबाहो निगृहीतानि सर्वशः ।
इन्द्रियाणीन्द्रियार्थेभ्यस्तस्य प्रज्ञा प्रतिष्ठिता

**IAST:** tasmādyasya mahābāho nigṛhītāni sarvaśaḥ |
indriyāṇīndriyārthebhyastasya prajñā pratiṣṭhitā

**Meaning:** So, mighty-armed one: hold the senses back from objects on every side. Then understanding stands firm.

**Takeaway:** The argument returns to fortify its own walls.

**Provenance:** sa:2:68, telang:T20, arnold:B16-shared

**Telang [T22]:** "Therefore, O )ou" "restrained on"

## 2:69

**Sanskrit:** या निशा सर्वभूतानां तस्यां जागर्ति संयमी ।
यस्यां जाग्रति भूतानि सा निशा पश्यतो मुनेः

**IAST:** yā niśā sarvabhūtānāṃ tasyāṃ jāgarti saṃyamī |
yasyāṃ jāgrati bhūtāni sā niśā paśyato muneḥ

**Meaning:** What is night for all beings is day for the self-controlled; what is day for beings is night for the seer.

**Takeaway:** The awake and the asleep have swapped places.

**Provenance:** sa:2:69, telang:T20, arnold:B16-shared

**Telang [T22]:** "is awake, when it is night" "right-seeing sage"

## 2:70

**Sanskrit:** आपूर्यमाणमचलप्रतिष्ठं समुद्रमापः प्रविशन्ति यद्वत् ।
तद्वत्कामा यं प्रविशन्ति सर्वे स शान्तिमाप्नोति न कामकामी

**IAST:** āpūryamāṇamacalapratiṣṭhaṃ samudramāpaḥ praviśanti yadvat |
tadvatkāmā yaṃ praviśanti sarve sa śāntimāpnoti na kāmakāmī

**Meaning:** Waters enter the full, unmoving ocean. So desires enter the sage. He finds peace — not the desirer.

**Takeaway:** Be the ocean, not the river.

**Provenance:** sa:2:70, telang:T20+T21-head, arnold:B17+B18

**Telang [T22]:** "waters enter the ocean" "obtains tranquillity"
