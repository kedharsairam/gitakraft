# Pack ch02-8 — Chapter 2: Wisdom of the Soul

Traditional: The Yoga of Knowledge · Verses 2:71–2:72 · Status: **pending**

## 2:71

**Sanskrit:** विहाय कामान्यः सर्वान् पुमांश्चरति निःस्पृहः ।
निर्ममो निरहंकारः स शान्तिमधिगच्छति

**IAST:** vihāya kāmānyaḥ sarvān pumāṃścarati niḥspṛhaḥ |
nirmamo nirahaṃkāraḥ sa śāntimadhigacchati

**Meaning:** Dropping all desires, living free of mine-ness, ego and pride — such a one finds peace.

**Takeaway:** Four things to drop. Count them on one hand.

**Provenance:** sa:2:71, telang:T21, arnold:B19-shared

**Telang [T23]:** "casting off all desires" "obtains tranquillity"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.56), Arnold does not (0.44)

## 2:72

**Sanskrit:** एषा ब्राह्मी स्थितिः पार्थ नैनां प्राप्य विमुह्यति ।
स्थित्वास्यामन्तकालेऽपि ब्रह्मनिर्वाणमृच्छति

**IAST:** eṣā brāhmī sthitiḥ pārtha naināṃ prāpya vimuhyati |
sthitvāsyāmantakāle'pi brahmanirvāṇamṛcchati

**Meaning:** This is the state of oneness with ultimate reality. Reaching it, one is never confused; settled in it even at death, one merges into its peace.

**Takeaway:** The chapter lands where it began — beyond grief.

**Provenance:** sa:2:72, telang:T21, arnold:B19-shared

**Telang [T23]:** "Brahmic" "Brahmic bliss"
