## 17:11

**Sanskrit:** अफलाकाङ्क्षिभिर्यज्ञो विधिदृष्टो य इज्यते ।
यष्टव्यमेवेति मनः समाधाय स सात्त्विकः

**IAST:** aphalākāṅkṣibhiryajño vidhidṛṣṭo ya ijyate |
yaṣṭavyameveti manaḥ samādhāya sa sāttvikaḥ

**Meaning:** Rite done as duty, by rule, without hunger for results — that ritual is clear.

**Takeaway:** Duty, not desire, lights the altar.

**Provenance:** sa:17:11, telang:T05+T08-head, arnold:D04-shared

**Notes:** Split across T05-tail/T08-head ('persons'/'not wishing'; 't-' debris dropped).

**Telang [T05]:** "prescribed" "performed by persons"
**Telang [T08]:** "not wishing" "must needs"

**Witness [gretil]:** SANDHI-JOINT

## 17:12

**Sanskrit:** अभिसंधाय तु फलं दम्भार्थमपि चैव यत् ।
इज्यते भरतश्रेष्ठ तं यज्ञं विद्धि राजसम्

**IAST:** abhisaṃdhāya tu phalaṃ dambhārthamapi caiva yat |
ijyate bharataśreṣṭha taṃ yajñaṃ viddhi rājasam

**Meaning:** Rite done for rewards and reputation. Know that ritual as passionate.

**Takeaway:** Spectacle spoils sacrifice.

**Provenance:** sa:17:12, telang:T08, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T08]:** "expectation" "ostentation"

## 17:13

**Sanskrit:** विधिहीनमसृष्टान्नं मन्त्रहीनमदक्षिणम् ।
श्रद्धाविरहितं यज्ञं तामसं परिचक्षते

**IAST:** vidhihīnamasṛṣṭānnaṃ mantrahīnamadakṣiṇam |
śraddhāvirahitaṃ yajñaṃ tāmasaṃ paricakṣate

**Meaning:** Rite against rule, without food-gifts, hymns, fees, or faith — that ritual is dark.

**Takeaway:** Empty ritual, voided warranty.

**Provenance:** sa:17:13, telang:T08, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T08]:** "no food is dealt out" "without faith"

## 17:14

**Takeaway:** The body trained in reverence.

## 17:15

स्वाध्यायाभ्यसनं चैव वाङ्मयं तप उच्यते
