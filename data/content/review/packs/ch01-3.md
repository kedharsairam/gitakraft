## 1:21 (अर्जुन)

**Sanskrit:** हृषीकेशं तदा वाक्यमिदमाह महीपते ।
सेनयोरुभयोर्मध्ये रथं स्थापय मेऽच्युत

**IAST:** hṛṣīkeśaṃ tadā vākyamidamāha mahīpate |
senayorubhayormadhye rathaṃ sthāpaya me'cyuta

**Meaning:** Arjuna addressed these words to Krishna.

**Takeaway:** Ask clearly for what you need.

**Provenance:** sa:1:21, telang:T08, arnold:A04-shared

**Telang [T08]:** "spake these words to Hmhike^a"

## 1:22

**Sanskrit:** यावदेतान्निरिक्षेऽहं योद्‌धुकामानवस्थितान् ।
कैर्मया सह योद्धव्यमस्मिन् रणसमुद्यमे

**IAST:** yāvadetānnirikṣe'haṃ yod‌dhukāmānavasthitān |
kairmayā saha yoddhavyamasmin raṇasamudyame

**Meaning:** O faultless Krishna, drive my chariot between the two armies. Let me see who stands here eager to fight, and with whom I must do battle.

**Takeaway:** Look first. Fight later — if at all.

**Provenance:** sa:1:22, telang:T08, arnold:A04-shared

**Telang [T08]:** "station my chariot" "must  do battle"

## 1:23

**Sanskrit:** योत्स्यमानानवेक्षेऽहं य एतेऽत्र समागताः ।
धार्तराष्ट्रस्य दुर्बुद्धेर्युद्धे प्रियचिकीर्षवः

**IAST:** yotsyamānānavekṣe'haṃ ya ete'tra samāgatāḥ |
dhārtarāṣṭrasya durbuddheryuddhe priyacikīrṣavaḥ

**Meaning:** I will observe all those gathered here to fight, wishing to serve Duryodhana, the ill-minded son of Dhritarashtra, in battle.

**Takeaway:** Name why everyone is here — even the uncomfortable parts.

**Provenance:** sa:1:23, telang:T08, arnold:A04-shared

## 1:24 (सञ्जय)

**Sanskrit:** एवमुक्तो हृषीकेशो गुडाकेशेन भारत ।
सेनयोरुभयोर्मध्ये स्थापयित्वा रथोत्तमम्

**IAST:** evamukto hṛṣīkeśo guḍākeśena bhārata |
senayorubhayormadhye sthāpayitvā rathottamam

**Meaning:** Sanjaya said: so addressed by Arjuna, conqueror of sleep, Krishna stationed the splendid chariot between the two armies. It faced Bhishma.

**Takeaway:** A good friend does what you ask, no questions yet.

**Provenance:** sa:1:24, telang:T09+T10, arnold:A05-shared

## 1:25

**Sanskrit:** भीष्मद्रोणप्रमुखतः सर्वेषां च महीक्षिताम् ।
उवाच पार्थ पश्यैतान्समवेतान्कुरूनिति

**IAST:** bhīṣmadroṇapramukhataḥ sarveṣāṃ ca mahīkṣitām |
uvāca pārtha paśyaitānsamavetānkurūniti

**Meaning:** And before Bhishma, Drona and all the kings of the earth, Krishna said: O Arjuna, behold these Kurus gathered together.

**Takeaway:** Sometimes you have to look, even when it hurts.

**Provenance:** sa:1:25, telang:T11, arnold:A05-shared

## 1:26

**Sanskrit:** तत्रापश्यत्स्थितान्पार्थः पितॄनथ पितामहान् ।
आचार्यान्मातुलान्भ्रातॄन्पुत्रान्पौत्रान्सखींस्तथा

**IAST:** tatrāpaśyatsthitānpārthaḥ pitṝnatha pitāmahān |
ācāryānmātulānbhrātṝnputrānpautrānsakhīṃstathā

**Meaning:** There Arjuna saw fathers and grandfathers. Teachers, uncles, brothers, sons and grandsons — and comrades.

**Takeaway:** The enemy has faces you know.

## 1:27

**Sanskrit:** श्वशुरान्सुहृदश्चैव सेनयोरुभयोरपि ।
तान्समीक्ष्य स कौन्तेयः सर्वान्बन्धूनवस्थितान्

**IAST:** śvaśurānsuhṛdaścaiva senayorubhayorapi |
tānsamīkṣya sa kaunteyaḥ sarvānbandhūnavasthitān

**Meaning:** Arjuna saw fathers-in-law and friends on both sides — all his kin arrayed for war. Pity overwhelmed him, and he spoke in despair.

**Takeaway:** Strength that cannot feel is just armor.

**Provenance:** sa:1:27, telang:T11, arnold:A05-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "And seeing all  those kinsmen" "spake thus  despondingly"

## 1:28 (अर्जुन)

**Sanskrit:** कृपया परयाविष्टो विषीदन्निदमब्रवीत् ।
दृष्ट्वेमं स्वजनं कृष्ण युयुत्सुं समुपस्थितम्

**IAST:** kṛpayā parayāviṣṭo viṣīdannidamabravīt |

**Meaning:** Arjuna said: seeing these kinsmen come to fight, Krishna, my limbs fail me and my mouth goes dry.

## 1:29

**Sanskrit:** सीदन्ति मम गात्राणि मुखं च परिशुष्यति ।
वेपथुश्च शरीरे मे रोमहर्षश्च जायते

**IAST:** sīdanti mama gātrāṇi mukhaṃ ca pariśuṣyati |
vepathuśca śarīre me romaharṣaśca jāyate

**Meaning:** My body trembles and my hair stands on end.

**Takeaway:** Fear has a physiology.

**Provenance:** sa:1:29, telang:T13, arnold:A06-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "limbs droop" "hairs stand on end"

## 1:30

**Sanskrit:** गाण्डीवं स्रंसते हस्तात्त्वक्चैव परिदह्यते ।
न च शक्नोम्यवस्थातुं भ्रमतीव च मे मनः

**IAST:** gāṇḍīvaṃ sraṃsate hastāttvakcaiva paridahyate |
na ca śaknomyavasthātuṃ bhramatīva ca me manaḥ

**Meaning:** My great bow slips from my hand, my skin burns, and I cannot even stand steady.

**Takeaway:** Collapse is data, not disgrace.

**Provenance:** sa:1:30, telang:T13, arnold:A06-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "Gawrt^iva" "unable, too, to stand up"
