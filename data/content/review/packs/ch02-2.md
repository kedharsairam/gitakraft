## 2:11

**Sanskrit:** श्रीभगवानुवाच
अशोच्यानन्वशोचस्त्वं प्रज्ञावादांश्च भाषसे ।
गतासूनगतासूंश्च नानुशोचन्ति पण्डिताः

**IAST:** śrībhagavānuvāca
aśocyānanvaśocastvaṃ prajñāvādāṃśca bhāṣase |
gatāsūnagatāsūṃśca nānuśocanti paṇḍitāḥ

**Meaning:** You grieve for those who deserve no grief, yet your words sound wise. The wise grieve neither for the living nor for the dead.

**Takeaway:** Grief has a proper object. Find it.

**Provenance:** sa:2:11, telang:T08+T09-head, arnold:B06-shared

**Telang [T10]:** "You fiave grieved"
**Telang [T11]:** "words of wisdom"

## 2:12

**Sanskrit:** न त्वेवाहं जातु नासं न त्वं नेमे जनाधिपाः ।
न चैव न भविष्यामः सर्वे वयमतः परम्

**IAST:** na tvevāhaṃ jātu nāsaṃ na tvaṃ neme janādhipāḥ |
na caiva na bhaviṣyāmaḥ sarve vayamataḥ param

**Meaning:** Never was there a time when I did not exist, nor you, nor these kings. And none of us will ever cease to be.

**Takeaway:** You are older than your birthday.

**Provenance:** sa:2:12, telang:T09, arnold:B06-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "Learned men" "cease to be"

## 2:13

**Sanskrit:** देहिनोऽस्मिन्यथा देहे कौमारं यौवनं जरा ।
तथा देहान्तरप्राप्तिर्धीरस्तत्र न मुह्यति

**IAST:** dehino'sminyathā dehe kaumāraṃ yauvanaṃ jarā |
tathā dehāntaraprāptirdhīrastatra na muhyati

**Meaning:** As childhood, youth, and old age come to the body, so the self moves on to another body. Steady people are not confused by this.

**Takeaway:** Change of address, not end of story.

**Provenance:** sa:2:13, telang:T09, arnold:B06-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "As" "not deceived"

## 2:14

**Sanskrit:** मात्रास्पर्शास्तु कौन्तेय शीतोष्णसुखदुःखदाः ।
आगमापायिनोऽनित्यास्तांस्तितिक्षस्व भारत

**IAST:** mātrāsparśāstu kaunteya śītoṣṇasukhaduḥkhadāḥ |
āgamāpāyino'nityāstāṃstitikṣasva bhārata

**Meaning:** Cold and heat, pleasure and pain come and go with the senses, Arjuna. They are not permanent — bear them.

**Takeaway:** Feelings are weather, not climate.

**Provenance:** sa:2:14, telang:T09, arnold:B06-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "contacts of the senses" "Bear them"

## 2:15

**Sanskrit:** यं हि न व्यथयन्त्येते पुरुषं पुरुषर्षभ ।
समदुःखसुखं धीरं सोऽमृतत्वाय कल्पते

**IAST:** yaṃ hi na vyathayantyete puruṣaṃ puruṣarṣabha |
samaduḥkhasukhaṃ dhīraṃ so'mṛtatvāya kalpate

**Meaning:** The person these cannot shake — steady in pain and pleasure alike — is fit for deathlessness.

**Takeaway:** Steadiness is a superpower.

**Provenance:** sa:2:15, telang:T09, arnold:B06-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "chief" "merits"

## 2:16

**Sanskrit:** नासतो विद्यते भावो नाभावो विद्यते सतः ।
उभयोरपि दृष्टोऽन्तस्त्वनयोस्तत्त्वदर्शिभिः

**IAST:** nāsato vidyate bhāvo nābhāvo vidyate sataḥ |
ubhayorapi dṛṣṭo'ntastvanayostattvadarśibhiḥ

**Meaning:** The unreal never truly is; the real never ceases to be. Those who see truth perceive both correctly.

**Takeaway:** Learn to tell the passing from the permanent.

**Provenance:** sa:2:16, telang:T09, arnold:B06-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "no existence" "perceive the truth"

## 2:17

**Sanskrit:** अविनाशि तु तद्विद्धि येन सर्वमिदं ततम् ।
विनाशमव्ययस्यास्य न कश्चित्कर्तुमर्हति

**IAST:** avināśi tu tadviddhi yena sarvamidaṃ tatam |
vināśamavyayasyāsya na kaścitkartumarhati

**Meaning:** Know that which fills this whole world to be indestructible. No one can destroy the imperishable.

**Takeaway:** What is everywhere cannot be broken anywhere.

**Provenance:** sa:2:17, telang:T09, arnold:B06-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "Know" "bring about"

## 2:18

**Sanskrit:** अन्तवन्त इमे देहा नित्यस्योक्ताः शरीरिणः ।
अनाशिनोऽप्रमेयस्य तस्माद्युध्यस्व भारत

**IAST:** antavanta ime dehā nityasyoktāḥ śarīriṇaḥ |
anāśino'prameyasya tasmādyudhyasva bhārata

**Meaning:** These bodies, belonging to the eternal, indestructible, immeasurable self, must come to an end. So fight, Arjuna.

**Takeaway:** The conclusion lands like a hammer — on purpose.

**Provenance:** sa:2:18, telang:T09+T10-head, arnold:B06-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "These bodies" "engage in battle"
**Telang [T11]:** "killer and he who thinks"

## 2:19

**Sanskrit:** य एनं वेत्ति हन्तारं यश्चैनं मन्यते हतम् ।
उभौ तौ न विजानीतो नायं हन्ति न हन्यते

**IAST:** ya enaṃ vetti hantāraṃ yaścainaṃ manyate hatam |
ubhau tau na vijānīto nāyaṃ hanti na hanyate

**Meaning:** Whoever thinks the self kills, and whoever thinks it is killed — both are wrong. It kills not, and is not killed.

**Takeaway:** The self is not the body. Sit with that.

**Provenance:** sa:2:19, telang:T10+T11-head, arnold:B06-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "killer and he who thinks"
**Telang [T12]:** "one to be killed, both know nothing"

**Witness [gretil]:** SANDHI-JOINT

## 2:20

**Sanskrit:** न जायते म्रियते वा कदाचिन्नायं भूत्वा भविता वा न भूयः ।
अजो नित्यः शाश्वतोऽयं पुराणो न हन्यते हन्यमाने शरीरे

**IAST:** na jāyate mriyate vā kadācinnāyaṃ bhūtvā bhavitā vā na bhūyaḥ |
ajo nityaḥ śāśvato'yaṃ purāṇo na hanyate hanyamāne śarīre

**Meaning:** It is never born and never dies. Unborn, eternal, ancient — it is not killed when the body is killed.

**Takeaway:** Older than old.

**Provenance:** sa:2:20, telang:T11, arnold:B06-shared

**Telang [T12]:** "He kills not" "body is killed"
