# Pack ch04-3 — Chapter 4: Wisdom in Action

Traditional: The Yoga of Knowledge and Renunciation · Verses 4:21–4:30 · Status: **pending**

## 4:21

**Sanskrit:** निराशीर्यतचित्तात्मा त्यक्तसर्वपरिग्रहः ।
शारीरं केवलं कर्म कुर्वन्नाप्नोति किल्बिषम्

**IAST:** nirāśīryatacittātmā tyaktasarvaparigrahaḥ |
śārīraṃ kevalaṃ karma kurvannāpnoti kilbiṣam

**Meaning:** Expecting nothing. Mind and self controlled. Owning nothing. One incurs no wrong by acting merely to keep the body going.

**Takeaway:** Act for upkeep, not for hunger.

**Provenance:** sa:4:21, telang:T09, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T09]:** "Devoid of expectations" "sake of the body"

## 4:22

**Sanskrit:** यदृच्छालाभसंतुष्टो द्वन्द्वातीतो विमत्सरः ।
समः सिद्धावसिद्धौ च कृत्वापि न निबध्यते

**IAST:** yadṛcchālābhasaṃtuṣṭo dvandvātīto vimatsaraḥ |
samaḥ siddhāvasiddhau ca kṛtvāpi na nibadhyate

**Meaning:** Content with whatever comes on its own. Beyond opposites. Free of envy. Steady in success and failure. Such a one is never chained, however hard one acts.

**Takeaway:** Contentment is the only wealth that compounds.

**Provenance:** sa:4:22, telang:T09, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T09]:** "Satisfied" "not fettered"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.69), Arnold does not (0.46)

## 4:23

**Sanskrit:** गतसङ्गस्य मुक्तस्य ज्ञानावस्थितचेतसः ।
यज्ञायाचरतः कर्म समग्रं प्रविलीयते

**IAST:** gatasaṅgasya muktasya jñānāvasthitacetasaḥ |
yajñāyācarataḥ karma samagraṃ pravilīyate

**Meaning:** For one free of clinging. Liberated. Mind anchored in wisdom. Acting only as offering. Every deed fully dissolves.

**Takeaway:** Offer it up and watch it dissolve.

**Provenance:** sa:4:23, telang:T09+T11-head, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T09]:** "devoid of attachment"
**Telang [T11]:** "destroyed" "oblation"

## 4:24

**Sanskrit:** ब्रह्मार्पणं ब्रह्म हविर्ब्रह्माग्नौ ब्रह्मणा हुतम् ।
ब्रह्मैव तेन गन्तव्यं ब्रह्मकर्मसमाधिना

**IAST:** brahmārpaṇaṃ brahma havirbrahmāgnau brahmaṇā hutam |
brahmaiva tena gantavyaṃ brahmakarmasamādhinā

**Meaning:** The offering is ultimate reality. The offered, into its fire. Ultimate reality is the goal — for one absorbed in it through action.

**Takeaway:** All sacred, all the way down.

**Provenance:** sa:4:24, telang:T11, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "Brahman is the oblation" "meditates on Brahman"

## 4:25

**Sanskrit:** दैवमेवापरे यज्ञं योगिनः पर्युपासते ।
ब्रह्माग्नावपरे यज्ञं यज्ञेनैवोपजुह्वति

**IAST:** daivamevāpare yajñaṃ yoginaḥ paryupāsate |
brahmāgnāvapare yajñaṃ yajñenaivopajuhvati

**Meaning:** Some worship the gods with offerings. Others offer the act itself into ultimate reality's fire.

**Takeaway:** Many altars, one fire.

**Provenance:** sa:4:25, telang:T11, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "in the fire  of Brahman"

## 4:26

**Sanskrit:** श्रोत्रादीनीन्द्रियाण्यन्ये संयमाग्निषु जुह्वति ।
शब्दादीन्विषयानन्य इन्द्रियाग्निषु जुह्वति

**IAST:** śrotrādīnīndriyāṇyanye saṃyamāgniṣu juhvati |
śabdādīnviṣayānanya indriyāgniṣu juhvati

**Meaning:** Others offer hearing and the rest of the senses into the fires of restraint. Others offer sights and sounds into the fires of the senses.

**Takeaway:** Everything can be fuel, aimed right.

**Provenance:** sa:4:26, telang:T11, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "offer up the senses" "fires of the senses"

**Witness [gretil]:** SANDHI-JOINT

## 4:27

**Sanskrit:** सर्वाणीन्द्रियकर्माणि प्राणकर्माणि चापरे ।
आत्मसंयमयोगाग्नौ जुह्वति ज्ञानदीपिते

**IAST:** sarvāṇīndriyakarmāṇi prāṇakarmāṇi cāpare |
ātmasaṃyamayogāgnau juhvati jñānadīpite

**Meaning:** Others offer every sense-act into the fire of self-restraint. And every breath. Lit by wisdom.

**Takeaway:** The inward sacrifice needs no temple.

**Provenance:** sa:4:27, telang:T11, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "Some again" "rigid vows"

## 4:28

**Sanskrit:** द्रव्ययज्ञास्तपोयज्ञा योगयज्ञास्तथापरे ।
स्वाध्यायज्ञानयज्ञाश्च यतयः संशितव्रताः

**IAST:** dravyayajñāstapoyajñā yogayajñāstathāpare |
svādhyāyajñānayajñāśca yatayaḥ saṃśitavratāḥ

**Meaning:** Others offer wealth. Or penance. Or focus, study, wisdom. Ascetics of strict vows.

**Takeaway:** Give what you have. It counts.

**Provenance:** sa:4:28, telang:T11, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "upward" "restraint of the life-breaths"

## 4:29

**Sanskrit:** अपाने जुह्वति प्राणं प्राणेऽपानं तथापरे ।
प्राणापानगती रुद्ध्वा प्राणायामपरायणाः

**IAST:** apāne juhvati prāṇaṃ prāṇe'pānaṃ tathāpare |
prāṇāpānagatī ruddhvā prāṇāyāmaparāyaṇāḥ

**Meaning:** Others, eating little, pour one breath into another, then hold both still — devoted to breath control.

**Takeaway:** The body itself becomes the altar.

**Provenance:** sa:4:29, telang:T11+T13-head, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "Others, who (take)"
**Telang [T13]:** "limited food" "lifebreaths"

## 4:30

**Sanskrit:** अपरे नियताहाराः प्राणान्प्राणेषु जुह्वति ।
सर्वेऽप्येते यज्ञविदो यज्ञक्षपितकल्मषाः

**IAST:** apare niyatāhārāḥ prāṇānprāṇeṣu juhvati |
sarve'pyete yajñavido yajñakṣapitakalmaṣāḥ

**Meaning:** All of these know sacrifice, and sacrifice burns away their wrongs.

**Takeaway:** Every sincere method works. Pick one and mean it.

**Provenance:** sa:4:30, telang:T13, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "All of these" "eternal Brahman"
