# Pack ch18-2 — Chapter 18: Freedom and Surrender

Traditional: Liberation and Renunciation · Verses 18:11–18:20 · Status: **pending**

## 18:11

**Sanskrit:** न हि देहभृता शक्यं त्यक्तुं कर्माण्यशेषतः ।
यस्तु कर्मफलत्यागी स त्यागीत्यभिधीयते

**IAST:** na hi dehabhṛtā śakyaṃ tyaktuṃ karmāṇyaśeṣataḥ |
yastu karmaphalatyāgī sa tyāgītyabhidhīyate

**Meaning:** No embodied being can quit acting entirely. Who quits the fruits is the true surrenderer.

**Takeaway:** Hands will act. Let motives rest.

**Provenance:** sa:18:11, telang:T05, arnold:D06-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "without exception" "abandons the fruit"

## 18:12

**Sanskrit:** अनिष्टमिष्टं मिश्रं च त्रिविधं कर्मणः फलम् ।
भवत्यत्यागिनां प्रेत्य न तु संन्यासिनां क्वचित्

**IAST:** aniṣṭamiṣṭaṃ miśraṃ ca trividhaṃ karmaṇaḥ phalam |
bhavatyatyāgināṃ pretya na tu saṃnyāsināṃ kvacit

**Meaning:** Pleasant, painful, mixed. Triple harvest for the un-surrendered hereafter. Never for the surrendered.

**Takeaway:** Three fruits for the clinging. None for the free.

**Provenance:** sa:18:12, telang:T05+T06-head, arnold:D07

**Notes:** Split across T05-tail/T06-head.

**Telang [T05]:** "mixed" "never to"
**Telang [T06]:** "renouncers"

## 18:13

**Sanskrit:** पञ्चैतानि महाबाहो कारणानि निबोध मे ।
सांख्ये कृतान्ते प्रोक्तानि सिद्धये सर्वकर्मणाम्

**IAST:** pañcaitāni mahābāho kāraṇāni nibodha me |
sāṃkhye kṛtānte proktāni siddhaye sarvakarmaṇām

**Meaning:** Learn the five makers of every act, taught in Sankhya wisdom.

**Takeaway:** Action has five parents. Meet them.

**Provenance:** sa:18:13, telang:T06, arnold:D08-shared

**Telang [T06]:** "five causes" "completion"

## 18:14

**Sanskrit:** अधिष्ठानं तथा कर्ता करणं च पृथग्विधम् ।
विविधाश्च पृथक्चेष्टा दैवं चैवात्र पञ्चमम्

**IAST:** adhiṣṭhānaṃ tathā kartā karaṇaṃ ca pṛthagvidham |
vividhāśca pṛthakceṣṭā daivaṃ caivātra pañcamam

**Meaning:** The body, the doer, the instruments, the efforts, and fate as fifth.

**Takeaway:** The full committee behind every deed.

**Provenance:** sa:18:14, telang:T06, arnold:D08-shared

**Telang [T06]:** "substratum" "fifth"

## 18:15

**Sanskrit:** शरीरवाङ्‌मनोभिर्यत्कर्म प्रारभते नरः ।
न्याय्यं वा विपरीतं वा पञ्चैते तस्य हेतवः

**IAST:** śarīravāṅ‌manobhiryatkarma prārabhate naraḥ |
nyāyyaṃ vā viparītaṃ vā pañcaite tasya hetavaḥ

**Meaning:** Whatever one does — body, speech, or mind, right or wrong — these five cause it.

**Takeaway:** No act has a single author.

**Provenance:** sa:18:15, telang:T06, arnold:D08-shared

**Telang [T06]:** "just  or otherwise" "its causes"

## 18:16

**Sanskrit:** तत्रैवं सति कर्तारमात्मानं केवलं तु यः ।
पश्यत्यकृतबुद्धित्वान्न स पश्यति दुर्मतिः

**IAST:** tatraivaṃ sati kartāramātmānaṃ kevalaṃ tu yaḥ |
paśyatyakṛtabuddhitvānna sa paśyati durmatiḥ

**Meaning:** Who sees the spotless Self as the lone doer, dim of mind, sees nothing rightly.

**Takeaway:** Blaming the soul for the committee's work.

**Provenance:** sa:18:16, telang:T06, arnold:D08-shared

**Telang [T06]:** "unrefined" "sees  not"

## 18:17

**Sanskrit:** यस्य नाहंकृतो भावो बुद्धिर्यस्य न लिप्यते ।
हत्वापि स इमाँल्लोकान्न हन्ति न निबध्यते

**IAST:** yasya nāhaṃkṛto bhāvo buddhiryasya na lipyate |
hatvāpi sa imām̐llokānna hanti na nibadhyate

**Meaning:** Ego gone, heart unstained — even slaying these armies, one slays not, nor is bound.

**Takeaway:** The verse Arjuna needed on page one.

**Provenance:** sa:18:17, telang:T06, arnold:D08-shared

**Telang [T06]:** "not tainted" "kills not"

## 18:18

**Sanskrit:** ज्ञानं ज्ञेयं परिज्ञाता त्रिविधा कर्मचोदना ।
करणं कर्म कर्तेति त्रिविधः कर्मसंग्रहः

**IAST:** jñānaṃ jñeyaṃ parijñātā trividhā karmacodanā |
karaṇaṃ karma karteti trividhaḥ karmasaṃgrahaḥ

**Meaning:** Knowing, known, knower — three spurs to action. Tool, deed, doer — action's triple shape.

**Takeaway:** The anatomy of every act.

**Provenance:** sa:18:18, telang:T06, arnold:D09-shared

**Telang [T06]:** "knower" "in brief"

## 18:19

**Sanskrit:** ज्ञानं कर्म च कर्ता च त्रिधैव गुणभेदतः ।
प्रोच्यते गुणसंख्याने यथावच्छृणु तान्यपि

**IAST:** jñānaṃ karma ca kartā ca tridhaiva guṇabhedataḥ |
procyate guṇasaṃkhyāne yathāvacchṛṇu tānyapi

**Meaning:** Wisdom, work, and worker each run threefold by nature's moods. Hear them truly told.

**Takeaway:** Three times three. Listen well.

**Provenance:** sa:18:19, telang:T06+T07-head, arnold:D09-shared

**Notes:** Split across T06-tail/T07-head.

**Telang [T06]:** "Knowledge and action and agent"
**Telang [T07]:** "three classes" "really are"

## 18:20

**Sanskrit:** सर्वभूतेषु येनैकं भावमव्ययमीक्षते ।
अविभक्तं विभक्तेषु तज्ज्ञानं विद्धि सात्त्विकम्

**IAST:** sarvabhūteṣu yenaikaṃ bhāvamavyayamīkṣate |
avibhaktaṃ vibhakteṣu tajjñānaṃ viddhi sāttvikam

**Meaning:** Seeing one imperishable being in all separate beings. Undivided in the divided. That is clear wisdom.

**Takeaway:** One sight, everywhere. Again.

**Provenance:** sa:18:20, telang:T07, arnold:D10-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "inexhaustible" "not different"
