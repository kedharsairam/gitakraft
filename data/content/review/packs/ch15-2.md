## 15:11

**Sanskrit:** यतन्तो योगिनश्चैनं पश्यन्त्यात्मन्यवस्थितम् ।
यतन्तोऽप्यकृतात्मानो नैनं पश्यन्त्यचेतसः

**IAST:** yatanto yoginaścainaṃ paśyantyātmanyavasthitam |
yatanto'pyakṛtātmāno nainaṃ paśyantyacetasaḥ

**Meaning:** Striving seers perceive the soul within. The unrefined and undiscerning never do — strive as they might.

**Takeaway:** Effort without refinement misses.

**Provenance:** sa:15:11, telang:T04, arnold:D10-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "abiding within" "no discernment"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Arnold covers (0.57), Telang does not (0.43)

## 15:12

**Sanskrit:** यदादित्यगतं तेजो जगद्भासयतेऽखिलम् ।
यच्चन्द्रमसि यच्चाग्नौ तत्तेजो विद्धि मामकम्

**IAST:** yadādityagataṃ tejo jagadbhāsayate'khilam |
yaccandramasi yaccāgnau tattejo viddhi māmakam

**Meaning:** The light in sun, moon, and fire that fills the world — know that glory as Mine.

**Takeaway:** Every photon on loan.

**Provenance:** sa:15:12, telang:T04, arnold:D11-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "dwelling in the  sun" "moon or fire"

## 15:13

**Sanskrit:** गामाविश्य च भूतानि धारयाम्यहमोजसा ।
पुष्णामि चौषधीः सर्वाः सोमो भूत्वा रसात्मकः

**IAST:** gāmāviśya ca bhūtāni dhārayāmyahamojasā |
puṣṇāmi cauṣadhīḥ sarvāḥ somo bhūtvā rasātmakaḥ

**Meaning:** I enter the earth and uphold all life. As the moon, I feed every plant.

**Takeaway:** Support from below, nourishment from above.

**Provenance:** sa:15:13, telang:T06, arnold:D11-shared

**Telang [T06]:** "support all  things" "nourish"

प्राणापानसमायुक्तः पचाम्यन्नं चतुर्विधम्

## 15:15

**Sanskrit:** सर्वस्य चाहं हृदि संनिविष्टो
मत्तः स्मृतिर्ज्ञानमपोहनं च ।
वेदैश्च सर्वैरहमेव वेद्यो
वेदान्तकृद्वेदविदेव चाहम्

**IAST:** sarvasya cāhaṃ hṛdi saṃniviṣṭo
mattaḥ smṛtirjñānamapohanaṃ ca |
vedaiśca sarvairahameva vedyo
vedāntakṛdvedavideva cāham

**Meaning:** I sit in every heart — source of memory, wisdom, and forgetting. I am the meaning of all scriptures, their author, their knower.

**Takeaway:** Memory, meaning, and their erasure — one resident.

**Provenance:** sa:15:15, telang:T06, arnold:none-omitted

**Notes:** Arnold omits 15:15 (memory/knowledge/Vedas-author verse); Telang + Sanskrit only.

**Telang [T06]:** "memory, knowledge" "author of the Vedantas"

**Consensus [ARNOLD-ABSENT]:** Arnold omits this verse; Telang-only by construction — check: forgett, knower, mean, scripture, sit, sourc
