# Pack ch09-3 — Chapter 9: The Kingly Secret

Traditional: The Yoga of Royal Knowledge · Verses 9:21–9:30 · Status: **pending**

## 9:21

**Sanskrit:** ते तं भुक्त्वा स्वर्गलोकं विशालं
क्षीणे पुण्ये मर्त्यलोकं विशन्ति ।
एवं त्रयीधर्ममनुप्रपन्ना
गतागतं कामकामा लभन्ते

**IAST:** te taṃ bhuktvā svargalokaṃ viśālaṃ
kṣīṇe puṇye martyalokaṃ viśanti |
evaṃ trayīdharmamanuprapannā
gatāgataṃ kāmakāmā labhante

**Meaning:** Chasers of desires, followers of rites — their fruit is going and coming, coming and going.

**Takeaway:** The commuter's reward: more commuting.

**Provenance:** sa:9:21, telang:T07, arnold:D13

**Telang [T07]:** "objects of  desire" "going and coming"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.83), Arnold does not (0.33)

## 9:22

**Sanskrit:** अनन्याश्चिन्तयन्तो मां ये जनाः पर्युपासते ।
तेषां नित्याभियुक्तानां योगक्षेमं वहाम्यहम्

**IAST:** ananyāścintayanto māṃ ye janāḥ paryupāsate |
teṣāṃ nityābhiyuktānāṃ yogakṣemaṃ vahāmyaham

**Meaning:** But those who worship Me alone. Meditating on nothing else, always yoked. I secure what they have and grant what they lack.

**Takeaway:** The divine insurance policy, stated plainly.

**Provenance:** sa:9:22, telang:T07+T08-head, arnold:D14

**Notes:** Split across T07-tail/T08-head.

**Telang [T07]:** "meditating on me and" "constantly devoted"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.78), Arnold does not (0.22)

## 9:23

**Sanskrit:** येऽप्यन्यदेवताभक्ता यजन्ते श्रद्धयान्विताः ।
तेऽपि मामेव कौन्तेय यजन्त्यविधिपूर्वकम्

**IAST:** ye'pyanyadevatābhaktā yajante śraddhayānvitāḥ |
te'pi māmeva kaunteya yajantyavidhipūrvakam

**Meaning:** Even devotees of other gods, worshipping with faith, truly worship Me. Only by the wrong road.

**Takeaway:** All prayers arrive. Some take detours.

**Provenance:** sa:9:23, telang:T08, arnold:D15-shared

**Telang [T08]:** "other divinities" "irregularly"

## 9:24

**Sanskrit:** अहं हि सर्वयज्ञानां भोक्ता च प्रभुरेव च ।
न तु मामभिजानन्ति तत्त्वेनातश्च्यवन्ति ते

**IAST:** ahaṃ hi sarvayajñānāṃ bhoktā ca prabhureva ca |
na tu māmabhijānanti tattvenātaścyavanti te

**Meaning:** For I receive and rule every sacrifice. But they do not know Me truly — so they fall back.

**Takeaway:** The host of every feast, mistaken for a guest.

**Provenance:** sa:9:24, telang:T08, arnold:D15-shared

**Telang [T08]:** "enjoyer" "do they fall"

## 9:25

**Sanskrit:** यान्ति देवव्रता देवान्पितॄन्यान्ति पितृव्रताः ।
भूतानि यान्ति भूतेज्या यान्ति मद्याजिनोऽपि माम्

**IAST:** yānti devavratā devānpitṝnyānti pitṛvratāḥ |
bhūtāni yānti bhūtejyā yānti madyājino'pi mām

**Meaning:** God-worshippers reach the gods. Ancestor-worshippers reach the ancestors. Ghost-worshippers reach the ghosts. My worshippers reach Me.

**Takeaway:** The destination matches the devotion. Exactly.

**Provenance:** sa:9:25, telang:T08+T09-head, arnold:D15-shared

**Notes:** Split across T08-tail/T09-head.

**Telang [T08]:** "gods go to the gods"
**Telang [T09]:** "manes" "go to me"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.60), Arnold does not (0.20)

## 9:26

**Sanskrit:** पत्रं पुष्पं फलं तोयं यो मे भक्त्या प्रयच्छति ।
तदहं भक्त्युपहृतमश्नामि प्रयतात्मनः

**IAST:** patraṃ puṣpaṃ phalaṃ toyaṃ yo me bhaktyā prayacchati |
tadahaṃ bhaktyupahṛtamaśnāmi prayatātmanaḥ

**Meaning:** Leaf, flower, fruit, water — whatever love offers, I accept from a pure heart.

**Takeaway:** The smallest gift counts, if love wraps it.

**Provenance:** sa:9:26, telang:T09, arnold:D15-shared

**Telang [T09]:** "leaf, flower" "I accept"

## 9:27

**Sanskrit:** यत्करोषि यदश्नासि यज्जुहोषि ददासि यत् ।
यत्तपस्यसि कौन्तेय तत्कुरुष्व मदर्पणम्

**IAST:** yatkaroṣi yadaśnāsi yajjuhoṣi dadāsi yat |
yattapasyasi kaunteya tatkuruṣva madarpaṇam

**Meaning:** Whatever you do, eat, sacrifice, give, or suffer — lay it all before Me.

**Takeaway:** Total offering. No exceptions column.

**Provenance:** sa:9:27, telang:T09, arnold:D15-shared

**Telang [T09]:** "you eat" "offered to me"

## 9:28

**Sanskrit:** शुभाशुभफलैरेवं मोक्ष्यसे कर्मबन्धनैः ।
संन्यासयोगयुक्तात्मा विमुक्तो मामुपैष्यसि

**IAST:** śubhāśubhaphalairevaṃ mokṣyase karmabandhanaiḥ |

**Takeaway:** Freedom is an offering laid correctly.

**Provenance:** sa:9:28, telang:T09, arnold:D15-shared

## 9:29

**Sanskrit:** समोऽहं सर्वभूतेषु न मे द्वेष्योऽस्ति न प्रियः ।
ये भजन्ति तु मां भक्त्या मयि ते तेषु चाप्यहम्

**IAST:** samo'haṃ sarvabhūteṣu na me dveṣyo'sti na priyaḥ |
ye bhajanti tu māṃ bhaktyā mayi te teṣu cāpyaham

**Meaning:** I am the same to all beings. None hateful, none dear. But lovers who dwell in Me — I dwell in them too.

**Takeaway:** Impartial to all. Intimate with lovers.

**Provenance:** sa:9:29, telang:T09, arnold:D16

**Telang [T09]:** "alike to all" "I too in them"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.86), Arnold does not (0.29)

## 9:30

**Sanskrit:** अपि चेत्सुदुराचारो भजते मामनन्यभाक् ।
साधुरेव स मन्तव्यः सम्यग्व्यवसितो हि सः

**IAST:** api cetsudurācāro bhajate māmananyabhāk |
sādhureva sa mantavyaḥ samyagvyavasito hi saḥ

**Meaning:** Even a lifelong wrongdoer who turns to Me alone counts as good. Such resolve sets one straight.

**Takeaway:** No past disqualifies a present turning.

**Provenance:** sa:9:30, telang:T09, arnold:D17-shared

**Telang [T09]:** "ill-conducted" "well resolved"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Arnold covers (0.67), Telang does not (0.44)
