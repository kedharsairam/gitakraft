## 17:1 (अर्जुन)

**Sanskrit:** ये शास्त्रविधिमुत्सृज्य यजन्ते श्रद्धयान्विताः ।
तेषां निष्ठा तु का कृष्ण सत्त्वमाहो रजस्तमः

**IAST:** ye śāstravidhimutsṛjya yajante śraddhayānvitāḥ |
teṣāṃ niṣṭhā tu kā kṛṣṇa sattvamāho rajastamaḥ

**Meaning:** Arjuna said: some worship with faith. But ditch scripture's rules. Clarity, passion, or inertia?

**Takeaway:** Faith without a rulebook. What governs it?

**Provenance:** sa:17:1, telang:T00+T01, arnold:D01

**Telang [T00]:** "Ar^na said ;"
**Telang [T01]:** "abandoning scripture" "or darkness"

## 17:2

**Sanskrit:** श्रीभगवानुवाच
त्रिविधा भवति श्रद्धा देहिनां सा स्वभावजा ।
सात्त्विकी राजसी चैव तामसी चेति तां शृणु

**IAST:** śrībhagavānuvāca
trividhā bhavati śraddhā dehināṃ sā svabhāvajā |
sāttvikī rājasī caiva tāmasī ceti tāṃ śṛṇu

**Meaning:** Krishna said: faith runs threefold, born of one's nature — clear, passionate, or dark. Hear how.

**Takeaway:** Nature writes the faith. Listen for yours.

**Provenance:** sa:17:2, telang:T02+T03+T05-head, arnold:D02+D03-shared

**Notes:** 17:2-tail ('conformable to the heart') opens T05-head.

**Telang [T02]:** "The Deity said ;"
**Telang [T03]:** "three kinds" "Hear abouc"
**Telang [T05]:** "heart"

## 17:3

**Sanskrit:** सत्त्वानुरूपा सर्वस्य श्रद्धा भवति भारत ।
श्रद्धामयोऽयं पुरुषो यो यच्छ्रद्धः स एव सः

**IAST:** sattvānurūpā sarvasya śraddhā bhavati bhārata |
śraddhāmayo'yaṃ puruṣo yo yacchraddhaḥ sa eva saḥ

**Meaning:** Each person's faith mirrors their being. As the faith, so the faithful.

**Takeaway:** You are what you trust.

**Provenance:** sa:17:3, telang:T05, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "full of faith" "a man himself"

**Witness [gretil]:** SANDHI-JOINT

## 17:4

**Sanskrit:** यजन्ते सात्त्विका देवान्यक्षरक्षांसि राजसाः ।
प्रेतान्भूतगणांश्चान्ये यजन्ते तामसा जनाः

**IAST:** yajante sāttvikā devānyakṣarakṣāṃsi rājasāḥ |
pretānbhūtagaṇāṃścānye yajante tāmasā janāḥ

**Meaning:** The clear worship gods. The passionate worship spirits of power. The dark worship ghosts and ghouls.

**Takeaway:** Worship reveals the worshipper.

**Provenance:** sa:17:4, telang:T05, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "quality of goodness worship" "Bhiitas"

## 17:5

**Sanskrit:** अशास्त्रविहितं घोरं तप्यन्ते ये तपो जनाः ।
दम्भाहंकारसंयुक्ताः कामरागबलान्विताः

**IAST:** aśāstravihitaṃ ghoraṃ tapyante ye tapo janāḥ |
dambhāhaṃkārasaṃyuktāḥ kāmarāgabalānvitāḥ

**Meaning:** Fierce self-torture outside scripture. Stuffed with ego, craving, clinging, stubbornness. Know it as demonic resolve.

**Takeaway:** Pain is not piety by itself.

**Provenance:** sa:17:5, telang:T05, arnold:D03-shared

## 17:7
