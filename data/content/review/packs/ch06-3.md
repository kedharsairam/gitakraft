# Pack ch06-3 — Chapter 6: The Disciplined Mind

Traditional: The Yoga of Self-Control · Verses 6:21–6:30 · Status: **pending**

## 6:21

**Sanskrit:** सुखमात्यन्तिकं यत्तद् बुद्धिग्राह्यमतीन्द्रियम् ।
वेत्ति यत्र न चैवायं स्थितश्चलति तत्त्वतः

**IAST:** sukhamātyantikaṃ yattad buddhigrāhyamatīndriyam |
vetti yatra na caivāyaṃ sthitaścalati tattvataḥ

**Meaning:** That endless joy lies beyond the senses. Grasped only by understanding. Holding it, one never wavers from truth.

**Takeaway:** The joy that doesn't depend on anything happening.

**Provenance:** sa:6:21, telang:T07, arnold:D07-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "infinite happiness" "understanding only"

## 6:22

**Sanskrit:** यं लब्ध्वा चापरं लाभं मन्यते नाधिकं ततः ।
यस्मिन्स्थितो न दुःखेन गुरुणापि विचाल्यते

**IAST:** yaṃ labdhvā cāparaṃ lābhaṃ manyate nādhikaṃ tataḥ |
yasminsthito na duḥkhena guruṇāpi vicālyate

**Meaning:** Gaining which, no greater gain seems possible. Rooted in which, even the heaviest grief cannot shake one.

**Takeaway:** Find the thing that relativizes everything else.

**Provenance:** sa:6:22, telang:T07, arnold:D07-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "never swerves" "higher than it"

## 6:23

**Sanskrit:** तं विद्याद्‌दुःखसंयोगवियोगं योगसंज्ञितम् ।
स निश्चयेन योक्तव्यो योगोऽनिर्विण्णचेतसा

**IAST:** taṃ vidyād‌duḥkhasaṃyogaviyogaṃ yogasaṃjñitam |
sa niścayena yoktavyo yogo'nirviṇṇacetasā

**Meaning:** Know that severance from pain-union as discipline. Practice it with resolve and an undaunted heart.

**Takeaway:** Discipline has a definition. Memorize it.

**Provenance:** sa:6:23, telang:T07, arnold:D07-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "great misery" "severance"

## 6:24

**Sanskrit:** संकल्पप्रभवान्कामांस्त्यक्त्वा सर्वानशेषतः ।
मनसैवेन्द्रियग्रामं विनियम्य समन्ततः

**IAST:** saṃkalpaprabhavānkāmāṃstyaktvā sarvānaśeṣataḥ |
manasaivendriyagrāmaṃ viniyamya samantataḥ

**Meaning:** Drop every desire born of imagination, every one. Rein in the sense-herd with the mind on all sides.

**Takeaway:** Round up every stray want.

**Provenance:** sa:6:24, telang:T07, arnold:D08-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "steadiness" "undesponding"

## 6:25

**Sanskrit:** शनैः शनैरुपरमेद्‌बुद्ध्या धृतिगृहीतया ।
आत्मसंस्थं मनः कृत्वा न किंचिदपि चिन्तयेत्

**IAST:** śanaiḥ śanairuparamed‌buddhyā dhṛtigṛhītayā |
ātmasaṃsthaṃ manaḥ kṛtvā na kiṃcidapi cintayet

**Meaning:** Step by step, grow quiet — resolve firm, courage up. Fix the mind on the self and think of nothing else.

**Takeaway:** Slow is smooth, smooth is still.

**Provenance:** sa:6:25, telang:T07, arnold:D08-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "slow steps" "think of nothing"

## 6:26

**Sanskrit:** यतो यतो निश्चरति मनश्चञ्चलमस्थिरम् ।
ततस्ततो नियम्यैतदात्मन्येव वशं नयेत्

**IAST:** yato yato niścarati manaścañcalamasthiram |
tatastato niyamyaitadātmanyeva vaśaṃ nayet

**Meaning:** Wherever the fickle, restless mind wanders off — pull it back, every time, and settle it on the self alone.

**Takeaway:** The rep is the return, not the stillness.

**Provenance:** sa:6:26, telang:T07, arnold:D08-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "breaks  forth" "self alone"

## 6:27

**Sanskrit:** प्रशान्तमनसं ह्येनं योगिनं सुखमुत्तमम् ।
उपैति शान्तरजसं ब्रह्मभूतमकल्मषम्

**IAST:** praśāntamanasaṃ hyenaṃ yoginaṃ sukhamuttamam |
upaiti śāntarajasaṃ brahmabhūtamakalmaṣam

**Meaning:** Supreme joy comes to the practitioner. Mind fully calm. Passions cooled. Sins gone. One with ultimate reality.

**Takeaway:** Cool, clean, calm, one.

**Provenance:** sa:6:27, telang:T07, arnold:D08-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "highest happiness" "become  (one with) the Brahman"

## 6:28

**Sanskrit:** युञ्जन्नेवं सदात्मानं योगी विगतकल्मषः ।
सुखेन ब्रह्मसंस्पर्शमत्यन्तं सुखमश्नुते

**IAST:** yuñjannevaṃ sadātmānaṃ yogī vigatakalmaṣaḥ |
sukhena brahmasaṃsparśamatyantaṃ sukhamaśnute

**Meaning:** Thus always yoked, one shakes off wrongdoing. Supreme joy is touched with ease. Contact with the ultimate.

**Takeaway:** Effort becomes effortless. That is the whole arc.

**Provenance:** sa:6:28, telang:T07+T08-head, arnold:D08-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "looking alike on everything"

## 6:29

**Sanskrit:** सर्वभूतस्थमात्मानं सर्वभूतानि चात्मनि ।
ईक्षते योगयुक्तात्मा सर्वत्र समदर्शनः

**IAST:** sarvabhūtasthamātmānaṃ sarvabhūtāni cātmani |
īkṣate yogayuktātmā sarvatra samadarśanaḥ

**Meaning:** Seeing the self in all beings. And all beings in the self. Such a seer, steady in discipline, sees everywhere the same.

**Takeaway:** One sight, everywhere.

**Provenance:** sa:6:29, telang:T08, arnold:D08-shared

**Notes:** T07-tail shared into T08-head span.

**Telang [T07]:** 
**Telang [T08]:** "abiding in all beings" "all beings in  the self"

## 6:30

**Sanskrit:** यो मां पश्यति सर्वत्र सर्वं च मयि पश्यति ।
तस्याहं न प्रणश्यामि स च मे न प्रणश्यति

**IAST:** yo māṃ paśyati sarvatra sarvaṃ ca mayi paśyati |
tasyāhaṃ na praṇaśyāmi sa ca me na praṇaśyati

**Meaning:** Whoever sees Me in everything. And everything in Me. I am never lost to such a one. Nor is such a one ever lost to Me.

**Takeaway:** Mutual never-lost-ness. Read that twice.

**Provenance:** sa:6:30, telang:T08, arnold:D08-shared

**Telang [T08]:** "never lost"
