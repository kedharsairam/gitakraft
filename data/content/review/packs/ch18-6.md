# Pack ch18-6 — Chapter 18: Freedom and Surrender

Traditional: Liberation and Renunciation · Verses 18:51–18:60 · Status: **pending**

## 18:51

**Sanskrit:** बुद्ध्या विशुद्ध्या युक्तो धृत्यात्मानं नियम्य च ।
शब्दादीन्विषयांस्त्यक्त्वा रागद्वेषौ व्युदस्य च

**IAST:** buddhyā viśuddhyā yukto dhṛtyātmānaṃ niyamya ca |
śabdādīnviṣayāṃstyaktvā rāgadveṣau vyudasya ca

**Meaning:** Pure intellect, self mastered by resolve. Senses set aside. Likes and dislikes dropped.

**Takeaway:** The equipment list for the final climb.

**Provenance:** sa:18:51, telang:T12+T13-head, arnold:D24-shared

**Notes:** Split across T12-tail/T13-head.

**Telang [T12]:** "pure  understanding" "casting off"
**Telang [T13]:** "affection and aversion"

## 18:52

**Sanskrit:** विविक्तसेवी लघ्वाशी यतवाक्कायमानसः ।
ध्यानयोगपरो नित्यं वैराग्यं समुपाश्रितः

**IAST:** viviktasevī laghvāśī yatavākkāyamānasaḥ |
dhyānayogaparo nityaṃ vairāgyaṃ samupāśritaḥ

**Meaning:** Lonely haunts, little food. Speech, body, mind restrained. Ever meditating. Desire renounced.

**Takeaway:** The regime of the God-bound.

**Provenance:** sa:18:52, telang:T13, arnold:D24-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "eats little" "unconcern"

## 18:53

**Sanskrit:** अहंकारं बलं दर्पं कामं क्रोधं परिग्रहम् ।
विमुच्य निर्ममः शान्तो ब्रह्मभूयाय कल्पते

**IAST:** ahaṃkāraṃ balaṃ darpaṃ kāmaṃ krodhaṃ parigraham |
vimucya nirmamaḥ śānto brahmabhūyāya kalpate

**Meaning:** Ego, force, arrogance, craving, anger, possessions — shed. Mine-less, tranquil — fit for oneness.

**Takeaway:** Six weights dropped. One fitness earned.

**Provenance:** sa:18:53, telang:T13, arnold:D24-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "belongings" "mine"

## 18:54

**Sanskrit:** ब्रह्मभूतः प्रसन्नात्मा न शोचति न काङ्क्षति ।
समः सर्वेषु भूतेषु मद्भक्तिं लभते पराम्

**IAST:** brahmabhūtaḥ prasannātmā na śocati na kāṅkṣati |
samaḥ sarveṣu bhūteṣu madbhaktiṃ labhate parām

**Meaning:** One with the ultimate, serene — no grief, no craving. Same to all beings. Highest love for Me.

**Takeaway:** Equality ripens into devotion.

**Provenance:** sa:18:54, telang:T13, arnold:D24-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "wishes not" "alike to all beings"

## 18:55

**Sanskrit:** भक्त्या मामभिजानाति यावान्यश्चास्मि तत्त्वतः ।
ततो मां तत्त्वतो ज्ञात्वा विशते तदनन्तरम्

**IAST:** bhaktyā māmabhijānāti yāvānyaścāsmi tattvataḥ |
tato māṃ tattvato jñātvā viśate tadanantaram

**Meaning:** Through love one knows Me truly — how great I am. Knowing truly, one enters Me.

**Takeaway:** Love knows. Knowledge enters.

**Provenance:** sa:18:55, telang:T13, arnold:D24-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "how great" "enters  into my"

## 18:56

**Sanskrit:** सर्वकर्माण्यपि सदा कुर्वाणो मद्व्यपाश्रयः ।
मत्प्रसादादवाप्नोति शाश्वतं पदमव्ययम्

**IAST:** sarvakarmāṇyapi sadā kurvāṇo madvyapāśrayaḥ |
matprasādādavāpnoti śāśvataṃ padamavyayam

**Meaning:** Doing all acts sheltered in Me, by My grace one wins the eternal, imperishable home.

**Takeaway:** Shelter first. Home after.

**Provenance:** sa:18:56, telang:T13, arnold:D24-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "depending on me" "eternal seat"

## 18:57

**Sanskrit:** चेतसा सर्वकर्माणि मयि संन्यस्य मत्परः ।
बुद्धियोगमुपाश्रित्य मच्चित्तः सततं भव

**IAST:** cetasā sarvakarmāṇi mayi saṃnyasya matparaḥ |
buddhiyogamupāśritya maccittaḥ satataṃ bhava

**Meaning:** Offer every act to Me in thought. Hold Me highest. Mind on Me always through steady focus.

**Takeaway:** The standing instruction.

**Provenance:** sa:18:57, telang:T13, arnold:D24-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "given up to me" "mental abstraction"

## 18:58

**Sanskrit:** मच्चित्तः सर्वदुर्गाणि मत्प्रसादात्तरिष्यसि ।
अथ चेत्त्वमहंकारान्न श्रोष्यसि विनङ्क्ष्यसि

**IAST:** maccittaḥ sarvadurgāṇi matprasādāttariṣyasi |
atha cettvamahaṃkārānna śroṣyasi vinaṅkṣyasi

**Meaning:** Mind on Me — cross every danger by My grace. Refuse through ego — and perish.

**Takeaway:** Two futures. Your call.

**Provenance:** sa:18:58, telang:T13, arnold:D24-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "difficulties" "egotism"

## 18:59

**Sanskrit:** यदहंकारमाश्रित्य न योत्स्य इति मन्यसे ।
मिथ्यैष व्यवसायस्ते प्रकृतिस्त्वां नियोक्ष्यति

**IAST:** yadahaṃkāramāśritya na yotsya iti manyase |
mithyaiṣa vyavasāyaste prakṛtistvāṃ niyokṣyati

**Meaning:** 'I will not fight' — vain resolve. Your own nature will drag you to war.

**Takeaway:** Nature outvotes resolutions.

**Provenance:** sa:18:59, telang:T13, arnold:D24-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "may not fight" "vain"

## 18:60

**Sanskrit:** स्वभावजेन कौन्तेय निबद्धः स्वेन कर्मणा ।
कर्तुं नेच्छसि यन्मोहात्करिष्यस्यवशोऽपि तत्

**IAST:** svabhāvajena kaunteya nibaddhaḥ svena karmaṇā |
kartuṃ necchasi yanmohātkariṣyasyavaśo'pi tat

**Meaning:** Bound by your own duty-born nature, helpless, you will do what delusion refuses.

**Takeaway:** The question answers itself.

**Provenance:** sa:18:60, telang:T13+T14-head, arnold:D24-shared

**Notes:** Split across T13-tail/T14-head.

**Telang [T13]:** "do not wish to do" "involuntarily"
**Telang [T14]:** "tied down" "flowing from your  nature"
