## 1:1 (धृतराष्ट्र)

**Sanskrit:** धर्मक्षेत्रे कुरुक्षेत्रे समवेता युयुत्सवः ।
मामकाः पाण्डवाश्चैव किमकुर्वत संजय

**IAST:** dharmakṣetre kurukṣetre samavetā yuyutsavaḥ |
māmakāḥ pāṇḍavāścaiva kimakurvata saṃjaya

**Meaning:** The blind king Dhritarashtra asks Sanjaya: what did my sons and Pandu's sons do when they gathered to fight on the holy field of Kurukshetra?

**Takeaway:** Every story starts with an honest question.

**Provenance:** sa:1:1, telang:T00+T01, arnold:A01

**Telang [T00]:** "Dhr/tarish/ra said ;"
**Telang [T01]:** "What did my" "desirous to do battle"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.62), Arnold does not (0.25)

## 1:2 (सञ्जय)

**Sanskrit:** दृष्ट्वा तु पाण्डवानीकं व्यूढं दुर्योधनस्तदा ।
आचार्यमुपसंगम्य राजा वचनमब्रवीत्

**IAST:** dṛṣṭvā tu pāṇḍavānīkaṃ vyūḍhaṃ duryodhanastadā |
ācāryamupasaṃgamya rājā vacanamabravīt

**Meaning:** Sanjaya answers: seeing the Pandava army drawn up for battle, Prince Duryodhana went to his teacher Drona and spoke.

**Takeaway:** Look at the field before you speak.

**Provenance:** sa:1:2, telang:T02+T03-head, arnold:A02

**Telang [T02]:** "Saw^ya said :"
**Telang [T03]:** "Seeing the army" "spoke (these) words"

## 1:3

**Sanskrit:** पश्यैतां पाण्डुपुत्राणामाचार्य महतीं चमूम् ।
व्यूढां द्रुपदपुत्रेण तव शिष्येण धीमता

**IAST:** paśyaitāṃ pāṇḍuputrāṇāmācārya mahatīṃ camūm |
vyūḍhāṃ drupadaputreṇa tava śiṣyeṇa dhīmatā

**Meaning:** Duryodhana says: O teacher, look at this mighty Pandava army, drawn up by your clever pupil, Drupada's son.

**Takeaway:** Give credit where it is due — even to rivals.

**Provenance:** sa:1:3, telang:T03, arnold:A02-shared

**Telang [T03]:** "O preceptor! observe" "son of Drupada"

## 1:4

**Sanskrit:** अत्र शूरा महेष्वासा भीमार्जुनसमा युधि ।
युयुधानो विराटश्च द्रुपदश्च महारथः

**IAST:** atra śūrā maheṣvāsā bhīmārjunasamā yudhi |
yuyudhāno virāṭaśca drupadaśca mahārathaḥ

**Meaning:** Here are heroes, great archers, the equals of Bhima and Arjuna in battle: Yuyudhana, Virata, and Drupada the great warrior.

**Takeaway:** Know the strengths across the field.

**Provenance:** sa:1:4, telang:T03, arnold:A02-shared

**Telang [T03]:** "In it are heroes" "master of a great car"

## 1:5

**Sanskrit:** धृष्टकेतुश्चेकितानः काशिराजश्च वीर्यवान् ।
पुरुजित्कुन्तिभोजश्च शैब्यश्च नरपुङ्गवः

**IAST:** dhṛṣṭaketuścekitānaḥ kāśirājaśca vīryavān |
purujitkuntibhojaśca śaibyaśca narapuṅgavaḥ

**Meaning:** Dhrishtaketu, Chekitana, the brave king of Kashi, Purujit, Kuntibhoja, and Shaibya, the best of men.

**Takeaway:** Count carefully before you conclude.

**Provenance:** sa:1:5, telang:T03, arnold:A02-shared

**Telang [T03]:** "Dhr/sh/aketu" "eminent  man"

## 1:6

**Sanskrit:** युधामन्युश्च विक्रान्त उत्तमौजाश्च वीर्यवान् ।
सौभद्रो द्रौपदेयाश्च सर्व एव महारथाः

**IAST:** yudhāmanyuśca vikrānta uttamaujāśca vīryavān |
saubhadro draupadeyāśca sarva eva mahārathāḥ

**Meaning:** Yudhamanyu and the strong Uttamaujas, Abhimanyu, and Draupadi's sons — all of them great warriors.

**Takeaway:** Finish the count before you judge.

**Provenance:** sa:1:6, telang:T03+T04, arnold:A02-shared

## 1:7

**Sanskrit:** अस्माकं तु विशिष्टा ये तान्निबोध द्विजोत्तम ।
नायका मम सैन्यस्य संज्ञार्थं तान्ब्रवीमि ते

**IAST:** asmākaṃ tu viśiṣṭā ye tānnibodha dvijottama |
nāyakā mama sainyasya saṃjñārthaṃ tānbravīmi te

**Meaning:** Now hear about our own chiefs, O best of learned men. I will name the leaders of my army so you know them.

**Takeaway:** Know your own team as well as the other side.

**Provenance:** sa:1:7, telang:T05+T06-head, arnold:A02-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "best of Brihmawas!"
**Telang [T06]:** "will name them" "Yourself, and Bhishma"

## 1:8

**Sanskrit:** भवान्भीष्मश्च कर्णश्च कृपश्च समितिंजयः ।
अश्वत्थामा विकर्णश्च सौमदत्तिस्तथैव च

**IAST:** bhavānbhīṣmaśca karṇaśca kṛpaśca samitiṃjayaḥ |
aśvatthāmā vikarṇaśca saumadattistathaiva ca

**Meaning:** Yourself, and Bhishma, Karna, Kripa the winner of battles, Ashvatthama, Vikarna, and Somadatta's son.

**Takeaway:** Name the pillars that hold you up.

**Provenance:** sa:1:8, telang:T06, arnold:A02-shared

**Telang [T06]:** "Karwa" "son of Somadatta"

## 1:9

**Sanskrit:** अन्ये च बहवः शूरा मदर्थे त्यक्तजीविताः ।
नानाशस्त्रप्रहरणाः सर्वे युद्धविशारदाः

**IAST:** anye ca bahavaḥ śūrā madarthe tyaktajīvitāḥ |
nānāśastrapraharaṇāḥ sarve yuddhaviśāradāḥ

**Meaning:** And many other heroes, skilled with every kind of weapon. They have given up their lives for me.

**Takeaway:** Loyalty itself is a kind of strength.

**Provenance:** sa:1:9, telang:T06, arnold:A02-shared

**Telang [T06]:** "many other brave men" "dexterous in battle"

## 1:10

**Sanskrit:** अपर्याप्तं तदस्माकं बलं भीष्माभिरक्षितम् ।
पर्याप्तं त्विदमेतेषां बलं भीमाभिरक्षितम्

**IAST:** aparyāptaṃ tadasmākaṃ balaṃ bhīṣmābhirakṣitam |
paryāptaṃ tvidameteṣāṃ balaṃ bhīmābhirakṣitam

**Meaning:** Our army, guarded by Bhishma, is beyond measure; theirs, guarded by Bhima, is small.

**Takeaway:** Notice how each leader counts strength in his own favor.

**Provenance:** sa:1:10, telang:T06, arnold:A02-shared

**Telang [T06]:** "Thus our army" "very limited"
