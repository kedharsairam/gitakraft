# Pack ch11-2 — Chapter 11: The Universe in One

Traditional: The Vision of the Cosmic Form · Verses 11:11–11:20 · Status: **pending**

## 11:11

**Sanskrit:** दिव्यमाल्याम्बरधरं दिव्यगन्धानुलेपनम् ।
सर्वाश्चर्यमयं देवमनन्तं विश्वतोमुखम्

**IAST:** divyamālyāmbaradharaṃ divyagandhānulepanam |
sarvāścaryamayaṃ devamanantaṃ viśvatomukham

**Meaning:** Garlands of stars, robes of light, perfumes of heaven. Every wonder, faces in every direction.

**Takeaway:** Dress for the cosmos itself.

**Provenance:** sa:11:11, telang:T06, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T06]:** "flowers and vestments" "all directions"

## 11:12

**Sanskrit:** दिवि सूर्यसहस्रस्य भवेद्युगपदुत्थिता ।
यदि भाः सदृशी सा स्याद्भासस्तस्य महात्मनः

**IAST:** divi sūryasahasrasya bhavedyugapadutthitā |
yadi bhāḥ sadṛśī sā syādbhāsastasya mahātmanaḥ

**Meaning:** If a thousand suns burst forth at once, that might match this splendor.

**Takeaway:** The famous simile. Understatement as praise.

**Provenance:** sa:11:12, telang:T06, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T06]:** "thousand suns" "mighty one"

## 11:13

**Sanskrit:** तत्रैकस्थं जगत्कृत्स्नं प्रविभक्तमनेकधा ।
अपश्यद्देवदेवस्य शरीरे पाण्डवस्तदा

**IAST:** tatraikasthaṃ jagatkṛtsnaṃ pravibhaktamanekadhā |
apaśyaddevadevasya śarīre pāṇḍavastadā

**Meaning:** There Arjuna saw the whole universe in one — yet divided in countless parts — within the god of gods.

**Takeaway:** One and many, simultaneously.

**Provenance:** sa:11:13, telang:T06, arnold:D05

**Telang [T06]:** "observed" "numerous"

## 11:14

**Sanskrit:** ततः स विस्मयाविष्टो हृष्टरोमा धनंजयः ।
प्रणम्य शिरसा देवं कृताञ्जलिरभाषत

**IAST:** tataḥ sa vismayāviṣṭo hṛṣṭaromā dhanaṃjayaḥ |
praṇamya śirasā devaṃ kṛtāñjalirabhāṣata

**Meaning:** Amazed, hair on end, he bowed his head and spoke with folded hands.

**Takeaway:** Terror and devotion in one posture.

**Provenance:** sa:11:14, telang:T06, arnold:D06

**Telang [T06]:** "amazement" "joined hands"

## 11:15 (अर्जुन)

**Sanskrit:** पश्यामि देवांस्तव देव देहे
सर्वांस्तथा भूतविशेषसंघान् ।
ब्रह्माणमीशं कमलासनस्थ-
मृषींश्च सर्वानुरगांश्च दिव्यान्

**IAST:** paśyāmi devāṃstava deva dehe
sarvāṃstathā bhūtaviśeṣasaṃghān |
brahmāṇamīśaṃ kamalāsanastha-
mṛṣīṃśca sarvānuragāṃśca divyān

**Meaning:** Arjuna said: I see all gods within Your body. All beings. The creator on His lotus. Sages and serpents.

**Takeaway:** The eyewitness account begins.

**Provenance:** sa:11:15, telang:T07+T08-head, arnold:D07-shared

**Notes:** T01 promotion note applies chapter-wide (see 11:1). Arnold 11:15 runs D07->D08 (Brahma/lotus at D08-head).

**Telang [T07]:** "Ar^una said :"
**Telang [T08]:** "within your body" "lotus seat"

## 11:16

**Sanskrit:** अनेकबाहूदरवक्त्रनेत्रं
पश्यामि त्वां सर्वतोऽनन्तरूपम् ।
नान्तं न मध्यं न पुनस्तवादिं
पश्यामि विश्वेश्वर विश्वरूप

**IAST:** anekabāhūdaravaktranetraṃ
paśyāmi tvāṃ sarvato'nantarūpam |
nāntaṃ na madhyaṃ na punastavādiṃ
paśyāmi viśveśvara viśvarūpa

**Meaning:** Countless arms, bellies, mouths, eyes everywhere. No end, no middle, no beginning in sight.

**Takeaway:** Infinity, reported plainly.

**Provenance:** sa:11:16, telang:T08, arnold:D08

**Telang [T08]:** "countless forms" "middle or beginning"

## 11:17

**Sanskrit:** किरीटिनं गदिनं चक्रिणं च
तेजोराशिं सर्वतो दीप्तिमन्तम् ।
पश्यामि त्वां दुर्निरीक्ष्यं समन्ता-
द्दीप्तानलार्कद्युतिमप्रमेयम्

**IAST:** kirīṭinaṃ gadinaṃ cakriṇaṃ ca
tejorāśiṃ sarvato dīptimantam |
paśyāmi tvāṃ durnirīkṣyaṃ samantā-
ddīptānalārkadyutimaprameyam

**Meaning:** Crown, mace, discus. A mass of radiance, blinding on every side, blazing like fire and sun.

**Takeaway:** The familiar emblems, made unbearable.

**Provenance:** sa:11:17, telang:T08+T09-head, arnold:D09

**Notes:** Split across T08-tail/T09-head.

**Telang [T08]:** "coronet" "difficult to look at"
**Telang [T09]:** "blazing fire" "indefinable"

## 11:18

**Sanskrit:** त्वमक्षरं परमं वेदितव्यं
त्वमस्य विश्वस्य परं निधानम् ।
त्वमव्ययः शाश्वतधर्मगोप्ता
सनातनस्त्वं पुरुषो मतो मे

**IAST:** tvamakṣaraṃ paramaṃ veditavyaṃ
tvamasya viśvasya paraṃ nidhānam |
tvamavyayaḥ śāśvatadharmagoptā
sanātanastvaṃ puruṣo mato me

**Meaning:** You are the imperishable truth to be known. The highest refuge. Guardian of eternal duty. The timeless being.

**Takeaway:** Theology inside the terror.

**Provenance:** sa:11:18, telang:T09, arnold:D10-shared

**Notes:** Arnold sentence runs D10->D11 ('To shelter Virtue's laws').

**Telang [T09]:** "highest support" "eternal  being"

## 11:19

**Sanskrit:** अनादिमध्यान्तमनन्तवीर्य-
मनन्तबाहुं शशिसूर्यनेत्रम् ।
पश्यामि त्वां दीप्तहुताशवक्त्रं
स्वतेजसा विश्वमिदं तपन्तम्

**IAST:** anādimadhyāntamanantavīrya-
manantabāhuṃ śaśisūryanetram |
paśyāmi tvāṃ dīptahutāśavaktraṃ
svatejasā viśvamidaṃ tapantam

**Meaning:** No beginning, middle, or end. Boundless power. Sun and moon for eyes. A mouth of blazing fire, warming the universe.

**Takeaway:** The face that heats the worlds.

**Provenance:** sa:11:19, telang:T09, arnold:D12

**Telang [T09]:** "unnumbered arms" "heating"

## 11:20

**Sanskrit:** द्यावापृथिव्योरिदमन्तरं हि
व्याप्तं त्वयैकेन दिशश्च सर्वाः ।
दृष्ट्वाद्भुतं रूपमुग्रं तवेदं
लोकत्रयं प्रव्यथितं महात्मन्

**IAST:** dyāvāpṛthivyoridamantaraṃ hi
vyāptaṃ tvayaikena diśaśca sarvāḥ |
dṛṣṭvādbhutaṃ rūpamugraṃ tavedaṃ
lokatrayaṃ pravyathitaṃ mahātman

**Meaning:** Earth and sky and every direction are filled by You alone. The three worlds tremble at this terrible wonder.

**Takeaway:** Aloneness at cosmic scale.

**Provenance:** sa:11:20, telang:T09, arnold:D13

**Telang [T09]:** "pervaded by you" "three worlds"
