# Pack ch01-4 — Chapter 1: Arjuna's Despair

Traditional: Arjuna's Despair · Verses 1:31–1:40 · Status: **pending**

## 1:31

**Sanskrit:** निमित्तानि च पश्यामि विपरीतानि केशव ।
न च श्रेयोऽनुपश्यामि हत्वा स्वजनमाहवे

**IAST:** nimittāni ca paśyāmi viparītāni keśava |
na ca śreyo'nupaśyāmi hatvā svajanamāhave

**Meaning:** My mind reels as if drunk. I see bad omens, Krishna, and no good at all in killing my own kin.

**Takeaway:** When the mind spins, name exactly what you see.

**Provenance:** sa:1:31, telang:T13, arnold:A06-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "mind whirls" "perceive any good"

## 1:32

**Sanskrit:** न काङ्क्षे विजयं कृष्ण न च राज्यं सुखानि च ।
किं नो राज्येन गोविन्द किं भोगैर्जीवितेन वा

**IAST:** na kāṅkṣe vijayaṃ kṛṣṇa na ca rājyaṃ sukhāni ca |
kiṃ no rājyena govinda kiṃ bhogairjīvitena vā

**Meaning:** I want no victory, Krishna, no kingdom, no pleasures.

**Takeaway:** Ask what the prize is actually worth.

**Provenance:** sa:1:32, telang:T13, arnold:A06-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "do not  wish for victory" "pleasures"

## 1:33

**Sanskrit:** येषामर्थे काङ्क्षितं नो राज्यं भोगाः सुखानि च ।
त इमेऽवस्थिता युद्धे प्राणांस्त्यक्त्वा धनानि च

**IAST:** yeṣāmarthe kāṅkṣitaṃ no rājyaṃ bhogāḥ sukhāni ca |
ta ime'vasthitā yuddhe prāṇāṃstyaktvā dhanāni ca

**Meaning:** Of what use is a kingdom to us, Govinda? What use are pleasures, or even life?

**Takeaway:** Count the cost before you name the prize.

**Provenance:** sa:1:33, telang:T13, arnold:A06-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "sovereignty to us" "abandoning  life and wealth"

## 1:34

**Sanskrit:** आचार्याः पितरः पुत्रास्तथैव च पितामहाः ।
मातुलाः श्वशुराः पौत्राः श्यालाः संबन्धिनस्तथा

**IAST:** ācāryāḥ pitaraḥ putrāstathaiva ca pitāmahāḥ |
mātulāḥ śvaśurāḥ pautrāḥ śyālāḥ saṃbandhinastathā

**Meaning:** The very people we want kingdoms and pleasures for stand here ready to die. Teachers, fathers, sons, grandfathers, uncles, in-laws, grandsons, brothers-in-law, kin.

**Takeaway:** Look at who pays for your win.

**Provenance:** sa:1:34, telang:T13, arnold:A06-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "preceptors, fathers" "as also (other) relatives"

## 1:35

**Sanskrit:** एतान्न हन्तुमिच्छामि घ्नतोऽपि मधुसूदन ।
अपि त्रैलोक्यराज्यस्य हेतोः किं नु महीकृते

**IAST:** etānna hantumicchāmi ghnato'pi madhusūdana |
api trailokyarājyasya hetoḥ kiṃ nu mahīkṛte

**Meaning:** I do not wish to kill these men, though they would kill me — not even to rule the three worlds, let alone this earth.

**Takeaway:** Some prices are too high at any scale.

**Provenance:** sa:1:35, telang:T13+T14-head, arnold:A07-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "do not wish to kill" "sovereignty over"
**Telang [T14]:** "three worlds" "earth"

## 1:36

**Sanskrit:** निहत्य धार्तराष्ट्रान्नः का प्रीतिः स्याज्जनार्दन ।
पापमेवाश्रयेदस्मान्हत्वैतानाततायिनः

**IAST:** nihatya dhārtarāṣṭrānnaḥ kā prītiḥ syājjanārdana |
pāpamevāśrayedasmānhatvaitānātatāyinaḥ

**Meaning:** What joy would be ours, Janardana, after killing Dhritarashtra's sons? Killing these criminals, sin alone would cling to us.

**Takeaway:** A victory that costs your conscience isn't one.

**Provenance:** sa:1:36, telang:T14, arnold:A07-shared

**Telang [T14]:** "not  proper" "shall wc be  happy"

## 1:37

**Sanskrit:** तस्मान्नार्हा वयं हन्तुं धार्तराष्ट्रान्स्वबान्धवान् ।
स्वजनं हि कथं हत्वा सुखिनः स्याम माधव

**IAST:** tasmānnārhā vayaṃ hantuṃ dhārtarāṣṭrānsvabāndhavān |
svajanaṃ hi kathaṃ hatvā sukhinaḥ syāma mādhava

**Meaning:** So we must not kill our kinsmen. How could we ever be happy, Madhava, after killing our own people?

**Takeaway:** Ask the morning-after question before, not after.

**Provenance:** sa:1:37, telang:T14, arnold:A07-shared

**Telang [T14]:** "Although  they" "do not see"

## 1:38

**Sanskrit:** यद्यप्येते न पश्यन्ति लोभोपहतचेतसः ।
कुलक्षयकृतं दोषं मित्रद्रोहे च पातकम्

**IAST:** yadyapyete na paśyanti lobhopahatacetasaḥ |
kulakṣayakṛtaṃ doṣaṃ mitradrohe ca pātakam

**Meaning:** Even though these men, blinded by greed, see no evil in wiping out a family or betraying friends —

**Takeaway:** Someone else's blindness doesn't excuse yours.

**Provenance:** sa:1:38, telang:T14, arnold:A07-shared

**Telang [T14]:** "should not we" "refrain"

## 1:39

**Sanskrit:** कथं न ज्ञेयमस्माभिः पापादस्मान्निवर्तितुम् ।
कुलक्षयकृतं दोषं प्रपश्यद्भिर्जनार्दन

**IAST:** kathaṃ na jñeyamasmābhiḥ pāpādasmānnivartitum |
kulakṣayakṛtaṃ doṣaṃ prapaśyadbhirjanārdana

**Meaning:** should not we, who DO see the evil in destroying a family, turn back from this sin?

**Takeaway:** Seeing clearly obligates you.

**Provenance:** sa:1:39, telang:T14, arnold:A07-shared

**Telang [T14]:** "extinction of a family" "rites of families are destroyed"

## 1:40

**Sanskrit:** कुलक्षये प्रणश्यन्ति कुलधर्माः सनातनाः ।
धर्मे नष्टे कुलं कृत्स्नमधर्मोऽभिभवत्युत

**IAST:** kulakṣaye praṇaśyanti kuladharmāḥ sanātanāḥ |
dharme naṣṭe kulaṃ kṛtsnamadharmo'bhibhavatyuta

**Meaning:** When a family dies out, its timeless duties die with it. And when duty dies, lawlessness takes over the whole clan.

**Takeaway:** What holds people together needs tending, or it rots.

**Provenance:** sa:1:40, telang:T14, arnold:A07-shared

**Telang [T14]:** "impiety predominates" "become corrupt"
