# Pack ch05-3 — Chapter 5: The Art of Letting Go

Traditional: The Yoga of Renunciation · Verses 5:21–5:29 · Status: **pending**

## 5:21

**Sanskrit:** बाह्यस्पर्शेष्वसक्तात्मा विन्दत्यात्मनि यत् सुखम् ।
स ब्रह्मयोगयुक्तात्मा सुखमक्षयमश्नुते

**IAST:** bāhyasparśeṣvasaktātmā vindatyātmani yat sukham |
sa brahmayogayuktātmā sukhamakṣayamaśnute

**Meaning:** Pleasures born of contact are wombs of suffering — they begin and they end. The wise take no delight in them.

**Takeaway:** Everything with a start date has an expiry date.

**Provenance:** sa:5:21, telang:T11, arnold:F04-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "bom of contact"

## 5:22

**Sanskrit:** ये हि संस्पर्शजा भोगा दुःखयोनय एव ते ।
आद्यन्तवन्तः कौन्तेय न तेषु रमते बुधः

**IAST:** ye hi saṃsparśajā bhogā duḥkhayonaya eva te |
ādyantavantaḥ kaunteya na teṣu ramate budhaḥ

**Meaning:** Whoever can bear, even in this life before death, the surges of desire and anger — that person is disciplined. That person is happy.

**Takeaway:** Bear it now, not later.

**Provenance:** sa:5:22, telang:T11, arnold:F04-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "bear the agitations" "happy man"

## 5:23

**Sanskrit:** शक्नोतीहैव यः सोढुं प्राक्शरीरविमोक्षणात् ।
कामक्रोधोद्भवं वेगं स युक्तः स सुखी नरः

**IAST:** śaknotīhaiva yaḥ soḍhuṃ prākśarīravimokṣaṇāt |
kāmakrodhodbhavaṃ vegaṃ sa yuktaḥ sa sukhī naraḥ

**Meaning:** Happy within. Sporting within. Lit from within. Such a devotee, one with ultimate reality, reaches ultimate peace.

**Takeaway:** Three 'withins' in one verse. The direction is clear.

**Provenance:** sa:5:23, telang:T11, arnold:F04-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "happiness  is within"

## 5:24

**Sanskrit:** योऽन्तःसुखोऽन्तरारामस्तथान्तर्ज्योतिरेव यः ।
स योगी ब्रह्मनिर्वाणं ब्रह्मभूतोऽधिगच्छति

**IAST:** yo'ntaḥsukho'ntarārāmastathāntarjyotireva yaḥ |
sa yogī brahmanirvāṇaṃ brahmabhūto'dhigacchati

**Meaning:** Sages whose wrongs have ended. Doubts cut away. Selves ruled. Devoted to every being's welfare. They reach ultimate peace.

**Takeaway:** Freedom that includes everyone, or it isn't freedom.

**Provenance:** sa:5:24, telang:T11, arnold:F04-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "sins have  perished"

## 5:25

**Sanskrit:** लभन्ते ब्रह्मनिर्वाणमृषयः क्षीणकल्मषाः ।
छिन्नद्वैधा यतात्मानः सर्वभूतहिते रताः

**IAST:** labhante brahmanirvāṇamṛṣayaḥ kṣīṇakalmaṣāḥ |
chinnadvaidhā yatātmānaḥ sarvabhūtahite ratāḥ

**Meaning:** Ascetics free of desire and anger. Minds ruled. Knowers of the self. Ultimate peace surrounds them — in life and after.

**Takeaway:** Peace on both sides of death.

**Provenance:** sa:5:25, telang:T11, arnold:F04-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "free from desire" "both sides"

## 5:26

**Sanskrit:** कामक्रोधवियुक्तानां यतीनां यतचेतसाम् ।
अभितो ब्रह्मनिर्वाणं वर्तते विदितात्मनाम्

**IAST:** kāmakrodhaviyuktānāṃ yatīnāṃ yatacetasām |
abhito brahmanirvāṇaṃ vartate viditātmanām

**Meaning:** The sage shuts out outer contacts. Gaze fixed between the brows. Breath in and out made even, held within the nose.

**Takeaway:** The book gives actual instructions here. Follow them literally once.

**Provenance:** sa:5:26, telang:T11+T12-head, arnold:F05-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "excludes"
**Telang [T12]:** "external objects" "between the brows"

## 5:27

**Sanskrit:** स्पर्शान्कृत्वा बहिर्बाह्यांश्चक्षुश्चैवान्तरे भ्रुवोः ।
प्राणापानौ समौ कृत्वा नासाभ्यन्तरचारिणौ

**IAST:** sparśānkṛtvā bahirbāhyāṃścakṣuścaivāntare bhruvoḥ |
prāṇāpānau samau kṛtvā nāsābhyantaracāriṇau

**Meaning:** Senses, mind, understanding restrained. Aimed at liberation. Rid of desire, fear, anger. Such a one is forever free.

**Takeaway:** The full setup, stated as one sentence.

**Provenance:** sa:5:27, telang:T12, arnold:F05-shared

**Telang [T12]:** "confines" "released"

## 5:28

**Sanskrit:** यतेन्द्रियमनोबुद्धिर्मुनिर्मोक्षपरायणः ।
विगतेच्छाभयक्रोधो यः सदा मुक्त एव सः

**IAST:** yatendriyamanobuddhirmunirmokṣaparāyaṇaḥ |
vigatecchābhayakrodho yaḥ sadā mukta eva saḥ

**Meaning:** Knowing Me as enjoyer of all sacrifices and penances, great lord of all worlds, friend of all beings — one finds peace.

**Takeaway:** The friend line changes everything. Read it twice.

**Provenance:** sa:5:28, telang:T12, arnold:F05-shared

**Telang [T12]:** "restrains senses" "highest goal" "departed"
