# Pack ch09-2 — Chapter 9: The Kingly Secret

Traditional: The Yoga of Royal Knowledge · Verses 9:11–9:20 · Status: **pending**

## 9:11

**Sanskrit:** अवजानन्ति मां मूढा मानुषीं तनुमाश्रितम् ।
परं भावमजानन्तो मम भूतमहेश्वरम्

**IAST:** avajānanti māṃ mūḍhā mānuṣīṃ tanumāśritam |
paraṃ bhāvamajānanto mama bhūtamaheśvaram

**Meaning:** Fools scorn Me in human form. Blind to My highest nature as great Lord of all beings.

**Takeaway:** The costume hides the King — for those who only see costumes.

**Provenance:** sa:9:11, telang:T03+T06-head, arnold:D10-shared

**Notes:** Split across T03-tail/T06-head (p83 break splits 'knowledge': 'knovv-'/'ledge').

**Telang [T03]:** "Deluded people"
**Telang [T06]:** "assumed  a human body"

## 9:12

**Sanskrit:** मोघाशा मोघकर्माणो मोघज्ञाना विचेतसः ।
राक्षसीमासुरीं चैव प्रकृतिं मोहिनीं श्रिताः

**IAST:** moghāśā moghakarmāṇo moghajñānā vicetasaḥ |
rākṣasīmāsurīṃ caiva prakṛtiṃ mohinīṃ śritāḥ

**Meaning:** Vain their hopes, vain their acts, vain their knowledge. Minds unhinged, they slide toward the demonic way.

**Takeaway:** Emptiness compounds. Choose the other direction.

**Provenance:** sa:9:12, telang:T03+T06-head, arnold:D10-shared

**Notes:** Split across T03-tail/T06-head (see 9:11 note).

**Telang [T03]:** "vain acts"
**Telang [T06]:** "minds are disordered"

## 9:13

**Sanskrit:** महात्मानस्तु मां पार्थ दैवीं प्रकृतिमाश्रिताः ।
भजन्त्यनन्यमनसो ज्ञात्वा भूतादिमव्ययम्

**IAST:** mahātmānastu māṃ pārtha daivīṃ prakṛtimāśritāḥ |
bhajantyananyamanaso jñātvā bhūtādimavyayam

**Meaning:** But great souls take refuge in the divine nature. Knowing Me as the endless source of all beings, they worship with undivided minds.

**Takeaway:** Two natures on offer. Pick the divine one.

**Provenance:** sa:9:13, telang:T06, arnold:D10-shared

**Telang [T06]:** "high-souled ones" "godlike"

## 9:14

**Sanskrit:** सततं कीर्तयन्तो मां यतन्तश्च दृढव्रताः ।
नमस्यन्तश्च मां भक्त्या नित्ययुक्ता उपासते

**IAST:** satataṃ kīrtayanto māṃ yatantaśca dṛḍhavratāḥ |
namasyantaśca māṃ bhaktyā nityayuktā upāsate

**Meaning:** They praise Me without ceasing. They strive, firm in vows. They bow in reverence. Always yoked, they worship.

**Takeaway:** Devotion as a daily practice, not a mood.

**Provenance:** sa:9:14, telang:T06, arnold:D10-shared

**Telang [T06]:** "glorifying me" "always devoted"

## 9:15

**Sanskrit:** ज्ञानयज्ञेन चाप्यन्ये यजन्तो मामुपासते ।
एकत्वेन पृथक्त्वेन बहुधा विश्वतोमुखम्

**IAST:** jñānayajñena cāpyanye yajanto māmupāsate |
ekatvena pṛthaktvena bahudhā viśvatomukham

**Meaning:** Others worship through the sacrifice of knowledge. Seeing Me as one, as separate, as all-pervading in countless forms.

**Takeaway:** Philosophy itself can be an offering.

**Provenance:** sa:9:15, telang:T06, arnold:D10-shared

**Telang [T06]:** "sacrifice of knowledge" "numerous forms"

## 9:16

**Sanskrit:** अहं क्रतुरहं यज्ञः स्वधाहमहमौषधम् ।
मन्त्रोऽहमहमेवाज्यमहमग्निरहं हुतम्

**IAST:** ahaṃ kraturahaṃ yajñaḥ svadhāhamahamauṣadham |
mantro'hamahamevājyamahamagnirahaṃ hutam

**Meaning:** I am the ritual and the prayer. The offering to ancestors and the healing herb. The hymn, the butter, the fire, the gift.

**Takeaway:** Every item on the altar is the same Person.

**Provenance:** sa:9:16, telang:T06, arnold:D10-shared

**Telang [T06]:** "Kratu" "I the offering"

## 9:17

**Sanskrit:** पिताहमस्य जगतो माता धाता पितामहः ।
वेद्यं पवित्रमोंकार ऋक्साम यजुरेव च

**IAST:** pitāhamasya jagato mātā dhātā pitāmahaḥ |
vedyaṃ pavitramoṃkāra ṛksāma yajureva ca

**Meaning:** I am father, mother, maker, grandsire of this universe. The thing to know. The purifier. The syllable Om. The three scriptures. The goal, keeper, Lord, witness.

**Takeaway:** The longest 'I am' list so far. Read it slowly.

**Provenance:** sa:9:17, telang:T06+T07-head, arnold:D10-shared

**Notes:** Split across T06-tail/T07-head.

**Telang [T06]:** "father of this" "the supervisor"
**Telang [T07]:** "residence"

## 9:18

**Sanskrit:** गतिर्भर्ता प्रभुः साक्षी निवासः शरणं सुहृत् ।
प्रभवः प्रलयः स्थानं निधानं बीजमव्ययम्

**IAST:** gatirbhartā prabhuḥ sākṣī nivāsaḥ śaraṇaṃ suhṛt |
prabhavaḥ pralayaḥ sthānaṃ nidhānaṃ bījamavyayam

**Meaning:** I send the heat and hold the rains. I am death and deathlessness both. Being and non-being alike, Arjuna.

**Takeaway:** Opposites are My two hands.

**Provenance:** sa:9:18, telang:T07, arnold:D10-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "cause heat" "immortality"

## 9:19

**Sanskrit:** तपाम्यहमहं वर्षं निगृह्णाम्युत्सृजामि च ।
अमृतं चैव मृत्युश्च सदसच्चाहमर्जुन

**IAST:** tapāmyahamahaṃ varṣaṃ nigṛhṇāmyutsṛjāmi ca |
amṛtaṃ caiva mṛtyuśca sadasaccāhamarjuna

**Meaning:** Scholars of the three scriptures drink the sacred draught, sins washed clean. They sacrifice, asking heaven — and win the shining world of the gods.

**Takeaway:** Virtue earns its wage. In full.

**Provenance:** sa:9:19, telang:T07, arnold:D11

**Telang [T07]:** "branches" "celestial pleasures"

## 9:20

**Sanskrit:** त्रैविद्या मां सोमपाः पूतपापा
यज्ञैरिष्ट्वा स्वर्गतिं प्रार्थयन्ते ।
ते पुण्यमासाद्य सुरेन्द्रलोक-
मश्नन्ति दिव्यान्दिवि देवभोगान्

**IAST:** traividyā māṃ somapāḥ pūtapāpā
yajñairiṣṭvā svargatiṃ prārthayante |
te puṇyamāsādya surendraloka-
maśnanti divyāndivi devabhogān

**Meaning:** But when the joy runs out and merit is spent, they fall back into the mortal world.

**Takeaway:** Every wage gets spent. Heaven included.

**Provenance:** sa:9:20, telang:T07, arnold:D12

**Telang [T07]:** "great heavenly" "merit is exhausted"
