# Pack ch18-5 — Chapter 18: Freedom and Surrender

Traditional: Liberation and Renunciation · Verses 18:41–18:50 · Status: **pending**

## 18:41

**Sanskrit:** ब्राह्मणक्षत्रियविशां शूद्राणां च परन्तप ।
कर्माणि प्रविभक्तानि स्वभावप्रभवैर्गुणैः

**IAST:** brāhmaṇakṣatriyaviśāṃ śūdrāṇāṃ ca parantapa |
karmāṇi pravibhaktāni svabhāvaprabhavairguṇaiḥ

**Meaning:** Priests, warriors, merchants, laborers. Each has work assigned by inborn nature.

**Takeaway:** Four natures. Four works.

**Provenance:** sa:18:41, telang:T10, arnold:D19

**Telang [T10]:** "Vai,ryas" "distinguished"

## 18:42

**Sanskrit:** शमो दमस्तपः शौचं क्षान्तिरार्जवमेव च ।
ज्ञानं विज्ञानमास्तिक्यं ब्रह्मकर्म स्वभावजम्

**IAST:** śamo damastapaḥ śaucaṃ kṣāntirārjavameva ca |
jñānaṃ vijñānamāstikyaṃ brahmakarma svabhāvajam

**Meaning:** Calm, restraint, discipline, purity. Patience, honesty, wisdom, experience, faith. The priest's work.

**Takeaway:** Nine notes of the priestly chord.

**Provenance:** sa:18:42, telang:T10, arnold:D20-shared

**Telang [T10]:** "straightforwardness" "Brdhma"

## 18:43

**Sanskrit:** शौर्यं तेजो धृतिर्दाक्ष्यं युद्धे चाप्यपलायनम् ।
दानमीश्वरभावश्च क्षात्रं कर्म स्वभावजम्

**IAST:** śauryaṃ tejo dhṛtirdākṣyaṃ yuddhe cāpyapalāyanam |
dānamīśvarabhāvaśca kṣātraṃ karma svabhāvajam

**Meaning:** Valor, fire, resolve, skill. Never fleeing battle. Giving. Commanding. The warrior's work.

**Takeaway:** Seven notes of the warrior chord.

**Provenance:** sa:18:43, telang:T10+T12-head, arnold:D20-shared

**Notes:** Split across T10-tail/T12-head (three-things gloss T11 between).

**Telang [T10]:** "Valour, glory, courage"
**Telang [T12]:** "slinking" "Kshatriyas"

## 18:44

**Sanskrit:** कृषिगौरक्ष्यवाणिज्यं वैश्यकर्म स्वभावजम् ।
परिचर्यात्मकं कर्म शूद्रस्यापि स्वभावजम्

**IAST:** kṛṣigaurakṣyavāṇijyaṃ vaiśyakarma svabhāvajam |
paricaryātmakaṃ karma śūdrasyāpi svabhāvajam

**Meaning:** Plowing, herding, trading — the merchant's work by nature. Serving — the laborer's work.

**Takeaway:** Work sorted by nature, not contempt.

**Provenance:** sa:18:44, telang:T12, arnold:D20-shared

**Telang [T12]:** "tending cattle" "service"

## 18:45

**Sanskrit:** स्वे स्वे कर्मण्यभिरतः संसिद्धिं लभते नरः ।
स्वकर्मनिरतः सिद्धिं यथा विन्दति तच्छृणु

**IAST:** sve sve karmaṇyabhirataḥ saṃsiddhiṃ labhate naraḥ |
svakarmanirataḥ siddhiṃ yathā vindati tacchṛṇu

**Meaning:** Loving one's own work, one reaches perfection. Hear how duty-done becomes perfection-won.

**Takeaway:** Excellence in place beats imitation elsewhere.

**Provenance:** sa:18:45, telang:T12, arnold:D21-shared

**Telang [T12]:** "obtains perfection" "Listen"

## 18:46

**Sanskrit:** यतः प्रवृत्तिर्भूतानां येन सर्वमिदं ततम् ।
स्वकर्मणा तमभ्यर्च्य सिद्धिं विन्दति मानवः

**IAST:** yataḥ pravṛttirbhūtānāṃ yena sarvamidaṃ tatam |
svakarmaṇā tamabhyarcya siddhiṃ vindati mānavaḥ

**Meaning:** Worshipping through duty the source of all, the all-pervader — one reaches perfection.

**Takeaway:** Work as worship. Duty as liturgy.

**Provenance:** sa:18:46, telang:T12, arnold:D21-shared

**Telang [T12]:** "all things proceed" "permeated"

## 18:47

**Sanskrit:** श्रेयान्स्वधर्मो विगुणः परधर्मात्स्वनुष्ठितात् ।
स्वभावनियतं कर्म कुर्वन्नाप्नोति किल्बिषम्

**IAST:** śreyānsvadharmo viguṇaḥ paradharmātsvanuṣṭhitāt |
svabhāvaniyataṃ karma kurvannāpnoti kilbiṣam

**Meaning:** Your own flawed duty beats another's perfect one. Doing nature's task incurs no sin.

**Takeaway:** Flawed and yours beats polished and borrowed.

**Provenance:** sa:18:47, telang:T12, arnold:D22-shared

**Telang [T12]:** "well performed" "incur sin"

## 18:48

**Sanskrit:** सहजं कर्म कौन्तेय सदोषमपि न त्यजेत् ।
सर्वारम्भा हि दोषेण धूमेनाग्निरिवावृताः

**IAST:** sahajaṃ karma kaunteya sadoṣamapi na tyajet |
sarvārambhā hi doṣeṇa dhūmenāgnirivāvṛtāḥ

**Meaning:** Never drop inborn work, flaws and all. Every enterprise carries flaws, as fire carries smoke.

**Takeaway:** Smoke proves fire. Flaws prove work.

**Provenance:** sa:18:48, telang:T12, arnold:D22-shared

**Telang [T12]:** "tainted" "fire by smoke"

## 18:49

**Sanskrit:** असक्तबुद्धिः सर्वत्र जितात्मा विगतस्पृहः ।
नैष्कर्म्यसिद्धिं परमां संन्यासेनाधिगच्छति

**IAST:** asaktabuddhiḥ sarvatra jitātmā vigataspṛhaḥ |
naiṣkarmyasiddhiṃ paramāṃ saṃnyāsenādhigacchati

**Meaning:** Self-ruled, detached everywhere, want-free. Through surrender one wins. Freedom from action itself.

**Takeaway:** The summit past the summit.

**Provenance:** sa:18:49, telang:T12, arnold:D22-shared

**Telang [T12]:** "affections have departed" "freedom from  action"

## 18:50

**Sanskrit:** सिद्धिं प्राप्तो यथा ब्रह्म तथाप्नोति निबोध मे ।
समासेनैव कौन्तेय निष्ठा ज्ञानस्य या परा

**IAST:** siddhiṃ prāpto yathā brahma tathāpnoti nibodha me |
samāsenaiva kaunteya niṣṭhā jñānasya yā parā

**Meaning:** Hear briefly how the perfected one reaches the ultimate. Wisdom's highest crown.

**Takeaway:** Perfection has a further shore.

**Provenance:** sa:18:50, telang:T12, arnold:D23

**Telang [T12]:** "obtained  perfection" "culmination"
