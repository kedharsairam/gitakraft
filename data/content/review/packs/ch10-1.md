# Pack ch10-1 — Chapter 10: Everywhere the Divine

Traditional: The Yoga of Divine Manifestations · Verses 10:1–10:10 · Status: **pending**

## 10:1

**Sanskrit:** भूय एव महाबाहो शृणु मे परमं वचः ।
यत्तेऽहं प्रीयमाणाय वक्ष्यामि हितकाम्यया

**IAST:** bhūya eva mahābāho śṛṇu me paramaṃ vacaḥ |
yatte'haṃ prīyamāṇāya vakṣyāmi hitakāmyayā

**Meaning:** Krishna said: hear once more My finest words. I speak them for your good, because you delight in them.

**Takeaway:** Flattery-free preamble. The list is the point.

**Provenance:** sa:10:1, telang:T00, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T00]:** "listen to" "delighted"

**Witness [gretil]:** SANDHI-JOINT

## 10:2

**Sanskrit:** न मे विदुः सुरगणाः प्रभवं न महर्षयः ।
अहमादिर्हि देवानां महर्षीणां च सर्वशः

**IAST:** na me viduḥ suragaṇāḥ prabhavaṃ na maharṣayaḥ |
ahamādirhi devānāṃ maharṣīṇāṃ ca sarvaśaḥ

**Meaning:** Neither gods nor great sages know My origin. For I am in every way the source of gods and sages alike.

**Takeaway:** The maker precedes the made. Including the gods.

**Provenance:** sa:10:2, telang:T00, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T00]:** "know my source" "origin"

## 10:3

**Sanskrit:** यो मामजमनादिं च वेत्ति लोकमहेश्वरम् ।
असंमूढः स मर्त्येषु सर्वपापैः प्रमुच्यते

**IAST:** yo māmajamanādiṃ ca vetti lokamaheśvaram |
asaṃmūḍhaḥ sa martyeṣu sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate

**Meaning:** Whoever knows Me as unborn. Beginningless. Great Lord of the world. Undeluded, such a mortal is freed from all wrong.

**Takeaway:** Three recognitions. One liberation.

**Provenance:** sa:10:3, telang:T00, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T00]:** "without  beginning" "released from all sins"

## 10:4

**Sanskrit:** बुद्धिर्ज्ञानमसंमोहः क्षमा सत्यं दमः शमः ।
सुखं दुःखं भवोऽभावो भयं चाभयमेव च

**IAST:** buddhirjñānamasaṃmohaḥ kṣamā satyaṃ damaḥ śamaḥ |
sukhaṃ duḥkhaṃ bhavo'bhāvo bhayaṃ cābhayameva ca

**Meaning:** Reason, wisdom, clarity, patience, truth, restraint, calm. Joy, sorrow, birth, death, fear, courage.

**Takeaway:** The full human weather report, in one verse.

**Provenance:** sa:10:4, telang:T00, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T00]:** "Intelligence" "birth"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.62), Arnold does not (0.46)

## 10:5

**Sanskrit:** अहिंसा समता तुष्टिस्तपो दानं यशोऽयशः ।
भवन्ति भावा भूतानां मत्त एव पृथग्विधाः

**IAST:** ahiṃsā samatā tuṣṭistapo dānaṃ yaśo'yaśaḥ |
bhavanti bhāvā bhūtānāṃ matta eva pṛthagvidhāḥ

**Meaning:** Non-harm, balance, contentment, discipline, generosity, fame, disgrace. All these varied moods of beings come from Me alone.

**Takeaway:** Even disgrace has a single source.

**Provenance:** sa:10:5, telang:T00, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T00]:** "contentment" "from  me alone"

**Witness [gretil]:** SANDHI-JOINT

## 10:6

**Sanskrit:** महर्षयः सप्त पूर्वे चत्वारो मनवस्तथा ।
मद्भावा मानसा जाता येषां लोक इमाः प्रजाः

**IAST:** maharṣayaḥ sapta pūrve catvāro manavastathā |
madbhāvā mānasā jātā yeṣāṃ loka imāḥ prajāḥ

**Meaning:** The seven ancient sages and the four Manus. Fathers of all peoples. All were born from My mind, sharing My power.

**Takeaway:** The family tree has one root.

**Provenance:** sa:10:6, telang:T00+T01-head, arnold:D01-shared

**Notes:** Split across T00-tail/T01-head.

**Telang [T00]:** "seven great sages" "Manus"
**Telang [T01]:** "mind"

**Witness [gretil]:** SANDHI-JOINT

## 10:7

**Sanskrit:** एतां विभूतिं योगं च मम यो वेत्ति तत्त्वतः ।
सोऽविकम्पेन योगेन युज्यते नात्र संशयः

**IAST:** etāṃ vibhūtiṃ yogaṃ ca mama yo vetti tattvataḥ |
so'vikampena yogena yujyate nātra saṃśayaḥ

**Meaning:** Whoever truly knows these powers and glories of Mine stands in unshakable discipline. Of this there is no doubt.

**Takeaway:** Knowledge that steadies. That is the test.

**Provenance:** sa:10:7, telang:T01, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T01]:** "emanations" "no doubt"

## 10:8

**Sanskrit:** अहं सर्वस्य प्रभवो मत्तः सर्वं प्रवर्तते ।
इति मत्वा भजन्ते मां बुधा भावसमन्विताः

**IAST:** ahaṃ sarvasya prabhavo mattaḥ sarvaṃ pravartate |
iti matvā bhajante māṃ budhā bhāvasamanvitāḥ

**Meaning:** I am the source of all, and all moves through Me. Knowing this, the wise worship Me with love.

**Takeaway:** One premise. One response.

**Provenance:** sa:10:8, telang:T01, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T01]:** "origin of all" "moves on through me"

## 10:9

**Sanskrit:** मच्चित्ता मद्गतप्राणा बोधयन्तः परस्परम् ।
कथयन्तश्च मां नित्यं तुष्यन्ति च रमन्ति च

**IAST:** maccittā madgataprāṇā bodhayantaḥ parasparam |
kathayantaśca māṃ nityaṃ tuṣyanti ca ramanti ca

**Meaning:** Minds on Me, lives offered to Me. Teaching each other, speaking of Me always. Content and joyful.

**Takeaway:** The portrait of a living community.

**Provenance:** sa:10:9, telang:T01, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T01]:** "offering" "contented and happy"

## 10:10

**Sanskrit:** तेषां सततयुक्तानां भजतां प्रीतिपूर्वकम् ।
ददामि बुद्धियोगं तं येन मामुपयान्ति ते

**IAST:** teṣāṃ satatayuktānāṃ bhajatāṃ prītipūrvakam |
dadāmi buddhiyogaṃ taṃ yena māmupayānti te

**Meaning:** To these, always yoked, worshipping with love, I grant the wisdom by which they reach Me.

**Takeaway:** Devotion earns instruction. Instruction completes devotion.

**Provenance:** sa:10:10, telang:T01, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T01]:** "constantly devoted" "attain to me"
