# Pack ch10-5 — Chapter 10: Everywhere the Divine

Traditional: The Yoga of Divine Manifestations · Verses 10:41–10:42 · Status: **pending**

## 10:41

**Sanskrit:** यद्यद्विभूतिमत्सत्त्वं श्रीमदूर्जितमेव वा ।
तत्तदेवावगच्छ त्वं मम तेजोंऽशसंभवम्

**IAST:** yadyadvibhūtimatsattvaṃ śrīmadūrjitameva vā |
tattadevāvagaccha tvaṃ mama tejoṃ'śasaṃbhavam

**Meaning:** Wherever you see power, glory, or brilliance — know it as a spark of My splendor.

**Takeaway:** The reader's field guide in one rule.

**Provenance:** sa:10:41, telang:T17, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T17]:** "splendid" "portions"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Arnold covers (0.57), Telang does not (0.43)

## 10:42

**Sanskrit:** अथवा बहुनैतेन किं ज्ञातेन तवार्जुन ।
विष्टभ्याहमिदं कृत्स्नमेकांशेन स्थितो जगत्

**IAST:** athavā bahunaitena kiṃ jñātena tavārjuna |
viṣṭabhyāhamidaṃ kṛtsnamekāṃśena sthito jagat

**Meaning:** Why know more? With a single fragment of Myself I uphold this entire universe.

**Takeaway:** One spark holds everything. End of list.

**Provenance:** sa:10:42, telang:T17, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T17]:** "at large" "single portion"

**Consensus [ORPHAN]:** neither source covers (telang 0.33, arnold 0.33) — check: entir, fragment, uphold
