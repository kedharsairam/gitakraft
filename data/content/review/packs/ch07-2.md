# Pack ch07-2 — Chapter 7: Knowing the Whole

Traditional: The Yoga of Knowledge and Wisdom · Verses 7:11–7:20 · Status: **pending**

## 7:11

**Sanskrit:** बलं बलवतां चाहं कामरागविवर्जितम् ।
धर्माविरुद्धो भूतेषु कामोऽस्मि भरतर्षभ

**IAST:** balaṃ balavatāṃ cāhaṃ kāmarāgavivarjitam |
dharmāviruddho bhūteṣu kāmo'smi bharatarṣabha

**Meaning:** I am the strength of the strong, free of craving and passion. And I am right desire, where it honors duty.

**Takeaway:** Strength without hunger. Wanting without grasping.

**Provenance:** sa:7:11, telang:T02+T03-head, arnold:D04-shared

**Notes:** Verse ends mid-T02; T03 opens with its tail ('beings.').

**Telang [T02]:** "fondness or desire" "love unopposed to piety"

## 7:12

**Sanskrit:** ये चैव सात्त्विका भावा राजसास्तामसाश्च ये ।
मत्त एवेति तान्विद्धि न त्वहं तेषु ते मयि

**IAST:** ye caiva sāttvikā bhāvā rājasāstāmasāśca ye |
matta eveti tānviddhi na tvahaṃ teṣu te mayi

**Meaning:** Calm, restless, or dark — every mood comes from Me. But I am not in them. They are in Me.

**Takeaway:** The ocean is not in the waves.

**Provenance:** sa:7:12, telang:T03, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T03]:** "quality of" "they are in me"

## 7:13

**Sanskrit:** त्रिभिर्गुणमयैर्भावैरेभिः सर्वमिदं जगत् ।
मोहितं नाभिजानाति मामेभ्यः परमव्ययम्

**IAST:** tribhirguṇamayairbhāvairebhiḥ sarvamidaṃ jagat |
mohitaṃ nābhijānāti māmebhyaḥ paramavyayam

**Meaning:** Fooled by these three moods, the whole world fails to see Me. I stand beyond them, beyond decay.

**Takeaway:** The show hides the stage.

**Provenance:** sa:7:13, telang:T03, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T03]:** "deluded by these three states" "beyond them and inexhaustible"

## 7:14

**Sanskrit:** दैवी ह्येषा गुणमयी मम माया दुरत्यया ।
मामेव ये प्रपद्यन्ते मायामेतां तरन्ति ते

**IAST:** daivī hyeṣā guṇamayī mama māyā duratyayā |
māmeva ye prapadyante māyāmetāṃ taranti te

**Meaning:** Hard to cross is this divine confusion of Mine. But whoever takes refuge in Me alone crosses beyond it.

**Takeaway:** The maze has one exit, clearly marked.

**Provenance:** sa:7:14, telang:T03, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T03]:** "difficult to transcend" "cross beyond this delusion"

## 7:15

**Sanskrit:** न मां दुष्कृतिनो मूढाः प्रपद्यन्ते नराधमाः ।
माययापहृतज्ञाना आसुरं भावमाश्रिताः

**IAST:** na māṃ duṣkṛtino mūḍhāḥ prapadyante narādhamāḥ |
māyayāpahṛtajñānā āsuraṃ bhāvamāśritāḥ

**Meaning:** Wrongdoers never reach Me. Neither the confused, nor the cruel, nor minds stolen by illusion, nor those who choose the demonic path.

**Takeaway:** Four locks on one door. All self-fitted.

**Provenance:** sa:7:15, telang:T03, arnold:D05

**Telang [T03]:** "Wicked" "demoniac"

## 7:16

**Sanskrit:** चतुर्विधा भजन्ते मां जनाः सुकृतिनोऽर्जुन ।
आर्तो जिज्ञासुरर्थार्थी ज्ञानी च भरतर्षभ

**IAST:** caturvidhā bhajante māṃ janāḥ sukṛtino'rjuna |
ārto jijñāsurarthārthī jñānī ca bharatarṣabha

**Meaning:** Four kinds of good people turn to Me. The suffering. The curious. The ambitious. And the wise.

**Takeaway:** Every motive is a valid entrance.

**Provenance:** sa:7:16, telang:T03, arnold:D06

**Telang [T03]:** "four classes" "possessed of knowledge"

## 7:17

**Sanskrit:** तेषां ज्ञानी नित्ययुक्त एकभक्तिर्विशिष्यते ।
प्रियो हि ज्ञानिनोऽत्यर्थमहं स च मम प्रियः

**IAST:** teṣāṃ jñānī nityayukta ekabhaktirviśiṣyate |
priyo hi jñānino'tyarthamahaṃ sa ca mama priyaḥ

**Meaning:** Highest among them stands the wise one. Always steady. Devoted to the One alone. I am dear beyond measure to such a one. And such a one is dear to Me.

**Takeaway:** Mutual dear-ness. The whole path in one exchange.

**Provenance:** sa:7:17, telang:T03, arnold:D07-shared

**Telang [T03]:** "devoted" "dear to me"

## 7:18

**Sanskrit:** उदाराः सर्व एवैते ज्ञानी त्वात्मैव मे मतम् ।
आस्थितः स हि युक्तात्मा मामेवानुत्तमां गतिम्

**IAST:** udārāḥ sarva evaite jñānī tvātmaiva me matam |
āsthitaḥ sa hi yuktātmā māmevānuttamāṃ gatim

**Meaning:** All four are noble. But the wise one, I count as My very self. Such a one rests in Me, the goal beyond which nothing stands.

**Takeaway:** Good, better, and identical.

**Provenance:** sa:7:18, telang:T03, arnold:D07-shared

**Telang [T03]:** "noble" "nothing higher"

## 7:19

**Sanskrit:** बहूनां जन्मनामन्ते ज्ञानवान्मां प्रपद्यते ।
वासुदेवः सर्वमिति स महात्मा सुदुर्लभः

**IAST:** bahūnāṃ janmanāmante jñānavānmāṃ prapadyate |
vāsudevaḥ sarvamiti sa mahātmā sudurlabhaḥ

**Meaning:** After many births, the wise one reaches Me, seeing the divine in everything. Such a great soul is the hardest thing to find.

**Takeaway:** The rarest sight in the world, described plainly.

**Provenance:** sa:7:19, telang:T03, arnold:D07-shared

**Telang [T03]:** "many lives" "Vasudeva is everything"

## 7:20

**Sanskrit:** कामैस्तैस्तैर्हृतज्ञानाः प्रपद्यन्तेऽन्यदेवताः ।
तं तं नियममास्थाय प्रकृत्या नियताः स्वया

**IAST:** kāmaistaistairhṛtajñānāḥ prapadyante'nyadevatāḥ |
taṃ taṃ niyamamāsthāya prakṛtyā niyatāḥ svayā

**Meaning:** But those robbed of judgment by cravings chase lesser gods. Following rites that suit their nature.

**Takeaway:** Want small, worship small.

**Provenance:** sa:7:20, telang:T03+T04-head, arnold:D08-shared

**Notes:** Split across T03-tail/T04-head.

**Telang [T03]:** "approach other"
**Telang [T04]:** "controlled by their own natures"
