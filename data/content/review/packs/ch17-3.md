## 17:21

**Sanskrit:** यत्तु प्रत्युपकारार्थं फलमुद्दिश्य वा पुनः ।
दीयते च परिक्लिष्टं तद्दानं राजसं स्मृतम्

**IAST:** yattu pratyupakārārthaṃ phalamuddiśya vā punaḥ |
dīyate ca parikliṣṭaṃ taddānaṃ rājasaṃ smṛtam

**Meaning:** A gift given grudgingly. Expecting returns or rewards. Is passionate.

**Takeaway:** Strings attached, merit detached.

**Provenance:** sa:17:21, telang:T10, arnold:D12

**Telang [T10]:** "return of services" "passionate"

## 17:22

**Sanskrit:** अदेशकाले यद्दानमपात्रेभ्यश्च दीयते ।
असत्कृतमवज्ञातं तत्तामसमुदाहृतम्

**IAST:** adeśakāle yaddānamapātrebhyaśca dīyate |
asatkṛtamavajñātaṃ tattāmasamudāhṛtam

**Meaning:** A gift flung at the unworthy, wrong place, wrong time, with contempt — is dark.

**Takeaway:** Contempt cancels charity.

**Provenance:** sa:17:22, telang:T10, arnold:D13

**Telang [T10]:** "unfit persons" "contempt"

## 17:23

**Sanskrit:** ॐतत्सदिति निर्देशो ब्रह्मणस्त्रिविधः स्मृतः ।
ब्राह्मणास्तेन वेदाश्च यज्ञाश्च विहिताः पुरा

**IAST:** oṃtatsaditi nirdeśo brahmaṇastrividhaḥ smṛtaḥ |
brāhmaṇāstena vedāśca yajñāśca vihitāḥ purā

**Meaning:** Om, Tat, Sat — the triple name of the ultimate. From it, priests, scriptures, and rites were made of old.

**Takeaway:** Three syllables older than ritual itself.

**Provenance:** sa:17:23, telang:T10, arnold:none-omitted

## 17:26

**Sanskrit:** सद्भावे साधुभावे च सदित्येतत्प्रयुज्यते ।
प्रशस्ते कर्मणि तथा सच्छब्दः पार्थ युज्यते

**IAST:** sadbhāve sādhubhāve ca sadityetatprayujyate |
praśaste karmaṇi tathā sacchabdaḥ pārtha yujyate

**Meaning:** Sat means what is real and good. Every worthy act is Sat. Steadfastness in rite, discipline, and giving is Sat.

**Takeaway:** Goodness has a name. Use it.

**Provenance:** sa:17:26, telang:T10+raw-tail, arnold:none-omitted

**Notes:** T10 truncated ('Constancy in'); raw: '(making) sacrifices, penances, and gifts, is called Sat; and (all) action, too, of which that is the object, is also called Sat.' Arnold omits.

**Telang [T10]:** "existence and goodness" "auspicious act"

## 17:27

## 17:28

**Sanskrit:** अश्रद्धया हुतं दत्तं तपस्तप्तं कृतं च यत् ।
असदित्युच्यते पार्थ न च तत्प्रेत्य नो इह

**IAST:** aśraddhayā hutaṃ dattaṃ tapastaptaṃ kṛtaṃ ca yat |
asadityucyate pārtha na ca tatpretya no iha

**Meaning:** But whatever is offered, given, or suffered without faith is Asat. Unreal, worthless, here and hereafter.

**Takeaway:** Faithlessness voids every offering.

**Provenance:** sa:17:28, telang:raw-only, arnold:none-omitted

**Notes:** NO TRIPTYCH WITNESS (XVIir-head cut). Raw: 'Whatever oblation is offered, whatever is given, whatever penance is performed, and whatever is done, without faith, that is called Asat, and that is nought, both after death and here.' Arnold omits.
