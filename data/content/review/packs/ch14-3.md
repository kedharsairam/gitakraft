# Pack ch14-3 — Chapter 14: Three Moods of Nature

Traditional: The Three Qualities · Verses 14:21–14:27 · Status: **pending**

## 14:21 (अर्जुन)

**Sanskrit:** कैर्लिङ्गैस्त्रीन्गुणानेतानतीतो भवति प्रभो ।
किमाचारः कथं चैतांस्त्रीन्गुणानतिवर्तते

**IAST:** kairliṅgaistrīnguṇānetānatīto bhavati prabho |
kimācāraḥ kathaṃ caitāṃstrīnguṇānativartate

**Meaning:** Arjuna said: what marks one past the moods? How does such a one live? How transcend them?

**Takeaway:** Three questions. The chapter's second half answers.

**Provenance:** sa:14:21, telang:T07+T08, arnold:D08

**Telang [T07]:** "Ar^ma said :"
**Telang [T08]:** "characteristics" "his  conduct"

## 14:22

**Sanskrit:** श्रीभगवानुवाच
प्रकाशं च प्रवृत्तिं च मोहमेव च पाण्डव ।
न द्वेष्टि संप्रवृत्तानि न निवृत्तानि काङ्क्षति

**IAST:** śrībhagavānuvāca
prakāśaṃ ca pravṛttiṃ ca mohameva ca pāṇḍava |
na dveṣṭi saṃpravṛttāni na nivṛttāni kāṅkṣati

**Meaning:** Krishna said: meet clarity, drive, and delusion. Without hatred at their coming. Nor craving at their going.

**Takeaway:** Welcome neither. Chase neither.

**Provenance:** sa:14:22, telang:T10+T11-head, arnold:D09-shared

**Telang [T10]:** "The Deity said :"
**Telang [T11]:** "not averse" "does not desire"

## 14:23

**Sanskrit:** उदासीनवदासीनो गुणैर्यो न विचाल्यते ।
गुणा वर्तन्त इत्येव योऽवतिष्ठति नेङ्गते

**IAST:** udāsīnavadāsīno guṇairyo na vicālyate |
guṇā vartanta ityeva yo'vatiṣṭhati neṅgate

**Meaning:** who sits aside, unshaken, steady, watching moods as mere moods —

**Takeaway:** The spectator simile, lived.

**Provenance:** sa:14:23, telang:T11, arnold:D09-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "unconcerned" "merely"

## 14:24

**Sanskrit:** समदुःखसुखः स्वस्थः समलोष्टाश्मकाञ्चनः ।
तुल्यप्रियाप्रियो धीरस्तुल्यनिन्दात्मसंस्तुतिः

**IAST:** samaduḥkhasukhaḥ svasthaḥ samaloṣṭāśmakāñcanaḥ |
tulyapriyāpriyo dhīrastulyanindātmasaṃstutiḥ

**Meaning:** self-contained, same in joy and pain, same toward clod, stone, and gold, steady through praise and blame —

**Takeaway:** Equality training, item by item.

**Provenance:** sa:14:24, telang:T11, arnold:D09-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "self-contained" "discernment"

## 14:25

**Sanskrit:** मानापमानयोस्तुल्यस्तुल्यो मित्रारिपक्षयोः ।
सर्वारम्भपरित्यागी गुणातीतः स उच्यते

**IAST:** mānāpamānayostulyastulyo mitrāripakṣayoḥ |
sarvārambhaparityāgī guṇātītaḥ sa ucyate

**Meaning:** Same in honor and disgrace. Same to friend and foe. Abandoning all selfish schemes. Such a one has transcended.

**Takeaway:** The full portrait. Nothing decorative.

**Provenance:** sa:14:25, telang:T11, arnold:D09-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "honour and dishonour" "abandons all  action"

## 14:26

**Sanskrit:** मां च योऽव्यभिचारेण भक्तियोगेन सेवते ।
स गुणान्समतीत्यैतान्ब्रह्मभूयाय कल्पते

**IAST:** māṃ ca yo'vyabhicāreṇa bhaktiyogena sevate |
sa guṇānsamatītyaitānbrahmabhūyāya kalpate

**Meaning:** And whoever serves Me with undivided love rises past all moods. Fit for oneness with ultimate reality.

**Takeaway:** Love outranks analysis. Stated, not argued.

**Provenance:** sa:14:26, telang:T11, arnold:D10

**Telang [T11]:** "unswerving" "fit for"

## 14:27

**Sanskrit:** ब्रह्मणो हि प्रतिष्ठाहममृतस्याव्ययस्य च ।
शाश्वतस्य च धर्मस्य सुखस्यैकान्तिकस्य च

**IAST:** brahmaṇo hi pratiṣṭhāhamamṛtasyāvyayasya ca |
śāśvatasya ca dharmasya sukhasyaikāntikasya ca

**Meaning:** For I am the home of the ultimate. Of deathless freedom. Eternal duty. Unbroken joy.

**Takeaway:** The chapter closes on the address itself.

**Provenance:** sa:14:27, telang:T11, arnold:D11

**Telang [T11]:** "indefeasible" "unbroken happiness"
