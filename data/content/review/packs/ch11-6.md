# Pack ch11-6 — Chapter 11: The Universe in One

Traditional: The Vision of the Cosmic Form · Verses 11:51–11:55 · Status: **pending**

## 11:51 (अर्जुन)

**Sanskrit:** दृष्ट्वेदं मानुषं रूपं तव सौम्यं जनार्दन ।
इदानीमस्मि संवृत्तः सचेताः प्रकृतिं गतः

**IAST:** dṛṣṭvedaṃ mānuṣaṃ rūpaṃ tava saumyaṃ janārdana |
idānīmasmi saṃvṛttaḥ sacetāḥ prakṛtiṃ gataḥ

**Meaning:** Arjuna said: seeing again this gentle human form, my mind steadies and returns to normal.

**Takeaway:** Recovery, reported simply.

**Provenance:** sa:11:51, telang:T26+T27, arnold:D48

**Notes:** T27 truncated ('my right'); raw: 'mind, and have come to my normal state.'

**Telang [T26]:** "Ar/tina said :"
**Telang [T27]:** "mild, human form" "my right"

## 11:52

**Sanskrit:** श्रीभगवानुवाच
सुदुर्दर्शमिदं रूपं दृष्टवानसि यन्मम ।
देवा अप्यस्य रूपस्य नित्यं दर्शनकाङ्क्षिणः

**IAST:** śrībhagavānuvāca
sudurdarśamidaṃ rūpaṃ dṛṣṭavānasi yanmama |
devā apyasya rūpasya nityaṃ darśanakāṅkṣiṇaḥ

**Meaning:** Krishna said: even gods forever long to see this form you have seen. It is hard won.

**Takeaway:** The vision envied by heaven itself.

**Provenance:** sa:11:52, telang:T28+T29-head, arnold:D49-shared

**Telang [T28]:** "The Deity said :"
**Telang [T29]:** "desiring to see" "difficult to get"

## 11:53

**Sanskrit:** नाहं वेदैर्न तपसा न दानेन न चेज्यया ।
शक्य एवंविधो द्रष्टुं दृष्टवानसि मां यथा

**IAST:** nāhaṃ vedairna tapasā na dānena na cejyayā |
śakya evaṃvidho draṣṭuṃ dṛṣṭavānasi māṃ yathā

**Meaning:** Not by scriptures, penance, gifts, or rites can I be seen as you have seen Me.

**Takeaway:** Unearnable — third stating, for emphasis kept.

**Provenance:** sa:11:53, telang:T29, arnold:D49-shared

**Telang [T29]:** "not by  penance" "sacrifice"

## 11:54

**Sanskrit:** भक्त्या त्वनन्यया शक्य अहमेवंविधोऽर्जुन ।
ज्ञातुं द्रष्टुं च तत्त्वेन प्रवेष्टुं च परंतप

**IAST:** bhaktyā tvananyayā śakya ahamevaṃvidho'rjuna |
jñātuṃ draṣṭuṃ ca tattvena praveṣṭuṃ ca paraṃtapa

**Meaning:** But by single-pointed devotion I can be known, seen, and entered truly.

**Takeaway:** The one key: undivided love.

**Provenance:** sa:11:54, telang:T29, arnold:D49-shared

**Telang [T29]:** "exclusively" "truly known"

## 11:55

**Sanskrit:** मत्कर्मकृन्मत्परमो मद्भक्तः सङ्गवर्जितः ।
निर्वैरः सर्वभूतेषु यः स मामेति पाण्डव

**IAST:** matkarmakṛnmatparamo madbhaktaḥ saṅgavarjitaḥ |
nirvairaḥ sarvabhūteṣu yaḥ sa māmeti pāṇḍava

**Meaning:** Who works for Me, holds Me highest, loves Me, drops attachments, hates no being — such a one reaches Me.

**Takeaway:** The chapter's whole teaching in one verse.

**Provenance:** sa:11:55, telang:T29, arnold:D49-shared

**Telang [T29]:** "highest  (object)" "comes to me"
