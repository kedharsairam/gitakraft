## 3:1 (अर्जुन)

**Sanskrit:** ज्यायसी चेत्कर्मणस्ते मता बुद्धिर्जनार्दन ।
तत्किं कर्मणि घोरे मां नियोजयसि केशव

**IAST:** jyāyasī cetkarmaṇaste matā buddhirjanārdana |
tatkiṃ karmaṇi ghore māṃ niyojayasi keśava

**Meaning:** Arjuna said: if calm understanding is better than action, Krishna, why do you push me toward this terrible deed?

**Takeaway:** When advice confuses you, ask which part confused you.

**Provenance:** sa:3:1, telang:T00+T01-head, arnold:C01

**Telang [T00]:** "Ar^na .said :"
**Telang [T01]:** "devotion is deemed" "fearful action"

## 3:2

**Sanskrit:** व्यामिश्रेणेव वाक्येन बुद्धिं मोहयसीव मे ।
तदेकं वद निश्चित्य येन श्रेयोऽहमाप्नुयाम्

**IAST:** vyāmiśreṇeva vākyena buddhiṃ mohayasīva me |
tadekaṃ vada niścitya yena śreyo'hamāpnuyām

**Meaning:** Your words sound double-edged and confuse my mind. Tell me one clear thing by which I can reach the highest good.

## 3:3

**Sanskrit:** श्रीभगवानुवाच

## 3:4

**Sanskrit:** न कर्मणामनारम्भान्नैष्कर्म्यं पुरुषोऽश्नुते ।
न च संन्यसनादेव सिद्धिं समधिगच्छति

**IAST:** na karmaṇāmanārambhānnaiṣkarmyaṃ puruṣo'śnute |
na ca saṃnyasanādeva siddhiṃ samadhigacchati

**Meaning:** No one earns freedom from action by simply avoiding action. And no one reaches perfection by merely giving things up.

**Takeaway:** You cannot quit your way to freedom.

**Provenance:** sa:3:4, telang:T03, arnold:C03-shared

**Telang [T03]:** "freedom" "mere * renunciation"

## 3:5

**Sanskrit:** न हि कश्चित्क्षणमपि जातु तिष्ठत्यकर्मकृत् ।
कार्यते ह्यवशः कर्म सर्वः प्रकृतिजैर्गुणैः

**IAST:** na hi kaścitkṣaṇamapi jātu tiṣṭhatyakarmakṛt |
kāryate hyavaśaḥ karma sarvaḥ prakṛtijairguṇaiḥ

**Meaning:** No one can stay even a moment without acting. Nature forces everyone to act, like it or not.

**Takeaway:** Inaction is also an action. Choose accordingly.

**Provenance:** sa:3:5, telang:T03+T04-head, arnold:C03-shared

**Telang [T03]:** "For nobody" "without"
**Telang [T04]:** "performing some action" "constrain"

**Consensus [ORPHAN]:** neither source covers (telang 0.25, arnold 0.00) — check: acting, everyon, force, like, moment, stay

## 3:6

**Sanskrit:** कर्मेन्द्रियाणि संयम्य य आस्ते मनसा स्मरन् ।
इन्द्रियार्थान्विमूढात्मा मिथ्याचारः स उच्यते

**IAST:** karmendriyāṇi saṃyamya ya āste manasā smaran |
indriyārthānvimūḍhātmā mithyācāraḥ sa ucyate

**Meaning:** Whoever locks down the senses but keeps dreaming of pleasures is a fraud — a hypocrite.

**Takeaway:** The performance of restraint is not restraint.

**Provenance:** sa:3:6, telang:T04, arnold:C03-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "deluded man" "hypocrite"

## 3:7

**Sanskrit:** यस्त्विन्द्रियाणि मनसा नियम्यारभतेऽर्जुन ।
कर्मेन्द्रियैः कर्मयोगमसक्तः स विशिष्यते

## 3:8

**Sanskrit:** नियतं कुरु कर्म त्वं कर्म ज्यायो ह्यकर्मणः ।
शरीरयात्रापि च ते न प्रसिद्ध्येदकर्मणः

## 3:9

**Sanskrit:** यज्ञार्थात्कर्मणोऽन्यत्र लोकोऽयं कर्मबन्धनः ।
तदर्थं कर्म कौन्तेय मुक्तसङ्गः समाचर

**IAST:** yajñārthātkarmaṇo'nyatra loko'yaṃ karmabandhanaḥ |
tadarthaṃ karma kaunteya muktasaṅgaḥ samācara

**Meaning:** This world is chained by every act except acts done as offering. So work as an offering, free of clinging.

**Takeaway:** Dedicate the work and the chains fall off.

**Provenance:** sa:3:9, telang:T04, arnold:C03-shared
