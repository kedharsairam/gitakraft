# Pack ch04-5 — Chapter 4: Wisdom in Action

Traditional: The Yoga of Knowledge and Renunciation · Verses 4:41–4:42 · Status: **pending**

## 4:41

**Sanskrit:** योगसंन्यस्तकर्माणं ज्ञानसंछिन्नसंशयम् ।
आत्मवन्तं न कर्माणि निबध्नन्ति धनंजय

**IAST:** yogasaṃnyastakarmāṇaṃ jñānasaṃchinnasaṃśayam |
ātmavantaṃ na karmāṇi nibadhnanti dhanaṃjaya

**Meaning:** Actions do not chain the self-possessed. The one who renounced action through discipline. Whose doubts wisdom destroyed.

**Takeaway:** Cut the chains before they tighten.

**Provenance:** sa:4:41, telang:T14, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T14]:** "do not  fetter"

## 4:42

**Sanskrit:** तस्मादज्ञानसम्भूतं हृत्स्थं ज्ञानासिनात्मनः ।
छित्त्वैनं संशयं योगमातिष्ठोत्तिष्ठ भारत

**IAST:** tasmādajñānasambhūtaṃ hṛtsthaṃ jñānāsinātmanaḥ |
chittvainaṃ saṃśayaṃ yogamātiṣṭhottiṣṭha bhārata

**Meaning:** So cut this doubt born of ignorance with the sword of wisdom. Stand in discipline and rise up to fight.

**Takeaway:** The chapter ends the way it began — with a sword and a sunrise.

**Provenance:** sa:4:42, telang:T14, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T14]:** "Therefore" "Arise"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Arnold covers (0.60), Telang does not (0.40)
