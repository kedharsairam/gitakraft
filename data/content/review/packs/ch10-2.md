# Pack ch10-2 — Chapter 10: Everywhere the Divine

Traditional: The Yoga of Divine Manifestations · Verses 10:11–10:20 · Status: **pending**

## 10:11

**Sanskrit:** तेषामेवानुकम्पार्थमहमज्ञानजं तमः ।
नाशयाम्यात्मभावस्थो ज्ञानदीपेन भास्वता

**IAST:** teṣāmevānukampārthamahamajñānajaṃ tamaḥ |
nāśayāmyātmabhāvastho jñānadīpena bhāsvatā

**Meaning:** Dwelling in their hearts, out of compassion I destroy. Their darkness-born ignorance. With wisdom's shining lamp.

**Takeaway:** The indwelling teacher lights His own lamp.

**Provenance:** sa:10:11, telang:T01, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T01]:** "hearts" "lamp of knowledge"

## 10:12 (अर्जुन)

**Sanskrit:** परं ब्रह्म परं धाम पवित्रं परमं भवान् ।
पुरुषं शाश्वतं दिव्यमादिदेवमजं विभुम्

**IAST:** paraṃ brahma paraṃ dhāma pavitraṃ paramaṃ bhavān |
puruṣaṃ śāśvataṃ divyamādidevamajaṃ vibhum

**Meaning:** Arjuna said: You are the supreme ultimate, the supreme home, the holiest of the holy.

**Takeaway:** The student answers with a hymn.

**Provenance:** sa:10:12, telang:T02+T03-head, arnold:D02-shared

**Telang [T02]:** "Ar^una said :"
**Telang [T03]:** "supreme Brahman" "holiest"

## 10:13

**Sanskrit:** आहुस्त्वामृषयः सर्वे देवर्षिर्नारदस्तथा ।
असितो देवलो व्यासः स्वयं चैव ब्रवीषि मे

**IAST:** āhustvāmṛṣayaḥ sarve devarṣirnāradastathā |
asito devalo vyāsaḥ svayaṃ caiva bravīṣi me

**Meaning:** Saints call You the eternal, divine, first god — Narada, Asita, Devala, Vyasa. And You Yourself tell me so.

**Takeaway:** Scripture, sages, and the source agree.

**Provenance:** sa:10:13, telang:T03, arnold:D02-shared

**Telang [T03]:** "Narada" "tell me"

## 10:14

**Sanskrit:** सर्वमेतदृतं मन्ये यन्मां वदसि केशव ।
न हि ते भगवन्व्यक्तिं विदुर्देवा न दानवाः

**IAST:** sarvametadṛtaṃ manye yanmāṃ vadasi keśava |
na hi te bhagavanvyaktiṃ vidurdevā na dānavāḥ

**Meaning:** All You tell me I hold true, Krishna. Neither gods nor demons grasp Your manifestation.

**Takeaway:** Belief stated before the asking. Good order.

**Provenance:** sa:10:14, telang:T03, arnold:D02-shared

**Telang [T03]:** "believe all this" "manifestation"

## 10:15

**Sanskrit:** स्वयमेवात्मनात्मानं वेत्थ त्वं पुरुषोत्तम ।
भूतभावन भूतेश देवदेव जगत्पते

**IAST:** svayamevātmanātmānaṃ vettha tvaṃ puruṣottama |
bhūtabhāvana bhūteśa devadeva jagatpate

**Meaning:** You alone know Yourself by Yourself. Best of beings. Maker of all. Lord of all. God of gods. Ruler of the universe.

**Takeaway:** The address before the request.

**Provenance:** sa:10:15, telang:T03, arnold:D02-shared

**Telang [T03]:** "knowyourself" "lord  of the universe"

## 10:16

**Sanskrit:** वक्तुमर्हस्यशेषेण दिव्या ह्यात्मविभूतयः ।
याभिर्विभूतिभिर्लोकानिमांस्त्वं व्याप्य तिष्ठसि

**IAST:** vaktumarhasyaśeṣeṇa divyā hyātmavibhūtayaḥ |
yābhirvibhūtibhirlokānimāṃstvaṃ vyāpya tiṣṭhasi

**Meaning:** So declare fully Your divine glories — by which You stand pervading all these worlds.

**Takeaway:** The request: everything, in full.

**Provenance:** sa:10:16, telang:T03+T04-head, arnold:D02-shared

**Notes:** Split across T03-tail/T04-head.

**Telang [T03]:** "declare without"
**Telang [T04]:** "pervading"

## 10:17

**Sanskrit:** कथं विद्यामहं योगिंस्त्वां सदा परिचिन्तयन् ।
केषु केषु च भावेषु चिन्त्योऽसि भगवन्मया

**IAST:** kathaṃ vidyāmahaṃ yogiṃstvāṃ sadā paricintayan |
keṣu keṣu ca bhāveṣu cintyo'si bhagavanmayā

**Meaning:** How shall I know You, ever meditating? In which things should I contemplate You, Lord?

**Takeaway:** Method and objects. The practical half of the request.

**Provenance:** sa:10:17, telang:T04+T05-head, arnold:D02-shared

**Notes:** Split across T04-tail/T05-head.

**Telang [T04]:** "mystic power" "various entities"
**Telang [T05]:** "meditate on you"

## 10:18

**Sanskrit:** विस्तरेणात्मनो योगं विभूतिं च जनार्दन ।
भूयः कथय तृप्तिर्हि शृण्वतो नास्ति मेऽमृतम्

**IAST:** vistareṇātmano yogaṃ vibhūtiṃ ca janārdana |
bhūyaḥ kathaya tṛptirhi śṛṇvato nāsti me'mṛtam

**Meaning:** Tell again, in full, Your power and glory. I never tire of hearing this nectar.

**Takeaway:** Hunger for the infinite, stated sweetly.

**Provenance:** sa:10:18, telang:T05, arnold:D02-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "declare your powers" "no satiety"

## 10:19

**Sanskrit:** श्रीभगवानुवाच
हन्त ते कथयिष्यामि दिव्या ह्यात्मविभूतयः ।
प्राधान्यतः कुरुश्रेष्ठ नास्त्यन्तो विस्तरस्य मे

**IAST:** śrībhagavānuvāca
hanta te kathayiṣyāmi divyā hyātmavibhūtayaḥ |
prādhānyataḥ kuruśreṣṭha nāstyanto vistarasya me

**Meaning:** Krishna said: then I will tell You My chief glories. For the full extent has no end.

**Takeaway:** The catalogue begins — explicitly abridged.

**Provenance:** sa:10:19, telang:T06+T07-head, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T06]:** "The Deity said :"
**Telang [T07]:** "best of Kauravas" "no end"

## 10:20

**Sanskrit:** अहमात्मा गुडाकेश सर्वभूताशयस्थितः ।
अहमादिश्च मध्यं च भूतानामन्त एव च

**IAST:** ahamātmā guḍākeśa sarvabhūtāśayasthitaḥ |
ahamādiśca madhyaṃ ca bhūtānāmanta eva ca

**Meaning:** I am the self seated in every heart. I am the beginning, middle, and end of all beings.

**Takeaway:** The master key to the whole list.

**Provenance:** sa:10:20, telang:T07, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "hearts of all beings" "middle"
