# Pack ch12-2 — Chapter 12: Love Is the Way

Traditional: The Yoga of Devotion · Verses 12:11–12:20 · Status: **pending**

## 12:11

**Sanskrit:** अथैतदप्यशक्तोऽसि कर्तुं मद्योगमाश्रितः ।
सर्वकर्मफलत्यागं ततः कुरु यतात्मवान्

**IAST:** athaitadapyaśakto'si kartuṃ madyogamāśritaḥ |
sarvakarmaphalatyāgaṃ tataḥ kuru yatātmavān

**Meaning:** If even that is beyond you, take refuge in devotion to Me. Restrain yourself. Give up every fruit of action.

**Takeaway:** Failure path three: surrender the results.

**Provenance:** sa:12:11, telang:T05, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "resort to devotion" "abandon all fruit"

## 12:12

**Sanskrit:** श्रेयो हि ज्ञानमभ्यासाज्ज्ञानाद्ध्यानं विशिष्यते ।
ध्यानात्कर्मफलत्यागस्त्यागाच्छान्तिरनन्तरम्

**IAST:** śreyo hi jñānamabhyāsājjñānāddhyānaṃ viśiṣyate |
dhyānātkarmaphalatyāgastyāgācchāntiranantaram

**Meaning:** For knowledge beats rote practice. Worship beats knowledge. Giving up results beats worship. And surrender brings peace swiftly.

**Takeaway:** The ladder, ranked rung by rung.

**Provenance:** sa:12:12, telang:T05+T07-head, arnold:D03-shared

**Notes:** Split across T05-tail/T07-head (page-101 break).

**Telang [T05]:** "better than continuous"
**Telang [T07]:** "abandonment" "tranquillity"

## 12:13

**Sanskrit:** अद्वेष्टा सर्वभूतानां मैत्रः करुण एव च ।
निर्ममो निरहंकारः समदुःखसुखः क्षमी

**IAST:** adveṣṭā sarvabhūtānāṃ maitraḥ karuṇa eva ca |
nirmamo nirahaṃkāraḥ samaduḥkhasukhaḥ kṣamī

**Meaning:** Who hates no being. Friendly, compassionate. Free of ego and possessiveness.

**Takeaway:** The portrait of dear-ness begins.

**Provenance:** sa:12:13, telang:T07, arnold:D04-shared

## 12:14

**Sanskrit:** संतुष्टः सततं योगी यतात्मा दृढनिश्चयः ।
मय्यर्पितमनोबुद्धिर्यो मद्भक्तः स मे प्रियः

**IAST:** saṃtuṣṭaḥ satataṃ yogī yatātmā dṛḍhaniścayaḥ |
mayyarpitamanobuddhiryo madbhaktaḥ sa me priyaḥ

**Meaning:** Even through joy and sorrow. Forgiving, content, always yoked. Self-ruled, resolute. Mind and heart Mine. Such a one is dear to Me.

**Takeaway:** Dear-ness, itemized.

**Provenance:** sa:12:14, telang:T07, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "forgiving" "dear to me"

## 12:15

**Sanskrit:** यस्मान्नोद्विजते लोको लोकान्नोद्विजते च यः ।
हर्षामर्षभयोद्वेगैर्मुक्तो यः स च मे प्रियः

**IAST:** yasmānnodvijate loko lokānnodvijate ca yaḥ |
harṣāmarṣabhayodvegairmukto yaḥ sa ca me priyaḥ

**Meaning:** Who disturbs no world, nor is disturbed by any. Free of excitement, anger, fear, and worry — such a one is dear to Me.

**Takeaway:** Untroubled and untroubling.

**Provenance:** sa:12:15, telang:T07, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "not agitated" "too is dear"

## 12:16

**Sanskrit:** अनपेक्षः शुचिर्दक्ष उदासीनो गतव्यथः ।
सर्वारम्भपरित्यागी यो मद्भक्तः स मे प्रियः

**IAST:** anapekṣaḥ śucirdakṣa udāsīno gatavyathaḥ |
sarvārambhaparityāgī yo madbhaktaḥ sa me priyaḥ

**Meaning:** Unconcerned with outcomes. Pure, efficient, neutral, untroubled. Surrendering every selfish act. Such a one is dear to Me.

**Takeaway:** Efficiency without anxiety.

**Provenance:** sa:12:16, telang:T07, arnold:D04-shared

## 12:17

**Sanskrit:** यो न हृष्यति न द्वेष्टि न शोचति न काङ्क्षति ।
शुभाशुभपरित्यागी भक्तिमान्यः स मे प्रियः

**IAST:** yo na hṛṣyati na dveṣṭi na śocati na kāṅkṣati |
śubhāśubhaparityāgī bhaktimānyaḥ sa me priyaḥ

**Meaning:** No craving, no aversion. No grief, no greed. Abandoning likes and dislikes alike — such a one is dear to Me.

**Takeaway:** The equanimity verse. The chapter's heart.

**Provenance:** sa:12:17, telang:T07, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "no aversion" "disagreeable"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Arnold covers (0.57), Telang does not (0.43)

## 12:18

**Sanskrit:** समः शत्रौ च मित्रे च तथा मानापमानयोः ।
शीतोष्णसुखदुःखेषु समः सङ्गविवर्जितः

**IAST:** samaḥ śatrau ca mitre ca tathā mānāpamānayoḥ |
śītoṣṇasukhaduḥkheṣu samaḥ saṅgavivarjitaḥ

**Meaning:** Same toward friend and foe, honor and disgrace. Same in heat and cold, pleasure and pain. Free of clinging.

**Takeaway:** Sameness as a discipline.

## 12:19

**Sanskrit:** तुल्यनिन्दास्तुतिर्मौनी सन्तुष्टो येन केनचित् ।
अनिकेतः स्थिरमतिर्भक्तिमान्मे प्रियो नरः

**IAST:** tulyanindāstutirmaunī santuṣṭo yena kenacit |
aniketaḥ sthiramatirbhaktimānme priyo naraḥ

**Meaning:** To whom praise and blame sound alike. Quiet, content with whatever comes, homeless, steady-minded, full of devotion — such a one is dear to Me.

**Takeaway:** Homeless, yet at home everywhere.

**Provenance:** sa:12:19, telang:T07+T08-head, arnold:D04-shared

**Notes:** Split across T07-tail/T08-head.

**Telang [T07]:** "taciturn" "homeless"
**Telang [T08]:** "that man is dear"

## 12:20

**Sanskrit:** ये तु धर्म्यामृतमिदं यथोक्तं पर्युपासते ।
श्रद्दधाना मत्परमा भक्तास्तेऽतीव मे प्रियाः

**IAST:** ye tu dharmyāmṛtamidaṃ yathoktaṃ paryupāsate |
śraddadhānā matparamā bhaktāste'tīva me priyāḥ

**Meaning:** But devotees who hold Me highest. And drink this immortal teaching with faith. They are supremely dear to Me.

**Takeaway:** Faith plus the teaching: the dearest combination.

**Provenance:** sa:12:20, telang:T08, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T08]:** "imbued with faith" "extremely dear"
