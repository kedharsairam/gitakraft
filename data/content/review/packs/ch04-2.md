# Pack ch04-2 — Chapter 4: Wisdom in Action

Traditional: The Yoga of Knowledge and Renunciation · Verses 4:11–4:20 · Status: **pending**

## 4:11

**Sanskrit:** ये यथा मां प्रपद्यन्ते तांस्तथैव भजाम्यहम् ।
मम वर्त्मानुवर्तन्ते मनुष्याः पार्थ सर्वशः

**IAST:** ye yathā māṃ prapadyante tāṃstathaiva bhajāmyaham |
mama vartmānuvartante manuṣyāḥ pārtha sarvaśaḥ

**Meaning:** However people approach Me, I meet them that way. All paths lead to Me in the end, Arjuna.

**Takeaway:** Every sincere road arrives.

**Provenance:** sa:4:11, telang:T07, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "serve men" "follow in my path"

## 4:12

**Sanskrit:** काङ्क्षन्तः कर्मणां सिद्धिं यजन्त इह देवताः ।
क्षिप्रं हि मानुषे लोके सिद्धिर्भवति कर्मजा

**IAST:** kāṅkṣantaḥ karmaṇāṃ siddhiṃ yajanta iha devatāḥ |
kṣipraṃ hi mānuṣe loke siddhirbhavati karmajā

**Meaning:** Wanting their actions to pay off, people worship the gods here — because in this human world, results come fast.

**Takeaway:** Impatience explains most worship.

**Provenance:** sa:4:12, telang:T07, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "Desiring the success" "soon obtained"

**Witness [gretil]:** SANDHI-JOINT

## 4:13

**Sanskrit:** चातुर्वर्ण्यं मया सृष्टं गुणकर्मविभागशः ।
तस्य कर्तारमपि मां विद्ध्यकर्तारमव्ययम्

**IAST:** cāturvarṇyaṃ mayā sṛṣṭaṃ guṇakarmavibhāgaśaḥ |
tasya kartāramapi māṃ viddhyakartāramavyayam

**Meaning:** The fourfold social order I created, matching inborn nature to work. And though I made it, know Me as the changeless non-maker.

**Takeaway:** Made the system, stands outside it.

**Provenance:** sa:4:13, telang:T07, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "fourfold division" "not the author"

**Consensus [ORPHAN]:** neither source covers (telang 0.46, arnold 0.38) — check: changeless, inborn, maker, match, order, social

## 4:14

**Sanskrit:** न मां कर्माणि लिम्पन्ति न मे कर्मफले स्पृहा ।
इति मां योऽभिजानाति कर्मभिर्न स बध्यते

**IAST:** na māṃ karmāṇi limpanti na me karmaphale spṛhā |
iti māṃ yo'bhijānāti karmabhirna sa badhyate

**Meaning:** Actions do not stain Me. I hunger for no fruits of action. Whoever knows Me this way is not chained by deeds.

**Takeaway:** Do the work. Skip the hunger.

**Provenance:** sa:4:14, telang:T07, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "defile me not" "not tied"

## 4:15

**Sanskrit:** एवं ज्ञात्वा कृतं कर्म पूर्वैरपि मुमुक्षुभिः ।
कुरु कर्मैव तस्मात्त्वं पूर्वैः पूर्वतरं कृतम्

**IAST:** evaṃ jñātvā kṛtaṃ karma pūrvairapi mumukṣubhiḥ |
kuru karmaiva tasmāttvaṃ pūrvaiḥ pūrvataraṃ kṛtam

**Meaning:** Knowing this, even the ancient seekers of freedom did their work. So you too — act, as the ancients acted in olden times.

**Takeaway:** Precedent matters. Follow the ones who got free.

**Provenance:** sa:4:15, telang:T07+T09-head, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "men of old" "confused as to what is"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.67), Arnold does not (0.44)

## 4:16

**Sanskrit:** किं कर्म किमकर्मेति कवयोऽप्यत्र मोहिताः ।
तत्ते कर्म प्रवक्ष्यामि यज्ज्ञात्वा मोक्ष्यसेऽशुभात्

**IAST:** kiṃ karma kimakarmeti kavayo'pyatra mohitāḥ |
tatte karma pravakṣyāmi yajjñātvā mokṣyase'śubhāt

**Meaning:** Even the wise get confused about what action is and what inaction is. I will explain action to you — knowing it, you will escape evil.

**Takeaway:** If the wise get confused, beginners may ask.

**Provenance:** sa:4:16, telang:T09-head, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T07]:** 
**Telang [T09]:** "action, what inaction" "will speak"

## 4:17

**Sanskrit:** कर्मणो ह्यपि बोद्धव्यं बोद्धव्यं च विकर्मणः ।
अकर्मणश्च बोद्धव्यं गहना कर्मणो गतिः

**IAST:** karmaṇo hyapi boddhavyaṃ boddhavyaṃ ca vikarmaṇaḥ |
akarmaṇaśca boddhavyaṃ gahanā karmaṇo gatiḥ

**Meaning:** You must understand action, and forbidden action, and inaction too. The truth about action runs very deep.

**Takeaway:** Three things to learn, not one.

**Provenance:** sa:4:17, telang:T09, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T09]:** "possess knowledge about action" "abstruse"

## 4:18

**Sanskrit:** कर्मण्यकर्म यः पश्येदकर्मणि च कर्म यः ।
स बुद्धिमान्मनुष्येषु स युक्तः कृत्स्नकर्मकृत्

**IAST:** karmaṇyakarma yaḥ paśyedakarmaṇi ca karma yaḥ |
sa buddhimānmanuṣyeṣu sa yuktaḥ kṛtsnakarmakṛt

**Meaning:** Whoever sees inaction in action, and action in inaction — that person is wise. Disciplined. Doing every deed fully.

**Takeaway:** The riddle at the heart of the chapter.

**Provenance:** sa:4:18, telang:T09, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T09]:** "He is wise" "performs all actions"

## 4:19

**Sanskrit:** यस्य सर्वे समारम्भाः कामसंकल्पवर्जिताः ।
ज्ञानाग्निदग्धकर्माणं तमाहुः पण्डितं बुधाः

**IAST:** yasya sarve samārambhāḥ kāmasaṃkalpavarjitāḥ |
jñānāgnidagdhakarmāṇaṃ tamāhuḥ paṇḍitaṃ budhāḥ

**Meaning:** The wise call learned the one whose every act is free of selfish desire. Free of planning. Whose deeds are burned up in the fire of wisdom.

**Takeaway:** Burn the motive, keep the motion.

**Provenance:** sa:4:19, telang:T09, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T09]:** "call him learned" "fire of knowledge"

## 4:20

**Sanskrit:** त्यक्त्वा कर्मफलासङ्गं नित्यतृप्तो निराश्रयः ।
कर्मण्यभिप्रवृत्तोऽपि नैव किंचित्करोति सः

**IAST:** tyaktvā karmaphalāsaṅgaṃ nityatṛpto nirāśrayaḥ |
karmaṇyabhipravṛtto'pi naiva kiṃcitkaroti saḥ

**Meaning:** Dropping clinging to results. Always content. Needing nothing. Such a one does nothing at all — even while acting hard.

**Takeaway:** The busiest idleness and idlest busyness meet here.

**Provenance:** sa:4:20, telang:T09, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T09]:** "Forsaking" "does nothing"
