# Pack ch07-3 — Chapter 7: Knowing the Whole

Traditional: The Yoga of Knowledge and Wisdom · Verses 7:21–7:30 · Status: **pending**

## 7:21

**Sanskrit:** यो यो यां यां तनुं भक्तः श्रद्धयार्चितुमिच्छति ।
तस्य तस्याचलां श्रद्धां तामेव विदधाम्यहम्

**IAST:** yo yo yāṃ yāṃ tanuṃ bhaktaḥ śraddhayārcitumicchati |
tasya tasyācalāṃ śraddhāṃ tāmeva vidadhāmyaham

**Meaning:** Whatever form any worshipper loves with faith — I make that very faith steady.

**Takeaway:** Sincerity is honored, whatever its address.

**Provenance:** sa:7:21, telang:T04, arnold:D08-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "Whichever form" "faith steady"

## 7:22

**Sanskrit:** स तया श्रद्धया युक्तस्तस्याराधनमीहते ।
लभते च ततः कामान्मयैव विहितान्हि तान्

**IAST:** sa tayā śraddhayā yuktastasyārādhanamīhate |
labhate ca tataḥ kāmānmayaiva vihitānhi tān

**Meaning:** Armed with that faith, one prays and wins one's wishes. Though every gift truly comes from Me.

**Takeaway:** All prayers are answered from one counter.

**Provenance:** sa:7:22, telang:T04, arnold:D08-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "that faith" "given by me"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.78), Arnold does not (0.44)

## 7:23

**Sanskrit:** अन्तवत्तु फलं तेषां तद्भवत्यल्पमेधसाम् ।
देवान्देवयजो यान्ति मद्भक्ता यान्ति मामपि

**IAST:** antavattu phalaṃ teṣāṃ tadbhavatyalpamedhasām |
devāndevayajo yānti madbhaktā yānti māmapi

**Meaning:** But small minds win small, passing fruits. Worshippers of lesser gods join their gods. My worshippers reach Me.

**Takeaway:** Choose the destination by choosing the devotion.

**Provenance:** sa:7:23, telang:T04, arnold:D08-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "perishable" "go to me"

## 7:24

**Sanskrit:** अव्यक्तं व्यक्तिमापन्नं मन्यन्ते मामबुद्धयः ।
परं भावमजानन्तो ममाव्ययमनुत्तमम्

**IAST:** avyaktaṃ vyaktimāpannaṃ manyante māmabuddhayaḥ |
paraṃ bhāvamajānanto mamāvyayamanuttamam

**Meaning:** The unwise think Me, the unseen, to have become merely seen. They miss My highest nature — changeless, beyond compare.

**Takeaway:** Do not shrink the infinite to fit the frame.

**Provenance:** sa:7:24, telang:T04, arnold:D08-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "undiscerning ones" "become perceptible"

## 7:25

**Sanskrit:** नाहं प्रकाशः सर्वस्य योगमायासमावृतः ।
मूढोऽयं नाभिजानाति लोको मामजमव्ययम्

**IAST:** nāhaṃ prakāśaḥ sarvasya yogamāyāsamāvṛtaḥ |
mūḍho'yaṃ nābhijānāti loko māmajamavyayam

**Meaning:** Veiled by My mystic power, I am not open to all. This confused world does not know Me — unborn, beyond decay.

**Takeaway:** Hidden in plain sight, by design.

**Provenance:** sa:7:25, telang:T04, arnold:D08-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "mystic power" "unborn and inexhaustible"

## 7:26

**Sanskrit:** वेदाहं समतीतानि वर्तमानानि चार्जुन ।
भविष्याणि च भूतानि मां तु वेद न कश्चन

**IAST:** vedāhaṃ samatītāni vartamānāni cārjuna |
bhaviṣyāṇi ca bhūtāni māṃ tu veda na kaścana

**Meaning:** I know all beings — past, present, and to come. But no one knows Me.

**Takeaway:** Total knowledge flows one way.

**Provenance:** sa:7:26, telang:T04, arnold:D08-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "things which have been" "But me nobody knows"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Arnold covers (0.60), Telang does not (0.40)

## 7:27

**Sanskrit:** इच्छाद्वेषसमुत्थेन द्वन्द्वमोहेन भारत ।
सर्वभूतानि संमोहं सर्गे यान्ति परन्तप

**IAST:** icchādveṣasamutthena dvandvamohena bhārata |
sarvabhūtāni saṃmohaṃ sarge yānti parantapa

**Meaning:** Every creature enters this world confused. Blinded by likes and dislikes, by the war of opposites, Arjuna.

**Takeaway:** Birth comes with built-in fog.

**Provenance:** sa:7:27, telang:T04, arnold:D09-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "terror" "aversion"

## 7:28

**Sanskrit:** येषां त्वन्तगतं पापं जनानां पुण्यकर्मणाम् ।
ते द्वन्द्वमोहनिर्मुक्ता भजन्ते मां दृढव्रताः

**IAST:** yeṣāṃ tvantagataṃ pāpaṃ janānāṃ puṇyakarmaṇām |
te dvandvamohanirmuktā bhajante māṃ dṛḍhavratāḥ

**Meaning:** But people of good deeds, whose wrongs have ended, worship Me with firm resolve. Free of the fog of opposites.

**Takeaway:** Character clears the lens.

## 7:29

**Sanskrit:** जरामरणमोक्षाय मामाश्रित्य यतन्ति ये ।
ते ब्रह्म तद्विदुः कृत्स्नमध्यात्मं कर्म चाखिलम्

**IAST:** jarāmaraṇamokṣāya māmāśritya yatanti ye |
te brahma tadviduḥ kṛtsnamadhyātmaṃ karma cākhilam

**Meaning:** Those who strive in Me for freedom from old age and death come to know the ultimate. The inner self. And all action.

**Takeaway:** Three knowledges in one striving.

**Provenance:** sa:7:29, telang:raw-only, arnold:D10-shared

## 7:30

**Sanskrit:** साधिभूताधिदैवं मां साधियज्ञं च ये विदुः ।
प्रयाणकालेऽपि च मां ते विदुर्युक्तचेतसः

**IAST:** sādhibhūtādhidaivaṃ māṃ sādhiyajñaṃ ca ye viduḥ |
prayāṇakāle'pi ca māṃ te viduryuktacetasaḥ

**Meaning:** Those who know Me as Lord of beings, of gods, and of sacrifice. Steady in mind. They know Me even at the hour of death.

**Takeaway:** The final exam is passed long before it arrives.

**Provenance:** sa:7:30, telang:raw-only, arnold:D10-shared

**Notes:** NO TRIPTYCH WITNESS (see 7:28 note). Raw: 'And those who know me with the Adhibhuta, the Adhidaiva, and the Adhiyajna, having minds devoted to abstraction, know me at the time of departure (from this world).' Arnold G10 covers 7:29-30.

**Consensus [TELANG-ABSENT]:** Telang text missing (scan gap); Arnold-only — check: steadi
