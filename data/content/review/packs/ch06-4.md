# Pack ch06-4 — Chapter 6: The Disciplined Mind

Traditional: The Yoga of Self-Control · Verses 6:31–6:40 · Status: **pending**

## 6:31

**Sanskrit:** सर्वभूतस्थितं यो मां भजत्येकत्वमास्थितः ।
सर्वथा वर्तमानोऽपि स योगी मयि वर्तते

**IAST:** sarvabhūtasthitaṃ yo māṃ bhajatyekatvamāsthitaḥ |
sarvathā vartamāno'pi sa yogī mayi vartate

**Meaning:** Whoever worships Me, dwelling in all beings. Knowing all is one. However such a one lives, one lives in Me.

**Takeaway:** Address is irrelevant. Residence is everything.

**Provenance:** sa:6:31, telang:T08, arnold:D08-shared

**Telang [T08]:** "worships me" "lives in me"

## 6:32

**Sanskrit:** आत्मौपम्येन सर्वत्र समं पश्यति योऽर्जुन ।
सुखं वा यदि वा दुःखं स योगी परमो मतः

**IAST:** ātmaupamyena sarvatra samaṃ paśyati yo'rjuna |
sukhaṃ vā yadi vā duḥkhaṃ sa yogī paramo mataḥ

**Meaning:** Judge pleasure and pain in all beings by your own measure. That practitioner counts as highest.

**Takeaway:** Empathy as a spiritual rank.

**Provenance:** sa:6:32, telang:T08, arnold:D08-shared

**Telang [T08]:** "deemed to be the best" "pleasure or pain"

## 6:33 (अर्जुन)

**Sanskrit:** योऽयं योगस्त्वया प्रोक्तः साम्येन मधुसूदन ।
एतस्याहं न पश्यामि चञ्चलत्वात्स्थितिं स्थिराम्

**IAST:** yo'yaṃ yogastvayā proktaḥ sāmyena madhusūdana |
etasyāhaṃ na paśyāmi cañcalatvātsthitiṃ sthirām

**Meaning:** Arjuna said: this discipline of evenness you teach. I see no stable ground in it. Because the mind is so restless, Krishna.

**Takeaway:** The student pushes back. Good. So should you.

**Provenance:** sa:6:33, telang:T09+T10-head, arnold:D09

**Telang [T09]:** "Ar^ma said;"
**Telang [T10]:** "sustained existence"

## 6:34

**Sanskrit:** चञ्चलं हि मनः कृष्ण प्रमाथि बलवद्दृढम् ।
तस्याहं निग्रहं मन्ये वायोरिव सुदुष्करम्

**IAST:** cañcalaṃ hi manaḥ kṛṣṇa pramāthi balavaddṛḍham |
tasyāhaṃ nigrahaṃ manye vāyoriva suduṣkaram

**Meaning:** The mind is fickle, turbulent, powerful, stubborn. Holding it, I think, is as hard as holding the wind.

**Takeaway:** Everyone's objection, stated perfectly.

**Provenance:** sa:6:34, telang:T10, arnold:D09

**Telang [T10]:** "fickle" "restrain it is as"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.78), Arnold does not (0.44)

## 6:35

**Sanskrit:** श्रीभगवानुवाच
असंशयं महाबाहो मनो दुर्निग्रहं चलम् ।
अभ्यासेन तु कौन्तेय वैराग्येण च गृह्यते

**IAST:** śrībhagavānuvāca
asaṃśayaṃ mahābāho mano durnigrahaṃ calam |
abhyāsena tu kaunteya vairāgyeṇa ca gṛhyate

**Meaning:** Krishna said: no doubt, strong-armed one, the mind is hard to hold and fickle. But it can be held — by practice and detachment.

**Takeaway:** No denial. Just method.

**Provenance:** sa:6:35, telang:T11+T12-head, arnold:D10-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "The Deity said :"
**Telang [T12]:** "Doubtless" "fickle"

## 6:36

**Sanskrit:** असंयतात्मना योगो दुष्प्राप इति मे मतिः ।
वश्यात्मना तु यतता शक्योऽवाप्तुमुपायतः

**IAST:** asaṃyatātmanā yogo duṣprāpa iti me matiḥ |
vaśyātmanā tu yatatā śakyo'vāptumupāyataḥ

**Meaning:** Discipline is hard for the uncontrolled. But the self-controlled can reach it by the right means, striving properly.

**Takeaway:** Hard is not impossible. Method matters.

**Provenance:** sa:6:36, telang:T12+T13, arnold:D10-shared

**Telang [T12]:** "Still"
**Telang [T13]:** "assiduous"

## 6:37 (अर्जुन)

**Sanskrit:** अयतिः श्रद्धयोपेतो योगाच्चलितमानसः ।
अप्राप्य योगसंसिद्धिं कां गतिं कृष्ण गच्छति

**IAST:** ayatiḥ śraddhayopeto yogāccalitamānasaḥ |
aprāpya yogasaṃsiddhiṃ kāṃ gatiṃ kṛṣṇa gacchati

**Meaning:** Arjuna said: one full of faith but uncontrolled. Mind wandering from discipline. Missing perfection. What path does such a one walk, Krishna?

**Takeaway:** What happens to the sincere failure? Asked with love.

**Provenance:** sa:6:37, telang:T14+T15, arnold:D11

**Telang [T14]:** "Ar^ina said :"

## 6:38

**Sanskrit:** कच्चिन्नोभयविभ्रष्टश्छिन्नाभ्रमिव नश्यति ।
अप्रतिष्ठो महाबाहो विमूढो ब्रह्मणः पथि

**IAST:** kaccinnobhayavibhraṣṭaśchinnābhramiva naśyati |
apratiṣṭho mahābāho vimūḍho brahmaṇaḥ pathi

**Meaning:** Fallen from both paths. Shattered like a broken cloud. Does such a one vanish, mighty-armed? Confused on the road to ultimate reality?

**Takeaway:** The anxious question behind every beginner.

**Provenance:** sa:6:38, telang:QUARANTINED-p72, arnold:none

## 6:40

**Sanskrit:** श्रीभगवानुवाच
पार्थ नैवेह नामुत्र विनाशस्तस्य विद्यते ।
न हि कल्याणकृत्कश्चिद्दुर्गतिं तात गच्छति

**IAST:** śrībhagavānuvāca
pārtha naiveha nāmutra vināśastasya vidyate |
na hi kalyāṇakṛtkaściddurgatiṃ tāta gacchati

**Meaning:** Krishna said: neither here nor hereafter does such a one perish. No one who does good ever meets a bad end, dear friend.

**Takeaway:** Goodness is never wasted. Even interrupted goodness.

**Provenance:** sa:6:40, telang:T16+T17-head, arnold:D12-shared

**Telang [T16]:** "The Deity said ;"
**Telang [T17]:** "neither in this world"
