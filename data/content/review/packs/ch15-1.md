# Pack ch15-1 — Chapter 15: Roots Above

Traditional: The Supreme Person · Verses 15:1–15:10 · Status: **pending**

## 15:1

**Sanskrit:** ऊर्ध्वमूलमधःशाखमश्वत्थं प्राहुरव्ययम् ।
छन्दांसि यस्य पर्णानि यस्तं वेद स वेदवित्

**IAST:** ūrdhvamūlamadhaḥśākhamaśvatthaṃ prāhuravyayam |
chandāṃsi yasya parṇāni yastaṃ veda sa vedavit

**Meaning:** Krishna said: roots above, branches below. The timeless fig tree. Its leaves are hymns. Who knows it knows all sacred lore.

**Takeaway:** The upside-down tree. Roots in heaven.

**Provenance:** sa:15:1, telang:T00+T01-head, arnold:D01

**Telang [T00]:** "The Deity said :"
**Telang [T01]:** "roots above" "leaves"

## 15:2

**Sanskrit:** अधश्चोर्ध्वं प्रसृतास्तस्य शाखा
गुणप्रवृद्धा विषयप्रवालाः ।
अधश्च मूलान्यनुसंततानि
कर्मानुबन्धीनि मनुष्यलोके

**IAST:** adhaścordhvaṃ prasṛtāstasya śākhā
guṇapravṛddhā viṣayapravālāḥ |
adhaśca mūlānyanusaṃtatāni
karmānubandhīni manuṣyaloke

**Meaning:** Its branches spread high and low, fed by nature's moods. Sense-objects are its shoots. Down in the human world, action-growing roots spread wide.

**Takeaway:** Botany as biography.

**Provenance:** sa:15:2, telang:T01, arnold:D02

**Telang [T01]:** "extend its branches" "lead  on to action"

## 15:3

**Sanskrit:** न रूपमस्येह तथोपलभ्यते
नान्तो न चादिर्न च संप्रतिष्ठा ।
अश्वत्थमेनं सुविरूढमूल-
मसङ्गशस्त्रेण दृढेन छित्त्वा

**IAST:** na rūpamasyeha tathopalabhyate
nānto na cādirna ca saṃpratiṣṭhā |
aśvatthamenaṃ suvirūḍhamūla-
masaṅgaśastreṇa dṛḍhena chittvā

**Meaning:** Neither its form, nor end, nor beginning, nor ground is seen here. So cut this deep-rooted tree with the tough axe of detachment.

**Takeaway:** Wield the axe. The tree is your own life.

**Provenance:** sa:15:3, telang:T01, arnold:D03-shared

**Notes:** Arnold 15:3 runs D03->D04 ('and, then'/'How it must end').

**Telang [T01]:** "nor beginning" "weapon of unconcern"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.67), Arnold does not (0.25)

## 15:4

**Sanskrit:** ततः पदं तत्परिमार्गितव्यं
यस्मिन्गता न निवर्तन्ति भूयः ।
तमेव चाद्यं पुरुषं प्रपद्ये
यतः प्रवृत्तिः प्रसृता पुराणी

**IAST:** tataḥ padaṃ tatparimārgitavyaṃ
yasmingatā na nivartanti bhūyaḥ |
tameva cādyaṃ puruṣaṃ prapadye
yataḥ pravṛttiḥ prasṛtā purāṇī

**Meaning:** Then seek the place of no return — resting on the primal being from whom this ancient world-tree grew.

**Takeaway:** Cut, then seek. In that order.

**Provenance:** sa:15:4, telang:T01, arnold:D05

**Telang [T01]:** "never  return" "ancient course"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.78), Arnold does not (0.00)

## 15:5

**Sanskrit:** निर्मानमोहा जितसङ्गदोषा
अध्यात्मनित्या विनिवृत्तकामाः ।
द्वन्द्वैर्विमुक्ताः सुखदुःखसंज्ञै-
र्गच्छन्त्यमूढाः पदमव्ययं तत्

**IAST:** nirmānamohā jitasaṅgadoṣā
adhyātmanityā vinivṛttakāmāḥ |
dvandvairvimuktāḥ sukhaduḥkhasaṃjñai-
rgacchantyamūḍhāḥ padamavyayaṃ tat

**Meaning:** The proud-free, delusion-free, attachment-conquered. Constant in self-knowledge. Wanting nothing. Beyond pleasure and pain. They reach the imperishable home, undeluded.

**Takeaway:** The passenger list for no-return.

**Provenance:** sa:15:5, telang:T01, arnold:D06-shared

**Notes:** Arnold 15:5 runs D06->D07 ('To the eternal world pass such as these!').

**Telang [T01]:** "free from pride" "imperishable seat"

**Witness [gretil]:** SANDHI-JOINT

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.71), Arnold does not (0.00)

## 15:6

**Sanskrit:** न तद्भासयते सूर्यो न शशाङ्को न पावकः ।
यद्गत्वा न निवर्तन्ते तद्धाम परमं मम

**IAST:** na tadbhāsayate sūryo na śaśāṅko na pāvakaḥ |
yadgatvā na nivartante taddhāma paramaṃ mama

**Meaning:** Neither sun, nor moon, nor fire lights that highest home of Mine. Going there, none return.

**Takeaway:** Unlit by every known light.

**Provenance:** sa:15:6, telang:T01+T04-head, arnold:D08

**Notes:** Split across T01-tail/T04-head (page-112 break).

**Telang [T01]:** "The sun"
**Telang [T04]:** "nor the moon" "none returns"

## 15:7

**Sanskrit:** ममैवांशो जीवलोके जीवभूतः सनातनः ।
मनःषष्ठानीन्द्रियाणि प्रकृतिस्थानि कर्षति

**IAST:** mamaivāṃśo jīvaloke jīvabhūtaḥ sanātanaḥ |
manaḥṣaṣṭhānīndriyāṇi prakṛtisthāni karṣati

**Meaning:** A spark of Me becomes the living soul in this mortal world — drawing mind and five senses along.

**Takeaway:** You are a fragment of the infinite, gathering instruments.

**Provenance:** sa:15:7, telang:T04, arnold:D09-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "eternal portion" "sixth"

## 15:8

**Sanskrit:** शरीरं यदवाप्नोति यच्चाप्युत्क्रामतीश्वरः ।
गृहीत्वैतानि संयाति वायुर्गन्धानिवाशयात्

**IAST:** śarīraṃ yadavāpnoti yaccāpyutkrāmatīśvaraḥ |
gṛhītvaitāni saṃyāti vāyurgandhānivāśayāt

**Meaning:** Moving house between bodies, the soul carries its senses along — as wind carries scent from flowers.

**Takeaway:** The gentlest simile for transmigration.

**Provenance:** sa:15:8, telang:T04, arnold:D09-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "obtains or quits" "wind"

## 15:9

**Sanskrit:** श्रोत्रं चक्षुः स्पर्शनं च रसनं घ्राणमेव च ।
अधिष्ठाय मनश्चायं विषयानुपसेवते

**IAST:** śrotraṃ cakṣuḥ sparśanaṃ ca rasanaṃ ghrāṇameva ca |
adhiṣṭhāya manaścāyaṃ viṣayānupasevate

**Meaning:** Through ear, eye, touch, taste, smell, and mind, the soul enjoys the sense-world.

**Takeaway:** Six windows. One watcher.

**Provenance:** sa:15:9, telang:T04, arnold:D09-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "hearing and seeing" "enjoys"

## 15:10

**Sanskrit:** उत्क्रामन्तं स्थितं वापि भुञ्जानं वा गुणान्वितम् ।
विमूढा नानुपश्यन्ति पश्यन्ति ज्ञानचक्षुषः

**IAST:** utkrāmantaṃ sthitaṃ vāpi bhuñjānaṃ vā guṇānvitam |
vimūḍhā nānupaśyanti paśyanti jñānacakṣuṣaḥ

**Meaning:** The deluded never notice the soul arriving, leaving, or enjoying. Those with wisdom's eyes see.

**Takeaway:** Eyesight, not absence, is the issue.

**Provenance:** sa:15:10, telang:T04, arnold:D10-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "deluded" "eyes  of knowledge"
