# Pack ch03-4 — Chapter 3: Selfless Action

Traditional: The Yoga of Action · Verses 3:31–3:40 · Status: **pending**

## 3:31

**Sanskrit:** ये मे मतमिदं नित्यमनुतिष्ठन्ति मानवाः ।
श्रद्धावन्तोऽनसूयन्तो मुच्यन्ते तेऽपि कर्मभिः

**IAST:** ye me matamidaṃ nityamanutiṣṭhanti mānavāḥ |
śraddhāvanto'nasūyanto mucyante te'pi karmabhiḥ

**Meaning:** Those who live by this teaching of Mine, full of faith and free of nitpicking — they are freed from the bonds of action.

**Takeaway:** Trust plus practice beats analysis alone.

**Provenance:** sa:3:31, telang:T08, arnold:C04-shared

**Telang [T08]:** "act on this opinion" "released"

**Consensus [ORPHAN]:** neither source covers (telang 0.44, arnold 0.44) — check: bond, nitpick

## 3:32

**Sanskrit:** ये त्वेतदभ्यसूयन्तो नानुतिष्ठन्ति मे मतम् ।
सर्वज्ञानविमूढांस्तान्विद्धि नष्टानचेतसः

**IAST:** ye tvetadabhyasūyanto nānutiṣṭhanti me matam |
sarvajñānavimūḍhāṃstānviddhi naṣṭānacetasaḥ

**Meaning:** But those who sneer at My teaching and ignore it are senseless, confused in all knowledge, and lost.

**Takeaway:** The teaching warns about rejecting the teaching. Noted.

**Provenance:** sa:3:32, telang:T08, arnold:C04-shared

**Telang [T08]:** "carp at my opinion" "ruined"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Arnold covers (0.57), Telang does not (0.29)

## 3:33

**Sanskrit:** सदृशं चेष्टते स्वस्याः प्रकृतेर्ज्ञानवानपि ।
प्रकृतिं यान्ति भूतानि निग्रहः किं करिष्यति

**IAST:** sadṛśaṃ ceṣṭate svasyāḥ prakṛterjñānavānapi |
prakṛtiṃ yānti bhūtāni nigrahaḥ kiṃ kariṣyati

**Meaning:** Even the wise act according to their nature. All beings follow nature — what can repression do?

**Takeaway:** Work with your nature, not against it.

**Provenance:** sa:3:33, telang:T08, arnold:C04-shared

**Telang [T08]:** "man of knowledge" "restraint effect"

## 3:34

**Sanskrit:** इन्द्रियस्येन्द्रियस्यार्थे रागद्वेषौ व्यवस्थितौ ।
तयोर्न वशमागच्छेत्तौ ह्यस्य परिपन्थिनौ

**IAST:** indriyasyendriyasyārthe rāgadveṣau vyavasthitau |
tayorna vaśamāgacchettau hyasya paripanthinau

**Meaning:** Every sense has its likes and dislikes toward its objects. Never bow to either — both are your enemies on the path.

**Takeaway:** Your preferences are not your friends.

**Provenance:** sa:3:34, telang:T08, arnold:C04-shared

**Telang [T08]:** "affections" "opponents"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Arnold covers (0.62), Telang does not (0.38)

## 3:35

**Sanskrit:** श्रेयान्स्वधर्मो विगुणः परधर्मात्स्वनुष्ठितात् ।
स्वधर्मे निधनं श्रेयः परधर्मो भयावहः

**IAST:** śreyānsvadharmo viguṇaḥ paradharmātsvanuṣṭhitāt |
svadharme nidhanaṃ śreyaḥ paradharmo bhayāvahaḥ

**Meaning:** Your own duty, done badly, beats another's duty done well. Death in your own duty is fine; a borrowed path is perilous.

**Takeaway:** Stay in your lane — gloriously.

**Provenance:** sa:3:35, telang:T08, arnold:C04-shared

**Telang [T08]:** "own duty" "dangerous"

**Consensus [ORPHAN]:** neither source covers (telang 0.40, arnold 0.30) — check: badly, beat, borrow, fine, perilou

## 3:36 (अर्जुन)

**Sanskrit:** अथ केन प्रयुक्तोऽयं पापं चरति पूरुषः ।
अनिच्छन्नपि वार्ष्णेय बलादिव नियोजितः

**IAST:** atha kena prayukto'yaṃ pāpaṃ carati pūruṣaḥ |
anicchannapi vārṣṇeya balādiva niyojitaḥ

**Meaning:** Arjuna said: but what drives a person to sin, Krishna, even unwillingly, as if dragged by force?

**Takeaway:** The oldest question in the book, asked plainly.

**Provenance:** sa:3:36, telang:T09+T10, arnold:C05

**Telang [T09]:** "Ar^una .said :"
**Telang [T10]:** "commit sin"

## 3:37

**Sanskrit:** श्रीभगवानुवाच
काम एष क्रोध एष रजोगुणसमुद्भवः ।
महाशनो महापाप्मा विद्ध्येनमिह वैरिणम्

**IAST:** śrībhagavānuvāca
kāma eṣa krodha eṣa rajoguṇasamudbhavaḥ |
mahāśano mahāpāpmā viddhyenamiha vairiṇam

**Meaning:** Krishna said: it is desire, it is anger — born of passion, all-devouring, terribly sinful. Know it as the enemy here.

**Takeaway:** Name the enemy: wanting.

**Provenance:** sa:3:37, telang:T12+T13-head, arnold:C06-shared

**Telang [T12]:** "The Deity said :"
**Telang [T13]:** "It is desire" "foe in this world"

## 3:38

**Sanskrit:** धूमेनाव्रियते वह्निर्यथादर्शो मलेन च ।
यथोल्बेनावृतो गर्भस्तथा तेनेदमावृतम्

**IAST:** dhūmenāvriyate vahniryathādarśo malena ca |
yatholbenāvṛto garbhastathā tenedamāvṛtam

**Meaning:** As fire is veiled by smoke, a mirror by dust, a baby by the womb — so wisdom is veiled by desire.

**Takeaway:** Three pictures for one blindness.

**Provenance:** sa:3:38, telang:T13, arnold:C06-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "enveloped  by smoke" "enveloped by desire"

## 3:39

**Sanskrit:** आवृतं ज्ञानमेतेन ज्ञानिनो नित्यवैरिणा ।
कामरूपेण कौन्तेय दुष्पूरेणानलेन च

**IAST:** āvṛtaṃ jñānametena jñānino nityavairiṇā |
kāmarūpeṇa kaunteya duṣpūreṇānalena ca

**Meaning:** Wisdom is smothered by this eternal enemy of the wise. Desire burns like fire. It is never satisfied.

**Takeaway:** Insatiable is the scariest word here.

**Provenance:** sa:3:39, telang:T13, arnold:C06-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "constant foe" "insatiable"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Arnold covers (0.50), Telang does not (0.40)

## 3:40

**Sanskrit:** इन्द्रियाणि मनो बुद्धिरस्याधिष्ठानमुच्यते ।
एतैर्विमोहयत्येष ज्ञानमावृत्य देहिनम्

**IAST:** indriyāṇi mano buddhirasyādhiṣṭhānamucyate |
etairvimohayatyeṣa jñānamāvṛtya dehinam

**Meaning:** The senses, the mind and understanding are its home. Through them desire confuses the embodied self, veiling wisdom.

**Takeaway:** Know where the enemy lives.

**Provenance:** sa:3:40, telang:T13, arnold:C06-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "its seat" "deludes"
