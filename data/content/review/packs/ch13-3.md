# Pack ch13-3 — Chapter 13: The Field and the Witness

Traditional: Field and Knower of the Field · Verses 13:21–13:30 · Status: **pending**

## 13:21

**Sanskrit:** पुरुषः प्रकृतिस्थो हि भुङ्‌क्ते प्रकृतिजान्गुणान् ।
कारणं गुणसङ्गोऽस्य सदसद्योनिजन्मसु

**IAST:** puruṣaḥ prakṛtistho hi bhuṅ‌kte prakṛtijānguṇān |
kāraṇaṃ guṇasaṅgo'sya sadasadyonijanmasu

**Meaning:** Spirit wedded to nature tastes nature's moods. Clinging to them decides the next womb, high or low.

**Takeaway:** Taste determines rebirth. Choose flavors wisely.

**Provenance:** sa:13:21, telang:T05, arnold:D08-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "joined" "evil wombs"

## 13:22

**Sanskrit:** उपद्रष्टानुमन्ता च भर्ता भोक्ता महेश्वरः ।
परमात्मेति चाप्युक्तो देहेऽस्मिन्पुरुषः परः

**IAST:** upadraṣṭānumantā ca bhartā bhoktā maheśvaraḥ |
paramātmeti cāpyukto dehe'sminpuruṣaḥ paraḥ

**Meaning:** Yet spirit in the body is also witness, approver, supporter, experiencer. Great Lord. Supreme Self.

**Takeaway:** The lodger is also the landlord.

**Provenance:** sa:13:22, telang:T05, arnold:D09

**Telang [T05]:** "adviser" "supreme self"

## 13:23

**Sanskrit:** य एवं वेत्ति पुरुषं प्रकृतिं च गुणैः सह ।
सर्वथा वर्तमानोऽपि न स भूयोऽभिजायते

**IAST:** ya evaṃ vetti puruṣaṃ prakṛtiṃ ca guṇaiḥ saha |
sarvathā vartamāno'pi na sa bhūyo'bhijāyate

**Meaning:** Whoever truly knows spirit, nature, and nature's moods is never reborn. Whatever life one leads.

**Takeaway:** Knowledge voids the return ticket.

**Provenance:** sa:13:23, telang:T05, arnold:D10-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "thus knows" "however living"

## 13:24

**Sanskrit:** ध्यानेनात्मनि पश्यन्ति केचिदात्मानमात्मना ।
अन्ये सांख्येन योगेन कर्मयोगेन चापरे

**IAST:** dhyānenātmani paśyanti kecidātmānamātmanā |
anye sāṃkhyena yogena karmayogena cāpare

**Meaning:** Some see the Self by meditation. Some by philosophy. Some by selfless action.

**Takeaway:** Three doors, one room.

**Provenance:** sa:13:24, telang:T05, arnold:D10-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "by the self" "Karma-yoga"

## 13:25

**Sanskrit:** अन्ये त्वेवमजानन्तः श्रुत्वान्येभ्य उपासते ।
तेऽपि चातितरन्त्येव मृत्युं श्रुतिपरायणाः

**IAST:** anye tvevamajānantaḥ śrutvānyebhya upāsate |
te'pi cātitarantyeva mṛtyuṃ śrutiparāyaṇāḥ

**Meaning:** Others only hear the teaching and trust it, devoted. They too cross beyond death.

**Takeaway:** Hearing with faith also saves.

**Provenance:** sa:13:25, telang:T05, arnold:D10-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "hearing from others" "cross  beyond death"

## 13:26

**Sanskrit:** यावत्संजायते किंचित्सत्त्वं स्थावरजङ्गमम् ।
क्षेत्रक्षेत्रज्ञसंयोगात्तद्विद्धि भरतर्षभ

**IAST:** yāvatsaṃjāyate kiṃcitsattvaṃ sthāvarajaṅgamam |
kṣetrakṣetrajñasaṃyogāttadviddhi bharatarṣabha

**Meaning:** Whatever lives and moves or stands still is born of the field meeting its knower.

**Takeaway:** Every birth has two parents.

**Provenance:** sa:13:26, telang:T05, arnold:D11-shared

**Notes:** Split across T05-tail/T07-head (footnotes + p106 between); T05 ends mid-13:27.

**Telang [T05]:** "comes into existence" "connexion"

## 13:27

**Sanskrit:** समं सर्वेषु भूतेषु तिष्ठन्तं परमेश्वरम् ।
विनश्यत्स्वविनश्यन्तं यः पश्यति स पश्यति

**IAST:** samaṃ sarveṣu bhūteṣu tiṣṭhantaṃ parameśvaram |
vinaśyatsvavinaśyantaṃ yaḥ paśyati sa paśyati

**Meaning:** Who sees the supreme Lord dwelling equally in all beings — undying amid the dying — truly sees.

**Takeaway:** Equal sight is true sight.

**Provenance:** sa:13:27, telang:T05+T07, arnold:D11-shared

**Notes:** Split across T05-tail/T07-head (see 13:26 note).

**Telang [T05]:** "He sees (truly),"
**Telang [T07]:** "abiding" "though they are destroyed"

## 13:28

**Sanskrit:** समं पश्यन्हि सर्वत्र समवस्थितमीश्वरम् ।
न हिनस्त्यात्मनात्मानं ततो याति परां गतिम्

**IAST:** samaṃ paśyanhi sarvatra samavasthitamīśvaram |
na hinastyātmanātmānaṃ tato yāti parāṃ gatim

**Meaning:** Seeing the Lord the same everywhere harms not the self by the self. Then comes the highest goal.

**Takeaway:** The seer is safe from himself.

**Provenance:** sa:13:28, telang:T07, arnold:D11-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "everywhere alike" "highest goal"

## 13:29

**Sanskrit:** प्रकृत्यैव च कर्माणि क्रियमाणानि सर्वशः ।
यः पश्यति तथात्मानमकर्तारं स पश्यति

**IAST:** prakṛtyaiva ca karmāṇi kriyamāṇāni sarvaśaḥ |
yaḥ paśyati tathātmānamakartāraṃ sa paśyati

**Meaning:** Who sees all acts done by nature alone — and the self as no doer — truly sees.

**Takeaway:** The non-doer doctrine, stated cleanly.

**Provenance:** sa:13:29, telang:T07, arnold:D11-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "nature alone" "not the  doer"

## 13:30

**Sanskrit:** यदा भूतपृथग्भावमेकस्थमनुपश्यति ।
तत एव च विस्तारं ब्रह्म संपद्यते तदा

**IAST:** yadā bhūtapṛthagbhāvamekasthamanupaśyati |
tata eva ca vistāraṃ brahma saṃpadyate tadā

**Meaning:** Seeing all separate beings rooted in One. Spreading from One. One becomes the ultimate.

**Takeaway:** Many branches. One trunk. One arrival.

**Provenance:** sa:13:30, telang:T07, arnold:D11-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "variety of entities" "Brahman"
