# Pack ch08-1 — Chapter 8: Death and Beyond

Traditional: The Yoga of the Imperishable · Verses 8:1–8:10 · Status: **pending**

## 8:1 (अर्जुन)

**Sanskrit:** किं तद्ब्रह्म किमध्यात्मं किं कर्म पुरुषोत्तम ।
अधिभूतं च किं प्रोक्तमधिदैवं किमुच्यते

**IAST:** kiṃ tadbrahma kimadhyātmaṃ kiṃ karma puruṣottama |
adhibhūtaṃ ca kiṃ proktamadhidaivaṃ kimucyate

**Meaning:** Arjuna said: what is the ultimate? What is the inner self? What is action? Tell me plainly, best of beings.

**Takeaway:** Eight questions. The chapter answers every one.

**Provenance:** sa:8:1, telang:T02+T03-head, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T02]:** "Ar^una said :"
**Telang [T03]:** "What is that Brahman" "is action"

## 8:2

**Sanskrit:** अधियज्ञः कथं कोऽत्र देहेऽस्मिन्मधुसूदन ।
प्रयाणकाले च कथं ज्ञेयोऽसि नियतात्मभिः

**IAST:** adhiyajñaḥ kathaṃ ko'tra dehe'sminmadhusūdana |
prayāṇakāle ca kathaṃ jñeyo'si niyatātmabhiḥ

**Meaning:** And what is the Lord of beings? The Lord of gods? How are You sacrifice itself, here in a body? And how do the self-controlled know You at the hour of death?

**Takeaway:** The questions continue. Definitions first, practice after.

**Provenance:** sa:8:2, telang:T03, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T03]:** "Adhibhilta" "seifs"

## 8:3

**Sanskrit:** श्रीभगवानुवाच
अक्षरं ब्रह्म परमं स्वभावोऽध्यात्ममुच्यते ।
भूतभावोद्भवकरो विसर्गः कर्मसंज्ञितः

**IAST:** śrībhagavānuvāca
akṣaraṃ brahma paramaṃ svabhāvo'dhyātmamucyate |
bhūtabhāvodbhavakaro visargaḥ karmasaṃjñitaḥ

**Meaning:** Krishna said: the ultimate is supreme and indestructible. Its dwelling in each person is the inner self. And action means the offering that creates and feeds all things.

**Takeaway:** Three definitions, no mystique.

**Provenance:** sa:8:3, telang:T04+P04hdr, arnold:D02-shared

**Notes:** Speaker header P04 ("'I'he Deity said :") misclassified as footnote by leading-quote rule; no verse text lost.

**Telang [T04]:** "supreme, the indestructible" "named action"

## 8:4

**Sanskrit:** अधिभूतं क्षरो भावः पुरुषश्चाधिदैवतम् ।
अधियज्ञोऽहमेवात्र देहे देहभृतां वर

**IAST:** adhibhūtaṃ kṣaro bhāvaḥ puruṣaścādhidaivatam |
adhiyajño'hamevātra dehe dehabhṛtāṃ vara

**Meaning:** The Lord of beings is all that perishes. The Lord of gods is the primal person. And the Lord of sacrifice is Myself, here in this body.

**Takeaway:** The remaining answers, equally direct.

**Provenance:** sa:8:4, telang:T04, arnold:D02-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "all perishable things" "I myself in this body"

## 8:5

**Sanskrit:** अन्तकाले च मामेव स्मरन्मुक्त्वा कलेवरम् ।
यः प्रयाति स मद्भावं याति नास्त्यत्र संशयः

**IAST:** antakāle ca māmeva smaranmuktvā kalevaram |
yaḥ prayāti sa madbhāvaṃ yāti nāstyatra saṃśayaḥ

**Meaning:** Whoever leaves the body remembering Me at the last moment enters My being. Of this there is no doubt.

**Takeaway:** The final thought is the final address.

**Provenance:** sa:8:5, telang:T06, arnold:D02-shared

**Telang [T06]:** "remembering me in (his) last moments" "no doubt of that"

## 8:6

**Sanskrit:** यं यं वापि स्मरन्भावं त्यजत्यन्ते कलेवरम् ।
तं तमेवैति कौन्तेय सदा तद्भावभावितः

**IAST:** yaṃ yaṃ vāpi smaranbhāvaṃ tyajatyante kalevaram |
taṃ tamevaiti kaunteya sadā tadbhāvabhāvitaḥ

**Meaning:** Whatever form one remembers at the end, to that one goes — shaped by a lifetime of dwelling on it.

**Takeaway:** You become what you rehearse.

**Provenance:** sa:8:6, telang:T06, arnold:D02-shared

**Telang [T06]:** "whichever form" "ponder on it"

## 8:7

**Sanskrit:** तस्मात्सर्वेषु कालेषु मामनुस्मर युध्य च ।
मय्यर्पितमनोबुद्धिर्मामेवैष्यस्यसंशयम्

**IAST:** tasmātsarveṣu kāleṣu māmanusmara yudhya ca |
mayyarpitamanobuddhirmāmevaiṣyasyasaṃśayam

**Meaning:** So remember Me always — and fight. Mind and understanding fixed on Me, you will surely reach Me.

**Takeaway:** Remembrance and duty, at the same time.

**Provenance:** sa:8:7, telang:T06, arnold:D03-shared

## 8:8

**Sanskrit:** अभ्यासयोगयुक्तेन चेतसा नान्यगामिना ।
परमं पुरुषं दिव्यं याति पार्थानुचिन्तयन्

**IAST:** abhyāsayogayuktena cetasā nānyagāminā |
paramaṃ puruṣaṃ divyaṃ yāti pārthānucintayan

**Meaning:** Mind unwavering. Trained by steady practice. One goes to the divine supreme Person.

**Takeaway:** Steadiness is a trained muscle.

**Provenance:** sa:8:8, telang:T06, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T06]:** "not(running)" "goes to him"

## 8:9

**Sanskrit:** कविं पुराणमनुशासितारमणोरणीयांसमनुस्मरेद्यः ।
सर्वस्य धातारमचिन्त्यरूपमादित्यवर्णं तमसः परस्तात्

**IAST:** kaviṃ purāṇamanuśāsitāramaṇoraṇīyāṃsamanusmaredyaḥ |
sarvasya dhātāramacintyarūpamādityavarṇaṃ tamasaḥ parastāt

**Meaning:** Meditate on the ancient Seer. The ruler. Subtler than the atom. Sustainer of all. Form beyond thought. Bright as the sun. Beyond all darkness.

**Takeaway:** The portrait to hold in mind.

**Provenance:** sa:8:9, telang:T06, arnold:D04+D05-cont

**Notes:** Arnold sentence runs G04->G05 (D04+D05); D05 tail ('Right life!') opens 8:10's stanza.

**Telang [T06]:** "minutest atom" "beyond all darkness"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.82), Arnold does not (0.27)

## 8:10

**Sanskrit:** प्रयाणकाले मनसाचलेन
भक्त्या युक्तो योगबलेन चैव ।
भ्रुवोर्मध्ये प्राणमावेश्य सम्यक्
स तं परं पुरुषमुपैति दिव्यम्

**IAST:** prayāṇakāle manasācalena
bhaktyā yukto yogabalena caiva |
bhruvormadhye prāṇamāveśya samyak
sa taṃ paraṃ puruṣamupaiti divyam

**Meaning:** At the hour of departure, steady in devotion. Breath gathered between the brows. One attains that divine Being.

**Takeaway:** The practice for the last moment, rehearsed beforehand.

**Provenance:** sa:8:10, telang:T06, arnold:D06

**Telang [T06]:** "transcendent" "briefly"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.70), Arnold does not (0.40)
