# Pack ch03-3 — Chapter 3: Selfless Action

Traditional: The Yoga of Action · Verses 3:21–3:30 · Status: **pending**

## 3:21

**Sanskrit:** यद्यदाचरति श्रेष्ठस्तत्तदेवेतरो जनः ।
स यत्प्रमाणं कुरुते लोकस्तदनुवर्तते

**IAST:** yadyadācarati śreṣṭhastattadevetaro janaḥ |
sa yatpramāṇaṃ kurute lokastadanuvartate

**Meaning:** There is nothing I must do in all three worlds. Nothing to gain that I have not gained. And yet I keep working.

**Takeaway:** Even perfection shows up to work. What is your excuse?

**Provenance:** sa:3:21, telang:T05, arnold:C04-shared

## 3:22

**Sanskrit:** न मे पार्थास्ति कर्तव्यं त्रिषु लोकेषु किंचन ।
नानवाप्तमवाप्तव्यं वर्त एव च कर्मणि

**IAST:** na me pārthāsti kartavyaṃ triṣu lokeṣu kiṃcana |
nānavāptamavāptavyaṃ varta eva ca karmaṇi

**Meaning:** For if I ever stopped working tirelessly, everyone would follow my path to ruin. If I abandoned action, these worlds would collapse, and I would destroy all beings.

**Takeaway:** The universe runs on someone showing up.

**Provenance:** sa:3:22, telang:T05+T07-head, arnold:C04-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "There is nothing" "three worlds"
**Telang [T07]:** "nothing to acquire" "Still I do engage"

**Witness [gretil]:** SANDHI-JOINT

## 3:23

**Sanskrit:** यदि ह्यहं न वर्तेयं जातु कर्मण्यतन्द्रितः ।
मम वर्त्मानुवर्तन्ते मनुष्याः पार्थ सर्वशः

**IAST:** yadi hyahaṃ na varteyaṃ jātu karmaṇyatandritaḥ |
mama vartmānuvartante manuṣyāḥ pārtha sarvaśaḥ

**Meaning:** If I stopped working, people would sink into laziness. Worlds would break. I would corrupt every race and ruin all beings.

**Takeaway:** Quitting has a blast radius.

**Provenance:** sa:3:23, telang:T07, arnold:C04-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "should I at any" "follow in my path"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Arnold covers (0.75), Telang does not (0.42)

## 3:24

**Sanskrit:** उत्सीदेयुरिमे लोका न कुर्यां कर्म चेदहम् ।
संकरस्य च कर्ता स्यामुपहन्यामिमाः प्रजाः

**IAST:** utsīdeyurime lokā na kuryāṃ karma cedaham |
saṃkarasya ca kartā syāmupahanyāmimāḥ prajāḥ

**Meaning:** The ignorant act attached to their deeds. So should the wise act — unattached, to hold the world together.

**Takeaway:** Same actions, different grip.

**Provenance:** sa:3:24, telang:T07, arnold:C04-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "If I did not perform" "ruining these people"

## 3:25

**Sanskrit:** सक्ताः कर्मण्यविद्वांसो यथा कुर्वन्ति भारत ।
कुर्याद्विद्वांस्तथासक्तश्चिकीर्षुर्लोकसंग्रहम्

**IAST:** saktāḥ karmaṇyavidvāṃso yathā kurvanti bhārata |
kuryādvidvāṃstathāsaktaścikīrṣurlokasaṃgraham

**Meaning:** The wise should not shake the minds of the ignorant, who cling to deeds. Working devoutly themselves, let them set everyone to work.

**Takeaway:** Do not break beginners. Employ them.

**Provenance:** sa:3:25, telang:T07, arnold:C04-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "As the ignorant act" "without attachment"

## 3:26

**Sanskrit:** न बुद्धिभेदं जनयेदज्ञानां कर्मसङ्गिनाम् ।
जोषयेत्सर्वकर्माणि विद्वान्युक्तः समाचरन्

**IAST:** na buddhibhedaṃ janayedajñānāṃ karmasaṅginām |
joṣayetsarvakarmāṇi vidvānyuktaḥ samācaran

**Meaning:** The self, blinded by ego, thinks 'I am the doer.' But nature's qualities perform every deed.

**Takeaway:** You are not the author you think you are.

**Provenance:** sa:3:26, telang:T07, arnold:C04-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "should not shake" "apply themselves"

## 3:27

**Sanskrit:** प्रकृतेः क्रियमाणानि गुणैः कर्माणि सर्वशः ।
अहंकारविमूढात्मा कर्ताहमिति मन्यते

**IAST:** prakṛteḥ kriyamāṇāni guṇaiḥ karmāṇi sarvaśaḥ |
ahaṃkāravimūḍhātmā kartāhamiti manyate

**Meaning:** But one who knows the truth stays unattached. It is only qualities playing among qualities.

**Takeaway:** Watch the play instead of grabbing the pieces.

**Provenance:** sa:3:27, telang:T07, arnold:C04-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "thinks himself  the doer"

## 3:28

**Sanskrit:** तत्त्ववित्तु महाबाहो गुणकर्मविभागयोः ।
गुणा गुणेषु वर्तन्त इति मत्वा न सज्जते

**IAST:** tattvavittu mahābāho guṇakarmavibhāgayoḥ |
guṇā guṇeṣu vartanta iti matvā na sajjate

**Meaning:** Those fooled by nature's qualities cling to its workings. The wise must not unsettle such half-knowing, slow minds.

**Takeaway:** Meet people inside their picture before redrawing it.

**Provenance:** sa:3:28, telang:T07, arnold:C04-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "knows the truth" "qualities deal with qualities"

**Witness [gretil]:** SANDHI-JOINT

## 3:29

**Sanskrit:** प्रकृतेर्गुणसंमूढाः सज्जन्ते गुणकर्मसु ।
तानकृत्स्नविदो मन्दान्कृत्स्नविन्न विचालयेत्

**IAST:** prakṛterguṇasaṃmūḍhāḥ sajjante guṇakarmasu |
tānakṛtsnavido mandānkṛtsnavinna vicālayet

**Meaning:** Hand all your actions to Me, mind fixed on the self, free of hope and ego. Then fight, your fever gone.

**Takeaway:** Surrender the results, keep the sword.

**Provenance:** sa:3:29, telang:T07, arnold:C04-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "those who are deluded" "imperfect knowledge"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Arnold covers (0.55), Telang does not (0.45)

## 3:30

**Sanskrit:** मयि सर्वाणि कर्माणि संन्यस्याध्यात्मचेतसा ।
निराशीर्निर्ममो भूत्वा युध्यस्व विगतज्वरः

**IAST:** mayi sarvāṇi karmāṇi saṃnyasyādhyātmacetasā |
nirāśīrnirmamo bhūtvā yudhyasva vigatajvaraḥ

**Meaning:** Dedicate all your actions to Me, mind anchored in the highest self. Fight free of hope, of mine-ness, of fever.

**Takeaway:** Three poisons out, one sword in hand.

**Provenance:** sa:3:30, telang:T07+T08-head, arnold:C04-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "Dedicating all actions  to me"
**Telang [T08]:** "desire, without" "mental trouble"
