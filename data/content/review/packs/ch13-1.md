# Pack ch13-1 — Chapter 13: The Field and the Witness

Traditional: Field and Knower of the Field · Verses 13:1–13:10 · Status: **pending**

## 13:1

**Sanskrit:** इदं शरीरं कौन्तेय क्षेत्रमित्यभिधीयते ।
एतद्यो वेत्ति तं प्राहुः क्षेत्रज्ञ इति तद्विदः

**IAST:** idaṃ śarīraṃ kaunteya kṣetramityabhidhīyate |
etadyo vetti taṃ prāhuḥ kṣetrajña iti tadvidaḥ

**Meaning:** Krishna said: this body is the field. Whoever knows it is the knower of the field.

**Takeaway:** Two words unlock the chapter: field and knower.

**Provenance:** sa:13:1, telang:T00+T01-head, arnold:D02-shared

**Notes:** Arnold prefixes a question stanza (G01=D01, no vulgate home); his G02 covers 13:1-3.

**Telang [T00]:** "The Deity said :"
**Telang [T01]:** "called Kshetra" "knows it"

**Witness [gretil]:** SANDHI-JOINT

## 13:2

**Sanskrit:** क्षेत्रज्ञं चापि मां विद्धि सर्वक्षेत्रेषु भारत ।
क्षेत्रक्षेत्रज्ञयोर्ज्ञानं यत्तज्ज्ञानं मतं मम

**IAST:** kṣetrajñaṃ cāpi māṃ viddhi sarvakṣetreṣu bhārata |
kṣetrakṣetrajñayorjñānaṃ yattajjñānaṃ mataṃ mama

**Meaning:** And know Me as the knower in every field. Knowing field and knower — that is true knowledge.

**Takeaway:** The owner of every field is one.

**Provenance:** sa:13:2, telang:T01, arnold:D02-shared

**Telang [T01]:** "in all Kshetras" "real) knowledge"

## 13:3

**Sanskrit:** तत्क्षेत्रं यच्च यादृक्च यद्विकारि यतश्च यत् ।
स च यो यत्प्रभावश्च तत्समासेन मे शृणु

**IAST:** tatkṣetraṃ yacca yādṛkca yadvikāri yataśca yat |
sa ca yo yatprabhāvaśca tatsamāsena me śṛṇu

**Meaning:** Hear briefly what the field is, what it is like, its changes, its source. Who the knower is, and his powers.

**Takeaway:** The syllabus, stated plainly.

**Provenance:** sa:13:3, telang:T01, arnold:D02-shared

**Telang [T01]:** "in brief what" "what his powers"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.62), Arnold does not (0.38)

## 13:4

**Sanskrit:** ऋषिभिर्बहुधा गीतं छन्दोभिर्विविधैः पृथक् ।
ब्रह्मसूत्रपदैश्चैव हेतुमद्भिर्विनिश्चितैः

**IAST:** ṛṣibhirbahudhā gītaṃ chandobhirvividhaiḥ pṛthak |
brahmasūtrapadaiścaiva hetumadbhirviniścitaiḥ

**Meaning:** Sages have sung this in many hymns, distinctly. In reasoned texts that reveal the ultimate.

**Takeaway:** Old songs, settled arguments, one subject.

**Provenance:** sa:13:4, telang:T01, arnold:none-omitted

**Notes:** Arnold omits 13:4 (G03 ellipsis divider); Telang + Sanskrit provenance only.

**Telang [T01]:** "sung in various ways" "full instruction"

**Consensus [ARNOLD-ABSENT]:** Arnold omits this verse; Telang-only by construction — check: reason, reveal

## 13:5

**Sanskrit:** महाभूतान्यहंकारो बुद्धिरव्यक्तमेव च ।
इन्द्रियाणि दशैकं च पञ्च चेन्द्रियगोचराः

**IAST:** mahābhūtānyahaṃkāro buddhiravyaktameva ca |
indriyāṇi daśaikaṃ ca pañca cendriyagocarāḥ

**Meaning:** The five elements, ego, intellect, the unseen. Ten senses plus mind. Five sense-objects.

**Takeaway:** The field's parts list, part one.

**Provenance:** sa:13:5, telang:T01, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T01]:** "great elements" "ten senses"

## 13:6

**Sanskrit:** इच्छा द्वेषः सुखं दुःखं संघातश्चेतना धृतिः ।
एतत्क्षेत्रं समासेन सविकारमुदाहृतम्

**IAST:** icchā dveṣaḥ sukhaṃ duḥkhaṃ saṃghātaścetanā dhṛtiḥ |
etatkṣetraṃ samāsena savikāramudāhṛtam

**Meaning:** Desire, hatred, pleasure, pain. Body, awareness, resolve. This, in brief, is the field with all its changes.

**Takeaway:** The inner weather counts as field too.

**Provenance:** sa:13:6, telang:T01+T03-head, arnold:D04-shared

**Notes:** Split across T01-tail/T03-head (citation fragment T02 between).

**Telang [T01]:** "five objects of sense"
**Telang [T03]:** "aversion" "with changes"

## 13:7

**Sanskrit:** अमानित्वमदम्भित्वमहिंसा क्षान्तिरार्जवम् ।
आचार्योपासनं शौचं स्थैर्यमात्मविनिग्रहः

**IAST:** amānitvamadambhitvamahiṃsā kṣāntirārjavam |
ācāryopāsanaṃ śaucaṃ sthairyamātmavinigrahaḥ

**Meaning:** Humility, honesty, harmlessness. Patience, honor, reverence for teachers. Purity, steadiness, self-rule.

**Takeaway:** Wisdom listed as character, not cleverness.

**Provenance:** sa:13:7, telang:T03, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T03]:** "vanity" "preceptor"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Arnold covers (0.70), Telang does not (0.40)

## 13:8

**Sanskrit:** इन्द्रियार्थेषु वैराग्यमनहंकार एव च ।
जन्ममृत्युजराव्याधिदुःखदोषानुदर्शनम्

**IAST:** indriyārtheṣu vairāgyamanahaṃkāra eva ca |
janmamṛtyujarāvyādhiduḥkhadoṣānudarśanam

**Meaning:** Freedom from sense slavery. Freedom from ego. Seeing the pain in birth, death, age, and disease.

**Takeaway:** Clear eyes about the human lot.

**Provenance:** sa:13:8, telang:T03, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T03]:** "indifference" "egoism"

**Witness [gretil]:** SANDHI-JOINT

## 13:9

**Sanskrit:** असक्तिरनभिष्वङ्गः पुत्रदारगृहादिषु ।
नित्यं च समचित्तत्वमिष्टानिष्टोपपत्तिषु

**IAST:** asaktiranabhiṣvaṅgaḥ putradāragṛhādiṣu |
nityaṃ ca samacittatvamiṣṭāniṣṭopapattiṣu

**Meaning:** No clinging to children, spouse, or home. Even-minded through welcome and unwelcome alike.

**Takeaway:** Love without clutching. Calm without numbness.

**Provenance:** sa:13:9, telang:T03, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T03]:** "birth, death" "son, wife"

## 13:10

**Sanskrit:** मयि चानन्ययोगेन भक्तिरव्यभिचारिणी ।
विविक्तदेशसेवित्वमरतिर्जनसंसदि

**IAST:** mayi cānanyayogena bhaktiravyabhicāriṇī |
viviktadeśasevitvamaratirjanasaṃsadi

**Meaning:** Unswerving devotion to Me alone. Love of solitude. Hunger for self-knowledge.

**Takeaway:** Three practices of the wise.

**Provenance:** sa:13:10, telang:T03, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T03]:** "equability" "without meditation"
