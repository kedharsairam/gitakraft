## 2:1 (सञ्जय)

**Sanskrit:** तं तथा कृपयाविष्टमश्रुपूर्णाकुलेक्षणम् ।
विषीदन्तमिदं वाक्यमुवाच मधुसूदनः

**IAST:** taṃ tathā kṛpayāviṣṭamaśrupūrṇākulekṣaṇam |
viṣīdantamidaṃ vākyamuvāca madhusūdanaḥ

**Meaning:** Sanjaya said: to Arjuna, drowned in pity, his eyes full of tears and troubled, Krishna spoke these words.

**Takeaway:** Help arrives when grief is deepest.

**Provenance:** sa:2:1, telang:T00+T01, arnold:B01

**Telang [T00]:** "Saw^aya said :"
**Telang [T01]:** "overcome with pity" "turbid"

## 2:2

**Sanskrit:** श्रीभगवानुवाच
कुतस्त्वा कश्मलमिदं विषमे समुपस्थितम् ।
अनार्यजुष्टमस्वर्ग्यमकीर्तिकरमर्जुन

**IAST:** śrībhagavānuvāca
kutastvā kaśmalamidaṃ viṣame samupasthitam |
anāryajuṣṭamasvargyamakīrtikaramarjuna

**Meaning:** Krishna said: where has this weakness come from, Arjuna, here on the field of battle? It suits no noble man, wins no heaven, and brings only shame.

**Takeaway:** Name weakness plainly before fighting it.

**Provenance:** sa:2:2, telang:T02+T03-head, arnold:B02-shared

## 2:3

**Sanskrit:** क्लैब्यं मा स्म गमः पार्थ नैतत्त्वय्युपपद्यते ।
क्षुद्रं हृदयदौर्बल्यं त्यक्त्वोत्तिष्ठ परन्तप

**IAST:** klaibyaṃ mā sma gamaḥ pārtha naitattvayyupapadyate |
kṣudraṃ hṛdayadaurbalyaṃ tyaktvottiṣṭha parantapa

**Meaning:** Do not turn coward, Arjuna. It does not suit you. Shake off this petty faint-heartedness and stand up, scorcher of foes.

**Takeaway:** You are bigger than this moment.

**Provenance:** sa:2:3, telang:T03, arnold:B02-shared

**Telang [T03]:** "Be not effeminate" "terror of"

**Consensus [ORPHAN]:** neither source covers (telang 0.27, arnold 0.36) — check: faint, heartedness, petty, scorcher, shake, stand, turn

## 2:4 (अर्जुन)

**Sanskrit:** कथं भीष्ममहं संख्ये द्रोणं च मधुसूदन ।
इषुभिः प्रति योत्स्यामि पूजार्हावरिसूदन

**IAST:** kathaṃ bhīṣmamahaṃ saṃkhye droṇaṃ ca madhusūdana |
iṣubhiḥ prati yotsyāmi pūjārhāvarisūdana

**Meaning:** Arjuna said: how can I shoot arrows at Bhishma and Drona in battle, Krishna? Both deserve my worship, destroyer of foes.

**Takeaway:** The hardest fights are against people you respect.

**Provenance:** sa:2:4, telang:T04+T05-head, arnold:B03

## 2:5

**Sanskrit:** गुरूनहत्वा हि महानुभावान् श्रेयो भोक्तुं भैक्ष्यमपीह लोके ।
हत्वार्थकामांस्तु गुरूनिहैव भुञ्जीय भोगान् रुधिरप्रदिग्धान्

**IAST:** gurūnahatvā hi mahānubhāvān śreyo bhoktuṃ bhaikṣyamapīha loke |
hatvārthakāmāṃstu gurūnihaiva bhuñjīya bhogān rudhirapradigdhān

**Meaning:** Better to beg for food in this world than kill these great teachers. Killing them, I would enjoy only blood-stained pleasures.

**Takeaway:** Count what victory eats, not just what it feeds.

**Provenance:** sa:2:5, telang:T05, arnold:B04-shared

## 2:6

**Sanskrit:** न चैतद्विद्मः कतरन्नो गरीयो यद्वा जयेम यदि वा नो जयेयुः ।
यानेव हत्वा न जिजीविषाम- स्तेऽवस्थिताः प्रमुखे धार्तराष्ट्राः

**IAST:** na caitadvidmaḥ kataranno garīyo yadvā jayema yadi vā no jayeyuḥ |
yāneva hatvā na jijīviṣāma- ste'vasthitāḥ pramukhe dhārtarāṣṭrāḥ

**Meaning:** We do not even know which is better for us — victory or defeat. The sons of Dhritarashtra stand before us, and we would not want to live after killing them.

**Takeaway:** Uncertainty is honest; pretending otherwise is not.

**Provenance:** sa:2:6, telang:T05, arnold:B04-shared

**Telang [T06]:** "but if killing them" "stand" "against us"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.82), Arnold does not (0.36)

## 2:7

**Sanskrit:** कार्पण्यदोषोपहतस्वभावः पृच्छामि त्वां धर्मसम्मूढचेताः ।
यच्छ्रेयः स्यान्निश्चितं ब्रूहि तन्मे शिष्यस्तेऽहं शाधि मां त्वां प्रपन्नम्

**IAST:** kārpaṇyadoṣopahatasvabhāvaḥ pṛcchāmi tvāṃ dharmasammūḍhacetāḥ |
yacchreyaḥ syānniścitaṃ brūhi tanme śiṣyaste'haṃ śādhi māṃ tvāṃ prapannam

**Meaning:** My heart is poisoned by pity, and my mind is confused about duty. Tell me plainly what is good. I am your student now — teach me.

**Takeaway:** Asking for help is a decision, not a defeat.

**Provenance:** sa:2:7, telang:T05, arnold:B04-shared

**Telang [T06]:** "contaminated" "disciple"

## 2:8

**Sanskrit:** न हि प्रपश्यामि ममापनुद्याद् यच्छोकमुच्छोषणमिन्द्रियाणाम् ।
अवाप्य भूमावसपत्नमृद्धं राज्यं सुराणामपि चाधिपत्यम्

**IAST:** na hi prapaśyāmi mamāpanudyād yacchokamucchoṣaṇamindriyāṇām |
avāpya bhūmāvasapatnamṛddhaṃ rājyaṃ surāṇāmapi cādhipatyam

**Meaning:** I see nothing that could drive away this grief. It dries up my senses. Not rule over a rich, unrivaled earth. Not even lordship of the gods.

**Takeaway:** Grief does not negotiate.

**Provenance:** sa:2:8, telang:T05, arnold:B04-shared

**Telang [T06]:** "do not perceive" "sovereignty of the gods"

**Consensus [ORPHAN]:** neither source covers (telang 0.36, arnold 0.43) — check: away, drie, lordship, nothing, over, unrival

## 2:9 (सञ्जय)

**Sanskrit:** एवमुक्त्वा हृषीकेशं गुडाकेशः परन्तप ।
न योत्स्य इति गोविन्दमुक्त्वा तूष्णीं बभूव ह

**IAST:** evamuktvā hṛṣīkeśaṃ guḍākeśaḥ parantapa |
na yotsya iti govindamuktvā tūṣṇīṃ babhūva ha

**Meaning:** Sanjaya said: having spoken thus to Krishna, Arjuna said, I will not fight — and fell silent.

**Takeaway:** Sometimes the sentence ends with silence.

**Provenance:** sa:2:9, telang:T06+T07-head, arnold:B05-shared
