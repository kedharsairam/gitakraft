# Pack ch10-4 — Chapter 10: Everywhere the Divine

Traditional: The Yoga of Divine Manifestations · Verses 10:31–10:40 · Status: **pending**

## 10:31

**Sanskrit:** पवनः पवतामस्मि रामः शस्त्रभृतामहम् ।
झषाणां मकरश्चास्मि स्रोतसामस्मि जाह्नवी

**IAST:** pavanaḥ pavatāmasmi rāmaḥ śastrabhṛtāmaham |
jhaṣāṇāṃ makaraścāsmi srotasāmasmi jāhnavī

**Meaning:** Among purifiers, the wind. Among warriors, Rama. Among fishes, the sea-beast. Among rivers, the Ganges.

**Takeaway:** The familiar made glorious.

**Provenance:** sa:10:31, telang:T13, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "those that blow" "streams"

## 10:32

**Sanskrit:** सर्गाणामादिरन्तश्च मध्यं चैवाहमर्जुन ।
अध्यात्मविद्या विद्यानां वादः प्रवदतामहम्

**IAST:** sargāṇāmādirantaśca madhyaṃ caivāhamarjuna |
adhyātmavidyā vidyānāṃ vādaḥ pravadatāmaham

**Meaning:** Of all that begins, I am beginning, middle, and end. Among learnings, self-knowledge. Among debaters, fair argument.

**Takeaway:** The frame repeated — lest the list distract.

**Provenance:** sa:10:32, telang:T13, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "created things" "controversialists"

## 10:33

**Sanskrit:** अक्षराणामकारोऽस्मि द्वन्द्वः सामासिकस्य च ।
अहमेवाक्षयः कालो धाताहं विश्वतोमुखः

**IAST:** akṣarāṇāmakāro'smi dvandvaḥ sāmāsikasya ca |
ahamevākṣayaḥ kālo dhātāhaṃ viśvatomukhaḥ

**Meaning:** Among letters, A. Among compounds, the pairing of equals. I am endless time, and the maker facing every way.

**Takeaway:** First letter. Fairest grammar. Endless time.

**Provenance:** sa:10:33, telang:T13, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "letter A" "all directions"

## 10:34

**Sanskrit:** मृत्युः सर्वहरश्चाहमुद्भवश्च भविष्यताम् ।
कीर्तिः श्रीर्वाक्च नारीणां स्मृतिर्मेधा धृतिः क्षमा

**IAST:** mṛtyuḥ sarvaharaścāhamudbhavaśca bhaviṣyatām |
kīrtiḥ śrīrvākca nārīṇāṃ smṛtirmedhā dhṛtiḥ kṣamā

**Meaning:** I am all-seizing death, and the birth of things to come. Among women: fame, fortune, speech, memory, wisdom, courage, patience.

**Takeaway:** Seven goddesses close the feminine roll.

**Provenance:** sa:10:34, telang:T13, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "seizes all" "females"

## 10:35

**Sanskrit:** बृहत्साम तथा साम्नां गायत्री छन्दसामहम् ।
मासानां मार्गशीर्षोऽहमृतूनां कुसुमाकरः

**IAST:** bṛhatsāma tathā sāmnāṃ gāyatrī chandasāmaham |
māsānāṃ mārgaśīrṣo'hamṛtūnāṃ kusumākaraḥ

**Meaning:** Among hymns, the great chant. Among metres, Gayatri. Among months, the harvest month. Among seasons, spring.

**Takeaway:** Poetry, calendar, and spring — claimed.

**Provenance:** sa:10:35, telang:T13+T16-head, arnold:D03-shared

**Notes:** Split across T13-tail/T16-head.

**Telang [T13]:** "metre.s" "months"
**Telang [T16]:** "spring among the seasons"

## 10:36

**Sanskrit:** द्यूतं छलयतामस्मि तेजस्तेजस्विनामहम् ।
जयोऽस्मि व्यवसायोऽस्मि सत्त्वं सत्त्ववतामहम्

**IAST:** dyūtaṃ chalayatāmasmi tejastejasvināmaham |
jayo'smi vyavasāyo'smi sattvaṃ sattvavatāmaham

**Meaning:** Among cheats, the dice-game. Among the splendid, splendor. I am victory, resolve, and the goodness of the good.

**Takeaway:** Even the dice table has its Lord.

**Provenance:** sa:10:36, telang:T16, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T16]:** "dice" "victory"

## 10:37

**Sanskrit:** वृष्णीनां वासुदेवोऽस्मि पाण्डवानां धनंजयः ।
मुनीनामप्यहं व्यासः कवीनामुशना कविः

**IAST:** vṛṣṇīnāṃ vāsudevo'smi pāṇḍavānāṃ dhanaṃjayaḥ |
munīnāmapyahaṃ vyāsaḥ kavīnāmuśanā kaviḥ

**Meaning:** Among clansmen, Krishna. Among archers, Arjuna. Among sages, Vyasa. Among poets, Usanas.

**Takeaway:** The speaker names Himself — and His listener.

**Provenance:** sa:10:37, telang:T16, arnold:D03-shared

**Notes:** Usanas gloss ('preceptor of the Daityas... work on politics') dropped as footnote (T18).

**Telang [T16]:** "Vnshwi" "U^anas"

## 10:38

**Sanskrit:** दण्डो दमयतामस्मि नीतिरस्मि जिगीषताम् ।
मौनं चैवास्मि गुह्यानां ज्ञानं ज्ञानवतामहम्

**IAST:** daṇḍo damayatāmasmi nītirasmi jigīṣatām |
maunaṃ caivāsmi guhyānāṃ jñānaṃ jñānavatāmaham

**Meaning:** Among punishers, the rod. Among strivers, fair policy. Among secrets, silence. Among knowers, wisdom.

**Takeaway:** Power, politics, secrecy, knowledge — each crowned.

**Provenance:** sa:10:38, telang:T16+T17-head, arnold:D03-shared

**Notes:** Split across T16-tail/T17-head; niti gloss ('Making peace, bribing') dropped as footnote (T19).

**Telang [T16]:** "rod" "silence"
**Telang [T17]:** "knowledge of those"

## 10:39

**Sanskrit:** यच्चापि सर्वभूतानां बीजं तदहमर्जुन ।
न तदस्ति विना यत्स्यान्मया भूतं चराचरम्

**IAST:** yaccāpi sarvabhūtānāṃ bījaṃ tadahamarjuna |
na tadasti vinā yatsyānmayā bhūtaṃ carācaram

**Meaning:** Whatever seeds all beings — that am I. Nothing moving or still can exist without Me.

**Takeaway:** The seed-clause. The metaphysics in one line.

**Provenance:** sa:10:39, telang:T17, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T17]:** "seed" "without me"

## 10:40

**Sanskrit:** नान्तोऽस्ति मम दिव्यानां विभूतीनां परन्तप ।
एष तूद्देशतः प्रोक्तो विभूतेर्विस्तरो मया

**IAST:** nānto'sti mama divyānāṃ vibhūtīnāṃ parantapa |
eṣa tūddeśataḥ prokto vibhūtervistaro mayā

**Meaning:** My glories have no end. What I have told is a fraction only.

**Takeaway:** The catalogue closes with humility — His own.

**Provenance:** sa:10:40, telang:T17, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T17]:** "no end" "in part"
