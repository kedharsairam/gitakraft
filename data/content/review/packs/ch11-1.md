# Pack ch11-1 — Chapter 11: The Universe in One

Traditional: The Vision of the Cosmic Form · Verses 11:1–11:10 · Status: **pending**

## 11:1 (अर्जुन)

**Sanskrit:** मदनुग्रहाय परमं गुह्यमध्यात्मसंज्ञितम् ।
यत्त्वयोक्तं वचस्तेन मोहोऽयं विगतो मम

**IAST:** madanugrahāya paramaṃ guhyamadhyātmasaṃjñitam |
yattvayoktaṃ vacastena moho'yaṃ vigato mama

**Meaning:** Arjuna said: the supreme mystery You spoke for my welfare has cleared my confusion.

**Takeaway:** Gratitude first. The vision request comes next.

**Provenance:** sa:11:1, telang:T00+T01, arnold:D01-shared

**Notes:** T01 (bullet-led) is verse text 11:1-4 misclassified as footnote; flag corrected in triptych.

**Telang [T00]:** "Ar^na said ;"
**Telang [T01]:** "gone away"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.83), Arnold does not (0.33)

## 11:2

**Sanskrit:** भवाप्ययौ हि भूतानां श्रुतौ विस्तरशो मया ।
त्वत्तः कमलपत्राक्ष माहात्म्यमपि चाव्ययम्

**IAST:** bhavāpyayau hi bhūtānāṃ śrutau vistaraśo mayā |
tvattaḥ kamalapatrākṣa māhātmyamapi cāvyayam

**Meaning:** I have heard at length of the birth and passing of beings. And of Your imperishable glory, lotus-eyed one.

**Takeaway:** He listened. Now he asks.

**Provenance:** sa:11:2, telang:T01, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T01]:** "production and dissolution" "greatness"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Arnold covers (0.78), Telang does not (0.44)

## 11:3

**Sanskrit:** एवमेतद्यथात्थ त्वमात्मानं परमेश्वर ।
द्रष्टुमिच्छामि ते रूपमैश्वरं पुरुषोत्तम

**IAST:** evametadyathāttha tvamātmānaṃ parameśvara |
draṣṭumicchāmi te rūpamaiśvaraṃ puruṣottama

**Meaning:** What You say of Yourself is so. Now I long to see Your divine form, highest Lord.

**Takeaway:** Belief first, vision second. In that order.

**Provenance:** sa:11:3, telang:T01, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T01]:** "said about yourself" "divine  form"

## 11:4

**Sanskrit:** मन्यसे यदि तच्छक्यं मया द्रष्टुमिति प्रभो ।
योगेश्वर ततो मे त्वं दर्शयात्मानमव्ययम्

**IAST:** manyase yadi tacchakyaṃ mayā draṣṭumiti prabho |
yogeśvara tato me tvaṃ darśayātmānamavyayam

**Meaning:** If I can bear the sight, Lord of discipline, show me Your imperishable Self.

**Takeaway:** The boldest request in the whole poem.

**Provenance:** sa:11:4, telang:T01, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T01]:** "possible for  me" "show your inexhaustible"

## 11:5

**Sanskrit:** श्रीभगवानुवाच
पश्य मे पार्थ रूपाणि शतशोऽथ सहस्रशः ।
नानाविधानि दिव्यानि नानावर्णाकृतीनि च

**IAST:** śrībhagavānuvāca
paśya me pārtha rūpāṇi śataśo'tha sahasraśaḥ |
nānāvidhāni divyāni nānāvarṇākṛtīni ca

**Meaning:** Krishna said: see My forms by the hundred and thousand. Countless, divine, multicolored.

**Takeaway:** The curtain speech before the curtain parts.

**Provenance:** sa:11:5, telang:T02+T03-head, arnold:D02-shared

**Telang [T02]:** "The Deity said ;"
**Telang [T03]:** "thousands see my forms" "colours"

## 11:6

**Sanskrit:** पश्यादित्यान्वसून्रुद्रानश्विनौ मरुतस्तथा ।
बहून्यदृष्टपूर्वाणि पश्याश्चर्याणि भारत

**IAST:** paśyādityānvasūnrudrānaśvinau marutastathā |
bahūnyadṛṣṭapūrvāṇi paśyāścaryāṇi bhārata

**Meaning:** See sun-gods, wind-gods, storm-gods, the twin horsemen. Wonders never seen before.

**Takeaway:** The guest list of the vision.

**Provenance:** sa:11:6, telang:T03, arnold:D02-shared

**Telang [T03]:** "Alvins" "unseen  before"

## 11:7

**Sanskrit:** इहैकस्थं जगत्कृत्स्नं पश्याद्य सचराचरम् ।
मम देहे गुडाकेश यच्चान्यद् द्रष्टुमिच्छसि

**IAST:** ihaikasthaṃ jagatkṛtsnaṃ paśyādya sacarācaram |
mama dehe guḍākeśa yaccānyad draṣṭumicchasi

**Meaning:** See the whole universe, moving and still, gathered in one within My body. And anything else you wish.

**Takeaway:** Everything, in one place, at once.

**Provenance:** sa:11:7, telang:T03, arnold:D02-shared

**Telang [T03]:** "Within my body" "whatever else"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.80), Arnold does not (0.40)

## 11:8

**Sanskrit:** न तु मां शक्यसे द्रष्टुमनेनैव स्वचक्षुषा ।
दिव्यं ददामि ते चक्षुः पश्य मे योगमैश्वरम्

**IAST:** na tu māṃ śakyase draṣṭumanenaiva svacakṣuṣā |
divyaṃ dadāmi te cakṣuḥ paśya me yogamaiśvaram

**Meaning:** But human eyes cannot see Me. I grant you a divine eye. Behold My majestic power.

**Takeaway:** New sight required for a new sight.

**Provenance:** sa:11:8, telang:T03, arnold:D03

**Telang [T03]:** "merely this eye" "divine power"

## 11:9 (सञ्जय)

**Sanskrit:** एवमुक्त्वा ततो राजन्महायोगेश्वरो हरिः ।
दर्शयामास पार्थाय परमं रूपमैश्वरम्

**Takeaway:** The narrator takes over. Eyes open.

## 11:10

**Sanskrit:** अनेकवक्त्रनयनमनेकाद्भुतदर्शनम् ।
अनेकदिव्याभरणं दिव्यानेकोद्यतायुधम्

**IAST:** anekavaktranayanamanekādbhutadarśanam |
anekadivyābharaṇaṃ divyānekodyatāyudham

**Meaning:** Countless mouths and eyes. Wondrous sights within. Celestial ornaments, weapons raised high.

**Takeaway:** The inventory of infinity, part one.

**Provenance:** sa:11:10, telang:T06, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T06]:** "mouths and eyes" "held erect"
