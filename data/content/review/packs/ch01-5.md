## 1:41

**Sanskrit:** अधर्माभिभवात्कृष्ण प्रदुष्यन्ति कुलस्त्रियः ।
स्त्रीषु दुष्टासु वार्ष्णेय जायते वर्णसंकरः

**IAST:** adharmābhibhavātkṛṣṇa praduṣyanti kulastriyaḥ |
strīṣu duṣṭāsu vārṣṇeya jāyate varṇasaṃkaraḥ

**Meaning:** When lawlessness wins, Krishna, the women of the house go astray, and from that comes the mixing of duties.

**Takeaway:** He traces one crack as it spreads through the whole wall.

**Provenance:** sa:1:41, telang:T14, arnold:A07-shared

**Telang [T14]:** "intermingling" "to hell"

**Consensus [ORPHAN]:** neither source covers (telang 0.33, arnold 0.44) — check: astray, go, lawlessness, win

## 1:42

**Sanskrit:** संकरो नरकायैव कुलघ्नानां कुलस्य च ।
पतन्ति पितरो ह्येषां लुप्तपिण्डोदकक्रियाः

**IAST:** saṃkaro narakāyaiva kulaghnānāṃ kulasya ca |
patanti pitaro hyeṣāṃ luptapiṇḍodakakriyāḥ

**Meaning:** That mixing drags the family and its destroyers down to hell. The forefathers fall, their offerings of food and water cut off.

**Takeaway:** Actions outlive their moment.

**Provenance:** sa:1:42, telang:T14, arnold:A07-shared

**Telang [T14]:** "ceremonies" "are"
**Telang [T15]:** "subverted"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.58), Arnold does not (0.33)

## 1:43

**Sanskrit:** दोषैरेतैः कुलघ्नानां वर्णसंकरकारकैः ।
उत्साद्यन्ते जातिधर्माः कुलधर्माश्च शाश्वताः

**IAST:** doṣairetaiḥ kulaghnānāṃ varṇasaṃkarakārakaiḥ |
utsādyante jātidharmāḥ kuladharmāśca śāśvatāḥ

**Meaning:** The destroyers' misdeeds wipe out the timeless duties of ranks and families.

**Takeaway:** Small breaks compound.

**Provenance:** sa:1:43, telang:T14+T15-head, arnold:A07-shared

**Notes:** Telang renders the 43/44 transition in one flow; span shared.

## 1:44

**Sanskrit:** उत्सन्नकुलधर्माणां मनुष्याणां जनार्दन ।
नरकेऽनियतं वासो भवतीत्यनुशुश्रुम

**IAST:** utsannakuladharmāṇāṃ manuṣyāṇāṃ janārdana |
narake'niyataṃ vāso bhavatītyanuśuśruma

**Meaning:** And we have heard, Janardana, that men without family duties must live in hell.

**Takeaway:** Tradition is cited as witness, not proof — note the honesty in 'we have heard.'

**Provenance:** sa:1:44, telang:T15, arnold:A07-shared

## 1:45

**Sanskrit:** अहो बत महत्पापं कर्तुं व्यवसिता वयम् ।
यद्राज्यसुखलोभेन हन्तुं स्वजनमुद्यताः

**IAST:** aho bata mahatpāpaṃ kartuṃ vyavasitā vayam |
yadrājyasukhalobhena hantuṃ svajanamudyatāḥ

**Meaning:** Alas! We are set to commit a terrible sin, ready to kill our own kin out of greed for a kingdom's pleasures.

**Takeaway:** Naming it plainly is the first honest act.

**Provenance:** sa:1:45, telang:T15, arnold:A07-shared

**Telang [T15]:** "Alas" "greed of"

## 1:46

**Sanskrit:** यदि मामप्रतीकारमशस्त्रं शस्त्रपाणयः ।
धार्तराष्ट्रा रणे हन्युस्तन्मे क्षेमतरं भवेत्

**IAST:** yadi māmapratīkāramaśastraṃ śastrapāṇayaḥ |
dhārtarāṣṭrā raṇe hanyustanme kṣemataraṃ bhavet

**Meaning:** If Dhritarashtra's sons killed me in battle — weaponless, refusing to fight back — that would be better for me.

**Takeaway:** He would rather die clean than win dirty.

**Provenance:** sa:1:46, telang:T15, arnold:A07-shared

**Telang [T15]:** "If the sons of DhrAarash/ra" "better for me"

## 1:47 (सञ्जय)

**Sanskrit:** एवमुक्त्वार्जुनः संख्ये रथोपस्थ उपाविशत् ।
विसृज्य सशरं चापं शोकसंविग्नमानसः

**IAST:** evamuktvārjunaḥ saṃkhye rathopastha upāviśat |
visṛjya saśaraṃ cāpaṃ śokasaṃvignamānasaḥ

**Meaning:** Sanjaya said: having spoken thus, Arjuna dropped his bow and arrows. He sank into his chariot, his mind drowned in grief.

**Takeaway:** Sometimes the bravest move is putting the weapon down.

**Provenance:** sa:1:47, telang:T16+T17, arnold:A08

**Telang [T16]:** "Saw^aya said:"
**Telang [T17]:** "cast aside his bow" "agitated by  grief"
