# Pack ch13-2 — Chapter 13: The Field and the Witness

Traditional: Field and Knower of the Field · Verses 13:11–13:20 · Status: **pending**

## 13:11

**Sanskrit:** अध्यात्मज्ञाननित्यत्वं तत्त्वज्ञानार्थदर्शनम् ।
एतज्ज्ञानमिति प्रोक्तमज्ञानं यदतोऽन्यथा

**IAST:** adhyātmajñānanityatvaṃ tattvajñānārthadarśanam |
etajjñānamiti proktamajñānaṃ yadato'nyathā

**Meaning:** Constancy in truth-seeking. Seeing what wisdom aims at. This is knowledge. Its opposite is ignorance.

**Takeaway:** The definition closes the list.

**Provenance:** sa:13:11, telang:T03, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T03]:** "clean places" "is ignorance"

## 13:12

**Sanskrit:** ज्ञेयं यत्तत्प्रवक्ष्यामि यज्ज्ञात्वामृतमश्नुते ।
अनादि मत्परं ब्रह्म न सत्तन्नासदुच्यते

**IAST:** jñeyaṃ yattatpravakṣyāmi yajjñātvāmṛtamaśnute |
anādi matparaṃ brahma na sattannāsaducyate

**Meaning:** Now hear the goal of knowing. The supreme ultimate. Beginningless. Neither being nor non-being.

**Takeaway:** The address of everything worth finding.

**Provenance:** sa:13:12, telang:T03, arnold:D06-shared

**Telang [T03]:** "reaches immortality" "non-existent"

## 13:13

**Sanskrit:** सर्वतः पाणिपादं तत्सर्वतोऽक्षिशिरोमुखम् ।
सर्वतः श्रुतिमल्लोके सर्वमावृत्य तिष्ठति

**IAST:** sarvataḥ pāṇipādaṃ tatsarvato'kṣiśiromukham |
sarvataḥ śrutimalloke sarvamāvṛtya tiṣṭhati

**Meaning:** Hands and feet everywhere. Eyes, heads, faces everywhere. Ears everywhere. Enfolding all.

**Takeaway:** Omnipresence, anatomized.

**Provenance:** sa:13:13, telang:T03+T04-head, arnold:D06-shared

**Notes:** Split across T03-tail/T04-head.

**Telang [T03]:** "hands and feet" "ears on all sides"
**Telang [T04]:** "pervading everything"

## 13:14

**Sanskrit:** सर्वेन्द्रियगुणाभासं सर्वेन्द्रियविवर्जितम् ।
असक्तं सर्वभृच्चैव निर्गुणं गुणभोक्तृ च

**IAST:** sarvendriyaguṇābhāsaṃ sarvendriyavivarjitam |
asaktaṃ sarvabhṛccaiva nirguṇaṃ guṇabhoktṛ ca

**Meaning:** Enjoying every sense, yet senseless. Detached, yet upholding all. Quality-free, yet tasting qualities.

**Takeaway:** Every paradox holds hands here.

**Provenance:** sa:13:14, telang:T04, arnold:D06-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "devoid" "enjoyer"

## 13:15

**Sanskrit:** बहिरन्तश्च भूतानामचरं चरमेव च ।
सूक्ष्मत्वात्तदविज्ञेयं दूरस्थं चान्तिके च तत्

**IAST:** bahirantaśca bhūtānāmacaraṃ carameva ca |
sūkṣmatvāttadavijñeyaṃ dūrasthaṃ cāntike ca tat

**Meaning:** Inside all, outside all. Moving and unmoving. Too subtle to grasp. Farther than far, nearer than near.

**Takeaway:** The geography of the infinite.

**Provenance:** sa:13:15, telang:T04, arnold:D06-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "without them" "afar and near"

## 13:16

**Sanskrit:** अविभक्तं च भूतेषु विभक्तमिव च स्थितम् ।
भूतभर्तृ च तज्ज्ञेयं ग्रसिष्णु प्रभविष्णु च

**IAST:** avibhaktaṃ ca bhūteṣu vibhaktamiva ca sthitam |
bhūtabhartṛ ca tajjñeyaṃ grasiṣṇu prabhaviṣṇu ca

**Meaning:** Undivided, yet seemingly divided among beings. To be known as sustainer, devourer, creator.

**Takeaway:** One actor, three roles, countless stages.

**Provenance:** sa:13:16, telang:T04, arnold:D06-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "though different" "absorbs"

## 13:17

**Sanskrit:** ज्योतिषामपि तज्ज्योतिस्तमसः परमुच्यते ।
ज्ञानं ज्ञेयं ज्ञानगम्यं हृदि सर्वस्य विष्ठितम्

**IAST:** jyotiṣāmapi tajjyotistamasaḥ paramucyate |
jñānaṃ jñeyaṃ jñānagamyaṃ hṛdi sarvasya viṣṭhitam

**Meaning:** Light of all lights, beyond darkness. Wisdom, its goal, its path — planted in every heart.

**Takeaway:** The lamp no darkness touches.

**Provenance:** sa:13:17, telang:T04, arnold:D06-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "beyond darkness" "heart of all"

## 13:18

**Sanskrit:** इति क्षेत्रं तथा ज्ञानं ज्ञेयं चोक्तं समासतः ।
मद्भक्त एतद्विज्ञाय मद्भावायोपपद्यते

**IAST:** iti kṣetraṃ tathā jñānaṃ jñeyaṃ coktaṃ samāsataḥ |
madbhakta etadvijñāya madbhāvāyopapadyate

**Meaning:** Field, wisdom, and goal — thus declared in brief. My devotee who grasps this grows fit for oneness with Me.

**Takeaway:** Summary plus fitness certificate.

**Provenance:** sa:13:18, telang:T04, arnold:D07

**Telang [T04]:** "in brief have" "assimilation"

## 13:19

**Sanskrit:** प्रकृतिं पुरुषं चैव विद्ध्यनादी उभावपि ।
विकारांश्च गुणांश्चैव विद्धि प्रकृतिसंभवान्

**IAST:** prakṛtiṃ puruṣaṃ caiva viddhyanādī ubhāvapi |
vikārāṃśca guṇāṃścaiva viddhi prakṛtisaṃbhavān

**Meaning:** Know this: both nature and spirit are beginningless. And every change and mood is born of nature.

**Takeaway:** Two eternals. One source of change.

**Provenance:** sa:13:19, telang:T04, arnold:D08-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "without beginning" "produced from nature"

## 13:20

**Sanskrit:** कार्यकरणकर्तृत्वे हेतुः प्रकृतिरुच्यते ।
पुरुषः सुखदुःखानां भोक्तृत्वे हेतुरुच्यते

**IAST:** kāryakaraṇakartṛtve hetuḥ prakṛtirucyate |
puruṣaḥ sukhaduḥkhānāṃ bhoktṛtve heturucyate

**Meaning:** Nature drives the body and senses to act. Spirit experiences pleasure and pain.

**Takeaway:** Doer and feeler, distinguished.

**Provenance:** sa:13:20, telang:T04+T05-head, arnold:D08-shared

**Notes:** Split across T04-tail/T05-head.

**Telang [T04]:** "capacity of working" "enjoying pleasures and"
**Telang [T05]:** "pains"
