# Pack ch16-3 — Chapter 16: Two Natures

Traditional: Divine and Demonic Natures · Verses 16:21–16:24 · Status: **pending**

## 16:21

**Sanskrit:** त्रिविधं नरकस्येदं द्वारं नाशनमात्मनः ।
कामः क्रोधस्तथा लोभस्तस्मादेतत्त्रयं त्यजेत्

**IAST:** trividhaṃ narakasyedaṃ dvāraṃ nāśanamātmanaḥ |
kāmaḥ krodhastathā lobhastasmādetattrayaṃ tyajet

**Meaning:** lust, anger, and greed. Abandon this triple gate.

**Takeaway:** Name them. Shun them.

**Provenance:** sa:16:21, telang:T08, arnold:D07-shared

**Telang [T08]:** "lust, anger" "abandon this triad"

## 16:22

**Sanskrit:** एतैर्विमुक्तः कौन्तेय तमोद्वारैस्त्रिभिर्नरः ।
आचरत्यात्मनः श्रेयस्ततो याति परां गतिम्

**IAST:** etairvimuktaḥ kaunteya tamodvāraistribhirnaraḥ |
ācaratyātmanaḥ śreyastato yāti parāṃ gatim

**Meaning:** Freed from these three doors of darkness, one works out one's own rescue — and climbs to the highest goal.

**Takeaway:** The exit posted clearly.

**Provenance:** sa:16:22, telang:T08, arnold:D07-shared

**Telang [T08]:** "own salvation" "highest goal"

## 16:23

**Sanskrit:** यः शास्त्रविधिमुत्सृज्य वर्तते कामकारतः ।
न स सिद्धिमवाप्नोति न सुखं न परां गतिम्

**IAST:** yaḥ śāstravidhimutsṛjya vartate kāmakārataḥ |
na sa siddhimavāpnoti na sukhaṃ na parāṃ gatim

**Meaning:** Who discards scripture and follows craving reaches nothing. Neither perfection, nor joy, nor the goal.

**Takeaway:** Unguided appetite arrives nowhere.

**Provenance:** sa:16:23, telang:T08, arnold:none-omitted

**Notes:** Arnold omits 16:23-24 (G08 ellipsis divider); Telang + Sanskrit only.

**Telang [T08]:** "impulse of desire" "perfection"

## 16:24

**Sanskrit:** तस्माच्छास्त्रं प्रमाणं ते कार्याकार्यव्यवस्थितौ ।
ज्ञात्वा शास्त्रविधानोक्तं कर्म कर्तुमिहार्हसि

**IAST:** tasmācchāstraṃ pramāṇaṃ te kāryākāryavyavasthitau |
jñātvā śāstravidhānoktaṃ karma kartumihārhasi

**Meaning:** So let scripture judge what to do and what to shun. Know its rule — then act in this world.

**Takeaway:** The closing gavel: the rulebook stands.

**Provenance:** sa:16:24, telang:T08, arnold:none-omitted

**Notes:** Arnold omits 16:23-24 (G08 ellipsis divider); Telang + Sanskrit only.

**Telang [T08]:** "authority" "perform action"
