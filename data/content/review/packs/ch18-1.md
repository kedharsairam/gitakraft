# Pack ch18-1 — Chapter 18: Freedom and Surrender

Traditional: Liberation and Renunciation · Verses 18:1–18:10 · Status: **pending**

## 18:1 (अर्जुन)

**Sanskrit:** संन्यासस्य महाबाहो तत्त्वमिच्छामि वेदितुम् ।
त्यागस्य च हृषीकेश पृथक्केशिनिषूदन

**IAST:** saṃnyāsasya mahābāho tattvamicchāmi veditum |
tyāgasya ca hṛṣīkeśa pṛthakkeśiniṣūdana

**Meaning:** Arjuna said: mighty-armed one, I want the truth about giving up acts and giving up their fruits — distinctly.

**Takeaway:** The final chapter opens with the final question.

**Provenance:** sa:18:1, telang:T01+T02, arnold:D01

**Telang [T01]:** "Ar^ina-said :"
**Telang [T02]:** "destroyer" "distinctly"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.70), Arnold does not (0.10)

## 18:2

**Sanskrit:** श्रीभगवानुवाच
काम्यानां कर्मणां न्यासं संन्यासं कवयो विदुः ।
सर्वकर्मफलत्यागं प्राहुस्त्यागं विचक्षणाः

**IAST:** śrībhagavānuvāca
kāmyānāṃ karmaṇāṃ nyāsaṃ saṃnyāsaṃ kavayo viduḥ |
sarvakarmaphalatyāgaṃ prāhustyāgaṃ vicakṣaṇāḥ

**Meaning:** Krishna said: dropping desire-driven acts, the poets call renunciation. Dropping every fruit, the wise call surrender.

**Takeaway:** Two givings-up, distinguished at last.

**Provenance:** sa:18:2, telang:T03+T04-head, arnold:D02

**Telang [T03]:** "The Deity said ;"
**Telang [T04]:** "rejection" "fruit of all actions"

## 18:3

**Sanskrit:** त्याज्यं दोषवदित्येके कर्म प्राहुर्मनीषिणः ।
यज्ञदानतपःकर्म न त्याज्यमिति चापरे

**IAST:** tyājyaṃ doṣavadityeke karma prāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ |
yajñadānatapaḥkarma na tyājyamiti cāpare

**Meaning:** Some sages say all action is tainted — abandon it. Others say ritual, charity, and discipline must stay.

**Takeaway:** The debate, fairly stated both ways.

**Provenance:** sa:18:3, telang:T04+T05-head, arnold:D03

**Notes:** Split across T04-tail/T05-head.

**Telang [T04]:** "full of evil" "penance"
**Telang [T05]:** "should not be abandoned"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.80), Arnold does not (0.40)

## 18:4

**Sanskrit:** निश्चयं शृणु मे तत्र त्यागे भरतसत्तम ।
त्यागो हि पुरुषव्याघ्र त्रिविधः संप्रकीर्तितः

**IAST:** niścayaṃ śṛṇu me tatra tyāge bharatasattama |
tyāgo hi puruṣavyāghra trividhaḥ saṃprakīrtitaḥ

**Meaning:** Hear My verdict on surrender. It runs threefold, best of Bharatas.

**Takeaway:** The judge speaks. Three kinds coming.

**Provenance:** sa:18:4, telang:T05, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "listen to my  decision" "threefold"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.50), Arnold does not (0.17)

## 18:5

**Sanskrit:** यज्ञदानतपःकर्म न त्याज्यं कार्यमेव तत् ।
यज्ञो दानं तपश्चैव पावनानि मनीषिणाम्

**IAST:** yajñadānatapaḥkarma na tyājyaṃ kāryameva tat |
yajño dānaṃ tapaścaiva pāvanāni manīṣiṇām

**Meaning:** Ritual, charity, discipline — never abandon these. They must be done. They purify even the wise.

**Takeaway:** Three keepers. Non-negotiable.

**Provenance:** sa:18:5, telang:T05, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "must needs" "sanctification"

## 18:6

**Sanskrit:** एतान्यपि तु कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा फलानि च ।
कर्तव्यानीति मे पार्थ निश्चितं मतमुत्तमम्

**IAST:** etānyapi tu karmāṇi saṅgaṃ tyaktvā phalāni ca |
kartavyānīti me pārtha niścitaṃ matamuttamam

**Meaning:** Yet do even these without clinging or hunger for results. That is My settled, final word.

**Takeaway:** Signed and sealed opinion.

**Provenance:** sa:18:6, telang:T05, arnold:D05

**Telang [T05]:** "abandoning attachment" "decided opinion"

**Consensus [ORPHAN]:** neither source covers (telang 0.17, arnold 0.17) — check: final, hunger, settl, take, word

## 18:7

**Sanskrit:** नियतस्य तु संन्यासः कर्मणो नोपपद्यते ।
मोहात्तस्य परित्यागस्तामसः परिकीर्तितः

**IAST:** niyatasya tu saṃnyāsaḥ karmaṇo nopapadyate |
mohāttasya parityāgastāmasaḥ parikīrtitaḥ

**Meaning:** Refusing required duty is wrong. Quitting it from confusion is dark surrender.

**Takeaway:** Duty dodged is not duty transcended.

**Provenance:** sa:18:7, telang:T05, arnold:D06-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "prescribed action" "darkness"

**Consensus [ORPHAN]:** neither source covers (telang 0.14, arnold 0.29) — check: dark, refus, requir, surrender, wrong

## 18:8

**Sanskrit:** दुःखमित्येव यत्कर्म कायक्लेशभयात्त्यजेत् ।
स कृत्वा राजसं त्यागं नैव त्यागफलं लभेत्

**IAST:** duḥkhamityeva yatkarma kāyakleśabhayāttyajet |
sa kṛtvā rājasaṃ tyāgaṃ naiva tyāgaphalaṃ labhet

**Meaning:** Quitting hard duty from fear of hardship earns nothing. That is passionate surrender.

**Takeaway:** Comfort is a poor guru.

**Provenance:** sa:18:8, telang:T05, arnold:D06-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "bodily affliction" "passionate abandonment"

**Consensus [ORPHAN]:** neither source covers (telang 0.25, arnold 0.38) — check: earn, hard, hardship, surrender

## 18:9

**Sanskrit:** कार्यमित्येव यत्कर्म नियतं क्रियतेऽर्जुन ।
सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा फलं चैव स त्यागः सात्त्विको मतः

**IAST:** kāryamityeva yatkarma niyataṃ kriyate'rjuna |
saṅgaṃ tyaktvā phalaṃ caiva sa tyāgaḥ sāttviko mataḥ

**Meaning:** Doing required duty detached and fruit-free. Simply because it must be done. That is clear surrender.

**Takeaway:** The gold standard, stated simply.

**Provenance:** sa:18:9, telang:T05, arnold:D06-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "ought to be performed" "good"

## 18:10

**Sanskrit:** न द्वेष्ट्यकुशलं कर्म कुशले नानुषज्जते ।
त्यागी सत्त्वसमाविष्टो मेधावी छिन्नसंशयः

**IAST:** na dveṣṭyakuśalaṃ karma kuśale nānuṣajjate |
tyāgī sattvasamāviṣṭo medhāvī chinnasaṃśayaḥ

**Meaning:** The clear surrenderer neither hates hard tasks nor clings to easy ones. Doubts gone, wisdom full.

**Takeaway:** Equanimity toward the task list.

**Provenance:** sa:18:10, telang:T05, arnold:D06-shared
