# Pack ch14-1 — Chapter 14: Three Moods of Nature

Traditional: The Three Qualities · Verses 14:1–14:10 · Status: **pending**

## 14:1

**Sanskrit:** यज्ज्ञात्वा मुनयः सर्वे परां सिद्धिमितो गताः

**IAST:** yajjñātvā munayaḥ sarve parāṃ siddhimito gatāḥ

**Meaning:** Krishna said: again I declare the highest wisdom. Learning it, all sages reached perfection.

**Takeaway:** The encore of all encores.

**Provenance:** sa:14:1, telang:T00+T01+T02-head, arnold:D01-shared

**Notes:** Split across T01-tail/T02-head ('having'/'learnt which').

**Telang [T00]:** "The Deity said :"
**Telang [T01]:** "highest knowledge"
**Telang [T02]:** "reached perfection"

## 14:2

**Sanskrit:** इदं ज्ञानमुपाश्रित्य मम साधर्म्यमागताः ।
सर्गेऽपि नोपजायन्ते प्रलये न व्यथन्ति च

**IAST:** idaṃ jñānamupāśritya mama sādharmyamāgatāḥ |
sarge'pi nopajāyante pralaye na vyathanti ca

**Meaning:** Rooted in this wisdom, united with My essence. No birth at creation. No pain at dissolution.

**Takeaway:** Beyond the cosmic schedule entirely.

**Provenance:** sa:14:2, telang:T02, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T02]:** "assimilation" "not born at the creation"

## 14:3

**Sanskrit:** मम योनिर्महद्ब्रह्म तस्मिन्गर्भं दधाम्यहम् ।
संभवः सर्वभूतानां ततो भवति भारत

**IAST:** mama yonirmahadbrahma tasmingarbhaṃ dadhāmyaham |
saṃbhavaḥ sarvabhūtānāṃ tato bhavati bhārata

**Meaning:** The great expanse is My womb. I plant the seed. From this union, all beings are born.

**Takeaway:** Cosmology in three lines.

**Provenance:** sa:14:3, telang:T02, arnold:D02-shared

**Telang [T02]:** "cast the seed" "birth of all things"

## 14:4

**Sanskrit:** सर्वयोनिषु कौन्तेय मूर्तयः संभवन्ति याः ।
तासां ब्रह्म महद्योनिरहं बीजप्रदः पिता

**IAST:** sarvayoniṣu kaunteya mūrtayaḥ saṃbhavanti yāḥ |
tāsāṃ brahma mahadyonirahaṃ bījapradaḥ pitā

**Meaning:** Whatever forms emerge from any womb — the expanse is mother, and I am the seed-giving father.

**Takeaway:** One fatherhood, countless wombs.

**Provenance:** sa:14:4, telang:T02, arnold:D02-shared

**Telang [T02]:** "born from all wombs" "father"

## 14:5

**Sanskrit:** सत्त्वं रजस्तम इति गुणाः प्रकृतिसंभवाः ।
निबध्नन्ति महाबाहो देहे देहिनमव्ययम्

**IAST:** sattvaṃ rajastama iti guṇāḥ prakṛtisaṃbhavāḥ |
nibadhnanti mahābāho dehe dehinamavyayam

**Meaning:** Clarity, passion, inertia. Nature's three moods. They bind the undying self inside the body.

**Takeaway:** Three ropes. One prisoner who is free.

**Provenance:** sa:14:5, telang:T02, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T02]:** "these qualities" "inexhaustible soul"

## 14:6

**Sanskrit:** तत्र सत्त्वं निर्मलत्वात्प्रकाशकमनामयम् ।
सुखसङ्गेन बध्नाति ज्ञानसङ्गेन चानघ

**IAST:** tatra sattvaṃ nirmalatvātprakāśakamanāmayam |
sukhasaṅgena badhnāti jñānasaṅgena cānagha

**Meaning:** Clarity, pure and luminous, free of stain, binds through joy and wisdom.

**Takeaway:** The golden chain is still a chain.

**Provenance:** sa:14:6, telang:T02, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T02]:** "enlightening" "pleasure and the bond"

## 14:7

**Sanskrit:** रजो रागात्मकं विद्धि तृष्णासङ्गसमुद्भवम् ।
तन्निबध्नाति कौन्तेय कर्मसङ्गेन देहिनम्

**IAST:** rajo rāgātmakaṃ viddhi tṛṣṇāsaṅgasamudbhavam |
tannibadhnāti kaunteya karmasaṅgena dehinam

**Meaning:** Passion is thirst — born of craving and clinging. It binds the embodied through endless doing.

**Takeaway:** Thirst that drinks effort.

**Provenance:** sa:14:7, telang:T02+T04-head, arnold:D03-shared

**Notes:** Split across T02-tail/T04-head (page-108 break).

**Telang [T02]:** "enamoured" "craving and attachment"
**Telang [T04]:** "bond of action"

## 14:8

**Sanskrit:** तमस्त्वज्ञानजं विद्धि मोहनं सर्वदेहिनाम् ।
प्रमादालस्यनिद्राभिस्तन्निबध्नाति भारत

**IAST:** tamastvajñānajaṃ viddhi mohanaṃ sarvadehinām |
pramādālasyanidrābhistannibadhnāti bhārata

**Meaning:** Inertia is ignorance-born. It deludes every embodied being. It binds through neglect, laziness, and sleep.

**Takeaway:** The heaviest chain feels like a blanket.

**Provenance:** sa:14:8, telang:T04, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "deludes all" "heedlessness"

## 14:9

**Sanskrit:** सत्त्वं सुखे संजयति रजः कर्मणि भारत ।
ज्ञानमावृत्य तु तमः प्रमादे संजयत्युत

**IAST:** sattvaṃ sukhe saṃjayati rajaḥ karmaṇi bhārata |
jñānamāvṛtya tu tamaḥ pramāde saṃjayatyuta

**Meaning:** Clarity pulls toward joy. Passion toward action. Inertia veils wisdom and pulls toward neglect.

**Takeaway:** Three magnets. Know which pulls you.

**Provenance:** sa:14:9, telang:T04, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "unites" "shrouding"

## 14:10

**Sanskrit:** रजस्तमश्चाभिभूय सत्त्वं भवति भारत ।
रजः सत्त्वं तमश्चैव तमः सत्त्वं रजस्तथा

**IAST:** rajastamaścābhibhūya sattvaṃ bhavati bhārata |
rajaḥ sattvaṃ tamaścaiva tamaḥ sattvaṃ rajastathā

**Meaning:** Each mood rules when the other two recede. Clarity, then passion, then inertia — by turns.

**Takeaway:** The inner weather cycle.

**Provenance:** sa:14:10, telang:T04, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "repressed" "stands"
