# Pack ch06-2 — Chapter 6: The Disciplined Mind

Traditional: The Yoga of Self-Control · Verses 6:11–6:20 · Status: **pending**

## 6:11

**Sanskrit:** शुचौ देशे प्रतिष्ठाप्य स्थिरमासनमात्मनः ।
नात्युच्छ्रितं नातिनीचं चैलाजिनकुशोत्तरम्

**IAST:** śucau deśe pratiṣṭhāpya sthiramāsanamātmanaḥ |
nātyucchritaṃ nātinīcaṃ cailājinakuśottaram

**Meaning:** Seat firm, neither too high nor too low, layered with cloth, deerskin and sacred grass —

**Takeaway:** Set the stage before the practice.

**Provenance:** sa:6:11, telang:T04, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "Fixing his  seat" "grass"

## 6:12

**Sanskrit:** तत्रैकाग्रं मनः कृत्वा यतचित्तेन्द्रियक्रियः ।
उपविश्यासने युञ्ज्याद्योगमात्मविशुद्धये

**IAST:** tatraikāgraṃ manaḥ kṛtvā yatacittendriyakriyaḥ |
upaviśyāsane yuñjyādyogamātmaviśuddhaye

**Meaning:** Sitting there. Mind pointed at one thing. Senses and workings stilled. Practice discipline, to purify yourself.

**Takeaway:** One point. Everything else can wait.

**Provenance:** sa:6:12, telang:T04+T05-head, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "exclusively on one"
**Telang [T05]:** "point, with the workings"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.89), Arnold does not (0.44)

## 6:13

**Sanskrit:** समं कायशिरोग्रीवं धारयन्नचलं स्थिरः ।
सम्प्रेक्ष्य नासिकाग्रं स्वं दिशश्चानवलोकयन्

**IAST:** samaṃ kāyaśirogrīvaṃ dhārayannacalaṃ sthiraḥ |
samprekṣya nāsikāgraṃ svaṃ diśaścānavalokayan

**Meaning:** Body, head and neck held straight and still. Gaze resting at the tip of the nose, looking nowhere else.

**Takeaway:** Posture is the first instruction for a reason.

**Provenance:** sa:6:13, telang:T05, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "tip of  his own nose"

## 6:14

**Sanskrit:** प्रशान्तात्मा विगतभीर्ब्रह्मचारिव्रते स्थितः ।
मनः संयम्य मच्चित्तो युक्त आसीत मत्परः

**IAST:** praśāntātmā vigatabhīrbrahmacārivrate sthitaḥ |
manaḥ saṃyamya maccitto yukta āsīta matparaḥ

**Meaning:** Calm in self. Fearless. Firm in celibacy. Mind restrained and fixed on Me. Sit devoted, with Me as the final goal.

**Takeaway:** The full sitting instruction in one verse.

**Provenance:** sa:6:14, telang:T05, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "tranquil self" "final goal"

**Witness [gretil]:** SANDHI-JOINT

## 6:15

**Sanskrit:** युञ्जन्नेवं सदात्मानं योगी नियतमानसः ।
शान्तिं निर्वाणपरमां मत्संस्थामधिगच्छति

**IAST:** yuñjannevaṃ sadātmānaṃ yogī niyatamānasaḥ |
śāntiṃ nirvāṇaparamāṃ matsaṃsthāmadhigacchati

**Meaning:** Practicing like this constantly, the disciplined mind finds peace — the highest peace, union with Me.

**Takeaway:** Repetition is the whole secret.

**Provenance:** sa:6:15, telang:T05, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "constantly devoting" "assimilation"

## 6:16

**Sanskrit:** नात्यश्नतस्तु योगोऽस्ति न चैकान्तमनश्नतः ।
न चाति स्वप्नशीलस्य जाग्रतो नैव चार्जुन

**IAST:** nātyaśnatastu yogo'sti na caikāntamanaśnataḥ |
na cāti svapnaśīlasya jāgrato naiva cārjuna

**Meaning:** This path is not for one who eats too much or fasts too much — not for the oversleeper, not for the insomniac.

**Takeaway:** Extremes break the instrument.

**Provenance:** sa:6:16, telang:T05, arnold:D06-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "(ever) awake"

## 6:17

**Sanskrit:** युक्ताहारविहारस्य  युक्तचेष्टस्य कर्मसु ।
युक्तस्वप्नावबोधस्य योगो भवति दुःखहा

**IAST:** yuktāhāravihārasya  yuktaceṣṭasya karmasu |
yuktasvapnāvabodhasya yogo bhavati duḥkhahā

**Meaning:** Be moderate in food and play. Measured in work. Balanced in sleep and waking. Then discipline dissolves all suffering.

**Takeaway:** Moderation in all things, including moderation applied wisely.

**Provenance:** sa:6:17, telang:T05, arnold:D06-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "takes due food" "(in) due (time)"

## 6:18

**Sanskrit:** यदा विनियतं चित्तमात्मन्येवावतिष्ठते ।
निःस्पृहः सर्वकामेभ्यो युक्त इत्युच्यते तदा

**IAST:** yadā viniyataṃ cittamātmanyevāvatiṣṭhate |
niḥspṛhaḥ sarvakāmebhyo yukta ityucyate tadā

**Meaning:** When the restrained mind settles steady on the self alone. Free of hunger for anything. Then one stands disciplined.

**Takeaway:** Want nothing, hold everything.

**Provenance:** sa:6:18, telang:T05, arnold:D06-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "indifferent" "devoted"

**Witness [gretil]:** SANDHI-JOINT

## 6:19

**Sanskrit:** यथा दीपो निवातस्थो नेङ्गते सोपमा स्मृता ।
योगिनो यतचित्तस्य युञ्जतो योगमात्मनः

**IAST:** yathā dīpo nivātastho neṅgate sopamā smṛtā |
yogino yatacittasya yuñjato yogamātmanaḥ

**Meaning:** A lamp sheltered from wind never flickers. So is the disciplined mind of one absorbed in the self.

**Takeaway:** Find your windless room.

**Provenance:** sa:6:19, telang:T05, arnold:D07-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "flickers not" "abstraction"

## 6:20

**Sanskrit:** यत्रोपरमते चित्तं निरुद्धं योगसेवया ।
यत्र चैवात्मनात्मानं पश्यन्नात्मनि तुष्यति

**IAST:** yatroparamate cittaṃ niruddhaṃ yogasevayā |
yatra caivātmanātmānaṃ paśyannātmani tuṣyati

**Meaning:** Where the mind, checked by practice, grows still — where seeing the self by the self, one rejoices in the self —

**Takeaway:** Happiness that needs no object.

**Provenance:** sa:6:20, telang:T05+T07-head, arnold:D07-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "ceases to work"
**Telang [T07]:** "seeing the self by the self"
