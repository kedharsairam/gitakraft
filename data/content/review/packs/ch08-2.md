# Pack ch08-2 — Chapter 8: Death and Beyond

Traditional: The Yoga of the Imperishable · Verses 8:11–8:20 · Status: **pending**

## 8:11

**Sanskrit:** यदक्षरं वेदविदो वदन्ति
विशन्ति यद्यतयो वीतरागाः ।
यदिच्छन्तो ब्रह्मचर्यं चरन्ति
तत्ते पदं संग्रहेण प्रवक्ष्ये

**IAST:** yadakṣaraṃ vedavido vadanti
viśanti yadyatayo vītarāgāḥ |
yadicchanto brahmacaryaṃ caranti
tatte padaṃ saṃgraheṇa pravakṣye

**Meaning:** The wise call that goal indestructible. Free of desire, the ascetics enter it. Seekers of it live the celibate path.

**Takeaway:** Three traditions point at one door.

**Provenance:** sa:8:11, telang:T06+T08-head, arnold:D07

**Notes:** Split across T06-tail/T08-head.

**Telang [T06]:** "indestructible" "pursue the"
**Telang [T08]:** "mode of life"

**Consensus [ORPHAN]:** neither source covers (telang 0.42, arnold 0.17) — check: call, celibat, free, live, seeker, wise

## 8:12

**Sanskrit:** सर्वद्वाराणि संयम्य मनो हृदि निरुध्य च ।
मूर्ध्न्याधायात्मनः प्राणमास्थितो योगधारणाम्

**IAST:** sarvadvārāṇi saṃyamya mano hṛdi nirudhya ca |
mūrdhnyādhāyātmanaḥ prāṇamāsthito yogadhāraṇām

**Meaning:** Close every gate of sense. Lock the mind in the heart. Hold the breath steady. Rest in unbroken meditation.

**Takeaway:** The full closing sequence.

**Provenance:** sa:8:12, telang:T08, arnold:D08-shared

**Telang [T08]:** "passages" "uninterrupted"

## 8:13

**Sanskrit:** ओमित्येकाक्षरं ब्रह्म व्याहरन्मामनुस्मरन् ।
यः प्रयाति त्यजन्देहं स याति परमां गतिम्

**IAST:** omityekākṣaraṃ brahma vyāharanmāmanusmaran |
yaḥ prayāti tyajandehaṃ sa yāti paramāṃ gatim

**Meaning:** Chant the single syllable Om — the eternal ultimate — meditating on Me. Such a one reaches the highest goal.

**Takeaway:** One syllable. One thought. One destination.

**Provenance:** sa:8:13, telang:T08, arnold:D08-shared

**Telang [T08]:** "syllable" "highest goal"

## 8:14

**Sanskrit:** अनन्यचेताः सततं यो मां स्मरति नित्यशः ।
तस्याहं सुलभः पार्थ नित्ययुक्तस्य योगिनः

**IAST:** ananyacetāḥ satataṃ yo māṃ smarati nityaśaḥ |
tasyāhaṃ sulabhaḥ pārtha nityayuktasya yoginaḥ

**Meaning:** To one always yoked, meditating on Me. Mind undivided. I am easy to reach.

## 8:15

**Sanskrit:** मामुपेत्य पुनर्जन्म दुःखालयमशाश्वतम् ।
नाप्नुवन्ति महात्मानः संसिद्धिं परमां गताः

**IAST:** māmupetya punarjanma duḥkhālayamaśāśvatam |
nāpnuvanti mahātmānaḥ saṃsiddhiṃ paramāṃ gatāḥ

**Meaning:** Great souls who reach Me never return to this passing life — this house of sorrow.

**Takeaway:** Graduation means no repeat term.

**Provenance:** sa:8:15, telang:T08, arnold:D09-shared

**Telang [T08]:** "highest perfection" "home of woes"

## 8:16

**Sanskrit:** आब्रह्मभुवनाल्लोकाः पुनरावर्तिनोऽर्जुन ।
मामुपेत्य तु कौन्तेय पुनर्जन्म न विद्यते

**IAST:** ābrahmabhuvanāllokāḥ punarāvartino'rjuna |
māmupetya tu kaunteya punarjanma na vidyate

**Meaning:** All worlds cycle back — even the highest heaven. But reaching Me, there is no rebirth.

**Takeaway:** Every address except one is temporary.

**Provenance:** sa:8:16, telang:T08, arnold:D10-shared

**Telang [T08]:** "world of- Brahman" "birth again"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.57), Arnold does not (0.43)

## 8:17

**Sanskrit:** सहस्रयुगपर्यन्तमहर्यद्ब्रह्मणो विदुः ।
रात्रिं युगसहस्रान्तां तेऽहोरात्रविदो जनाः

**IAST:** sahasrayugaparyantamaharyadbrahmaṇo viduḥ |
rātriṃ yugasahasrāntāṃ te'horātravido janāḥ

**Meaning:** Those who know a day of Brahma lasts a thousand ages — and the night as long — truly know day and night.

**Takeaway:** Cosmic time, stated as fact.

**Provenance:** sa:8:17, telang:T08+T09-head, arnold:D10-shared

**Notes:** Split across T08-tail/T09-head.

**Telang [T08]:** "thousand ages" "are the persons"
**Telang [T09]:** "day and night"

## 8:18

**Sanskrit:** अव्यक्ताद्व्यक्तयः सर्वाः प्रभवन्त्यहरागमे ।
रात्र्यागमे प्रलीयन्ते तत्रैवाव्यक्तसंज्ञके

**IAST:** avyaktādvyaktayaḥ sarvāḥ prabhavantyaharāgame |
rātryāgame pralīyante tatraivāvyaktasaṃjñake

**Meaning:** At daybreak, all visible things emerge from the unseen. At nightfall, they dissolve back into it.

**Takeaway:** The universe breathes in and out.

**Provenance:** sa:8:18, telang:T09, arnold:D10-shared

**Telang [T09]:** "advent of" "dissolve in"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Arnold covers (0.50), Telang does not (0.38)

## 8:19

**Sanskrit:** भूतग्रामः स एवायं भूत्वा भूत्वा प्रलीयते ।
रात्र्यागमेऽवशः पार्थ प्रभवत्यहरागमे

**IAST:** bhūtagrāmaḥ sa evāyaṃ bhūtvā bhūtvā pralīyate |
rātryāgame'vaśaḥ pārtha prabhavatyaharāgame

**Meaning:** Again and again this host of beings is born with the day. And helplessly dissolved with the night.

**Takeaway:** The subjunctive mood of existence.

**Provenance:** sa:8:19, telang:T09, arnold:D10-shared

**Telang [T09]:** "again and" "will of its own"

**Witness [gretil]:** SANDHI-JOINT

## 8:20

**Sanskrit:** परस्तस्मात्तु भावोऽन्योऽव्यक्तोऽव्यक्तात्सनातनः ।
यः स सर्वेषु भूतेषु नश्यत्सु न विनश्यति

**IAST:** parastasmāttu bhāvo'nyo'vyakto'vyaktātsanātanaḥ |
yaḥ sa sarveṣu bhūteṣu naśyatsu na vinaśyati

**Meaning:** But beyond that unseen stands another — eternal, unperceived. Never destroyed when all is destroyed.

**Takeaway:** Behind the cycle, something that never cycles.

**Provenance:** sa:8:20, telang:T09, arnold:D10-shared

**Telang [T09]:** "another entity" "not destroyed"
