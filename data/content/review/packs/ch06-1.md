## 6:1

**Sanskrit:** अनाश्रितः कर्मफलं कार्यं कर्म करोति यः ।
स संन्यासी च योगी च न निरग्निर्न चाक्रियः

**IAST:** anāśritaḥ karmaphalaṃ kāryaṃ karma karoti yaḥ |
sa saṃnyāsī ca yogī ca na niragnirna cākriyaḥ

**Meaning:** Krishna said: whoever does the work that must be done, wanting no reward — that person is both renouncer and disciplined. Not one who just skips rituals or sits idle.

**Takeaway:** Do the work. Skip the theater.

**Provenance:** sa:6:1, telang:T00+T01, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T00]:** "The Deity said :"
**Telang [T01]:** "ought to be performed" "renonnccr"

## 6:2

**Sanskrit:** यं संन्यासमिति प्राहुर्योगं तं विद्धि पाण्डव ।
न ह्यसंन्यस्तसंकल्पो योगी भवति कश्चन

**IAST:** yaṃ saṃnyāsamiti prāhuryogaṃ taṃ viddhi pāṇḍava |
na hyasaṃnyastasaṃkalpo yogī bhavati kaścana

**Meaning:** Know this. What they call renunciation is discipline itself. No one becomes disciplined without giving up selfish plans.

**Takeaway:** Different doors, same house — again, because we forget.

**Provenance:** sa:6:2, telang:T01, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T01]:** "renunciation is  devotion"

## 6:3

**Sanskrit:** आरुरुक्षोर्मुनेर्योगं कर्म कारणमुच्यते ।
योगारूढस्य तस्यैव शमः कारणमुच्यते

**IAST:** ārurukṣormuneryogaṃ karma kāraṇamucyate |
yogārūḍhasya tasyaiva śamaḥ kāraṇamucyate

**Meaning:** For a beginner on the path, action is the vehicle. For one who has arrived, stillness becomes the vehicle.

**Takeaway:** Climb with effort. Rest in arrival.

**Provenance:** sa:6:3, telang:T01, arnold:D02-shared

**Telang [T01]:** "wishes  to rise" "means"

## 6:4

**Sanskrit:** यदा हि नेन्द्रियार्थेषु न कर्मस्वनुषज्जते ।
सर्वसंकल्पसंन्यासी योगारूढस्तदोच्यते

**IAST:** yadā hi nendriyārtheṣu na karmasvanuṣajjate |
sarvasaṃkalpasaṃnyāsī yogārūḍhastadocyate

**Meaning:** When one clings to neither sense-objects nor actions. Having dropped every selfish plan. Then one has truly risen.

**Takeaway:** Freedom is what you stop grabbing.

**Provenance:** sa:6:4, telang:T01, arnold:D02-shared

**Telang [T01]:** "does not attach" "risen to devotion"

## 6:5

**Sanskrit:** उद्धरेदात्मनात्मानं नात्मानमवसादयेत् ।
आत्मैव ह्यात्मनो बन्धुरात्मैव रिपुरात्मनः

**IAST:** uddharedātmanātmānaṃ nātmānamavasādayet |
ātmaiva hyātmano bandhurātmaiva ripurātmanaḥ

**Meaning:** Lift yourself by yourself. Never put yourself down. For your self can be your best friend — and your worst enemy.

**Takeaway:** You are both the patient and the doctor.

**Provenance:** sa:6:5, telang:T01+T03, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T01]:** "elevate" "own self is his"
**Telang [T03]:** "friend" "enemy"

## 6:6

**Sanskrit:** बन्धुरात्मात्मनस्तस्य येनात्मैवात्मना जितः ।
अनात्मनस्तु शत्रुत्वे वर्तेतात्मैव शत्रुवत्

**IAST:** bandhurātmātmanastasya yenātmaivātmanā jitaḥ |
anātmanastu śatrutve vartetātmaiva śatruvat

**Meaning:** For one who has conquered the self, the self is a friend. For one who hasn't, the self behaves like an enemy.

**Takeaway:** Befriend yourself before demanding anything of yourself.

**Provenance:** sa:6:6, telang:T04, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "subjugated" "like an  enemy"

## 6:7

**Sanskrit:** जितात्मनः प्रशान्तस्य परमात्मा समाहितः ।
शीतोष्णसुखदुःखेषु तथा मानापमानयोः

**IAST:** jitātmanaḥ praśāntasya paramātmā samāhitaḥ |
śītoṣṇasukhaduḥkheṣu tathā mānāpamānayoḥ

**Meaning:** The self-controlled self stays steady amid cold and heat, pleasure and pain, honor and disgrace.

**Takeaway:**  Thermometer people react. Thermostat people remain.

**Provenance:** sa:6:7, telang:T04, arnold:D04-shared

## 6:8

**Sanskrit:** ज्ञानविज्ञानतृप्तात्मा कूटस्थो विजितेन्द्रियः ।
युक्त इत्युच्यते योगी समलोष्टाश्मकाञ्चनः

**IAST:** jñānavijñānatṛptātmā kūṭastho vijitendriyaḥ |
yukta ityucyate yogī samaloṣṭāśmakāñcanaḥ

**Meaning:** Content with wisdom and experience, unshaken, senses ruled — to such a one, a lump of earth, a stone, and gold weigh the same. That is discipline.

**Takeaway:** Same weight, mud or gold.

## 6:9

**Sanskrit:** सुहृन्मित्रार्युदासीनमध्यस्थद्वेष्यबन्धुषु ।
साधुष्वपि च पापेषु समबुद्धिर्विशिष्यते

**IAST:** suhṛnmitrāryudāsīnamadhyasthadveṣyabandhuṣu |
sādhuṣvapi ca pāpeṣu samabuddhirviśiṣyate

**Meaning:** Whoever sees alike. Well-wisher, friend, foe, stranger. Mediator, the hateful, kin. Good and wicked alike. Such a one stands tallest of all.

**Takeaway:** The seating chart of an equal mind.

**Provenance:** sa:6:9, telang:T04, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "thinks alike" "good and  the sinful"

## 6:10

**Sanskrit:** योगी युञ्जीत सततमात्मानं रहसि स्थितः ।
एकाकी यतचित्तात्मा निराशीरपरिग्रहः

**IAST:** yogī yuñjīta satatamātmānaṃ rahasi sthitaḥ |
ekākī yatacittātmā nirāśīraparigrahaḥ

**Meaning:** Practice alone, in a clean, quiet place. Body and mind controlled. Wanting nothing. Owning nothing.

**Takeaway:** Solitude is equipment, not escape.

**Provenance:** sa:6:10, telang:T04, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "secret place" "belongings"
