# Pack ch09-4 — Chapter 9: The Kingly Secret

Traditional: The Yoga of Royal Knowledge · Verses 9:31–9:34 · Status: **pending**

## 9:31

**Sanskrit:** क्षिप्रं भवति धर्मात्मा शश्वच्छान्तिं निगच्छति ।
कौन्तेय प्रति जानीहि न मे भक्तः प्रणश्यति

**IAST:** kṣipraṃ bhavati dharmātmā śaśvacchāntiṃ nigacchati |
kaunteya prati jānīhi na me bhaktaḥ praṇaśyati

**Meaning:** Such a one soon grows righteous and finds lasting peace. Proclaim it: My devotee never perishes.

**Takeaway:** The guarantee, in writing.

**Provenance:** sa:9:31, telang:T09, arnold:D17-shared

**Telang [T09]:** "devout of heart" "never ruined"

## 9:32

**Sanskrit:** मां हि पार्थ व्यपाश्रित्य येऽपि स्युः पापयोनयः ।
स्त्रियो वैश्यास्तथा शूद्रास्तेऽपि यान्ति परां गतिम्

**IAST:** māṃ hi pārtha vyapāśritya ye'pi syuḥ pāpayonayaḥ |
striyo vaiśyāstathā śūdrāste'pi yānti parāṃ gatim

**Meaning:** Even the lowborn, women, merchants, laborers — all who take refuge reach the highest goal.

**Takeaway:** The gate has no caste, no gender, no lock.

**Provenance:** sa:9:32, telang:T09, arnold:D17-shared

**Telang [T09]:** "sinful" "supreme goal"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.56), Arnold does not (0.22)

## 9:33

**Sanskrit:** किं पुनर्ब्राह्मणाः पुण्या भक्ता राजर्षयस्तथा ।
अनित्यमसुखं लोकमिमं प्राप्य भजस्व माम्

**IAST:** kiṃ punarbrāhmaṇāḥ puṇyā bhaktā rājarṣayastathā |
anityamasukhaṃ lokamimaṃ prāpya bhajasva mām

**Meaning:** How much more, then, holy priests and royal sages devoted to Me? Landed in this fleeting, painful world — worship Me.

**Takeaway:** If the lowest reach, what excuse has the highest?

**Provenance:** sa:9:33, telang:T09+T10-head, arnold:D17-shared

**Notes:** Split across T09-tail/T10-head.

**Telang [T09]:** "What then (need"
**Telang [T10]:** "royal saints"

## 9:34

**Sanskrit:** मन्मना भव मद्भक्तो मद्याजी मां नमस्कुरु ।
मामेवैष्यसि युक्त्वैवमात्मानं मत्परायणः

**IAST:** manmanā bhava madbhakto madyājī māṃ namaskuru |
māmevaiṣyasi yuktvaivamātmānaṃ matparāyaṇaḥ

**Meaning:** Fix your mind on Me. Love Me. Serve Me. Bow to Me. Make Me your highest goal, your deepest practice — and you will surely reach Me.

**Takeaway:** The whole Gita in four imperatives.

**Provenance:** sa:9:34, telang:T10, arnold:D17-shared

**Telang [T10]:** "transient unhappy" "certainly"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.55), Arnold does not (0.45)
