# Pack ch16-2 — Chapter 16: Two Natures

## 16:11

**Sanskrit:** चिन्तामपरिमेयां च प्रलयान्तामुपाश्रिताः ।
कामोपभोगपरमा एतावदिति निश्चिताः

**IAST:** cintāmaparimeyāṃ ca pralayāntāmupāśritāḥ |
kāmopabhogaparamā etāvaditi niścitāḥ

**Meaning:** Endless anxieties ending only at death. Pleasure as the highest good. 'This much is everything' — so they decide.

**Takeaway:** The creed of the closed horizon.

**Provenance:** sa:16:11, telang:T02, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T02]:** "ending with death" "resolved"

**Witness [gretil]:** SANDHI-JOINT

**Consensus [ORPHAN]:** neither source covers (telang 0.33, arnold 0.33) — check: anxiety, endless, everything, highest

## 16:12

**Sanskrit:** आशापाशशतैर्बद्धाः कामक्रोधपरायणाः ।
ईहन्ते कामभोगार्थमन्यायेनार्थसञ्चयान्

**IAST:** āśāpāśaśatairbaddhāḥ kāmakrodhaparāyaṇāḥ |
īhante kāmabhogārthamanyāyenārthasañcayān

**Meaning:** Netted in a hundred hopes, enslaved to lust and rage, they hoard wealth unjustly to feed desire.

**Takeaway:** Hope as a hunting net.

**Provenance:** sa:16:12, telang:T02+T07-head, arnold:D06-shared

**Notes:** Split across T02-tail/T07-head (glosses + p116 between).

**Telang [T02]:** "nets of hopes" "they wish"
**Telang [T07]:** "unfairly"

## 16:13

**Sanskrit:** इदमद्य मया लब्धमिमं प्राप्स्ये मनोरथम् ।
इदमस्तीदमपि मे भविष्यति पुनर्धनम्

**IAST:** idamadya mayā labdhamimaṃ prāpsye manoratham |
idamastīdamapi me bhaviṣyati punardhanam

**Meaning:** 'I won this today. That wish comes next. This wealth is mine — and that will be too.'

**Takeaway:** The autobiography of greed, first person.

## 16:14

**Sanskrit:** असौ मया हतः शत्रुर्हनिष्ये चापरानपि ।
ईश्वरोऽहमहं भोगी सिद्धोऽहं बलवान्सुखी

**IAST:** asau mayā hataḥ śatrurhaniṣye cāparānapi |
īśvaro'hamahaṃ bhogī siddho'haṃ balavānsukhī

**Meaning:** 'I killed this foe. I will kill the rest. I am lord, enjoyer, perfect, strong, happy. Highborn. Who compares? I will sacrifice, give, and feast.'

**Takeaway:** Ego reciting its own liturgy.

**Provenance:** sa:16:14, telang:T07, arnold:D06-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "I am lord" "like me"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.69), Arnold does not (0.38)

## 16:15

**Sanskrit:** आढ्योऽभिजनवानस्मि कोऽन्योऽस्ति सदृशो मया ।
यक्ष्ये दास्यामि मोदिष्य इत्यज्ञानविमोहिताः

**IAST:** āḍhyo'bhijanavānasmi ko'nyo'sti sadṛśo mayā |
yakṣye dāsyāmi modiṣya ityajñānavimohitāḥ

**Meaning:** Deluded, storm-tossed by schemes, meshed in confusion. Wedded to pleasure. Down they slide into foul hell.

**Takeaway:** The descent, stage by stage.

**Provenance:** sa:16:15, telang:T07, arnold:D06-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "net of delusion" "impure hell"

**Witness [gretil]:** SANDHI-JOINT

**Consensus [ORPHAN]:** neither source covers (telang 0.45, arnold 0.36) — check: mesh, scheme, slide, storm, wedd

## 16:16

**Sanskrit:** अनेकचित्तविभ्रान्ता मोहजालसमावृताः ।
प्रसक्ताः कामभोगेषु पतन्ति नरकेऽशुचौ

**IAST:** anekacittavibhrāntā mohajālasamāvṛtāḥ |
prasaktāḥ kāmabhogeṣu patanti narake'śucau

**Meaning:** Self-worshipping, stubborn, drunk on wealth and pride. Their rites are theater, against every rule.

**Takeaway:** Piety as costume.

**Provenance:** sa:16:16, telang:T07, arnold:D06-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "void  of humility" "only in name"

## 16:17

**Sanskrit:** आत्मसंभाविताः स्तब्धा धनमानमदान्विताः ।
यजन्ते नामयज्ञैस्ते दम्भेनाविधिपूर्वकम्

**IAST:** ātmasaṃbhāvitāḥ stabdhā dhanamānamadānvitāḥ |
yajante nāmayajñaiste dambhenāvidhipūrvakam

**Meaning:** Clutching ego, force, arrogance, lust, and rage — they hate Me in their own flesh and in others'.

**Takeaway:** Hatred of God begins as hatred of neighbor.

**Provenance:** sa:16:17, telang:T07, arnold:D06-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "arrogance" "in  those of others"

## 16:18

**Sanskrit:** अहंकारं बलं दर्पं कामं क्रोधं च संश्रिताः ।
मामात्मपरदेहेषु प्रद्विषन्तोऽभ्यसूयकाः

**IAST:** ahaṃkāraṃ balaṃ darpaṃ kāmaṃ krodhaṃ ca saṃśritāḥ |
māmātmaparadeheṣu pradviṣanto'bhyasūyakāḥ

**Meaning:** These cruel, vile, lowest enemies I hurl. Again and again into demonic wombs.

**Takeaway:** The sentence, pronounced plainly.

**Provenance:** sa:16:18, telang:T07, arnold:D06-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "meanest  of men" "hurl down"

## 16:19

क्षिपाम्यजस्रमशुभानासुरीष्वेव योनिषु

**Takeaway:** The long road away.
