# Pack ch05-2 — Chapter 5: The Art of Letting Go

Traditional: The Yoga of Renunciation · Verses 5:11–5:20 · Status: **pending**

## 5:11

**Sanskrit:** कायेन मनसा बुद्ध्या केवलैरिन्द्रियैरपि ।
योगिनः कर्म कुर्वन्ति सङ्गं त्यक्त्वात्मशुद्धये

**IAST:** kāyena manasā buddhyā kevalairindriyairapi |
yoginaḥ karma kurvanti saṅgaṃ tyaktvātmaśuddhaye

**Meaning:** Dropping results, one gains the highest peace. But the undisciplined, hungry for results, stay chained by desire.

**Takeaway:** Two workers, same work, opposite fates.

**Provenance:** sa:5:11, telang:T10, arnold:F03-shared

**Telang [T10]:** "without devotion" "tied down"

## 5:12

**Sanskrit:** युक्तः कर्मफलं त्यक्त्वा शान्तिमाप्नोति नैष्ठिकीम् ।
अयुक्तः कामकारेण फले सक्तो निबध्यते

**IAST:** yuktaḥ karmaphalaṃ tyaktvā śāntimāpnoti naiṣṭhikīm |
ayuktaḥ kāmakāreṇa phale sakto nibadhyate

**Meaning:** The self-ruled self sits at ease in the nine-gated city of the body. Neither acting. Nor causing action.

**Takeaway:** You are the resident, not the building.

**Provenance:** sa:5:12, telang:T10, arnold:F03-shared

**Telang [T10]:** "city of nine portals" "causing"

## 5:13

**Sanskrit:** सर्वकर्माणि मनसा संन्यस्यास्ते सुखं वशी ।
नवद्वारे पुरे देही नैव कुर्वन्न कारयन्

**IAST:** sarvakarmāṇi manasā saṃnyasyāste sukhaṃ vaśī |
navadvāre pure dehī naiva kurvanna kārayan

**Meaning:** The Lord starts no one's actions, nor anyone's capacity to act, nor links deeds to results. Nature alone does all that.

**Takeaway:** God doesn't micromanage. Nature runs the office.

**Provenance:** sa:5:13, telang:T10, arnold:F03-shared

**Telang [T10]:** "not the cause" "nature only works"

## 5:14

**Sanskrit:** न कर्तृत्वं न कर्माणि लोकस्य सृजति प्रभुः ।
न कर्मफलसंयोगं स्वभावस्तु प्रवर्तते

**IAST:** na kartṛtvaṃ na karmāṇi lokasya sṛjati prabhuḥ |
na karmaphalasaṃyogaṃ svabhāvastu pravartate

**Meaning:** The Lord takes on no one's sin or merit. Wisdom lies covered by ignorance — and so all beings wander confused.

**Takeaway:** The sun is shining. The curtains are yours.

**Provenance:** sa:5:14, telang:T10, arnold:F03-shared

**Telang [T10]:** "receives  no one"

## 5:15

**Sanskrit:** नादत्ते कस्यचित्पापं न चैव सुकृतं विभुः ।
अज्ञानेनावृतं ज्ञानं तेन मुह्यन्ति जन्तवः

**IAST:** nādatte kasyacitpāpaṃ na caiva sukṛtaṃ vibhuḥ |
ajñānenāvṛtaṃ jñānaṃ tena muhyanti jantavaḥ

**Meaning:** But those whose ignorance wisdom destroys — for them, wisdom shines like the sun on the highest truth.

**Takeaway:** Light doesn't argue with darkness. It just arrives.

**Provenance:** sa:5:15, telang:T10, arnold:F03-shared

**Telang [T10]:** "destroyed that  ignorance"

## 5:16

**Sanskrit:** ज्ञानेन तु तदज्ञानं येषां नाशितमात्मनः ।
तेषामादित्यवज्ज्ञानं प्रकाशयति तत्परम्

**IAST:** jñānena tu tadajñānaṃ yeṣāṃ nāśitamātmanaḥ |
teṣāmādityavajjñānaṃ prakāśayati tatparam

**Meaning:** Minds fixed on That. Selves merged in That. Devoted to That. That as their final goal. They never return, their wrongs washed away by wisdom.

**Takeaway:** Point everything at one thing.

**Provenance:** sa:5:16, telang:T10, arnold:F03-shared

**Telang [T10]:** "mind is" "never to return"

## 5:17

**Sanskrit:** तद्‌बुद्धयस्तदात्मानस्तन्निष्ठास्तत्परायणाः ।
गच्छन्त्यपुनरावृत्तिं ज्ञाननिर्धूतकल्मषाः

**IAST:** tad‌buddhayastadātmānastanniṣṭhāstatparāyaṇāḥ |
gacchantyapunarāvṛttiṃ jñānanirdhūtakalmaṣāḥ

**Meaning:** The wise see the same in a learned humble priest, a cow, an elephant, a dog — even a dog-eating outcast.

**Takeaway:** The book's most radical sentence. Sit with it.

**Provenance:** sa:5:17, telang:T10, arnold:F03-shared

**Telang [T10]:** "look upon" "as alike"

## 5:18

**Sanskrit:** विद्याविनयसंपन्ने ब्राह्मणे गवि हस्तिनि ।
शुनि चैव श्वपाके च पण्डिताः समदर्शिनः

**IAST:** vidyāvinayasaṃpanne brāhmaṇe gavi hastini |
śuni caiva śvapāke ca paṇḍitāḥ samadarśinaḥ

**Meaning:** Even here on earth, the steady-minded conquer rebirth. For ultimate reality is flawless and steady. And in it they rest.

**Takeaway:** Win the war before the battlefield.

**Provenance:** sa:5:18, telang:T10+T11-head, arnold:F03-shared

**Telang [T10]:** "conquered" "rest in"
**Telang [T11]:** "Brahman"

## 5:19

**Sanskrit:** इहैव तैर्जितः सर्गो येषां साम्ये स्थितं मनः ।
निर्दोषं हि समं ब्रह्म तस्माद्ब्रह्मणि ते स्थिताः

**IAST:** ihaiva tairjitaḥ sargo yeṣāṃ sāmye sthitaṃ manaḥ |
nirdoṣaṃ hi samaṃ brahma tasmādbrahmaṇi te sthitāḥ

**Meaning:** One who knows ultimate reality, mind steady, undeluded, resting in it. Such a one neither cheers at pleasant things nor mourns unpleasant ones.

**Takeaway:** Good news and bad news get the same face.

**Provenance:** sa:5:19, telang:T11, arnold:F04-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "does he grieve"

## 5:20

**Sanskrit:** न प्रहृष्येत्प्रियं प्राप्य नोद्विजेत्प्राप्य चाप्रियम् ।
स्थिरबुद्धिरसंमूढो ब्रह्मविद्ब्रह्मणि स्थितः

**IAST:** na prahṛṣyetpriyaṃ prāpya nodvijetprāpya cāpriyam |
sthirabuddhirasaṃmūḍho brahmavidbrahmaṇi sthitaḥ

**Meaning:** Detached from outer contacts, one finds the happiness inside the self. Joined to ultimate reality through focus. Unbreakable happiness.

**Takeaway:** The happiness that needs nothing external.

**Provenance:** sa:5:20, telang:T11, arnold:F04-shared

**Telang [T11]:** "indestructible  happiness"
