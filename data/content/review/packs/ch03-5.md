# Pack ch03-5 — Chapter 3: Selfless Action

Traditional: The Yoga of Action · Verses 3:41–3:43 · Status: **pending**

## 3:41

**Sanskrit:** तस्मात्त्वमिन्द्रियाण्यादौ नियम्य भरतर्षभ ।
पाप्मानं प्रजहि ह्येनं ज्ञानविज्ञाननाशनम्

**IAST:** tasmāttvamindriyāṇyādau niyamya bharatarṣabha |
pāpmānaṃ prajahi hyenaṃ jñānavijñānanāśanam

**Meaning:** So first rein in the senses, Arjuna, then strike down this evil thing that murders wisdom and learning.

**Takeaway:** Gates first, fortress second.

**Provenance:** sa:3:41, telang:T13, arnold:C06-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "restrain your senses" "sinful"

## 3:42

**Sanskrit:** इन्द्रियाणि पराण्याहुरिन्द्रियेभ्यः परं मनः ।
मनसस्तु परा बुद्धिर्यो बुद्धेः परतस्तु सः

**IAST:** indriyāṇi parāṇyāhurindriyebhyaḥ paraṃ manaḥ |
manasastu parā buddhiryo buddheḥ paratastu saḥ

**Meaning:** The senses are great. Greater than the senses is the mind. Greater than the mind is understanding. And greater than understanding is That.

**Takeaway:** Climb the ladder rung by rung.

**Provenance:** sa:3:42, telang:T13, arnold:C06-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "Great  are the senses" "is that Thus"

## 3:43

**Sanskrit:** एवं बुद्धेः परं बुद्ध्वा संस्तभ्यात्मानमात्मना ।
जहि शत्रुं महाबाहो कामरूपं दुरासदम्

**IAST:** evaṃ buddheḥ paraṃ buddhvā saṃstabhyātmānamātmanā |
jahi śatruṃ mahābāho kāmarūpaṃ durāsadam

**Meaning:** Know That which lies beyond understanding. Steady yourself by yourself. And destroy this elusive enemy called desire.

**Takeaway:** Last word of the chapter: destroy it.

**Provenance:** sa:3:43, telang:T13+T15, arnold:C06-shared

**Telang [T13]:** 
**Telang [T15]:** "mighty arms" "unmanageable enemy"
