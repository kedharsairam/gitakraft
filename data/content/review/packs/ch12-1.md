## 12:1 (अर्जुन)

**Sanskrit:** एवं सततयुक्ता ये भक्तास्त्वां पर्युपासते ।
ये चाप्यक्षरमव्यक्तं तेषां के योगवित्तमाः

**IAST:** evaṃ satatayuktā ye bhaktāstvāṃ paryupāsate |
ye cāpyakṣaramavyaktaṃ teṣāṃ ke yogavittamāḥ

**Meaning:** Arjuna said: two kinds of worshippers. Those devoted to Your visible form. And those seeking the unseen imperishable. Who knows discipline best?

**Takeaway:** The chapter's single question. Everything answers it.

**Provenance:** sa:12:1, telang:T00+T01, arnold:D01

**Telang [T00]:** "Ar^na said :"
**Telang [T01]:** "constantly devoted" "best know"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.58), Arnold does not (0.33)

## 12:2

**Sanskrit:** श्रीभगवानुवाच
मय्यावेश्य मनो ये मां नित्ययुक्ता उपासते ।
श्रद्धया परयोपेतास्ते मे युक्ततमा मताः

**IAST:** śrībhagavānuvāca
mayyāveśya mano ye māṃ nityayuktā upāsate |
śraddhayā parayopetāste me yuktatamā matāḥ

**Meaning:** Krishna said: those constantly devoted. Full of highest faith. Minds fixed on Me. I count them most disciplined.

**Takeaway:** The verdict first. Reasons after.

**Provenance:** sa:12:2, telang:T02+T03-head, arnold:D02-shared

**Telang [T02]:** "The Deity said :"
**Telang [T03]:** "highest faith" "most  devoted"

## 12:3

**Sanskrit:** ये त्वक्षरमनिर्देश्यमव्यक्तं पर्युपासते ।
सर्वत्रगमचिन्त्यं च कूटस्थमचलं ध्रुवम्

**IAST:** ye tvakṣaramanirdeśyamavyaktaṃ paryupāsate |
sarvatragamacintyaṃ ca kūṭasthamacalaṃ dhruvam

**Meaning:** But others restrain every sense. Minds steady at all times. Meditating on the indescribable, imperishable, unseen.

**Takeaway:** The harder road, described fairly.

**Provenance:** sa:12:3, telang:T03+T05-head, arnold:D02-shared

## 12:4

**Sanskrit:** संनियम्येन्द्रियग्रामं सर्वत्र समबुद्धयः ।
ते प्राप्नुवन्ति मामेव सर्वभूतहिते रताः

**IAST:** saṃniyamyendriyagrāmaṃ sarvatra samabuddhayaḥ |
te prāpnuvanti māmeva sarvabhūtahite ratāḥ

**Meaning:** The all-pervading, unthinkable, changeless, constant. Working for the good of all beings. They too reach Me.

**Takeaway:** Both roads arrive. Costs differ.

**Provenance:** sa:12:4, telang:T05, arnold:D02-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "all-pervading" "attain to me"

## 12:5

**Sanskrit:** क्लेशोऽधिकतरस्तेषामव्यक्तासक्तचेतसाम् ।
अव्यक्ता हि गतिर्दुःखं देहवद्भिरवाप्यते

**IAST:** kleśo'dhikatarasteṣāmavyaktāsaktacetasām |
avyaktā hi gatirduḥkhaṃ dehavadbhiravāpyate

**Meaning:** Still, the unseen path strains embodied beings sorely. The formless goal comes hard to those with bodies.

**Takeaway:** Honest advertising for the abstract.

**Provenance:** sa:12:5, telang:T05, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "much greater" "difficulty"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Arnold covers (0.64), Telang does not (0.45)

## 12:6

**Sanskrit:** ये तु सर्वाणि कर्माणि मयि संन्यस्य मत्पराः ।
अनन्येनैव योगेन मां ध्यायन्त उपासते

**IAST:** ye tu sarvāṇi karmāṇi mayi saṃnyasya matparāḥ |
ananyenaiva yogena māṃ dhyāyanta upāsate

**Meaning:** But those who dedicate every act to Me. Holding Me highest. Worshipping with love for none besides.

**Takeaway:** The easier road, with one condition: exclusivity.

**Provenance:** sa:12:6, telang:T05, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "dedicating" "none besides me"

## 12:7

**Sanskrit:** तेषामहं समुद्धर्ता मृत्युसंसारसागरात् ।
भवामि नचिरात्पार्थ मय्यावेशितचेतसाम्

**IAST:** teṣāmahaṃ samuddhartā mṛtyusaṃsārasāgarāt |

**Meaning:** minds fixed on Me — I swiftly lift them from the ocean of mortal death.

**Takeaway:** Swiftness promised. Ocean crossed.

**Provenance:** sa:12:7, telang:T05, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "without delay" "deliverer"

## 12:8

**Sanskrit:** मय्येव मन आधत्स्व मयि बुद्धिं निवेशय ।
निवसिष्यसि मय्येव अत ऊर्ध्वं न संशयः

**IAST:** mayyeva mana ādhatsva mayi buddhiṃ niveśaya |
nivasiṣyasi mayyeva ata ūrdhvaṃ na saṃśayaḥ

**Meaning:** Fix your mind on Me alone, your understanding too. In Me you will dwell hereafter. No doubt.

**Takeaway:** The instruction in two lines.

**Provenance:** sa:12:8, telang:T05, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "fix your understanding" "no doubt"

**Witness [gretil]:** SANDHI-JOINT

## 12:9

**Sanskrit:** अथ चित्तं समाधातुं न शक्नोषि मयि स्थिरम् ।
अभ्यासयोगेन ततो मामिच्छाप्तुं धनंजय

**IAST:** atha cittaṃ samādhātuṃ na śaknoṣi mayi sthiram |
abhyāsayogena tato māmicchāptuṃ dhanaṃjaya

**Meaning:** If you cannot steady your mind on Me, then reach Me through constant practice of meditation.

**Takeaway:** Failure path one: practice.

**Provenance:** sa:12:9, telang:T05, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "unable to fix" "continuous meditation"

## 12:10

**Sanskrit:** अभ्यासेऽप्यसमर्थोऽसि मत्कर्मपरमो भव ।
मदर्थमपि कर्माणि कुर्वन्सिद्धिमवाप्स्यसि

**IAST:** abhyāse'pyasamartho'si matkarmaparamo bhava |
madarthamapi karmāṇi kurvansiddhimavāpsyasi

**Meaning:** If even practice defeats you, work for My sake above all. Acting for Me, you will reach perfection.

**Takeaway:** Failure path two: dedicated work.

**Provenance:** sa:12:10, telang:T05, arnold:D03-shared

**Telang [T05]:** "unequal" "attain perfection"
