# Pack ch05-1 — Chapter 5: The Art of Letting Go

Traditional: The Yoga of Renunciation · Verses 5:1–5:10 · Status: **pending**

## 5:1 (अर्जुन)

**Sanskrit:** संन्यासं कर्मणां कृष्ण पुनर्योगं च शंससि ।
यच्छ्रेय एतयोरेकं तन्मे ब्रूहि सुनिश्चितम्

**IAST:** saṃnyāsaṃ karmaṇāṃ kṛṣṇa punaryogaṃ ca śaṃsasi |
yacchreya etayorekaṃ tanme brūhi suniścitam

**Meaning:** Arjuna said: you praise giving up actions, and also doing them. Tell me plainly which of the two is better.

**Takeaway:** When two goods compete, ask which is greater.

**Provenance:** sa:5:1, telang:T00+T01, arnold:F01

**Telang [T00]:** "Ar^una said :"
**Telang [T01]:** "praise renunciation" "superior"

**Witness [gretil]:** SANDHI-JOINT

## 5:2

**Sanskrit:** संन्यासः कर्मयोगश्च निःश्रेयसकरावुभौ ।
तयोस्तु कर्मसंन्यासात्कर्मयोगो विशिष्यते

**IAST:** saṃnyāsaḥ karmayogaśca niḥśreyasakarāvubhau |
tayostu karmasaṃnyāsātkarmayogo viśiṣyate

**Meaning:** Krishna said: both giving up and doing lead to freedom. But of the two, disciplined doing is the better path.

**Takeaway:** Act, don't abstain.

**Provenance:** sa:5:2, telang:T02+T03-head, arnold:F02

**Telang [T02]:** "The Deity said :"
**Telang [T03]:** "instruments of happiness"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.50), Arnold does not (0.25)

## 5:3

**Sanskrit:** ज्ञेयः स नित्यसंन्यासी यो न द्वेष्टि न काङ्क्षति ।
निर्द्वन्द्वो हि महाबाहो सुखं बन्धात्प्रमुच्यते

**IAST:** jñeyaḥ sa nityasaṃnyāsī yo na dveṣṭi na kāṅkṣati |
nirdvandvo hi mahābāho sukhaṃ bandhātpramucyate

**Meaning:** Know that the true renouncer is one with no dislikes and no cravings. Free of opposites, such a one is easily freed from chains.

**Takeaway:** Renunciation is a state of mind, not an empty calendar.

**Provenance:** sa:5:3, telang:T03, arnold:F03-shared

**Telang [T03]:** "always an ascetic" "released from"

## 5:4

**Sanskrit:** सांख्ययोगौ पृथग्बालाः प्रवदन्ति न पण्डिताः ।
एकमप्यास्थितः सम्यगुभयोर्विन्दते फलम्

**IAST:** sāṃkhyayogau pṛthagbālāḥ pravadanti na paṇḍitāḥ |
ekamapyāsthitaḥ samyagubhayorvindate phalam

**Meaning:** Only children, not wise people, say understanding and disciplined action are different things. Master either one fully and you reap both rewards.

**Takeaway:** Different doors, same room.

**Provenance:** sa:5:4, telang:T03+T04-head, arnold:F03-shared

**Telang [T03]:** "Children"
**Telang [T04]:** "tinct" "fruit  of both"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.58), Arnold does not (0.42)

## 5:5

**Sanskrit:** यत्सांख्यैः प्राप्यते स्थानं तद्योगैरपि गम्यते ।
एकं सांख्यं च योगं च यः पश्यति स: पश्यति

**IAST:** yatsāṃkhyaiḥ prāpyate sthānaṃ tadyogairapi gamyate |
ekaṃ sāṃkhyaṃ ca yogaṃ ca yaḥ paśyati sa: paśyati

**Meaning:** The peace that thinkers reach, doers reach too. Whoever sees understanding and action as one — sees truly.

**Takeaway:** Stop choosing between them.

**Provenance:** sa:5:5, telang:T04, arnold:F03-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "seat" "as one"
**Telang [T04]:** "fruit  of both"

## 5:6

**Sanskrit:** संन्यासस्तु महाबाहो दुःखमाप्तुमयोगतः ।
योगयुक्तो मुनिर्ब्रह्म नचिरेणाधिगच्छति

**IAST:** saṃnyāsastu mahābāho duḥkhamāptumayogataḥ |
yogayukto munirbrahma nacireṇādhigacchati

**Meaning:** Giving up without discipline is hard and painful. But the sage grounded in discipline soon reaches ultimate reality.

**Takeaway:** You cannot skip to the end.

**Provenance:** sa:5:6, telang:T04, arnold:F03-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "difficult to reach" "without delay"

## 5:7

**Sanskrit:** योगयुक्तो विशुद्धात्मा विजितात्मा जितेन्द्रियः ।
सर्वभूतात्मभूतात्मा कुर्वन्नपि न लिप्यते

**IAST:** yogayukto viśuddhātmā vijitātmā jitendriyaḥ |
sarvabhūtātmabhūtātmā kurvannapi na lipyate

**Meaning:** One whose self is pure. Self controlled. Senses ruled. Who sees the same self in every being. Acts without getting stained.

**Takeaway:** Clean hands, full schedule.

**Provenance:** sa:5:7, telang:T04, arnold:F03-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "pure" "not tainted"

## 5:8

**Sanskrit:** नैव किंचित्करोमीति युक्तो मन्येत तत्त्ववित् ।
पश्यञ्श्रृण्वन्स्पृशञ्जिघ्रन्नश्नन्‌गच्छन्स्वपञ्श्वसन्

**IAST:** naiva kiṃcitkaromīti yukto manyeta tattvavit |
paśyañśrṛṇvanspṛśañjighrannaśnan‌gacchansvapañśvasan

**Meaning:** The disciplined knower thinks 'I do nothing at all.' This while seeing, hearing, touching, smelling, eating, moving. Sleeping, breathing, speaking, excreting, grasping. Opening and closing the eyes.

**Takeaway:** Watch yourself live for one hour as a spectator.

**Provenance:** sa:5:8, telang:T04, arnold:F03-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "thinks he does  nothing"

## 5:9

**Sanskrit:** प्रलपन्विसृजन्गृह्णन्नुन्मिषन्निमिषन्नपि ।
इन्द्रियाणीन्द्रियार्थेषु वर्तन्त इति धारयन्

**IAST:** pralapanvisṛjangṛhṇannunmiṣannimiṣannapi |
indriyāṇīndriyārtheṣu vartanta iti dhārayan

**Meaning:** Casting off clinging, one who acts as an offering to ultimate reality stays unstained. Like a lotus leaf untouched by water.

**Takeaway:** The book's most beautiful image of freedom.

**Provenance:** sa:5:9, telang:T04+T10-head, arnold:F03-shared

**Telang [T04]:** "casting off" "lotus-leaf"
**Telang [T10]:** "egoistic notions"

**Witness [gretil]:** SANDHI-JOINT

## 5:10

**Sanskrit:** ब्रह्मण्याधाय कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा करोति यः ।
लिप्यते न स पापेन पद्मपत्रमिवाम्भसा

**IAST:** brahmaṇyādhāya karmāṇi saṅgaṃ tyaktvā karoti yaḥ |
lipyate na sa pāpena padmapatramivāmbhasā

**Meaning:** Devotees act for self-purification alone. With body, mind, understanding — even senses. Dropping all clinging.

**Takeaway:** Use everything you have. Cling to nothing.

**Provenance:** sa:5:10, telang:T10, arnold:F03-shared

**Telang [T10]:** "abandoning the fruit" "tranquillity"
