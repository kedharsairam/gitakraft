# Pack ch07-1 — Chapter 7: Knowing the Whole

Traditional: The Yoga of Knowledge and Wisdom · Verses 7:1–7:10 · Status: **pending**

## 7:1

**Sanskrit:** असंशयं समग्रं मां यथा ज्ञास्यसि तच्छृणु

**IAST:** asaṃśayaṃ samagraṃ māṃ yathā jñāsyasi tacchṛṇu

**Meaning:** Krishna said: hear how you can know Me fully, beyond all doubt. Fix your mind on Me. Rest in Me. Practice discipline.

**Takeaway:** The chapter's promise, stated up front.

**Provenance:** sa:7:1, telang:T00+T01, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T00]:** "The Deity said :"
**Telang [T01]:** "now hear how you can without" "know me fully"

## 7:2

**Sanskrit:** ज्ञानं तेऽहं सविज्ञानमिदं वक्ष्याम्यशेषतः ।
यज्ज्ञात्वा नेह भूयोऽन्यज्ज्ञातव्यमवशिष्यते

**IAST:** jñānaṃ te'haṃ savijñānamidaṃ vakṣyāmyaśeṣataḥ |
yajjñātvā neha bhūyo'nyajjñātavyamavaśiṣyate

**Meaning:** I will tell you the whole story. Both knowledge and lived experience. Once known, nothing more remains to be known.

**Takeaway:** Theory plus practice equals done.

**Provenance:** sa:7:2, telang:T01, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T01]:** "tell you exhaustively" "further left"

## 7:3

**Sanskrit:** मनुष्याणां सहस्रेषु कश्चिद्यतति सिद्धये ।
यततामपि सिद्धानां कश्चिन्मां वेत्ति तत्त्वतः

**IAST:** manuṣyāṇāṃ sahasreṣu kaścidyatati siddhaye |
yatatāmapi siddhānāṃ kaścinmāṃ vetti tattvataḥ

**Meaning:** Among thousands, hardly one strives for perfection. And among those who strive, hardly one knows Me as I truly am.

**Takeaway:** Rarity is not discouragement. It is direction.

**Provenance:** sa:7:3, telang:T01, arnold:D02

**Telang [T01]:** "Among thousands of men" "only some know me truly"

## 7:4

**Sanskrit:** भूमिरापोऽनलो वायुः खं मनो बुद्धिरेव च ।
अहंकार इतीयं मे भिन्ना प्रकृतिरष्टधा

**IAST:** bhūmirāpo'nalo vāyuḥ khaṃ mano buddhireva ca |
ahaṃkāra itīyaṃ me bhinnā prakṛtiraṣṭadhā

**Meaning:** Earth, water, fire, air, space. Mind, understanding, ego. These eight make up My lower nature.

**Takeaway:** The inventory of everything you can touch or think.

**Provenance:** sa:7:4, telang:T01+T02-head, arnold:D03

**Notes:** Split across T01-tail/T02-head (page-74 break residue).

**Telang [T01]:** "Earth, water, fire"
**Telang [T02]:** "and egoism" "eightfold"

## 7:5

**Sanskrit:** अपरेयमितस्त्वन्यां प्रकृतिं विद्धि मे पराम् ।
जीवभूतां महाबाहो ययेदं धार्यते जगत्

**IAST:** apareyamitastvanyāṃ prakṛtiṃ viddhi me parām |
jīvabhūtāṃ mahābāho yayedaṃ dhāryate jagat

**Meaning:** But beyond this lower nature sits My higher nature. It is alive, mighty-armed one. And it holds up the whole universe.

**Takeaway:** Matter is the instrument. Life is the player.

**Provenance:** sa:7:5, telang:T02, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T02]:** "another (form of my) nature" "universe is upheld"

## 7:6

**Sanskrit:** एतद्योनीनि भूतानि सर्वाणीत्युपधारय ।
अहं कृत्स्नस्य जगतः प्रभवः प्रलयस्तथा

**IAST:** etadyonīni bhūtāni sarvāṇītyupadhāraya |
ahaṃ kṛtsnasya jagataḥ prabhavaḥ pralayastathā

**Meaning:** Know that all beings spring from these two natures. I am the birth and the ending of the entire universe.

**Takeaway:** Every arrival and every departure passes through one door.

**Provenance:** sa:7:6, telang:T02, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T02]:** "all things have these" "producer and the destroyer"

## 7:7

**Sanskrit:** मत्तः परतरं नान्यत्किंचिदस्ति धनंजय ।
मयि सर्वमिदं प्रोतं सूत्रे मणिगणा इव

**IAST:** mattaḥ parataraṃ nānyatkiṃcidasti dhanaṃjaya |
mayi sarvamidaṃ protaṃ sūtre maṇigaṇā iva

**Meaning:** Nothing stands higher than Me, Arjuna. All this hangs upon Me, like pearls strung on a thread.

**Takeaway:** One string. Many pearls.

**Provenance:** sa:7:7, telang:T02, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T02]:** "nothing else" "pearls upon a thread"

## 7:8

**Sanskrit:** रसोऽहमप्सु कौन्तेय प्रभास्मि शशिसूर्ययोः ।
प्रणवः सर्ववेदेषु शब्दः खे पौरुषं नृषु

**IAST:** raso'hamapsu kaunteya prabhāsmi śaśisūryayoḥ |
praṇavaḥ sarvavedeṣu śabdaḥ khe pauruṣaṃ nṛṣu

**Meaning:** I am the taste in water. The light in sun and moon. The sacred syllable in the scriptures. Sound in space. Courage in human beings.

**Takeaway:** Find the divine in the nearest things first.

**Provenance:** sa:7:8, telang:T02, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T02]:** "taste in water" "manliness in human beings"

## 7:9

**Sanskrit:** पुण्यो गन्धः पृथिव्यां च तेजश्चास्मि विभावसौ ।
जीवनं सर्वभूतेषु तपश्चास्मि तपस्विषु

**IAST:** puṇyo gandhaḥ pṛthivyāṃ ca tejaścāsmi vibhāvasau |
jīvanaṃ sarvabhūteṣu tapaścāsmi tapasviṣu

**Meaning:** I am the sweet smell in earth. The glow in fire. The life in all beings. And the discipline in the disciplined.

**Takeaway:** The signature inside every sense.

**Provenance:** sa:7:9, telang:T02, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T02]:** "fragrant smell" "penance"

## 7:10

**Sanskrit:** बीजं मां सर्वभूतानां विद्धि पार्थ सनातनम् ।
बुद्धिर्बुद्धिमतामस्मि तेजस्तेजस्विनामहम्

**IAST:** bījaṃ māṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ viddhi pārtha sanātanam |
buddhirbuddhimatāmasmi tejastejasvināmaham

**Meaning:** Know Me as the timeless seed of all beings. I am the wisdom of the wise and the splendor of the splendid.

**Takeaway:** Every excellence has one address.

**Provenance:** sa:7:10, telang:T02, arnold:D04-shared

**Telang [T02]:** "eternal seed" "glory of the glorious"
