# Pack ch04-4 — Chapter 4: Wisdom in Action

Traditional: The Yoga of Knowledge and Renunciation · Verses 4:31–4:40 · Status: **pending**

## 4:31

**Sanskrit:** यज्ञशिष्टामृतभुजो यान्ति ब्रह्म सनातनम् ।
नायं लोकोऽस्त्ययज्ञस्य कुतोऽन्यः कुरुसत्तम

**IAST:** yajñaśiṣṭāmṛtabhujo yānti brahma sanātanam |
nāyaṃ loko'styayajñasya kuto'nyaḥ kurusattama

**Meaning:** Those who taste the nectar left from sacrifice reach the eternal ultimate reality. This world is not for the non-sacrificer — how then the next?

**Takeaway:** You cannot skip giving and arrive at having.

**Provenance:** sa:4:31, telang:T13, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "nectar-like leavings" "perform no sacrifice"

## 4:32

**Sanskrit:** एवं बहुविधा यज्ञा वितता ब्रह्मणो मुखे ।
कर्मजान्विद्धि तान्सर्वानेवं ज्ञात्वा विमोक्ष्यसे

**IAST:** evaṃ bahuvidhā yajñā vitatā brahmaṇo mukhe |
karmajānviddhi tānsarvānevaṃ jñātvā vimokṣyase

**Meaning:** So sacrifices of many kinds lie spread in the Vedas. Know them all born of action — and knowing this, you will be free.

**Takeaway:** Map the territory, then walk out of it.

**Provenance:** sa:4:32, telang:T13, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "various sorts" "released"

## 4:33

**Sanskrit:** श्रेयान्द्रव्यमयाद्यज्ञाज्ज्ञानयज्ञः परन्तप ।
सर्वं कर्माखिलं पार्थ ज्ञाने परिसमाप्यते

**IAST:** śreyāndravyamayādyajñājjñānayajñaḥ parantapa |
sarvaṃ karmākhilaṃ pārtha jñāne parisamāpyate

**Meaning:** The sacrifice of wisdom beats any gift of wealth. Every action is fully contained in wisdom.

**Takeaway:** Understanding outranks offerings.

**Provenance:** sa:4:33, telang:T13, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "sacrifice of knowledge" "comprehended in knowledge"

## 4:34

**Sanskrit:** तद्विद्धि प्रणिपातेन परिप्रश्नेन सेवया ।
उपदेक्ष्यन्ति ते ज्ञानं ज्ञानिनस्तत्त्वदर्शिनः

**IAST:** tadviddhi praṇipātena paripraśnena sevayā |
upadekṣyanti te jñānaṃ jñāninastattvadarśinaḥ

**Meaning:** Learn it by bowing down, by asking, by serving. Those who see truth will teach you wisdom.

**Takeaway:** The student contract: humility, questions, service.

**Provenance:** sa:4:34, telang:T13, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "salutation" "will  teach"

## 4:35

**Sanskrit:** यज्ज्ञात्वा न पुनर्मोहमेवं यास्यसि पाण्डव ।
येन भूतान्यशेषेण द्रक्ष्यस्यात्मन्यथो मयि

**IAST:** yajjñātvā na punarmohamevaṃ yāsyasi pāṇḍava |
yena bhūtānyaśeṣeṇa drakṣyasyātmanyatho mayi

**Meaning:** Learning it, you will never fall into this confusion again. Through it you will see all beings — every one — first in yourself, then in Me.

**Takeaway:** Other people are yourself, wearing different faces.

**Provenance:** sa:4:35, telang:T13, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "Having learnt" "first in yourself"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.88), Arnold does not (0.38)

## 4:36

**Sanskrit:** अपि चेदसि पापेभ्यः सर्वेभ्यः पापकृत्तमः ।
सर्वं ज्ञानप्लवेनैव वृजिनं सन्तरिष्यसि

**IAST:** api cedasi pāpebhyaḥ sarvebhyaḥ pāpakṛttamaḥ |
sarvaṃ jñānaplavenaiva vṛjinaṃ santariṣyasi

**Meaning:** Even if you are the worst sinner alive, you will cross all wrongdoing on the raft of wisdom alone.

**Takeaway:** No one is past rescuing. No one.

**Provenance:** sa:4:36, telang:T13, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "most sinful" "boat of knowledge"

**Consensus [ORPHAN]:** neither source covers (telang 0.44, arnold 0.22) — check: live, raft, sinner, wrongdo

## 4:37

**Sanskrit:** यथैधांसि समिद्धोऽग्निर्भस्मसात्कुरुतेऽर्जुन ।
ज्ञानाग्निः सर्वकर्माणि भस्मसात्कुरुते तथा

**IAST:** yathaidhāṃsi samiddho'gnirbhasmasātkurute'rjuna |
jñānāgniḥ sarvakarmāṇi bhasmasātkurute tathā

**Meaning:** As a blazing fire turns fuel to ash, so wisdom's fire turns all actions to ash.

**Takeaway:** The second great fire image — it returns because it works.

**Provenance:** sa:4:37, telang:T13, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "fire well kindled" "reduces"

## 4:38

**Sanskrit:** न हि ज्ञानेन सदृशं पवित्रमिह विद्यते ।
तत्स्वयं योगसंसिद्धः कालेनात्मनि विन्दति

**IAST:** na hi jñānena sadṛśaṃ pavitramiha vidyate |
tatsvayaṃ yogasaṃsiddhaḥ kālenātmani vindati

**Meaning:** Nothing purifies in this world like wisdom. In time, the self, perfected in discipline, finds it within.

**Takeaway:** The treasure was inside the house all along.

**Provenance:** sa:4:38, telang:T13+T14-head, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T13]:** "sanctification like knowledge" "finds"
**Telang [T14]:** "within one"

## 4:39

**Sanskrit:** श्रद्धावाँल्लभते ज्ञानं तत्परः संयतेन्द्रियः ।
ज्ञानं लब्ध्वा परां शान्तिमचिरेणाधिगच्छति

**IAST:** śraddhāvām̐llabhate jñānaṃ tatparaḥ saṃyatendriyaḥ |
jñānaṃ labdhvā parāṃ śāntimacireṇādhigacchati

**Meaning:** The faithful one, senses restrained, devoted — gains wisdom. Gaining wisdom, one soon reaches supreme peace.

**Takeaway:** Faith, focus, devotion: the three keys on one ring.

**Provenance:** sa:4:39, telang:T14, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T14]:** "obtains  knowledge"

## 4:40

**Sanskrit:** अज्ञश्चाश्रद्दधानश्च संशयात्मा विनश्यति ।
नायं लोकोऽस्ति न परो न सुखं संशयात्मनः

**IAST:** ajñaścāśraddadhānaśca saṃśayātmā vinaśyati |
nāyaṃ loko'sti na paro na sukhaṃ saṃśayātmanaḥ

**Meaning:** The ignorant, the faithless, the doubting self destroys itself. Neither this world nor the next — nor any happiness — belongs to the doubter.

**Takeaway:** Doubt, not failure, is the dead end.

**Provenance:** sa:4:40, telang:T14, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T14]:** "nor happiness"
