# Pack ch09-1 — Chapter 9: The Kingly Secret

Traditional: The Yoga of Royal Knowledge · Verses 9:1–9:10 · Status: **pending**

## 9:1

**Sanskrit:** इदं तु ते गुह्यतमं प्रवक्ष्याम्यनसूयवे ।
ज्ञानं विज्ञानसहितं यज्ज्ञात्वा मोक्ष्यसेऽशुभात्

**IAST:** idaṃ tu te guhyatamaṃ pravakṣyāmyanasūyave |
jñānaṃ vijñānasahitaṃ yajjñātvā mokṣyase'śubhāt

**Meaning:** Krishna said: to you who never carp, I will speak the deepest secret. Knowledge married to experience. Knowing it, you escape all evil.

**Takeaway:** The king of secrets, for an open mind.

**Provenance:** sa:9:1, telang:T00, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T00]:** "not given to  carping" "released from evil"

## 9:2

**Sanskrit:** राजविद्या राजगुह्यं पवित्रमिदमुत्तमम् ।
प्रत्यक्षावगमं धर्म्यं सुसुखं कर्तुमव्ययम्

**IAST:** rājavidyā rājaguhyaṃ pavitramidamuttamam |
pratyakṣāvagamaṃ dharmyaṃ susukhaṃ kartumavyayam

**Meaning:** It is the king of sciences, the king of mysteries. It purifies. It lasts forever. It asks nothing against duty. It is seen directly and done with ease.

**Takeaway:** Six praises. Memorize the weight of what follows.

**Provenance:** sa:9:2, telang:T00+T03-head, arnold:D01-shared

**Notes:** Split across T00-tail/T03-head.

**Telang [T00]:** "chief among the  sciences" "imperishable, not"
**Telang [T03]:** "opposed to the sacred law" "easy to practise"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.77), Arnold does not (0.38)

## 9:3

**Sanskrit:** अश्रद्दधानाः पुरुषा धर्मस्यास्य परन्तप ।
अप्राप्य मां निवर्तन्ते मृत्युसंसारवर्त्मनि

**IAST:** aśraddadhānāḥ puruṣā dharmasyāsya parantapa |
aprāpya māṃ nivartante mṛtyusaṃsāravartmani

**Meaning:** Whoever lacks faith in this truth never reaches Me. Such people sink back into the mortal round.

**Takeaway:** Faith is the ticket. Doubt is the undertow.

**Provenance:** sa:9:3, telang:T03, arnold:D02

**Telang [T03]:** "faitli" "mortal world"

## 9:4

**Sanskrit:** मया ततमिदं सर्वं जगदव्यक्तमूर्तिना ।
मत्स्थानि सर्वभूतानि न चाहं तेष्ववस्थितः

**IAST:** mayā tatamidaṃ sarvaṃ jagadavyaktamūrtinā |
matsthāni sarvabhūtāni na cāhaṃ teṣvavasthitaḥ

**Meaning:** I fill this whole universe in unseen form. All beings live in Me. But I do not live in them.

**Takeaway:** Contained by none, containing all.

**Provenance:** sa:9:4, telang:T03, arnold:D03

**Telang [T03]:** "pervaded" "live in them"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.86), Arnold does not (0.43)

## 9:5

**Sanskrit:** न च मत्स्थानि भूतानि पश्य मे योगमैश्वरम् ।
भूतभृन्न च भूतस्थो ममात्मा भूतभावनः

**IAST:** na ca matsthāni bhūtāni paśya me yogamaiśvaram |
bhūtabhṛnna ca bhūtastho mamātmā bhūtabhāvanaḥ

**Meaning:** And yet — beings do not live in Me either. See the mystery of My power. I uphold all beings and make all beings. Yet My self rests in none of them.

**Takeaway:** Both true at once. Sit with that.

**Provenance:** sa:9:5, telang:T03, arnold:D04

**Telang [T03]:** "Nor yet" "divine power"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.80), Arnold does not (0.40)

## 9:6

**Sanskrit:** यथाकाशस्थितो नित्यं वायुः सर्वत्रगो महान् ।
तथा सर्वाणि भूतानि मत्स्थानीत्युपधारय

**IAST:** yathākāśasthito nityaṃ vāyuḥ sarvatrago mahān |
tathā sarvāṇi bhūtāni matsthānītyupadhāraya

**Meaning:** As the mighty wind roams everywhere within space — yet space holds it lightly — so all beings rest in Me.

**Takeaway:** The sky is never burdened by weather.

**Provenance:** sa:9:6, telang:T03, arnold:D05

**Telang [T03]:** "ubiquitous atmosphere" "similarly"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.50), Arnold does not (0.30)

## 9:7

**Sanskrit:** सर्वभूतानि कौन्तेय प्रकृतिं यान्ति मामिकाम् ।
कल्पक्षये पुनस्तानि कल्पादौ विसृजाम्यहम्

**IAST:** sarvabhūtāni kaunteya prakṛtiṃ yānti māmikām |
kalpakṣaye punastāni kalpādau visṛjāmyaham

**Meaning:** When an age ends, all beings fold back into My nature. When the next begins, I pour them forth again.

**Takeaway:** The cosmos breathes. I am the lungs.

**Provenance:** sa:9:7, telang:T03, arnold:D06

**Telang [T03]:** "expiration" "bring them forth"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.73), Arnold does not (0.45)

## 9:8

**Sanskrit:** प्रकृतिं स्वामवष्टभ्य विसृजामि पुनः पुनः ।
भूतग्राममिमं कृत्स्नमवशं प्रकृतेर्वशात्

**IAST:** prakṛtiṃ svāmavaṣṭabhya visṛjāmi punaḥ punaḥ |
bhūtagrāmamimaṃ kṛtsnamavaśaṃ prakṛtervaśāt

**Meaning:** Guiding My own nature, I create this whole gathering again and again. Helpless before nature's force, they come.

**Takeaway:** The potter's wheel never stops. Neither does the Potter.

**Provenance:** sa:9:8, telang:T03, arnold:D07

**Telang [T03]:** "Taking the control" "again and again"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.56), Arnold does not (0.44)

## 9:9

**Sanskrit:** न च मां तानि कर्माणि निबध्नन्ति धनंजय ।
उदासीनवदासीनमसक्तं तेषु कर्मसु

**IAST:** na ca māṃ tāni karmāṇi nibadhnanti dhanaṃjaya |
udāsīnavadāsīnamasaktaṃ teṣu karmasu

**Meaning:** But these acts never bind Me. I sit apart, unconcerned, unattached to the doing.

**Takeaway:** The busiest hands in the universe, the freest heart.

**Provenance:** sa:9:9, telang:T03, arnold:D08

**Telang [T03]:** "fetter" "unconcerned"

## 9:10

**Sanskrit:** मयाध्यक्षेण प्रकृतिः सूयते सचराचरम् ।
हेतुनानेन कौन्तेय जगद्विपरिवर्तते

**IAST:** mayādhyakṣeṇa prakṛtiḥ sūyate sacarācaram |
hetunānena kaunteya jagadviparivartate

**Meaning:** Through Me as witness, nature births everything that moves or stands still. And so the universe turns.

**Takeaway:** One witness. Endless motion.

**Provenance:** sa:9:10, telang:T03, arnold:D09

**Telang [T03]:** "supervisor" "revolves"
