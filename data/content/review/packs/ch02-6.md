# Pack ch02-6 — Chapter 2: Wisdom of the Soul

Traditional: The Yoga of Knowledge · Verses 2:51–2:60 · Status: **pending**

## 2:51

**Sanskrit:** कर्मजं बुद्धियुक्ता हि फलं त्यक्त्वा मनीषिणः ।
जन्मबन्धविनिर्मुक्ताः पदं गच्छन्त्यनामयम्

**IAST:** karmajaṃ buddhiyuktā hi phalaṃ tyaktvā manīṣiṇaḥ |
janmabandhavinirmuktāḥ padaṃ gacchantyanāmayam

**Meaning:** The wise join themselves to understanding. They drop the fruits of action. They walk free of rebirth's chains, to the place beyond sorrow.

**Takeaway:** The destination has a name: beyond sorrow.

**Provenance:** sa:2:51, telang:T16, arnold:B13-shared

**Telang [T16]:** "seat"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.85), Arnold does not (0.38)

## 2:52

**Sanskrit:** यदा ते मोहकलिलं बुद्धिर्व्यतितरिष्यति ।
तदा गन्तासि निर्वेदं श्रोतव्यस्य श्रुतस्य च

**IAST:** yadā te mohakalilaṃ buddhirvyatitariṣyati |
tadā gantāsi nirvedaṃ śrotavyasya śrutasya ca

**Meaning:** When your understanding crosses past confusion, you will turn indifferent. Indifferent to everything heard — or yet to be heard.

**Takeaway:** Outgrow even the scriptures — it says so itself.

**Provenance:** sa:2:52, telang:T16, arnold:B13-shared

**Telang [T16]:** "crossed beyond" "heard ,or will hear"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.75), Arnold does not (0.12)

## 2:53

**Sanskrit:** श्रुतिविप्रतिपन्ना ते यदा स्थास्यति निश्चला ।
समाधावचला बुद्धिस्तदा योगमवाप्स्यसि

**IAST:** śrutivipratipannā te yadā sthāsyati niścalā |
samādhāvacalā buddhistadā yogamavāpsyasi

**Meaning:** Your understanding is confused by hearsay today. When it stands firm and steady in contemplation, you will reach discipline.

**Takeaway:** Hold still long enough and stillness holds you.

**Provenance:** sa:2:53, telang:T16, arnold:B13-shared

**Telang [T16]:** "stand" "acquire devotion"

## 2:54 (अर्जुन)

**Sanskrit:** स्थितप्रज्ञस्य का भाषा समाधिस्थस्य केशव ।
स्थितधीः किं प्रभाषेत किमासीत व्रजेत किम्

**IAST:** sthitaprajñasya kā bhāṣā samādhisthasya keśava |
sthitadhīḥ kiṃ prabhāṣeta kimāsīta vrajeta kim

**Meaning:** Arjuna said: what marks the steady-minded, Krishna? How does such a one speak, sit, walk?

**Takeaway:** The student asks the practical question.

**Provenance:** sa:2:54, telang:T17+T18, arnold:B14

**Telang [T17]:** "Ar4nina said :"
**Telang [T18]:** "characteristics" "how move"

## 2:55

**Sanskrit:** श्रीभगवानुवाच
प्रजहाति यदा कामान्सर्वान्पार्थ मनोगतान् ।
आत्मन्येवात्मना तुष्टः स्थितप्रज्ञस्तदोच्यते

**IAST:** śrībhagavānuvāca
prajahāti yadā kāmānsarvānpārtha manogatān |
ātmanyevātmanā tuṣṭaḥ sthitaprajñastadocyate

**Meaning:** When one drops all the mind's desires and rests content in the self, by the self — that one is called steady.

**Takeaway:** Want nothing, have everything.

**Provenance:** sa:2:55, telang:T18+T19-head, arnold:B15-shared

**Telang [T19]:** "The Deity said :"
**Telang [T20]:** "abandons all" "self only"
**Telang [T21]:** "by his self" "steady mind"

## 2:56

**Sanskrit:** दुःखेष्वनुद्विग्नमनाः सुखेषु विगतस्पृहः ।
वीतरागभयक्रोधः स्थितधीर्मुनिरुच्यते

**IAST:** duḥkheṣvanudvignamanāḥ sukheṣu vigataspṛhaḥ |
vītarāgabhayakrodhaḥ sthitadhīrmunirucyate

**Meaning:** Unshaken in sorrow. Uninterested in pleasures. Free of craving, fear, and anger. Such a sage is steady.

**Takeaway:** Steadiness has four enemies. Name yours.

**Provenance:** sa:2:56, telang:T19, arnold:B15-shared

**Telang [T21]:** "not agitated" "steady mind"

## 2:57

**Sanskrit:** यः सर्वत्रानभिस्नेहस्तत्तत्प्राप्य शुभाशुभम् ।
नाभिनन्दति न द्वेष्टि तस्य प्रज्ञा प्रतिष्ठिता

**IAST:** yaḥ sarvatrānabhisnehastattatprāpya śubhāśubham |
nābhinandati na dveṣṭi tasya prajñā pratiṣṭhitā

**Meaning:** Untied everywhere. Neither thrilled by good fortune nor disgusted by bad. Such understanding is steady.

**Takeaway:** Equanimity is not indifference. It is freedom.

**Provenance:** sa:2:57, telang:T19, arnold:B15-shared

**Telang [T21]:** "without" "agreeable and"

## 2:58

**Sanskrit:** यदा संहरते चायं कूर्मोऽङ्गानीव सर्वशः ।
इन्द्रियाणीन्द्रियार्थेभ्यस्तस्य प्रज्ञा प्रतिष्ठिता

**IAST:** yadā saṃharate cāyaṃ kūrmo'ṅgānīva sarvaśaḥ |
indriyāṇīndriyārthebhyastasya prajñā pratiṣṭhitā

**Meaning:** When one pulls the senses back from their objects, as a tortoise pulls in its limbs — understanding holds steady.

**Takeaway:** The tortoise is the book's favorite yogi.

**Provenance:** sa:2:58, telang:T19, arnold:B15-shared

**Telang [T21]:** "withdraws his senses" "tortoise"

## 2:59

**Sanskrit:** विषया विनिवर्तन्ते निराहारस्य देहिनः ।
रसवर्जं रसोऽप्यस्य परं दृष्ट्वा निवर्तते

**IAST:** viṣayā vinivartante nirāhārasya dehinaḥ |
rasavarjaṃ raso'pyasya paraṃ dṛṣṭvā nivartate

**Meaning:** Sense objects fade for the abstinent — but the taste lingers. Even taste leaves once the highest is seen.

**Takeaway:** Willpower starves; vision cures.

**Provenance:** sa:2:59, telang:T19, arnold:B15-shared

**Telang [T21]:** "withdraw" "seen the Supreme"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.70), Arnold does not (0.40)

## 2:60

**Sanskrit:** यततो ह्यपि कौन्तेय पुरुषस्य विपश्चितः ।
इन्द्रियाणि प्रमाथीनि हरन्ति प्रसभं मनः

**IAST:** yatato hyapi kaunteya puruṣasya vipaścitaḥ |
indriyāṇi pramāthīni haranti prasabhaṃ manaḥ

**Meaning:** The senses are turbulent. They violently carry off the mind — even of a striving wise man.

**Takeaway:** Respect the opponent.

**Provenance:** sa:2:60, telang:T19, arnold:B15-shared

**Telang [T21]:** "boisterous senses" "exerts"
