# Pack ch16-1 — Chapter 16: Two Natures

Traditional: Divine and Demonic Natures · Verses 16:1–16:10 · Status: **pending**

## 16:1

**Sanskrit:** अभयं सत्त्वसंशुद्धिर्ज्ञानयोगव्यवस्थितिः ।
दानं दमश्च यज्ञश्च स्वाध्यायस्तप आर्जवम्

**IAST:** abhayaṃ sattvasaṃśuddhirjñānayogavyavasthitiḥ |
dānaṃ damaśca yajñaśca svādhyāyastapa ārjavam

**Meaning:** Krishna said: fearlessness, pure heart. Steady wisdom-seeking. Generosity, restraint, sacrifice. Study, discipline, honesty.

**Takeaway:** The divine inventory opens.

**Provenance:** sa:16:1, telang:T00, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T00]:** "purity of heart" "abstraction"

**Witness [gretil]:** SANDHI-JOINT

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.54), Arnold does not (0.31)

## 16:2

**Sanskrit:** अहिंसा सत्यमक्रोधस्त्यागः शान्तिरपैशुनम् ।
दया भूतेष्वलोलुप्त्वं मार्दवं ह्रीरचापलम्

**IAST:** ahiṃsā satyamakrodhastyāgaḥ śāntirapaiśunam |
dayā bhūteṣvaloluptvaṃ mārdavaṃ hrīracāpalam

**Meaning:** Harmlessness, truth, calm anger. Charity, peace, no backbiting. Pity for all beings.

**Takeaway:** Virtues two through eleven.

**Provenance:** sa:16:2, telang:T00, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T00]:** "straightforwardness" "tranquillity"

## 16:3

**Sanskrit:** तेजः क्षमा धृतिः शौचमद्रोहो नातिमानिता ।
भवन्ति संपदं दैवीमभिजातस्य भारत

**IAST:** tejaḥ kṣamā dhṛtiḥ śaucamadroho nātimānitā |
bhavanti saṃpadaṃ daivīmabhijātasya bhārata

**Meaning:** No greed, gentleness, modesty, no idleness. Nobility, forgiveness, courage, purity. No cruelty, no conceit. These mark the heaven-born.

**Takeaway:** The list closes. The birthmark named.

**Provenance:** sa:16:3, telang:T00, arnold:D01-shared

**Telang [T00]:** "compassion" "godlike"

**Consensus:** SINGLE-SOURCE — Telang covers (0.50), Arnold does not (0.33)

अज्ञानं चाभिजातस्य पार्थ संपदमासुरीम्

## 16:5

**Sanskrit:** दैवी संपद्विमोक्षाय निबन्धायासुरी मता ।
मा शुचः संपदं दैवीमभिजातोऽसि पाण्डव

**IAST:** daivī saṃpadvimokṣāya nibandhāyāsurī matā |
mā śucaḥ saṃpadaṃ daivīmabhijāto'si pāṇḍava

**Meaning:** The divine leads outward to freedom. The demonic inward to chains. Do not grieve — you are heaven-born.

**Takeaway:** Two doors. Your birth chose correctly.

**Provenance:** sa:16:5, telang:T02, arnold:D03

**Telang [T02]:** "bondage" "Grieve  not"

**Consensus [ORPHAN]:** neither source covers (telang 0.40, arnold 0.30) — check: chain, divin, inward, lead, outward

## 16:6

**Sanskrit:** द्वौ भूतसर्गौ लोकेऽस्मिन्दैव आसुर एव च ।
दैवो विस्तरशः प्रोक्त आसुरं पार्थ मे शृणु

**IAST:** dvau bhūtasargau loke'smindaiva āsura eva ca |
daivo vistaraśaḥ prokta āsuraṃ pārtha me śṛṇu

**Meaning:** Two races walk this world: the divine and the demonic. The divine you know. Now hear the demonic.

**Takeaway:** The chapter's subject turns.

**Provenance:** sa:16:6, telang:T02, arnold:D04

**Telang [T02]:** "two classes" "described at length"

**Witness [gretil]:** SANDHI-JOINT

## 16:7

**Sanskrit:** प्रवृत्तिं च निवृत्तिं च जना न विदुरासुराः ।
न शौचं नापि चाचारो न सत्यं तेषु विद्यते

**IAST:** pravṛttiṃ ca nivṛttiṃ ca janā na vidurāsurāḥ |
na śaucaṃ nāpi cācāro na satyaṃ teṣu vidyate

**Meaning:** They know neither right action nor restraint. Neither purity, nor honor, nor truth lives in them.

**Takeaway:** Four absences define the type.

**Provenance:** sa:16:7, telang:T02, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T02]:** "inaction" "veracity"

## 16:9

**Sanskrit:** एतां दृष्टिमवष्टभ्य नष्टात्मानोऽल्पबुद्धयः ।
प्रभवन्त्युग्रकर्माणः क्षयाय जगतोऽहिताः

**IAST:** etāṃ dṛṣṭimavaṣṭabhya naṣṭātmāno'lpabuddhayaḥ |

**Meaning:** Wrecked selves, small minds, cruel deeds. Holding this view, they are born to ruin the world.

**Takeaway:** Ideas have casualties.

**Provenance:** sa:16:9, telang:T02, arnold:D05-shared

**Telang [T02]:** "little knowledge" "destruction"

मोहाद्गृहीत्वासद्ग्राहान्प्रवर्तन्तेऽशुचिव्रताः
