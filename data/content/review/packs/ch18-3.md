# Pack ch18-3 — Chapter 18: Freedom and Surrender

Traditional: Liberation and Renunciation · Verses 18:21–18:30 · Status: **pending**

## 18:21

**Sanskrit:** पृथक्त्वेन तु यज्ज्ञानं नानाभावान्पृथग्विधान् ।
वेत्ति सर्वेषु भूतेषु तज्ज्ञानं विद्धि राजसम्

**IAST:** pṛthaktvena tu yajjñānaṃ nānābhāvānpṛthagvidhān |
vetti sarveṣu bhūteṣu tajjñānaṃ viddhi rājasam

**Meaning:** Seeing only scattered manyness in every being. Separate and apart. That is passionate wisdom.

**Takeaway:** Division as doctrine.

**Provenance:** sa:18:21, telang:T07, arnold:D10-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "distinctions" "various entities"

## 18:22

**Sanskrit:** यत्तु कृत्स्नवदेकस्मिन्कार्ये सक्तमहैतुकम् ।
अतत्त्वार्थवदल्पं च तत्तामसमुदाहृतम्

**IAST:** yattu kṛtsnavadekasminkārye saktamahaitukam |
atattvārthavadalpaṃ ca tattāmasamudāhṛtam

**Meaning:** Clinging to one fragment as the whole, reasonless, petty, narrow — that is dark wisdom.

**Takeaway:** The part mistaken for the all.

**Provenance:** sa:18:22, telang:T07, arnold:D10-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "clings  to one" "insignificant"

## 18:23

**Sanskrit:** नियतं सङ्गरहितमरागद्वेषतः कृतम् ।
अफलप्रेप्सुना कर्म यत्तत्सात्त्विकमुच्यते

**IAST:** niyataṃ saṅgarahitamarāgadveṣataḥ kṛtam |
aphalaprepsunā karma yattatsāttvikamucyate

**Meaning:** Duty done detached, without likes or dislikes. Wanting no fruit. That is clear action.

**Takeaway:** The cleanest definition of right action.

**Provenance:** sa:18:23, telang:T07, arnold:D11-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "devoid of attachment" "not wishing"

## 18:24

**Sanskrit:** यत्तु कामेप्सुना कर्म साहंकारेण वा पुनः ।
क्रियते बहुलायासं तद्राजसमुदाहृतम्

**IAST:** yattu kāmepsunā karma sāhaṃkāreṇa vā punaḥ |
kriyate bahulāyāsaṃ tadrājasamudāhṛtam

**Meaning:** Action straining with craving and ego. Heavy with effort. That is passionate action.

**Takeaway:** Sweat with self in it.

**Provenance:** sa:18:24, telang:T07, arnold:D11-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "much trouble" "egotism"

## 18:25

**Sanskrit:** अनुबन्धं क्षयं हिंसामनवेक्ष्य च पौरुषम् ।
मोहादारभ्यते कर्म यत्तत्तामसमुच्यते

**IAST:** anubandhaṃ kṣayaṃ hiṃsāmanavekṣya ca pauruṣam |
mohādārabhyate karma yattattāmasamucyate

**Meaning:** Action begun in delusion. Blind to cost, harm, injury, or capacity. That is dark action.

**Takeaway:** Blind doing, double damage.

**Provenance:** sa:18:25, telang:T07, arnold:D11-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "commenced" "injury"

## 18:26

**Sanskrit:** मुक्तसङ्गोऽनहंवादी धृत्युत्साहसमन्वितः ।
सिद्ध्यसिद्ध्योर्निर्विकारः कर्ता सात्त्विक उच्यते

**IAST:** muktasaṅgo'nahaṃvādī dhṛtyutsāhasamanvitaḥ |
siddhyasiddhyornirvikāraḥ kartā sāttvika ucyate

**Meaning:** Free of clinging and self-talk, brave, energetic, steady in win or loss — that is the clear doer.

**Takeaway:** The portrait of the steady worker.

**Provenance:** sa:18:26, telang:T07, arnold:D12-shared

**Telang [T07]:** "egotistic talk" "ill-success"

## 18:27

**Sanskrit:** रागी कर्मफलप्रेप्सुर्लुब्धो हिंसात्मकोऽशुचिः ।
हर्षशोकान्वितः कर्ता राजसः परिकीर्तितः

**IAST:** rāgī karmaphalaprepsurlubdho hiṃsātmako'śuciḥ |
harṣaśokānvitaḥ kartā rājasaḥ parikīrtitaḥ

**Meaning:** Greedy for results, grasping, cruel, impure, tossed by joys and sorrows — that is the passionate doer.

**Takeaway:** The portrait of the hungry worker.

**Provenance:** sa:18:27, telang:T07+T09-head, arnold:D12-shared

**Notes:** Split across T07-tail/T09-head (consequence-gloss T08 between).

**Telang [T07]:** "full of affections"
**Telang [T09]:** "covetous" "joy and sorrow"

## 18:28

**Sanskrit:** अयुक्तः प्राकृतः स्तब्धः शठो नैष्कृतिकोऽलसः ।
विषादी दीर्घसूत्री च कर्ता तामस उच्यते

**IAST:** ayuktaḥ prākṛtaḥ stabdhaḥ śaṭho naiṣkṛtiko'lasaḥ |
viṣādī dīrghasūtrī ca kartā tāmasa ucyate

**Meaning:** Slack, vulgar, stubborn, cheating. Spiteful, lazy, gloomy, stalling. That is the dark doer.

**Takeaway:** Eight stains, one worker.

**Provenance:** sa:18:28, telang:T09, arnold:D12-shared

**Telang [T09]:** "crafty" "slow"

## 18:29

**Sanskrit:** बुद्धेर्भेदं धृतेश्चैव गुणतस्त्रिविधं शृणु ।
प्रोच्यमानमशेषेण पृथक्त्वेन धनंजय

**IAST:** buddherbhedaṃ dhṛteścaiva guṇatastrividhaṃ śṛṇu |
procyamānamaśeṣeṇa pṛthaktvena dhanaṃjaya

**Meaning:** Now hear wisdom and willpower. Each threefold by nature. Told fully and distinctly.

**Takeaway:** Two faculties. Three shades each.

**Provenance:** sa:18:29, telang:T09, arnold:D13

**Telang [T09]:** "intelligence  and courage" "exhaustively"

## 18:30

**Sanskrit:** प्रवृत्तिं च निवृत्तिं च कार्याकार्ये भयाभये ।
बन्धं मोक्षं च या वेत्ति बुद्धिः सा पार्थ सात्त्विकी

**IAST:** pravṛttiṃ ca nivṛttiṃ ca kāryākārye bhayābhaye |
bandhaṃ mokṣaṃ ca yā vetti buddhiḥ sā pārtha sāttvikī

**Meaning:** Knowing duty from non-duty, courage from fear, bondage from freedom — that is clear wisdom.

**Takeaway:** Discrimination as a superpower.

**Provenance:** sa:18:30, telang:T09, arnold:D14-shared

**Telang [T09]:** "ought not to be done" "emancipation"
