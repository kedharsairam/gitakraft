# Pack ch06-5 — Chapter 6: The Disciplined Mind

Traditional: The Yoga of Self-Control · Verses 6:41–6:47 · Status: **pending**

## 6:41

**Sanskrit:** प्राप्य पुण्यकृतां लोकानुषित्वा शाश्वतीः समाः ।
शुचीनां श्रीमतां गेहे योगभ्रष्टोऽभिजायते

**IAST:** prāpya puṇyakṛtāṃ lokānuṣitvā śāśvatīḥ samāḥ |
śucīnāṃ śrīmatāṃ gehe yogabhraṣṭo'bhijāyate

**Meaning:** Fallen from discipline, one reaches the worlds of the good. Dwells there long years. Then is born into a pure, prosperous family.

**Takeaway:** Failure gets a good address and a second chance.

**Provenance:** sa:6:41, telang:T17, arnold:D12-shared

**Telang [T17]:** "fallen from devotion" "holy and"

## 6:42

**Sanskrit:** अथवा योगिनामेव कुले भवति धीमताम् ।
एतद्धि दुर्लभतरं लोके जन्म यदीदृशम्

**IAST:** athavā yogināmeva kule bhavati dhīmatām |
etaddhi durlabhataraṃ loke janma yadīdṛśam

**Meaning:** Or one is born into a family of wise practitioners. A birth like that is the hardest thing in this world to get.

**Takeaway:** Some second chances are rarer than first ones.

**Provenance:** sa:6:42, telang:T17, arnold:D12-shared

**Telang [T17]:** "talented devotees" "works for perfection"

## 6:43

**Sanskrit:** तत्र तं बुद्धिसंयोगं लभते पौर्वदेहिकम् ।
यतते च ततो भूयः संसिद्धौ कुरुनन्दन

**IAST:** tatra taṃ buddhisaṃyogaṃ labhate paurvadehikam |
yatate ca tato bhūyaḥ saṃsiddhau kurunandana

**Meaning:** There one regains the understanding of a former life. And strives again toward perfection.

**Takeaway:** Nothing learned is ever truly lost.

**Provenance:** sa:6:43, telang:T17, arnold:D12-shared

**Telang [T17]:** "contact" "former body"

## 6:44

**Sanskrit:** पूर्वाभ्यासेन तेनैव ह्रियते ह्यवशोऽपि सः ।
जिज्ञासुरपि योगस्य शब्दब्रह्मातिवर्तते

**IAST:** pūrvābhyāsena tenaiva hriyate hyavaśo'pi saḥ |
jijñāsurapi yogasya śabdabrahmātivartate

**Meaning:** Drawn on by former practice. Even unwilling, one rises above scriptural rules.

**Takeaway:** Momentum outlives memory.

**Provenance:** sa:6:44, telang:T17+T18-head, arnold:D12-shared

**Telang [T17]:** "led awa^"
**Telang [T18]:** "former practice" "rises above"

## 6:45

**Sanskrit:** प्रयत्नाद्यतमानस्तु योगी संशुद्धकिल्बिषः ।
अनेकजन्मसंसिद्धस्ततो याति परां गतिम्

**IAST:** prayatnādyatamānastu yogī saṃśuddhakilbiṣaḥ |
anekajanmasaṃsiddhastato yāti parāṃ gatim

**Meaning:** But the practitioner who strives hard. Cleansed of wrong. Perfected over many births. Reaches the highest goal.

**Takeaway:** Many births. Keep going.

**Provenance:** sa:6:45, telang:T18, arnold:D12-shared

**Telang [T18]:** "great efforts" "supreme goal"

## 6:46

**Sanskrit:** तपस्विभ्योऽधिको योगी ज्ञानिभ्योऽपि मतोऽधिकः ।
कर्मिभ्यश्चाधिको योगी तस्माद्योगी भवार्जुन

**IAST:** tapasvibhyo'dhiko yogī jñānibhyo'pi mato'dhikaḥ |
karmibhyaścādhiko yogī tasmādyogī bhavārjuna

**Meaning:** Higher than penance-doers, higher than scholars, higher even than ritualists is the practitioner. So become one, Arjuna.

**Takeaway:** The final ranking, stated without apology.

**Provenance:** sa:6:46, telang:T18, arnold:D12-shared

**Telang [T18]:** "higher than" "become a devotee"

## 6:47

**Sanskrit:** योगिनामपि सर्वेषां मद्गतेनान्तरात्मना ।
श्रद्धावान् भजते यो मां स मे युक्ततमो मतः

**IAST:** yogināmapi sarveṣāṃ madgatenāntarātmanā |
śraddhāvān bhajate yo māṃ sa me yuktatamo mataḥ

**Meaning:** And among all practitioners: the one who worships Me with faith. Inmost self merged in Me. That one I hold most devoted.

**Takeaway:** The chapter's last word: love wins.

**Provenance:** sa:6:47, telang:T18, arnold:D12-shared

**Telang [T18]:** "most devoted"
